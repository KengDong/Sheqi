# Candidate Selection & False-Negative Policy V1
## 早期高召回，后期高精度；不按名额强杀候选

# 0. Core Principle

The funnel is NOT:
> every gate cuts the pool in half until one remains.

It is:
> **cheap early gates maximize recall; expensive later gates maximize precision.**

Reason:
- early AI/research tests are noisy and cheap;
- false negatives destroy upside permanently;
- false positives can still be killed later;
- prose, serialization and human-reader tests are more expensive but much more diagnostic.

Therefore:
> **do not trade away potentially great books just to make the candidate count look tidy.**

# 1. No Forced Quota

Counts such as:
> 6 -> 4 -> 3 -> 2 -> 1

are planning estimates only.

They are NOT elimination quotas.

A Gate may produce:
- 5 survivors;
- 2 survivors;
- 0 survivors.

Decision must follow evidence, not a predetermined slot count.

# 2. Three Statuses

Every non-final candidate is one of:

## ACTIVE
Has enough positive evidence to justify the next expensive test.

## RESERVE
Not leading now, but has:
- positive evidence on at least one important axis;
- a distinctive mode not duplicated by ACTIVE candidates;
- a known weakness that the current Gate is poorly equipped to judge.

Reserve candidates are NOT dead.

Each Reserve must record:
> reactivation trigger.

## DROP
Use only when evidence shows a structural failure or repeated same-axis failure.

# 3. When DROP Is Justified

A candidate may be dropped when at least one is true:

### A. Structural zero
Examples:
- no repeatable engine;
- obvious derivative/legal problem;
- production burden incompatible with first-time + AI model;
- no plausible recurring social/relationship layer;
- premise cannot honestly be packaged clearly.

### B. Repeated behavioral failure on the SAME critical axis
Default:
> at least two independent signals.

Examples:
- two clean readers both understand the package but do not want to click;
- multiple prose readers voluntarily stop in the same region for the same root reason;
- author + target readers independently reject the same core experience.

### C. Root mutation already tried once and the same failure remains
No endless rescue.

# 4. What Does NOT Justify DROP Alone

Do NOT kill merely because:
- not Top 3 in one Shelf Reader;
- one platform is weaker;
- title has repairable friction;
- one AI reader prefers another genre;
- Reservoir is uneven but still contains high-desire scenes;
- character layer is not yet mature before Character Gate;
- AI production cost is slightly higher;
- candidate is currently #4 instead of #2.

These are:
> risk / routing / reserve signals.

# 5. Mutable vs Structural Dimensions

## More mutable
- title;
- blurb;
- platform routing;
- opening density;
- protagonist presentation;
- scene order;
- chapter-end hook.

Weakness here should usually trigger:
> repair / route / reserve,
not immediate Concept death.

## More structural
- repeatable engine;
- reward sameness;
- no-fresh-gimmick survival;
- long-run state compounding;
- story supply;
- originality;
- production variance;
- whether recurring humans can generate scenes.

Weakness here deserves stronger kill weight.

# 6. Diversity Insurance

Until at least P3:

> retain 1–2 RESERVE candidates with genuinely different story modes if they have non-zero reader pull.

Purpose:
- prevent the market/AI-production filters from collapsing all survivors into one genre or operating model;
- protect high-story-pressure/high-emotion candidates whose strengths may only appear in prose.

Diversity Reserve does NOT mean saving obviously weak books.
It means:
> do not use a packaging gate to eliminate a candidate whose main advantage is scene/story execution.

# 7. Evidence Matrix, Not Average Score

Track each candidate across independent axes:

- Packaging Pull
- Premise Clarity
- Story Reservoir
- Character Pull
- P0 Continuation
- P1 Delivery
- P3 Variation
- Opening Arc Payoff
- P7 Post-Novelty Retention
- Production Sustainability
- Platform Fit
- Originality Risk

Do NOT calculate one weighted total.

A book may be:
- medium packaging;
- exceptional prose;
- strong long-run engine.

That is different from:
- strong packaging;
- weak prose;
- no long-run supply.

The Gate should identify:
> which axis is actually failing.

# 8. Candidate Count Philosophy

Recommended INTERNAL portfolio, not quotas:

- Post-Concept/Packaging: ~4–6 ACTIVE/RESERVE combined.
- Reservoir: ~4–5 can still be tested cheaply.
- P0: ~3–4.
- P1/P3: ~2–3.
- Opening/P7: ~2.
- P20/P50: 1 primary contender + 1 challenger if evidence remains close.

Author-visible:
> normally only 1–2 strongest current artifacts.

Important:
> **author-visible count and internal survivor count are different.**

This preserves low author burden without premature Concept death.

# 9. Champion + Challenger

Once one candidate leads clearly:

## Champion
Receives the next main resource allocation.

## Challenger
A different-mode candidate with real evidence remains alive until the Champion clears a deeper behavior gate.

Reason:
> early leader can fail in prose, novelty decay or serialization.

Do not crown a sole Primary merely from:
- Shelf;
- Reservoir;
- Concept theory.

# 10. Reopen / Reactivation

Reserve candidates need explicit triggers.

Examples:
- Champion fails Reservoir on repetition -> reactivate reserve with stronger scene diversity.
- Champion passes Shelf but fails P0 voice -> reactivate story-forward reserve.
- all Active candidates converge into management/operation fiction -> reactivate adventure/high-danger reserve.
- platform routing becomes decisive -> reactivate candidate stronger on that platform.

No arbitrary nostalgia resurrection.

# 11. One Mutation Budget

At early gates a promising candidate may receive:
> one root-level mutation attempt for a clearly diagnosed, repairable weakness.

If the same root weakness survives:
> DROP / RESERVE, not endless patching.

This protects against both:
- false negatives;
- sunk-cost rescue loops.

# 12. One Sentence

> **早期不要为了“筛得漂亮”错杀潜在赢家；只有当证据越来越接近真实阅读行为，淘汰才应该越来越狠。**
