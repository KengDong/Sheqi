# D-0100｜Unknown-Author Breakout Layer
## 没有作者名气时，作品必须自己制造“名气替代物”

# 0. First Principle

A known author can borrow trust from:
> author name / old readers / reputation.

An unknown author cannot.

Therefore the book must manufacture trust from page one using:

> **Premise Signal × Personality Signal × Immediate Payoff × Future Scale Signal.**

This is not only prose style.
It is architecture-facing.

# 1. Four Surfaces

## Surface A｜Shelf Signal
Before clicking, reader should understand:
- genre chassis;
- one weird difference;
- expected food.

Literary/abstract title is allowed only if synopsis/cover carries the commercial load.

For an unknown author:
> title should usually reduce uncertainty, not increase it.

## Surface B｜First-Screen Signature
Within first 500–1000 Chinese characters:
- one visual/behavioral event that cannot be mistaken for generic genre fiction;
- protagonist reacts in a book-specific way.

## Surface C｜First-3-Ch Reward
By Ch3:
- a tangible gain/reversal;
- a recurring relationship with chemistry;
- evidence this is not a one-trick premise.

## Surface D｜Terminal Spine Shadow
By ~Ch10–20:
> the reader should glimpse a crisis/secret/destination that can plausibly pull 100–500 chapters.

Not a lore dump.
A physical symptom, person, countdown, impossible evidence, public artifact or contradiction.

# 2. Unknown-Author Discovery Test

A stranger should be able to retell the book in three sentences:

1. “这书是讲___的。”
2. “主角这个人特别___。”
3. “而且后面好像要牵到___。”

If sentence 2 or 3 is empty:
> discovery is underpowered.

# 3. First-Chapter Shareability

At least one moment in Ch1–3 should be easy to retell:
- absurd reversal;
- sharp line;
- visual set-piece;
- relationship exchange;
- impossible choice.

Not because “viral” is mandatory,
but because retellability is evidence of identity density.

# 4. Terminal Spine

A long book benefits from one ultimate pull that is visible early.

Valid forms:
- approaching catastrophe;
- hidden world truth;
- destination;
- identity mystery;
- enemy/faction;
- irreversible countdown;
- world transformation.

Best version often combines:
> **secret + crisis.**

The secret explains:
> what is happening.

The crisis answers:
> why the protagonist cannot simply ignore it.

# 5. Danger of Pure Mystery

“Who did this?” is not enough for 300 chapters.

A terminal spine must periodically:
- hurt current life;
- alter available choices;
- create new enemies/allies;
- change scale;
- pay partial answers.

# 6. Danger of Pure Crisis

“World ends in 300 days” alone can become generic.

Need:
> book-specific causal link to the Premium.

# 7. New-Author Hard Gate

Before Voice Lab, priority candidate must have:
- shelf-level commercial signal;
- first-screen signature;
- first-3-ch reward;
- protagonist non-replaceability;
- tangible next rung;
- terminal-spine shadow.

If not:
> Voice Lab is premature.

# 8. Market Calibration

Current examples show multiple successful routes:

- 《没钱修什么仙？》: familiar cultivation + high-concept social/economic distortion + black humor.
- 《谁让他修仙的！》: personality/comedy is itself a high-conversion engine and later reached ten-wan average subscription.
- 2026 《让仙门再次伟大》: strong voice/personality packaging and official first-subscription >10k, average subscription >10k, 300k collections by 2026-09-22.
- 2026 new-book field also shows highly explicit high-concept pitches (“modern cultivation college × ancient world”, “the forbidden ritual is actually worshipping me”) receiving strong early attention in Qidian reader-curated new-book tracking.

Conclusion:
> **Unknown authors should not ask readers to infer uniqueness slowly.**

# Final Rule

> **先在书架上显眼，再在第一页有脸，再在三章内给东西，再在二十章前让Reader看见远方。**
