# RULE-REALITY EXECUTION OS V1

This document does NOT add a new story engine.

It converts:
> D-0102 Framework V2 + D-0104 Field Supply
into executable quality gates.

# A. Before Any Field Prose

A Field packet must contain:

1. one-line human situation;
2. visual place identity;
3. dominant rule family;
4. fair-play evidence plan;
5. named human objective;
6. one magnetic co-lead;
7. Cheng Ye personality conflict;
8. midpoint reinterpretation;
9. real failure/cost possibility;
10. exit/returned-rule choice;
11. one future-fragment clue;
12. intended reality-side human consequence.

If more than 12 items are needed to understand the Field:
> simplify the Field.

# B. Field Experience Rotation

Do not select the next Field by rule noun alone.

Track:
- genre;
- topology;
- cast size;
- tempo;
- dominant emotion;
- human objective.

At least 3 must differ from previous major Field.

# C. Fair Play

Solution may surprise.
It may not depend on unplanted information.

Plant evidence before payoff.
A reread should improve the scene.

# D. Cheng Ye Scene Test

Each major Field must contain:

- one action that proves his specific personality;
- one mistake caused by his flaw;
- one competence payoff;
- one moment another character resists his way of deciding.

If the same plot works with a generic smart protagonist:
> rewrite.

# E. Co-Lead Test

The co-lead must:
- win one scene;
- lose one scene;
- have one private desire;
- disagree on one rule choice;
- remain useful in reality.

Reader desire, not architecture intent, decides recurrence.

# F. Reality Chapter Test

A reality chapter cannot exist primarily to demonstrate society.

It must have:
> named person + concrete desire + conflict + changed state.

Then wider society may be shown through the consequence.

# G. Complexity Budget

Reader foreground budget:
- 1 dominant Field logic;
- 1–3 public Returned Rules;
- 4–6 current people;
- 1 big mystery question.

Backstage complexity may be larger.
Frontstage may not.

# H. Tone

Field:
> tension first.

Reality:
> black comedy may become stronger.

Humor must come from:
> person × impossible rule × serious consequence.

# I. Payoff Ledger

Each 8–15 chapters should normally pay at least one:
- agency;
- asset;
- money;
- relationship;
- status;
- answer;
- rule consequence.

No 30-ch stretch of pure setup/mystery.

# J. Continuity

Before next prose:
> update five ledgers from D-0106 audit.

No silent retcon.

# K. Unknown-Author Standard

The book must earn patience itself.

Before Ch10-ish, Reader should already have:
- premise proof;
- Cheng Ye personality;
- one memorable relationship;
- one visible gain/cost;
- countdown/next rung;
- actual Field on page or imminent enough to create desire;
- larger future clue.

# L. Highest Behavioral Test

After any prototype:
> would a reader voluntarily keep going if the author name were unknown?

Everything else is diagnostic.
