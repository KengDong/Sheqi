# AUTHOR RANKING + READER FANTASY POLICY V1

# 1. Final comparative authority

For candidate comparison:
> AUTHOR ranks the surviving candidates.

AI does not produce the final comparative ranking.

AI role:
- evidence collection;
- market mapping;
- hard-failure elimination;
- originality / logic QA;
- difference summaries.

# 2. Reader Fantasy before Engine

Concept eligibility order:

1. current-market reader fantasy demand;
2. distinctive premium;
3. package clarity;
4. micro-prose desire;
5. only then story engine / compounding / production.

Do not advance a concept merely because it:
- has 50 situations;
- compounds state;
- is AI-friendly;
- is easy to serialize.

# 3. Vehicle vs Fantasy

Every candidate must explicitly state:

> PRIMARY READER FANTASY

and separately:

> DELIVERY VEHICLE

Example:
- “special-agency intern” is vehicle;
- “I kill monsters others fear and become an elite abnormal hunter” is fantasy.

Hard:
> no profession may be treated as demand by itself.

# 4. Author-facing comparison package

When 3–6 candidates survive factual eligibility:
present to author:
- title + one-line;
- primary fantasy;
- 3 must-see scenes;
- current market analogs and actual evidence;
- opening micro-prose;
- key downside;
- why it differs.

No AI winner label.
No AI numerical score.
No “recommended #1”.

Author ranks:
> WANT TO READ / MAYBE / NO.

# 5. Synthetic reader use

AI clean readers may be used only for:
- confusion;
- exact stop point;
- expectation mismatch;
- memory extraction;
- hard readability warnings.

They must not decide:
> final candidate order.

# 6. One sentence

> **AI负责把垃圾挡在门外；真正剩下的书，由作者亲自决定哪本最想看。**
