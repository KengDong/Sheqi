# FANQIE PLATFORM LOCK V1
## Sheqi first launch platform = 番茄小说

# 0. Platform Decision

The intended first publication platform is:
> **番茄小说 / Fanqie**

Therefore:
> cross-platform Qidian + Fanqie market evidence is background only.

Future concept selection must be justified primarily by:
> **current Fanqie male readership evidence.**

# 1. What changes

Do NOT:
- average Qidian and Fanqie demand;
- use a Qidian hit to validate a Fanqie mother chassis by itself;
- keep a concept because it is “Qidian viable” if Fanqie evidence is weak;
- optimize for paid-subscription pacing first.

Do:
- study current Fanqie reading/new-book ranks;
- prioritize low-author-credit/newcomer breakout evidence;
- map exact Fanqie reader pools;
- study title/blurb/tag/package patterns;
- study early continuation / read-through behavior;
- study recommendation-validation mechanics;
- study update stability requirements;
- study current content-governance / signing standards.

# 2. Fanqie evidence hierarchy for market entry

Highest external evidence:
1. current/recent Fanqie official reading ranking;
2. current/recent Fanqie new-book ranking;
3. current/recent low-author-credit/new-author breakout;
4. sustained active-reader evidence after 30w chars;
5. reader feedback / public reaction;
6. cross-platform hits as support only.

Qidian success alone:
> insufficient.

# 3. New-book relevance

Because the author is unknown,
the key question is not:
> what is the biggest mature hit?

It is:
> what kinds of books can survive Fanqie's new-book / recommendation validation environment without author credit?

Every selected lane must therefore have:
- at least 2 current/recent Fanqie direct strong comps;
- preferably 1 current/recent new-book or newcomer breakout;
- evidence that package + first chapters visibly deliver the fantasy.

# 4. Packaging becomes part of story design

On Fanqie:
> title / blurb / tags / first-screen promise are acquisition machinery.

They must truthfully match:
> repeated chapter pleasure.

No “clever title once, different book afterward.”

# 5. Early behavior

Before launch-scale work,
prototypes must test:
- first-screen continuation;
- first-chapter completion desire;
- next-chapter desire;
- first 3-chapter binge desire;
- whether the promised fantasy repeats quickly.

No “it becomes good after 20 chapters.”

# 6. Update / production

Stable updating is a platform constraint.

However:
> production convenience is still NOT a positive topic-selection signal.

It is only:
- feasibility gate;
- launch-readiness gate.

# 7. Monetization

Primary commercial model:
> Fanqie free-reading ecosystem + platform revenue sharing / incentives / copyright opportunities.

Therefore repeated reading time / follow desire matters more than one-time paid-chapter value.

# 8. AI use

Current platform quality governance raises the cost of:
- generic AI prose;
- mechanical expansion;
- templated repetition;
- imitation.

AI remains:
> research / state / variant / QA tool.

Human Taste Gate remains mandatory for publishable prose.

# 9. One sentence

> **从现在起，Sheqi不是“做一部两站都能发的小说”，而是先做一部番茄原生、陌生作者能冷启动的男频长篇。**
