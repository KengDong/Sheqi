# MARKET ENTRY QUALIFICATION GATE V1
## 热门母题材只是准入，不等于值得下注

After AUTHOR marks a validated mother chassis WANT/MAYBE,
no concept may be generated until that entry passes this gate.

# Required fields

## M1 Exact Reader Pool
Define the narrow commercial pool.
Not merely:
> 玄幻 / 历史 / 都市.

## M2 Current Demand
At least 2 current/recent strong direct comps.

## M3 Newcomer Access
At least 1 current/recent newcomer / low-author-credit success preferred.
If absent:
> HIGH ENTRY RISK.

## M4 Competition Density
Document:
- number / density of recent similar launches;
- packaging sameness;
- direct substitution pressure.

## M5 Trend Lifecycle
Classify:
- rising;
- durable/stable;
- late-cycle/saturated;
- uncertain.

No AI numerical score required.

## M6 Fantasy Bundle
State:
- PRIMARY fantasy;
- SECONDARY fantasy;
- emotional/relationship amplifier.

## M7 Protagonist Fantasy
State:
> what kind of person the reader repeatedly wants to follow / become / watch.

## M8 Platform Route
Specify:
- Qidian;
- Fanqie;
- or separate platform-native variants later.

Need evidence for the route.

## M9 Monetization Logic
Explain:
- why the fantasy supports repeated chapter consumption;
- what keeps value after curiosity hook;
- likely production burden versus income model.

## M10 Matched Failure Controls
At least 2 ordinary/weaker same-pool examples where accessible.

Need explain:
> what fashionable features they had but still did not convert.

## M11 Substitution Breaker
Answer:
> why would a reader already reading current top books in this pool try ours?

Must be a meaningful Distinctive Premium, not a job-name swap.

## M12 Three Must-See Scene Functions
Before concept architecture:
> name 3 scene experiences readers in this pool demonstrably want.

Project concept later must generate its own original versions.

## M13 Package Truth
Title / blurb / cover promise must match repeated chapter pleasure.

## M14 Compliance Risk
Check current platform policy / IP / sensitive-content risks before P1-scale investment.

# Gate outcome

Only:
- QUALIFIED
- CONDITIONAL
- BLOCKED

No AI winner ranking.

AUTHOR still chooses among QUALIFIED / CONDITIONAL entries.

# Hard

A chassis can be hot and still be BLOCKED because:
- competition is saturated;
- only veteran hits exist;
- the exact reader pool is unclear;
- monetization is weak;
- concept differentiation would be cosmetic.

# One sentence

> **先证明“这个热门池现在值得新人进去”，再生成小说。**
