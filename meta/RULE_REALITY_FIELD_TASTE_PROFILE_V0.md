# RULE-REALITY FIELD TASTE PROFILE V0

This file is NOT a Field design.
It exists because repeated concept generation missed the author's taste.

# 1. What The Author Has Rejected

Repeatedly rejected:
- one elegant mechanic as the whole Field;
- environment-first catastrophe;
- “bigger” achieved by scale words: city overlap / disappearing floors / void / reality erosion;
- rule lists or rule-selection as the main drama;
- puzzle-box design that feels like the writer is demonstrating cleverness;
- checklist escalation: threat A + threat B + betrayal + near-death + lore clue;
- Returned Rule occupying the Field's identity;
- Fields that can be summarized as “名字本 / 门本 / 空间本”;
- abstract systems with little specific human story;
- danger that exists mainly to prove a framework.

Author reaction language:
> 太AI
> 机械
> 堆叠
> 低级
> 不恰当
> 不适合我意

Treat these as DESIGN FAIL, not prose issues.

# 2. Current Best Taste Hypothesis

The author appears to want:

> **剧情型绝境 / 事件型副本**
>
> A Field should feel like a complete horror-thriller-disaster story that happens to contain supernatural rules,
> not a supernatural-rule puzzle that happens to contain characters.

The Reader should remember:
- what happened;
- who they met;
- who died / betrayed / saved whom;
- one or two terrifying images;
- one mystery;
- one reversal;
- the emotional cost;

before remembering:
> “the rule was X.”

# 3. Rule Placement

Rules should be:
> the hidden physics of the event.

Not:
> the foreground topic.

Desired feeling:
- characters discover rules because something awful happens;
- rules shape choices continuously;
- characters rarely stop to discuss an abstract rule system for long;
- the rule is felt in action before fully named.

Returned Rule:
> only one residue / extract from the Field.

The Field should remain excellent if the Returned Rule is removed from the summary.

# 4. Threat Composition

The author does NOT appear to want pure environmental danger.

A strong Field likely needs a mixture of:
- a distinctive supernatural threat with presence / personality / ritual / behavior;
- human beings with strong conflicting goals;
- a specific place with history;
- a concrete task;
- an unfolding mystery;
- physical danger;
- rules embedded in all of the above.

The supernatural threat should feel:
> specific enough to have a face / behavior / icon,
but not:
> so human that it can be negotiated like another politician.

# 5. Narrative Shape

Likely desired experience:

1. **Immediate incident**
   Something is already badly wrong.
   No long setup.

2. **Concrete objective**
   rescue / escape / deliver / survive until / find / stop / reach.

3. **Human cast with desires**
   not anonymous test subjects.

4. **First danger**
   violent / frightening / memorable.

5. **Apparent understanding**
   characters think they know enough.

6. **Story reversal**
   reveals that the situation itself was misunderstood.

7. **Betrayal / conflict / loss**
   caused by a person with understandable motives, not random evil.

8. **Deeper supernatural layer**
   the Field is worse than the human conflict.

9. **Cheng Ye near-death**
   his plan fails for a planted reason.

10. **Counterplay**
    he uses observation + preparation + nerve + other people,
    not a sudden genius reveal.

11. **Exit with incomplete truth**
    survive the story,
    but one hidden thing remains.

12. **Later recontextualization**
    50–300 chapters later an object/person/scene from Field #1 gains a different meaning.

This is a STORY spine, not a mechanical checklist.

# 6. Cheng Ye Taste

Before Field #1:
> optimistic / humorous / warm / relatively trusting.

Field #1 must visibly create:
> his second face for confirmed enemies.

Important:
- do not turn him permanently cold;
- warmth survives;
- ruthlessness is learned through one deeply personal, causal event;
- he should nearly die / lose someone because his pre-Field instinct was insufficient.

His intelligence:
> active, tactical, tool-using, situational, improvisational.

Not:
> abstract philosopher / perfect solver / rule lecturer.

# 7. Benchmark Signals

Useful reader signals from popular works:

## 《诡舍》
Reader praise clusters around doors where:
> story + horror + logic + life/death pressure all work together.

Reader criticism increases when:
- ghosts/threats lose pressure;
- death is purely mechanical;
- human scheming replaces supernatural horror;
- characters act stupidly just to trigger rules.

## 《神秘复苏》
A strong event feels like:
> a supernatural INCIDENT in a familiar world,
not:
> a formal dungeon exercise.

Pressure comes from:
- a clear uncanny presence;
- ordinary actions becoming dangerous;
- humans making choices under an inhuman rule;
- solving one event opening a larger mystery.

## 《死亡万花筒》
Negative reader signal:
> unclear rule triggers + one expert carrying everyone = low participation / weak fairness.

## 《十日终焉》
Useful:
> strong character play + tactical counterplay + larger recontextualization.

Risk to avoid:
> protagonist reveals a solution based on evidence the reader never really had.

# 8. Field #1 Special Function

Field #1 must do MORE than a normal later Field.

It should:
- be independently excellent;
- make Cheng Ye's 18-day survival feel earned;
- forge his enemy-side personality;
- create several pieces of mature experience later used in reality;
- hide at least 2 long-term mysteries;
- contain at least 1 person/object/place that later gains a radically different meaning;
- make the Reader eventually think:
  > “原来第一副本早就把大主线放在我脸上了。”

But:
> none of these can make the first read feel like setup homework.
