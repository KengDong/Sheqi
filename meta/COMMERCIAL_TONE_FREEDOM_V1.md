# D-0097D｜Commercial Tone Freedom / Mania-Comedy Is a Valid Primary Route

## Author Principle

The author explicitly clarifies:

> 第一性原理不是“写一部端正的大书”，而是“写出真正能大爆、能持续赚钱、自己都想猛追的小说”。

The author personally consumes:
> 发癫 / 搞笑 / 强人设 / 高反差 male web fiction.

Therefore:
> if a manic-comedy / personality-first execution genuinely creates stronger continuation desire, it is fully acceptable as a PRIMARY commercial route.

# 1. Process Correction

Do NOT assume:
- restrained = higher quality;
- serious = more commercial;
- literary depth = stronger retention;
- professional neutrality = safer;
- humor = second-tier;
- "疯" = cheap.

Evaluate only:
> click -> continuation -> reward -> attachment -> long-run compounding -> willingness to keep reading/pay.

# 2. Two Valid Hit Surfaces

## Route A｜Deep-System / Serious-Immersive
Examples of functions:
- dense world interlock;
- social systems;
- long mystery;
- grounded character relationships;
- institutional/economic scale.

## Route B｜High-Conversion Personality / Mania-Comedy
Examples of functions:
- protagonist impossible to confuse with another MC;
- funny or outrageous behavioral logic;
- quotable dialogue;
- fast social reversals;
- high chapter-level reward;
- strong recurring character chemistry;
- absurdity that exposes world rules;
- memeability / retellability without relying on ephemeral memes.

Both may reach head-tier scale.

The best outcome can combine:
> **Route B cold-start + Route A long-run architecture.**

# 3. Mania-Comedy Quality Test

A comedic / manic execution is commercially strong only if:

1. jokes come from character/world logic, not random internet slang;
2. protagonist behavior creates plot, not merely commentary;
3. supporting cast has distinct reactions and agendas;
4. consequences remain real;
5. emotional stakes survive the comedy;
6. repeated joke patterns evolve;
7. the same personality can still generate scenes at Ch100;
8. humor helps expose the world rather than pausing it;
9. rewards/advancement remain visible;
10. the book can occasionally become serious without feeling like another novel.

# 4. Author-Taste Evidence

Because the author is a genuine target reader for male commercial fiction and frequently reads this tone:
> spontaneous “我想猛追” response is high-value evidence.

It is not absolute market proof.
But it is more useful than an internal architecture score when deciding whether an opening has visceral pull.

Future evaluation should record separately:
- structural viability;
- market evidence;
- clean-room reader behavior;
- author visceral continuation desire.

# 5. Candidate Implications

## 《劫痕道途》
Do NOT force full clown-comedy.

Potential strongest tonal lane:
> dry pain humor + medical banter + 周野/阮青禾 chemistry + absurd cultivation-cost industry.

Opportunity:
> serious stakes, funny humans.

Test later:
- baseline serious version;
- stronger personality/banter version.

## 《把一条副本规则带回现实》
Potentially supports stronger black comedy / absurd social reality.

Because:
> one ridiculous but absolute rule applied to normal urban life naturally creates darkly funny scenes.

Opportunity:
- family attempts to live normally under impossible rules;
- officials/police/criminals/platform users adapt in absurdly rational ways;
- protagonist develops a distinct reaction style.

Hard:
> never let comedy trivialize death/survival.

## 《天道赊境》
Author score 90.
Potentially strong personality route:
> debt/bargain/accounting worldview can generate humor without destroying cultivation stakes.

Do not modify yet; preserve current evidence.

# 6. New Benchmark Requirement

D-0097C Cold-Start Benchmark must include:
> **at least 3 commercially successful personality/comedy-forward books where humor/persona is not merely an opening trick but remains a long-run engine.**

For each:
- what repeats at Ch50/100 without becoming stale;
- how character comedy creates events;
- how serious stakes and progression remain intact;
- what makes the protagonist quotable/retellable;
- how supporting cast prevents one-note comedy.

# 7. Future Prototype Rule

For priority survivors, before locking final prose voice:
> allow controlled tonal prototypes when evidence suggests the Concept can support more personality/humor.

A tonal prototype may vary:
- narration;
- protagonist reaction style;
- dialogue chemistry;
- comedic density;

but MUST NOT change:
- root Concept;
- key plot event;
- reward timing;
- major factual outcome.

This isolates:
> “story engine” vs “voice/conversion” effect.

# 8. Final Rule

> **We are not trying to write the most respectable version of the Concept.**
>
> **We are trying to write the version readers most violently want to continue, provided it can still compound into a long-form commercial engine.**
