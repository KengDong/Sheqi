# MARKET-VALIDATED CHASSIS GATE V1
## 后续题材只能从当前热门已经验证过的母盘里选

# 0. Hard Rule

A new Sheqi concept may enter Concept Forge ONLY if:

> **its dominant market chassis / genre pool is already validated by current or recent hit fiction.**

A concept is NOT eligible merely because:
- its structure is clever;
- its job/profession is unusual;
- AI can serialize it;
- it has a stable engine;
- it can generate 50 scenes;
- it wears a popular genre label as background.

# 1. Minimum validation

Default requirement for a WHITELISTED mother chassis:

- at least 2 current/recent strong works in 2025–2026 evidence;
- preferably across more than one author;
- at least one newcomer / new-generation / low-author-credit success where available;
- repeated reader-fantasy evidence, not only one lucky title.

If only one strong current example exists:
> CONDITIONAL / not default-eligible until a second direct comp is found.

If evidence comes only from veteran authors:
> VETERAN-ONLY RISK / not preferred for first book.

# 2. Dominant-scene test

A popular label cannot be used as camouflage.

Example:
> “末日背景 + 80%篇幅修水泵、排班、分配资源”

does NOT automatically qualify as:
> “末日生存题材”.

Ask:
> what are the repeated scenes / rewards the reader actually consumes?

The DOMINANT SCENE GRAMMAR must still belong to the validated market chassis and fantasy.

# 3. Innovation boundary

Innovation is allowed in:
- Distinctive Premium;
- protagonist angle;
- one core mechanic;
- relationship configuration;
- setting-specific flavor;
- reward expression.

Innovation is NOT allowed, by default, in:
> the mother market itself.

Goal:
> **成熟热门母盘 × 原创Premium**

not:
> “invent a new reader demand and hope the market follows.”

# 4. Profession / job hard rule

Profession is never enough by itself.

The following are NOT eligible as a primary chassis without direct current-hit proof:
- utility repair;
- property management;
- insurance claims;
- logistics operations;
- factory operations;
- ordinary maintenance;
- civic administration;
- infrastructure restoration;
- generic shopkeeping;
- brokerage.

They may appear only as:
> secondary delivery texture inside an already validated chassis / fantasy.

# 5. Direct-comp requirement before concept generation

Every future concept card must name:
- 2–4 current/recent direct hit comps in the SAME reader pool;
- what Primary Reader Fantasy they validate;
- why our concept competes for the same reader appetite;
- what original Premium differentiates us.

If the team cannot name at least 2 direct current/recent hit comps:
> concept is BLOCKED.

# 6. No adjacent-evidence laundering

Forbidden:
> “A is popular and B is structurally similar, therefore C should be popular.”

Example:
> 749 is popular
> + task/reward organization structure
> -> therefore repair shop / factory / utilities should work.

This is invalid unless the new chassis itself has market proof.

# 7. Author gate

AI does not choose the final chassis.

AI prepares:
> evidence-qualified whitelist.

AUTHOR marks:
> WANT / MAYBE / NO.

Only author-selected WANT / MAYBE chassis proceed.

# 8. One sentence

> **以后不是“热门结构 + 冷门题材”，而是“热门题材母盘 + 已验证Reader Fantasy + 原创Premium”。**
