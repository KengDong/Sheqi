Goal:
> minimize invention-risk by mirroring proven hit-level reader/commercial functions as closely as legally/originally safe.

> 1 primary benchmark + at most 2 support benchmarks.

May mirror:
- title function;
- premise clarity;
- first-screen density;
- chapter rhythm;
- reward timing;
- cast cadence;
- comic/serious ratio;
- relationship functions;
- progression cadence;
- arc length;
- cliffhanger functions;
- long-run compounding functions.

May NOT copy:
- characters;
- unique settings;
- exact powers/systems;
- iconic scenes;
- jokes/dialogue;
- mysteries/answers;
- same event sequence;
- prose voice.

Target:
> **same commercial class, different book.**

Strength:
> lower cold-start design risk.

Risk:
> derivative smell / low Breakout Premium.

Hard gate:
> Reader must be able to state at least one memorable difference that is not cosmetic.
