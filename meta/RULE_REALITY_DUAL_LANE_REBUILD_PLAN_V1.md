## 3.1 Primary benchmark candidates

### Primary Model A｜《谁让他修仙的！》
Why:
- current/near-current strong commercial proof;
- high-frequency anti-routine comedy;
- characters bounce jokes off one another;
- comic premise is embedded in world logic rather than only dialogue;
- strong “乐子文” reader identity.

Transferable functions:
- straight-faced absurdity;
- protagonist and companions mutually create trouble;
- serious world rules produce comic outcomes;
- recurring character traits become joke engines;
- chapter-end comic reversals.

Risks:
- everyone becoming a comedian reduces protagonist distinctiveness;
- too much intentional weirdness can feel forced;
- weak mainline pressure if comedy has no hard spine.

### Primary Model B｜《修真聊天群》
Why:
- long-run comedy proof;
- modern everyday life collides with extraordinary people;
- supporting cast have memorable recurring gag identities;
- humor can come from relationship familiarity, not only punchlines.

Transferable functions:
- normal-person perspective against absurd supernatural normality;
- recurring cast joke memory;
- relationship-based running gags;
- mundane objects used in extraordinary contexts;
- warm group chemistry.

Risks:
- meme/gag repetition can become self-referential;
- too much ensemble comedy can weaken horror transition.

### Structural Support｜《亏成首富从游戏开始》
Use for:
- expectation inversion;
- one stable comic engine repeatedly generating new situations;
- protagonist goal vs everyone else's interpretation.

Do NOT import:
- business/system skin.

Potential Sheqi function:
> Cheng Ye tries to use mature Field logic conservatively;
> ordinary people repeatedly interpret / exploit the Returned Rule in ways he did not expect.

### Character/Banter Support｜《大王饶命》 / 《一念永恒》
Research further before use.

Potential functions:
- protagonist banter;
- recurring relationship comedy;
- humor coexisting with danger / emotional seriousness.

- serious switch is visually obvious because humor disappears.
