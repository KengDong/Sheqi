# B LANE｜Newcomer Breakout First Principles

# 0. Highest Goal

B Lane does NOT serve:
- Cheng Ye;
- current Sheqi prose;
- current Field design;
- current character canon;
- preserving old architecture.

B Lane serves only:

> **A completely unknown new author maximizes the probability that the first commercially launched novel breaks out, retains readers, can be serialized for a long time, and produces sustained income.**

Existing Sheqi assets may be kept ONLY if they improve that objective.

If they reduce commercial probability:
> change or discard them.

# 1. Revenue Equation

For a new web-fiction author, sustained income requires all of:

> **Exposure**
> × **Click conversion**
> × **early retention**
> × **chapter-to-chapter continuation**
> × **long-run story supply**
> × **stable update production**
> × **monetization / fan conversion**
> × **IP / backlist upside**

A concept that excels at only one factor is not enough.

# 2. Market Reality

2025 China web-literature:
- ~2.85 million newly registered authors;
- ~220,000 newly signed authors;
- industry revenue ~RMB 49 billion.

Implication:
> “good enough” is economically weak.
> A new author needs a sharp cold-start hook AND a repeatable long-run engine.

Fanqie explicitly rewards:
- new-book follow-up;
- high-follow updating;
- quality updates;
- long-run completed works;
- IP / adaptation revenue sharing.

Qidian continues to operate:
- newcomer / newly signed book discovery;
- subscription / fan economy;
- long-tail IP incubation.

# 3. B-Lane Design Priorities

Order:

## P1｜One-Sentence Hook
A stranger should understand the fun in one sentence.

Hard reject:
- concept needs worldbuilding explanation;
- premise is only “this world is mysterious”;
- selling point appears after 3 chapters.

## P2｜First-3-Chapter Conversion
The first 1–3 chapters must repeatedly兑现 the title / premise.

Not:
> promise now, payoff chapter 20.

## P3｜Stable Story Engine
The premise must naturally generate:
- new situations;
- new relationships;
- new rewards;
- escalating stakes.

Without needing a new gimmick each arc.

## P4｜High-Frequency Reader Reward
Each chapter should usually contain at least one of:
- humor;
- wish-fulfillment;
- emotional chemistry;
- reversal;
- reveal;
- tactical payoff;
- progress / acquisition.

## P5｜Character Attachment
Readers who only follow “the setting” churn when novelty fades.

Need:
> at least 2–4 recurring people readers want to see again.

## P6｜Production Sustainability
For a new author, the engine must be writable at commercial update speed.

Avoid a structure where every 2,000 words requires:
- new murder puzzle;
- complex rule system;
- heavy research;
- perfect foreshadowing;
- bespoke worldbuilding.

## P7｜Long-Run Monetization
A breakout that collapses at 300k words is inferior to one that can:
- serialize 1.5–3M characters if demand remains;
- create recurring arcs;
- sustain fan attachment;
- support adaptations / derivative media.

# 4. Comedy Position

Comedy is NOT the business goal.

Comedy is valuable because it can improve:
- early retention;
- relationship attachment;
- low-friction daily reading;
- reread / sharing;
- author update sustainability.

B Lane should prefer:
> **high-concept engine + comedy / relationship reward**

over:
> pure joke collection.

# 5. Current Market Pattern Worth Studying

Recent successful signals show strong value in:
> **one instantly legible contradiction that self-generates scenes.**

Examples for research only:

- 《刚准备高考，离婚逆袭系统来了》
  - teenage protagonist receives a divorce-revenge system decades too early;
  - one contradiction immediately explains the comedy / relationship engine;
  - achieved major Qidian commercial milestones and 2025 Twelve Heavenly Kings recognition.

- 《谁让他修仙的！》
  - serious cultivation logic continuously produces absurd results;
  - high comedy density but still serializes through adventure/progression.

- 《没钱修什么仙？》
  - cultivation + modern financial/capital logic;
  - humor/absurdity grows from the premise, not detached jokes.

- current Fanqie high-reading books repeatedly use:
  - immediate one-line hook;
  - explicit tags / promise;
  - clear reward or reversal loop;
  - low explanation cost.

This suggests B Lane should NOT start from:
> “What funny personality should Cheng Ye have?”

It should start from:
> **What commercially legible contradiction can generate hundreds of funny / rewarding scenes?**

# 6. Newcomer Advantage Strategy

A new author does NOT have:
- brand trust;
- guaranteed reader patience;
- fanbase;
- editor confidence;
- “slow burn privilege.”

Therefore B Lane defaults to:
- lower comprehension cost;
- earlier payoff;
- faster character establishment;
- clearer chapter desire;
- stronger recurring engine;
- fewer front-loaded lore terms;
- easier title/blurb conversion.

# 7. Platform Split To Test

Do not assume one universal version.

## B-Q｜Qidian Paid-Reading Prototype
Optimize for:
- subscription conversion;
- character/fan loyalty;
- longer chapter appetite;
- deeper long-run engine;
- stronger book identity.

## B-F｜Fanqie Free-Reading Prototype
Optimize for:
- cold-start clarity;
- first-page retention;
- rapid payoff;
- follow rate;
- algorithm-friendly continuation;
- high scene readability.

Same core concept may be adapted differently,
but do NOT force one manuscript to satisfy both if the incentives conflict.

# 8. Copyright / Imitation Boundary

B Lane MAY mirror from proven hits:
- premise legibility;
- payoff frequency;
- chapter rhythm;
- relationship density;
- comic engine structure;
- progression cadence;
- arc length;
- cliffhanger function;
- title/blurb packaging functions.

B Lane MUST NOT copy:
- distinctive characters;
- exact system/mechanic;
- iconic scenes;
- exact sequence of events;
- jokes / dialogue;
- prose voice;
- unique worldbuilding.

Goal:
> **market-proven commercial causality, original story skin and causal chain.**

# 9. Kill Rules

Kill a B-Lane concept before prose if:
- title needs explanation;
- hook is not visible in Ch1;
- first 20 scene seeds repeat;
- comedy depends on internet memes;
- the protagonist must constantly act stupid;
- each arc requires an entirely new mechanism;
- writer workload is too high for steady updates;
- the fun disappears when the initial gimmick becomes familiar;
- it reads like a recognizable hit with names changed.

# 10. Author Burden

The author should NOT point-fix weak AI drafts.

Internal process:
1. benchmark current breakout books;
2. generate 20–30 commercial nuclei;
3. kill obvious weak ones internally;
4. expand top 5 to scene reservoirs;
5. micro-prose top 2;
6. voluntary readers;
7. show author only surviving 1–2.

# 11. Next Research Gate

Before any B-Lane fiction design:
> build a **2024–2026 Newcomer Commercial Breakout Dossier**.

Focus:
- newer breakout authors / breakout books;
- Qidian + Fanqie;
- comedy/light tone as one axis, not the only axis;
- title/blurb/hook;
- first chapters;
- repeatable engine;
- long-run data where available;
- update pattern;
- reader complaints;
- why the book survived beyond initial gimmick.

Only after this dossier:
> generate B-Lane commercial concepts from first principles.
