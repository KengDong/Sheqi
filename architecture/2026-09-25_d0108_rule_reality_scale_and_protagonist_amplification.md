# D-0108｜Rule-Reality Scale + Protagonist Amplification
## 家仍然是情感锚，但世界不能只是“顺带受到影响”

# 0. Executive Judgment

Current problem is NOT:
> the book begins with a family.

The real problem is:
> the family currently feels like the CENTER OF SCALE instead of the EMOTIONAL MEASURING DEVICE for something much larger.

Do NOT solve this by:
- adding a secret god;
- adding an ancient organization;
- adding another system;
- making the protagonist a chosen one;
- replacing home/family with generic world-saving stakes.

Solve it by making the existing failed-future engine visibly BIG:

> **Returners are not merely bringing bad laws home.**
>
> **Every Returned Rule is helping assemble a second reality out of fragments of failed futures.**
>
> **The final “boss” is the failed world that human victories are building.**

This strengthens the existing engine instead of adding a new one.

---

# 1. Macro Reframe｜From “Failed Future Fragments” to “The Assembled Second Reality”

Old working truth:
> Rule Fields are fragments of failed future branches.

Keep this.

Add the causal scale consequence:

> **Each Returned Rule is a load-bearing law that makes one failed fragment more compatible with present reality.**
>
> **As more compatible laws accumulate, previously disconnected failed fragments begin to connect into one increasingly coherent SECOND REALITY.**

Important:
> there was NOT necessarily one pre-existing “bad future.”

The horror is stronger:

> **human survivors are accidentally constructing one.**

They salvage individually survivable laws from many dead futures.
Those laws were never meant to coexist.
But reality uses them to assemble a coherent replacement world.

This gives the premise true macro scale while preserving the unique premium:
> Field -> choose law -> reality consequence -> world convergence.

---

# 2. The Big World｜Second Reality / 第二层现实

Early reader language:
> “城市后面好像还有一座错开的城市。”

Do NOT explain a world taxonomy.

Physical progression:

## Early
- one building exists in the Field before it is built in reality;
- reflections contain a station/street that does not exist;
- one map line is wrong.

## Mid
- two previously separate Field locations can be physically connected;
- a door/stair/road in one Field opens toward a place first seen in another;
- the second skyline becomes more complete.

## Later
- whole districts briefly overlap;
- different cities develop different Returned-Rule ecologies;
- present roads and Second-Reality routes no longer agree;
- people remember incompatible versions of the same place.

## Terminal
> the Second Reality becomes coherent enough to overwrite the present.

The reader should eventually realize:
> **all these “副本” were becoming a world.**

This provides the “big world” feeling that current architecture lacks.

---

# 3. The Big Boss｜Do Not Add a Generic God

The macro antagonist has TWO layers.

## Layer A｜World-Scale Antagonist
> **the assembled Second Reality itself.**

It is not necessarily conscious.

It is dangerous because:
- every successful return makes it more coherent;
- each imported law gives it more purchase on present reality;
- local safety decisions accumulate into world replacement.

This is the non-human final pressure.

## Layer B｜Human Face
> **Future-Wanter / long-term rival.**

Upgrade their role:
- they do NOT merely debate rule policy;
- they deliberately prefer Returned Rules that strengthen one specific failed fragment;
- their short-term results are often better;
- someone they lost in current reality exists in that fragment;
- therefore they want the Second Reality to become real enough to restore that person.

They become:
> the human face of the world-scale threat.

No secret mastermind is needed.

---

# 4. 归一日 Reframe｜Not Just a Date

Old:
> future records stop around the same date.

Upgrade:

> **归一日 is the point at which the assembled Second Reality becomes internally coherent enough that present reality can no longer coexist with it.**

Before that:
- overlaps are local and reversible;
- contradictory versions can coexist briefly.

At threshold:
> one set of laws / one world must win.

Therefore every Returned Rule changes:
- daily life;
- branch compatibility;
- distance to 归一.

This makes the terminal crisis causally native to the core mechanic.

---

# 5. Keep Home Small｜But Make It the First Seismograph

Do NOT enlarge the family into a world-saving family.

Home should remain:
- meals;
- rent/store/work;
- names;
- doors;
- arguments;
- safety routines;
- ordinary wants.

Its function becomes:

> **the first place where world-scale change becomes emotionally measurable.**

Reader sees:
- a global law through one mother who can no longer call her son by name at night;
- a city overlap through the apartment window;
- a returned rule through a sister's changed daily freedom.

This is not “small story.”
It is:
> **small human instrument measuring a giant disaster.**

Hard:
> world expansion may never make the family feel like exposition furniture.

---

# 6. World Scale Ladder｜No Sudden Global Jump

Scale should widen through causal proof, not author announcement.

## Local
home / one street / one Field anchor.

## City
multiple anchors reveal a second urban geography.

## Multi-city
different cities have different Returned-Rule stacks and incompatible normal lives.

## Network
Field routes / present transport / returner information connect cities into a larger impossible map.

## World
evidence shows these are not isolated anomalies:
> Second Reality has global coherence pressure.

The reader should FEEL enlargement before characters can name it.

No “Chapter 50: actually the whole world is affected” exposition dump.

---

# 7. Cheng Ye Problem｜Current Trait Is Good, But Not Yet Reassuring Enough

D-0107 solved:
> generic morality.

It did NOT fully solve:
> reader reassurance / strategic charisma.

Target feeling:
> **“程野不是永远猜对，而是有他在，大家不会因为一次猜错就一起死。”**

This is distinct from omniscient mastermind fantasy.

His signature is:
> **uncertainty management.**

He does not win because:
> “I knew everything all along.”

He wins because:
> **he designs the next move so that being wrong is survivable.**

This creates calm, competence and trust without copying a genius-mastermind archetype.

---

# 8. Cheng Ye Strategic Method｜Backstage Character OS

Do NOT turn this into spoken checklist every chapter.

His natural decision pattern:

## Step 1｜Result First
> “先别解释规则。告诉我已经发生了什么。”

He reconstructs from irreversible evidence:
- injury;
- locked door;
- missing person;
- timing;
- physical trace;
- who paid.

## Step 2｜Minimum-Cost Test
> never test a theory with the most expensive human stake first.

He asks:
> “有没有一个猜错了也不会死人/不会失去全部退路的试法？”

## Step 3｜Cost Has a Name
Before accepting sacrifice:
> identify exactly who pays.

## Step 4｜Fallback Before Bet
A plan is not complete unless:
> there is a branch for “we are wrong.”

This is the source of reader reassurance.

---

# 9. Occupation Calibration｜Recommended, Not Mandatory Lore

Best functional fit:
> **保险理赔 / 事故调查方向。**

Why it works:
- he reconstructs cause from aftermath;
- he distinguishes statement from evidence;
- he naturally asks who bears loss;
- he is used to people hiding responsibility;
- he understands that “most likely” is not the same as “proven.”

Use lightly.

Do NOT write workplace procedural fiction.

If occupation feels artificial in prose:
> preserve the cognition, downgrade the job importance.

---

# 10. Cheng Ye Iconic Combination

The protagonist should be remembered through FOUR layers.

## 1. Reader Trust
> plans survive wrong assumptions.

## 2. Moral Edge
> refuses anonymous cost.

## 3. Practical Irritation
He hates:
> “差不多 / 应该 / 大概没事.”

He asks for:
> observable evidence.

## 4. Flaw
He over-designs responsibility and backup plans.
This can:
- delay action;
- irritate teammates;
- become controlling;
- make him take on costs other people should choose for themselves.

Therefore competence creates conflict.

---

# 11. Cheng Ye Must Have “Command Scenes”

Every major Field / reality crisis should eventually contain one moment where:

> panic is high -> Cheng Ye reduces the problem to a plan -> other people begin acting because they trust the structure.

Not speeches.

Observable examples:
- he divides floors / routes / door permissions;
- assigns one person to test, one to observe, one to hold fallback;
- leaves an escape branch before moving;
- tells someone exactly what signal means abort.

Reader payoff:
> **chaos becomes legible when he takes over.**

Important:
> sometimes the co-lead refuses his plan and is right.

This prevents god-mode.

---

# 12. Progression Must Become Visceral Without RPG Stats

Keep Access Authority.

But every upgrade must have THREE proofs:

1. **Action proof**
   - he can now do something impossible earlier.

2. **Recognition proof**
   - another person visibly recognizes the difference.

3. **Cost proof**
   - greater agency forces a larger decision.

Example progression functions:

- forced entry
  -> survivor.

- early entry
  -> can arrive before a Field fully closes, but loses normal prep time.

- differentiate openings
  -> can tell two entrances lead to different danger families.

- choose entrance
  -> choosing one means abandoning another.

- synchronize teammate
  -> relationship power, but their exit conditions become linked.

- reject one return
  -> spectacular negative reward at major accumulated cost.

The progression fantasy is:
> **from being dragged by the rules to deciding which reality gets a chance to exist.**

That is big enough.

---

# 13. Ensemble Upgrade｜Fewer People, Stronger Rule Philosophies

Do NOT add a giant cast just to imitate ensemble fiction.

Early durable conflict set:

## Cheng Ye
> cost must be explicit; uncertainty must be survivable.

## Field #2 Co-lead
> emergencies reward decisive action before certainty.

## Fang Cen
> a rule is only acceptable if thousands of ordinary people can live under it.

## Future-Wanter
> survivability outranks comfort; stronger laws may justify uglier lives.

## Xiaoman / family ordinary-person voice
> safety that destroys ordinary autonomy is not safety.

These are not debate positions.
Each must have:
- job/life desire;
- action competence;
- private stakes;
- scenes where they are right without Cheng Ye.

---

# 14. Iconic Visual System｜Two Symbols Are Enough

Do not add more branding objects.

Use:

## Micro Icon
> **black rule plaque**

Meaning:
> a human choice is about to become law.

## Macro Icon
> **Second Skyline / 第二重城市**

Meaning:
> those choices are accumulating into another world.

Whenever possible:
> plaque choice -> later skyline consequence.

This visually links local decisions to macro scale.

---

# 15. Field Design Upgrade｜Each Field Must Pay Three Scales

A head-tier Field should pay:

## Local Story
> the Field itself is a compelling survival mini-novel.

## Recurring Human
> one relationship changes permanently.

## Macro Geography
> the Field adds / contradicts / connects one piece of Second Reality.

If a Field only pays:
> “we survived + got a rule”
it underperforms.

---

# 16. Reality Arc Upgrade｜Reality Is Not “Aftermath”

Reality chapters should become:

> **the battlefield where different imported futures compete for ordinary life.**

Not policy montage.

Containers:
- family;
- rescue;
- crime;
- property;
- work;
- school;
- relationship;
- travel;
- public panic;
- returner conflict.

Macro scale is shown through:
> people discovering that two ordinary expectations can no longer coexist.

# 18. Trade-offs｜What NOT to Chase

## Do NOT chase “bigger” by:
- global exposition in Ch1;
- twelve countries/factions early;
- giant cosmology vocabulary;
- literal gods;
- chosen-one lineage;
- endless power ranks.

These would dilute the book.

## Do NOT chase “smarter protagonist” by:
- making Cheng Ye predict everything;
- hiding information from Reader so he can reveal a master plan;
- letting all characters become dumb;
- making every rule solution verbal.

Better:
> **reliable process + prepared fallback + visible command under pressure.**

## Do NOT chase ensemble by:
- 12 recurring names in first 30 chapters.

Better:
> 3–5 people readers actively miss when absent.

# 20. Final Standard

The book should eventually be retellable as:

> **程野每次活着通关，都必须把一条副本规则带回现实。**
>
> **那些规则一边救人，一边把现实改得越来越像另一个世界。**
>
> **后来他发现，他们不是在从失败未来里捡规则——他们正在亲手把无数失败未来拼成一个会取代现实的新世界。**

And Cheng Ye should be retellable as:

> **他不是永远猜对。**
>
> **他厉害在于，连自己猜错这件事，都提前算进计划里。**
>
> **而且他从不允许“代价”只是一个数字。**

If prose can make readers spontaneously retell BOTH:
> premise scale + Cheng Ye identity

then the candidate has a credible route to full-spectrum head-tier competition.
