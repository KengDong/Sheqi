# D-0101 V2.1｜Rule-Reality Architecture Closure

status: completed
date: 2026-09-24
candidate: 《把一条副本规则带回现实》

## 1. Rule Field Causal Model

A Rule Field is a failed future version of the SAME geographic area.

It formed under a small set of public laws whose combination once remained coherent long enough for people to build a life, but eventually failed.

Therefore a cleared field does not offer random loot.

The surviving returner may salvage ONE foundational public law from that failed configuration and anchor it into the present.

Why this is possible:
- the survivor has continuous lived experience across present -> failed future -> present;
- that makes the survivor a human anchor;
- the present can absorb only one stable law fragment per return event.

The mechanism can test stability.
The human must judge livability.

This closes:
> field story -> rule candidates -> human choice -> reality change -> future-branch pressure.

## 2. Why a Law From a Failed Future Can Still Be Useful

A failed future does not mean every single law was bad.

Failure may come from:
- bad combinations;
- human adaptation;
- useful local law becoming harmful at larger scale;
- a good rule serving the wrong objective.

The present is already developing instability.
Imported laws are therefore:
> locally proven patches borrowed from worlds that eventually failed.

This creates the core paradox:
> each patch may save today while making one failed future more compatible with tomorrow.

## 3. Future Knowledge Limits

Rule Fields are NOT future-tourism.

Hard limits:
- failed-future objects normally cannot return;
- precise market/lottery knowledge is branch-unstable;
- future technology cannot be copied trivially;
- reliable information mainly concerns law consequences, human adaptations, relationships, and physical places.

The book stays about:
> public rules and choices,
not future-information arbitrage.

## 4. Rule Cadence Budget

Reader-facing permanent rules are deliberately scarce.

- Ch1-100: maximum 3 CORE public rules.
- Ch101-200: add maximum 2.
- Ch201-400: add maximum 2-3.
- Ch400+: genuinely new CORE laws become rare.

Target full-book memory budget:
> about 8-12 CORE laws.

Other local rules may exist as background environment but:
- remain geographically bounded;
- are not surprise solutions;
- do not require exact wording memory.

Principle:
> deepen old laws more often than introducing new ones.

## 5. Rule Maturity

A core rule moves through:

A. Foreground Threat
- exact wording matters.

B. Social Adaptation
- people build habits and infrastructure around it.

C. World Texture
- the text shows changed behavior without repeatedly explaining the rule.

Permanence should increase immersion, not exposition burden.

## 6. Cheng Ye's Embodied Anchor Sense

Because Cheng Ye is the human anchor for laws he personally returns, he gradually develops a limited sense for:
- where his returned law is tightly attached to reality;
- where the boundary is unstable;
- where another reality is beginning to rub against it.

This is NOT immunity.
It cannot solve a field's local rules.

It gives scene-level agency for:
- routes;
- rescue;
- field choice;
- overlap timing.

Cost:
> conflicting laws and stronger overlaps can overload or mislead this sense.

## 7. Access Growth Must Have a Price

A0 Forced Entry
- no choice.

A1 Early Entry
- can go before countdown ends;
- loses preparation time and may stay exposed longer.

A2 Field Choice
- can choose between two unstable openings;
- people in the unchosen opening still face their own outcome.

A3 Synchronized Entry
- may enter with one consenting proven teammate;
- exit conditions become partly coupled.

A4 Anchor Influence
- may influence where a returned law attaches;
- also accepts more public responsibility and anchor burden.

A5 Reject One Return Path
- may prevent one disastrous candidate law;
- must sacrifice a major accumulated opportunity or worsen pressure elsewhere.

No growth is merely a badge.

## 8. Material Progress Through Ch100

Opening:
- missed work;
- unstable cash;
- sister debt;
- mother's store closed;
- no preparation assets.

Ch20:
- store partially reopens;
- first legitimate compensation/income connected to Cheng Ye's experience;
- a small preparation cache exists.

Ch50:
- family debt pressure materially reduced;
- real emergency gear/transport/field notes;
- Cheng Ye can refuse one exploitative paid offer.

Ch100:
- ordinary life is stable enough to preserve;
- team has a dedicated preparation space;
- Cheng Ye has social leverage but is not suddenly rich.

## 9. Magnetic Co-Lead Gate

The second major Rule Field must introduce ONE recurring survivor who passes all of:

- memorable without profession;
- strong behavioral contrast with Cheng Ye;
- personal desire unrelated to rules;
- can win a scene without him;
- can make him angry, laugh, or reconsider;
- different rule-choice instinct;
- useful in reality chapters too.

Do not lock romance in architecture.

Behavioral pass:
> clean readers spontaneously want this person to return.

## 10. Rival Must Sometimes Be Right

The long-term rival favors harsher, more controllable public laws.

Those choices must sometimes produce:
- fewer immediate casualties;
- faster containment;
- stronger short-term order.

Cheng Ye's more livable choices must sometimes cost:
- speed;
- certainty;
- short-term safety.

Otherwise the ideological conflict is fake.

## 11. Family Store Is an Anchor, Not a Stage

Use the store only when it:
- measures ordinary-life recovery;
- changes family economics;
- reveals recurring relationships;
- becomes a powerful overlap image;
- forces a major adaptation.

Reality stories still move through:
- streets;
- schools;
- transport;
- housing;
- police/rescue;
- other homes;
- public places.

## 12. Mystery Answers Must Unlock Action

Other returners exist
-> Cheng Ye can seek/avoid/bargain with them.

Fields map to future versions of present places
-> current geography becomes preparation data.

Futures are branches
-> clues stop being prophecy and become consequence models.

Returned laws weight branches
-> rule choice gains strategic future cost.

Present reality is unstable
-> “bring nothing back” is no longer an easy moral solution.

Convergence becomes measurable
-> the team can prioritize overlap points.

Knowledge without changed action is not enough payoff.

## 13. Convergence Has Physical Indicators

Avoid a generic numeric doomsday timer.

Use recurring physical tightening:
- overlap lasts longer;
- the same future structure returns more often;
- boundaries recover more slowly;
- someone keeps an incompatible memory after overlap;
- two rule zones fail to separate cleanly.

The reader feels urgency without a game UI.

## 14. Endgame Mechanic Is Not Locked

Do NOT canonize an early “write your own rule” power.

Only lock the functional requirement:
> the eventual solution cannot rely forever on importing laws from failed futures; it must come from the living present and from accumulated human choices.

Exact mechanics remain open until earned.

## 15. Reward Rhythm

For an unknown author, most 8-15 chapter spans should usually leave at least one visible state change:
- material;
- authority;
- relationship;
- answered mystery;
- status/access;
- major rule consequence.

Most 30-50 chapter spans should close one meaningful state transition.

This is rhythm guidance, not a chapter checklist.

## 16. Final Engine Stack

The candidate now has nine coupled engines:

1. Rule Field survival
2. irreversible public-rule choice
3. reality adaptation / black comedy
4. personal anchor-agency growth
5. material/social asset growth
6. recurring ensemble
7. other-returner/rival conflict
8. failed-future mystery repayment
9. reality-overlap terminal crisis

Above all nine remains the unique Premium:
> every victory permanently changes home.

## 17. Deliberate Non-Features

Do NOT add:
- combat-stat system;
- loot inventory tree;
- rule-edit cheat;
- protagonist immunity;
- giant agency in the opening;
- one mastermind behind all fields;
- easy future-information arbitrage;
- dozens of core public rules;
- early native-rule-writing ability.

Breadth comes from coupling, not feature bloat.

## 18. Architecture-Level Status

After this closure:
> no obvious head-tier engine is missing relative to the selected competitor field.

This is NOT a claim of executed superiority.

The biggest unknowns are now prose/behavioral:

1. Can the second Rule Field be excellent without relying on its reward?
2. Can Cheng Ye and one co-lead become magnetic?
3. Are reality chapters actively wanted?
4. Do failed-future clues feel fresh?
5. Does black comedy become a signature rather than decoration?

These must be tested, not solved by more architecture documents.
