# 《把一条副本规则带回现实》｜Full-Stack Head-Tier Architecture V2

# 0. Strategic Goal

Not:
> 写一部“有几个点比竞品有意思”的规则文。

Target:
> **核心商业维度无明显短板，并在5个只有本书最自然的轴上形成领先。**

Minimum floor:
- shelf click;
- Ch1–3;
- protagonist;
- dungeon quality;
- visible growth;
- recurring cast;
- hub/assets;
- reality engine;
- macro mystery;
- terminal crisis;
- Ch100/300 mode shift.

Chosen lead axes:
1. returned rule permanently rewrites ordinary reality;
2. victory reward is also a public externality;
3. every dungeon is a failed-future mirror of reality;
4. the first rule remains a core social asset hundreds of chapters later;
5. all prior reward choices literally build the final crisis.

# 1. New Master Pitch

## Cold commercial pitch

> **每通关一个规则场，程野都必须带一条规则回现实。**
>
> **他以为这是通关奖励，后来才发现——那些副本，可能全是现实已经失败过的未来。**

## Long-form Book Contract

> 程野要在一次次规则场中活下来，选择哪些规则能被现实承受，并和其他拥有不同选择逻辑的返还者争夺“未来该长成什么样”。最终他必须面对一个事实：现实本身已经不再完整，不能简单停止返还；人类需要在无数失败未来留下的规则里，拼出一套真正能活下去的新现实。

# 2. The Core Ontology｜Rules Are Survival Laws of Dead Futures

Deep canon, revealed slowly:

> **规则场 = 某个现实地点在一种规则组合下走向失败后的未来断面。**

Not every field looks exactly like current city.
But every field has:
- a present-world anchor place / social system;
- a recognizable human need;
- a set of laws people once used to survive.

The rules are not arbitrary “game master riddles”.

They are:
> **that future society's survival adaptations, taken to the point where daily life became monstrous.**

This solves three problems:
1. rules feel socially meaningful;
2. dungeon and reality naturally couple;
3. future-fragment mystery is present in every field without exposition.

# 3. Why a Rule MUST Come Back

At field closure:

> the return door will not open unless one survivor carries one eligible “survival rule” back through it.

This keeps current Root Concept.

The returned rule:
- becomes permanent in the present anchor zone;
- affects everyone under the condition;
- is not the carrier's private superpower;
- has benefit + external cost.

Deep later truth:
> current reality is itself losing causal stability.
> returned rules are functioning as patches.

Therefore:
> stopping all returns is NOT a clean solution.

But importing rules from dead futures increases those branches' weight.

Final dilemma:
> no patch -> present tears;
> careless patching -> a failed future overwrites the present.

# 4. Iconic Return Ritual｜Black Plaque

Existing Opening asset:
> black wooden plaque.

Canonize its function.

At the end of a rule field:
- a black plaque appears at/near the return door;
- it bears a SMALL set of eligible one-sentence return rules;
- one survivor must physically take responsibility for the selection;
- carrying it through the door anchors that rule to reality;
- the plaque cracks/burns/shatters after return.

No floating UI.
No stats.

This gives the book an iconic repeated image:
> **everyone can survive the field together, but someone must be the person who brings the consequence home.**

The climax is therefore not:
> “boss dead / puzzle solved.”

It is:
> **“which consequence do we force real people to live under?”**

# 5. Rule Scope｜Mirror Anchor

Do NOT use arbitrary map radii.

Preferred V2 logic:

> **a returned rule anchors first to the present-world area/system corresponding to that future fragment.**

Examples of functions:
- a future district fragment -> current district;
- a hospital fragment -> current hospital network / local medical anchor;
- a transit fragment -> corresponding line/area.

The reader discovers boundaries empirically:
- a phone call outside zone;
- a road crossing;
- a person stepping through a boundary;
- two rules colliding.

No minimap.
No “radius 30km” info dump.

Later:
> overlapping anchor zones create rule ecologies.

# 6. Protagonist V2｜程野 Must Be a Person, Not a Rule Solver

## Desire
Near:
> keep his mother/sister actually able to live, not merely survive.

Long:
> keep the present world “livable” while rules become unavoidable.

## Behavioral bias

> **程野遇到任何超凡规则，第一反应不是“强不强”，而是“带回家以后谁最先倒霉”。**

This is his repeatable scene engine.

He converts abstract rules into ordinary consequences.

In dungeon:
> “这条规则让我们活，那正常人靠它怎么过一天？”

In reality:
> “最强没用，凌晨两点我妈打不开门怎么办？”

This creates:
- strategy;
- humor;
- morality without speeches;
- unique rule-selection logic.

## Flaw
> over-responsibility / control.

He tends to decide “for everyone's safety”.
Family and teammates increasingly refuse to let him own all choices.

This creates real relationship conflict.

## Social style
- blunt practical questions;
- dry humor;
- can look embarrassingly mundane inside cosmic danger;
- does not posture as genius.

## Strategic intelligence type

Do NOT compete with 齐夏 by making Cheng Ye a universal deduction god.

His strength:
> **life-consequence simulation.**

He is good at inferring:
- what routine produced this rule;
- what human behavior the environment is optimized around;
- who benefits;
- who cannot live under it.

That skill becomes increasingly valuable once the failed-future truth appears.

# 7. First Rule R1 Remains Foundational

R1:
> **天黑后不能说真名 / 真名一旦被说出，所有听见者知道其位置。**

Do not downgrade this after Rule #2.

Long-run functions:

## Personal
- family naming habits;
- identity safety;
- Cheng Ye's original guilt.

## Tactical
- tracking;
- decoys/aliases;
- information warfare.

## Social
- night work;
- police;
- stalking;
- public communication.

## Economic
- identity data becomes valuable/dangerous;
- alias services / information controls.

## Hub
- creates a natural place where nobody uses real names.

## Endgame
- identity under overlapping futures becomes crucial.
R1 becomes one of the few rules that can stabilize “who is who” across incompatible reality branches.

Thus:
> **the first reward can still matter at Ch500+.**

# 8. Iconic Hub｜“无名店” (Working Name)

Use an EXISTING family asset:
> the mother's closed shop/business from Opening.

Do not invent a giant base immediately.

After the R1 crisis:
- daytime: ordinary family shop/business slowly reopens;
- nighttime: no one uses real names;
- affected people/returners naturally begin treating it as a neutral meeting point.

Working nickname:
> **无名店**

Not official organization.

## Ch20 state
- aliases written on removable cards;
- one table;
- one locked cabinet;
- basic field-prep gear.

## Ch50
- recurring returners;
- field maps/photos/future receipts;
- gear shelf;
- one back room used for rule tests.

## Ch100
- a known but still small “returner neutral place”;
- no official sign saying guild;
- people come because R1 makes its customs useful.

## Ch200+
- the same place exists in several future fragments in altered forms.
This makes the hub emotionally and cosmologically important.

Why this beats a generic safehouse:
> **the first returned rule CREATED the social institution.**

# 9. Tangible Resource Engine｜No Second RPG System

Only three recurring resources:

## A. Real Money
Returners still:
- lose work time;
- buy gear;
- support family;
- pay rent/medical costs;
- repair damage.

Keep reality material.

## B. Preparation Gear
One ordinary backpack carry limit.

This creates recurring pre-field pleasure:
> what do we pack this time?

Useful categories:
- analog time;
- marking;
- lighting;
- rope;
- mirror;
- food/water;
- masks/aliases;
- medical;
- ordinary tools.

No endless enchanted-item catalogue.

Old gear can become emotional/strategic assets.

## C. Trusted Information
Highest scarce resource:
- future maps;
- returner testimony;
- candidate-rule history;
- anchor-zone boundaries;
- names/aliases;
- field similarities.

Information can be:
- traded;
- lied about;
- withheld;
- inherited after death.

This creates an economy without a coin system.

# 10. Personal Growth Ladder｜Access Authority

No level/attribute panel.

Reader-visible ladder:

## Stage 0｜被带走
- countdown reaches zero;
- forced entry.

## Stage 1｜提前
- may enter before zero;
- cannot delay.

Value:
> chooses preparation timing.

## Stage 2｜择门
When multiple nearby fields are available:
- can perceive more than one opening;
- choose which future fragment to enter.

Value:
> personal agency + strategy.

## Stage 3｜同行
- can synchronize with ONE consenting survivor who has already shared a field.

Value:
> recurring team becomes tactical asset.

## Stage 4｜锚定
Late-mid:
- can influence which compatible real anchor receives the rule within a limited set generated by the field.

Value:
> huge responsibility, not free power.

## Stage 5｜拒返
Late:
- can destroy/reject one eligible return rule at a serious permanent cost / lost future option.

## Endgame｜编纂
Not “rewrite any law”.

Only after understanding branch mechanism:
> can combine compatible fragments into a rule that never existed whole in any dead future.

This is the final proof:
> humanity no longer only copies failed futures.

Every authority increases responsibility.

# 11. Field Design Charter｜Avoid Dungeon-of-the-Week

A field is NOT valid because:
> the rule puzzle is clever.

Every major field must contain 5 layers:

1. **One-sentence abnormal premise**
2. **Human role conflict**
3. **Physical survival pressure**
4. **A hidden social assumption**
5. **Return choice with real-world cost**

# 12. Field Mode Rotation

Do not repeat the same solving grammar.

## Mode A｜Written Rules
Classic visible rules, but some are survival customs rather than truth.

## Mode B｜No Written Rules
Infer from environment/NPC routines.

## Mode C｜Identity / Role
Who counts as employee/family/patient/passenger matters.

## Mode D｜Spatial Pursuit
Movement, doors, routes, chase.

## Mode E｜Resource / Time
Limited food/light/time/sleep.

## Mode F｜Intelligent Counterparty
Negotiate / deceive / trade with an entity or human group that understands rules.

## Mode G｜Reality Overlap
No clean portal; the future fragment intrudes into current city.

Rule:
> no two major fields in a row should use the same dominant mode.

# 13. Rule Quality Gate

Every returnable rule must pass:

1. one sentence understandable;
2. useful to somebody;
3. harmful to somebody;
4. creates a dungeon tactic;
5. creates a reality-life consequence;
6. interacts with at least one old rule;
7. can matter at a larger scale later;
8. does not require a paragraph of legal interpretation;
9. chosen because a CHARACTER wants it, not because architecture needs a topic.

If rule fails 3/6/9:
> reject.

# 14. Reward Structure｜Three Fruits per Major Arc

A major arc should usually settle across three different currencies:

## Field Fruit
- survival;
- person;
- clue;
- access authority.

## Reality Fruit
- base;
- money;
- relationship;
- status;
- social adaptation.

## Macro Fruit
- partial answer;
- future-fragment clue;
- new choice;
- larger threat.

No need for a new returned rule every arc.

# 18. Reality Engine V2｜Human First, System Second

Rule:
> every society-scale consequence first hits a known human.

Bad:
> “hospitals changed procedures.”

Good function:
> Fang Cen cannot enter one locked emergency room under a returned rule; one patient pays the price.
Then:
> wider hospital adaptation becomes meaningful.

Bad:
> “identity data became valuable.”

Good:
> Xiao Man's old loan app / school roster is exploited.
Then:
> the market implication appears.

# 19. Dungeon ↔ Reality Mirror Engine

This is a chosen LEAD axis.

Every major field leaves at least one mirror:

- a building;
- road;
- person role;
- product;
- public sign;
- future date;
- changed family relation.

Later reality revisits it.

Likewise, a reality change can become a clue for a future field.

Examples of FUNCTION:
- future subway line -> current construction project;
- abandoned future hospital -> current hospital expansion;
- “missing” district in field -> current neighborhood.

This ensures:
> dungeon episodes are never disposable.

# 20. Terminal Spine V2｜Failed Futures + Broken Present

## Layer 1｜Other Source
Another impossible rule exists.

Question:
> who else is bringing things back?

## Layer 2｜Spatial Echo
A field contains a place that resembles a real location with impossible future details.

Question:
> why is this place familiar?

## Layer 3｜Failed Future
Evidence proves:
> fields are future fragments, not alien worlds.

Question:
> whose future?

## Layer 4｜Choice Weight
Rules selected by returners increase corresponding branch overlap.

Question:
> are we causing this?

## Layer 5｜Broken Present
Major reveal:
> current reality is already losing stable laws/causal consistency.

Rule returns are emergency patches.

Question:
> can we stop importing without killing the present?

## Layer 6｜Branch Restoration Conflict
Future Restorer deliberately assembles rules from one branch because a loved one exists there.

Question:
> is saving our present worth erasing people who are real in another branch?

## Layer 7｜Convergence / 归一
One branch is becoming dominant.

The terminal solution cannot be:
> “destroy all dungeons.”

Need:
> create a compatible rule set that belongs to no failed future.

This closes the Root Concept:
> from “bring a rule back”
to
> “learn to choose/build a reality worth bringing home.”

> **每一个副本，都是一个已经把某条生存规则活到极端、最终仍然失败的未来；程野活着回来时必须把其中一条规则带回现在，而他真正成长的，不是拥有更多规则，而是越来越有资格决定——什么样的现实值得所有人一起活下去。**
