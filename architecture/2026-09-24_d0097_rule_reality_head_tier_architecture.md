# 《把一条副本规则带回现实》｜Head-Tier Two-Engine Architecture V1

# 0. One-Sentence Book Contract

> **程野每次从致命规则场活着回来，都必须把一条规则永久带进现实；很快他发现别的幸存者也在做同样的事——他们不是在通关副本，而是在一点点决定人类以后要生活在什么样的世界里。**

# 1. Core Pleasure

三个同时存在的爽点：

1. **副本求生**
   - 简单规则；
   - 极端压力；
   - 人与人博弈。

2. **不可逆选择**
   - 通关不是结束；
   - “带哪一条回来”本身比奖励更重要。

3. **现实复利**
   - 旧规则不会消失；
   - 家庭、警察、犯罪、商业、城市生活会认真适应。

# 2. Entry Mechanism｜Lock

> **每次归来后，程野手腕上会出现下一次规则场开启的倒计时；归零必入，可以提前进入，但不能延期。**

Properties:
- countdown is visible only to the returner;
- ordinary carried items fit in one normal backpack;
- no living thing can be carried deliberately;
- injuries remain;
- old returned public rules remain baseline reality inside later rule fields unless that field explicitly overrides one.

Deep “why” is deferred.

This creates:
> preparation + agency + inevitability.

# 3. Rule Scope｜Lock

To avoid instant global cognitive collapse:

> **a returned rule first anchors to the metropolitan reality zone where the returner comes back; as zones overlap and repeat returns accumulate, rules can spread/merge.**

This preserves the current city-scale Opening while enabling:
> regional rule ecologies -> overlapping zones -> national/global scale.

No game map UI is shown to ordinary people.

# 4. Cold-Start Personality Lane

程野 current core:
> responsible, practical, willing to own irreversible consequences.

Need stronger signature in Voice Lab:
> **he treats absurd rules with brutally practical household logic, and hates vague promises because “you can't refund an irreversible rule.”**

Tone opportunity:
> black comedy from normal people taking impossible rules seriously.

Reader without mentioning power should say:
> “那个遇到再离谱的事都先问‘今晚家里怎么过’、选错了也不甩锅的人。”

Do not turn him into quip machine.

# 7. First Three Focal Rules

Architecture uses only three focal rules for first ~100 chapters.

## R1｜Existing
> 天黑后不能说真名。

Primary effects:
- identity;
- tracking/exposure;
- family speech;
- police/crime;
- online/public behavior.

## R2｜Protagonist's second returned rule
> **关上的门，外面的人不能强行打开，除非里面有人允许。**

Why it works:
- one sentence;
- immediate survival value;
- huge reality cost.

Positive:
- safe rooms;
- anti-home-invasion;
- refuge.

Negative:
- fire rescue;
- domestic violence;
- emergency medicine;
- prisons;
- children/elderly locked inside;
- criminals exploiting “permission”.

Inside future dungeons:
> doors become strategic assets and traps.

## R3｜First important OTHER-returner rule
> **标明价格的东西，只要有人按价付款，卖方不能拒绝成交。**

Purpose:
- prove other returners choose according to their own lives;
- opens commerce/crime/class conflict;
- creates black comedy;
- not a rule Cheng Ye would necessarily choose.

It affects:
- online listings;
- stores;
- auctions;
- extortion;
- accidental prices;
- emergency supplies.

R3 appears after the reader is comfortable with R1/R2.

# 8. Rule Interaction Matrix

## R1 × R2
At night:
- your name may reveal/locate you;
- a closed door may still physically protect you.

Creates:
> “known but unreachable.”

Also:
- responders may know exactly who is trapped but cannot enter.

## R1 × R3
Anonymous/alias transactions become strategically valuable after dark.
But if a written price binds sale:
> identity concealment does not erase economic obligation.

## R2 × R3
A safe room/property can become economically valuable.
But coercive buyers may force situations around marked prices/entry permissions.

## All three
Do NOT foreground all simultaneously.
Only one pair per arc.

# 10. Ch21–40｜Second Rule Field

Dominant mode:
> team survival, not solo puzzle.

New field should NOT be name/identity-based.

Recommended functional setting:
> a residential/high-rise evacuation field where “inside/outside/permission/safe room” matters.

This makes R2 thematically earned rather than random.

Introduce 2 recurring survivors:
- one person who wants the safest rule for family;
- one person who wants the most exploitable rule.

They survive long enough to recur.

## Choice
At end, survivors can bring back only ONE of several candidate rules.

程野 chooses R2 because:
> it gives ordinary households a defendable boundary.

But he understands:
> emergency rescue will pay the price.

This is an irreversible moral/strategic choice, not loot.

- commerce;
- conflict between zones;
- large rule fields.

Different places adapt differently to same rules:
- architecture;
- nightlife;
- trade;
- emergency response;

Old rules become background infrastructure.

not by adding Rule #27.

# 15. Ch401–700｜Reality and Rule Fields Converge

Macro mystery:
> rule fields are not separate games; they are possible “laws” competing for admission into reality.

Returners' choices have been selecting the future world.

Endgame mode:
- survival;
- coalition;
- city/world events;
- personal family cost.

Ultimate question:
> **not “can Cheng Ye beat the final dungeon?”**
>
> **but “what rules can humanity live under without ceasing to be human?”**

Must still resolve through:
- entering fields;
- choosing;
- saving specific people;
- giving up useful rules;
- consequences at home.
