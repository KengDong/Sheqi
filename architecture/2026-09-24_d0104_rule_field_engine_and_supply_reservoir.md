# D-0104｜Rule Field Engine + Supply Reservoir
## 解决“副本本身够不够强”“规则怎么选”“长期会不会创意枯竭”

# 0. Core Mechanic Lock｜锚返者

Each Rule Field has ONE **Anchor Returner / 锚返者**.

Properties:
- the countdown belongs to this person;
- the Field forms around / claims this returner;
- other ordinary people may be pulled in;
- later, proven companions may enter through Cheng Ye's Access Authority;
- clearing the Field creates ONE public Returned Rule for that anchor's reality zone.

This prevents:
> every survivor importing a rule and exploding cognitive load.

Other Returners exist independently:
> each has their own countdown / Field chain.

Later:
> two Anchor Returners / Field chains can intersect.

That becomes a high-level event.

# 1. Death Rule

If the Anchor Returner dies before normal exit:

> **the Field loses human selection and automatically exports its dominant rule into the anchor's reality zone.**

The dominant rule is usually:
- simpler;
- harsher;
- less socially survivable.

Therefore:
> suicide cannot bypass responsibility.

This creates a strong root tension:
> survive not only to live, but to earn the right to choose the least terrible consequence.

# 2. Exit Mechanism｜Black Rule Plaques

Keep the physical black wooden plaque from Opening.

At a completed Field:
- 2–4 candidate black plaques remain;
- each represents one rule that proved “stable enough” to survive the Field;
- Anchor Returner can carry only ONE through the exit;
- the others collapse with the Field.

Selection should happen under action pressure:
- fire;
- collapsing route;
- injured teammate;
- limited carrying path;
- antagonist grabbing a plaque.

No conference-room vote as default.

# 3. Rule-Choice Quality Standard

A candidate return rule is strong only if ALL five work:

1. **In-Field Utility**
   - reader can see why someone wants it.

2. **Reality Benefit**
   - ordinary people genuinely gain something.

3. **Reality Damage**
   - innocent daily life pays a cost.

4. **Old-Rule Interaction**
   - it changes the value of at least one old rule.

5. **Character Meaning**
   - choosing it says something about the chooser.

If a rule exists mainly to create clever social commentary:
> reject it.

# 4. Field Quality Standard

Before a Field enters prose ask:

> **Would this still be a good 30k–60k-word survival story if no rule could be taken home?**

If NO:
> the Field is reward-dependent and not head-tier.

# 5. Twelve Field Motherboards
## These are SUPPLY PROOFS, not fixed canon arcs.

Each motherboard can generate multiple actual Fields.

---

## F1｜Identity / Name

Human situation:
> a community must find/protect people when identifiers themselves create danger.

Food:
- secrecy;
- trust;
- impersonation;
- intimacy.

Reality potential:
- privacy;
- policing;
- family;
- internet.

Already represented by R1.
Do NOT immediately revisit.

---

## F2｜Boundary / Permission

Human situation:
> people need shelter, but safety only works if inside/outside permission is respected absolutely.

Food:
- siege;
- rescue;
- betrayal;
- “let me in.”

Reality:
- home;
- hospital;
- fire rescue;
- property.

Risk:
> easy to become logic puzzle; needs strong human trapped-inside story.

---

## F3｜Ownership / Possession

Human situation:
> touching/holding/using an object changes who legally/physically owns it.

Food:
- theft;
- inheritance;
- shared tools;
- weapons;
- family property.

Reality:
- property law;
- logistics;
- theft;
- marriage/family assets.

Potential:
> very commercial because material value is obvious.

---

## F4｜Price / Exchange

Human situation:
> value statements become binding.

Food:
- market;
- bargaining;
- sacrifice;
- “what is a life worth?”

Reality:
- retail;
- auctions;
- emergency supplies;
- fraud.

Risk:
> too abstract/economic if no concrete person.

---

## F5｜Promise / Obligation

Food:

- betrayal;

- negotiation.

Reality:

Strong character engine.

## F6｜Order / Sequence

Food:

Reality:

- treatment;
- transport;
- production.

Risk:

---

## F7｜Memory / Recognition

Food:

Reality:

- legal existence.

Strong terminal-spine bridge.

Use sparingly; high cognitive cost.

## F8｜Visibility / Witness

Reality:

Strong modern-society engine.

## F10｜Debt / Compensation

Strong but overlaps thematic “cost.”

Reality:

# 6. Motherboard Rotation

Never use the same cognitive verb consecutively.

Bad:
> identify loophole -> identify loophole -> identify loophole.

Rotate:
- hide;
- protect;
- own;
- bargain;
- promise;
- sequence;
- remember;
- witness;
- escort;
- repay;
- perform role;
- coordinate.

This changes what the reader FEELS, not just the rules.

# 8. Field Supply Stress

The 12 motherboards prove:
> there is enough semantic variety beyond “written rules on a wall.”

But this is NOT sufficient evidence.

Before serious serialization:
> create 3 full Field Cards from different motherboards.

Each Card must contain:
- physical setting;
- human cast;
- desire conflicts;
- 3–5 local rules maximum;
- midpoint reinterpretation;
- climax;
- candidate return plaques;
- reality costs;
- future clue.

Then Fresh Reader selects which Field feels most bingeable.

# 9. Reality-Side Supply Stress

One Returned Rule can generate multiple modes without new rules.

For each rule, test:

1. family accident;
2. helpful use;
3. criminal exploit;
4. professional/public adaptation;
5. asset-price shift;
6. relationship conflict;
7. old-rule combination;
8. opponent counterplay;
9. normalization;
10. later revaluation.

If a rule only supports 1–3 of these:
> it is too narrow to return.

# 10. Why This Can Exceed Standard Infinite Flow

Standard infinite-flow reward:
> the character leaves with power/item/info.

Our strongest differentiated version:
> **the character leaves with a law everyone else must now live under.**

Therefore a good Field creates TWO books at once:
- the Field story;
- the changed-reality story.

If either half is weak:
> Premium is wasted.

# 11. Hard Rule Against Overdesign

Do NOT prewrite 20 exact rules.

Keep:
- motherboards;
- selection principles;
- cognitive budget.

Actual rules must be invented near the arc so they can serve:
> current people + current assets + current mystery.
