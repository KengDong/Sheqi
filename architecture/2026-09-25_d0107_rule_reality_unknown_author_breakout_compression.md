# D-0107｜Rule-Reality Unknown-Author Breakout Compression
## 不增加宏观发动机，把已经存在的优势压成一个新人也能被看见的“钉子”

# 0. Executive Verdict

Do NOT rebuild the book.

The current framework already has a head-tier-capable core engine:
> **通关副本 -> 必须带一条规则回现实 -> 所有人从此都要活在它下面。**

The remaining breakout problem is not lack of concepts.

It is:
> **too many good ideas are still distributed across the framework instead of collapsing into one immediate reader identity.**

D-0107 therefore does only four things:

1. compress the Book Contract;
2. bind Field and Reality more tightly;
3. sharpen Cheng Ye from “good smart lead” into a non-replaceable decision-maker;
4. move the second on-page Field forward for unknown-author pacing.

No new macro engine.
No market rescan.
No new long-range outline.

---

# 1. The Book Contract Must Become One Emotional Blade

## Surface Mechanic

> **每通关一次，程野都必须把一条副本规则带回现实。**

This remains the clearest commercial mechanic.

## Emotional Contract

Promote this to equal importance:

> **他每活着回来一次，现实就坏一点。**
>
> **最先坏掉的，通常是家。**

Alternative reader-language compression:

> **想活着回家，就得让家坏一点。**

This is not only copywriting.

It becomes an architecture test:
> every major successful return must visibly buy something AND permanently damage/rewrite one ordinary part of life.

A victory with no irreversible home/reality wound is underpowered.

## Long-Line Question

Internal long-form question:

> **如果所有能带回来的规则都来自已经失败的未来，人类还能不能活出一个没有失败过的现实？**

Do NOT explain this early.
Early prose only shows physical contradictions.

---

# 2. Returned Rule Double-Entry Ledger

Every important Returned Rule must have BOTH columns.

## CREDIT｜What survival gained
Examples:
- rescue;
- security;
- access;
- information;
- agency;
- asset;
- status;
- preparation advantage.

## DEBIT｜What normal life permanently lost
Examples:
- privacy;
- trust;
- freedom of movement;
- ordinary family habit;
- social assumption;
- safe public behavior;
- legal certainty;
- relationship simplicity.

Hard:
> **no Returned Rule may function mainly as a cool new tool.**

The first reality consequence after a Field should normally hit:
> named person / home / recurring relationship

before expanding into:
> institution / city / macro society.

This operationalizes:
> “赢一次，现实输一点。”

---

# 3. Field-Reality Anchor Echo｜The Two Engines Must Bite Each Other

The strongest visual/structural upgrade is NOT:
> every Field is a random strange world.

Instead, major Fields should increasingly feel like:
> **现实里某个东西未来死掉后的样子。**

But do NOT force a rigid 1:1 map for every Field forever.

## Rule

Every major on-page Field must contain at least ONE persistent Reality Anchor:
- place;
- building;
- institution;
- person;
- object;
- route;
- public project.

The anchor must exist, be planned, or become discoverable in current reality.

## Early Strong Form

For the first THREE on-page Fields:
> at least TWO should be visibly connected to recognizable present-day locations.

This gives the book a repeatable visual brand:
> **白天是现实地点，进Field以后是它坏掉的未来版本。**

## Why Not Every Field 1:1 Forever

Making every Field an exact future copy would:
- become predictable;
- narrow geography;
- expose the deep answer too early;
- reduce genre/topology variety.

Therefore:
> Anchor Echo is mandatory.
> Exact-location future mirror is frequent early, optional later.

# 4. 《封门楼》Must Become the First Hard Proof of Anchor Echo

Keep Card A.
Do not rerun the three-way tournament.

Upgrade its relationship to reality:

## Present Reality
At the address:
> there is currently only an unfinished construction pit / announced residential project.

## Field Reality
When Cheng Ye enters:
> the same address contains a completed forty-floor residential tower undergoing mass evacuation.

Reader must feel:
> **this place should not exist yet.**

Do NOT explain why.

One physical clue only:
- completion plaque;
- property notice;
- elevator inspection date;
- resident notice.

Choose ONE.

## Stronger Visual Entrance

Preferred transition grammar:
> unfinished pit / hoarding / concrete skeleton
> ->
> one cut later, forty floors of lit windows and emergency lights already exist above him.

The Field should be visually unforgettable before the reader fully understands the door rules.

# 5. Cheng Ye Reforge｜His Signature Is Not “Smart”

Current useful core:
- hates vague responsibility;
- asks who bears the worst outcome;
- over-assumes responsibility.

Sharpen it into one character law:

> **程野最讨厌“总得有人付代价”这句话。**
>
> **因为只要不说名字、不说门牌、不说是谁，什么代价都能被合理化。**

His signature action is:
> **force abstract cost back onto concrete people.**

Not:
> solve wording faster than everyone.

## Scene Signature

When someone says:
> “牺牲两户，总比整栋楼死强。”

A generic moral MC says:
> “不能放弃任何人。”

Cheng Ye instead says:
> **“哪两户？说门牌。”**

Then:
> “里面几个人？”
> “谁去告诉他们这是我们的决定？”
> “如果没人肯认，这就不叫方案。”

This is specific.
It fits his existing Opening behavior:
> Fang Cen makes him personally feel name-exposure before he broadcasts Wei Pingzhou's name.

## Flaw

This strength must hurt him.

Because he refuses anonymous sacrifice, he may:
- delay a necessary decision;
- take responsibility that should belong to others;
- become controlling;
- refuse to let affected people choose their own risk;
- put himself in the cost column too often.

Growth:
> from “如果是我的决定，我必须替所有人承担”
to
> “被影响的人有权知道、选择、反对，也有权自己承担。”

This creates a long character arc rather than a virtue badge.

# 6. The First Recurring Co-Lead Must Counter This Exact Flaw

Do not merely invent “a capable survivor.”

Field #2's co-lead geometry should be:

## Cheng Ye instinct
> first make the cost explicit; do not hide victims inside statistics.

## Co-lead instinct
> emergencies do not wait for moral certainty; act first or everyone dies.

A suitable professional competence:
> emergency medicine / rescue / triage / disaster response.

This person must sometimes be RIGHT where Cheng Ye is wrong.

Required Field #2 relation beats:
1. co-lead saves someone because they act before Cheng Ye has enough certainty;
2. Cheng Ye later catches a systemic cost the co-lead would have missed;
3. each causes one problem;
4. neither becomes follower;
5. by prototype stop, reader should understand why these two would want—and hate—to work together again.

Do not force romance.
Chemistry is required; romance is not.

- he understands this cannot be undone;

  > R1 came from Cheng Ye.

By the end of Ch3, deliver:
- payoff;
- cost;
- Cheng Ye signature;
- memorable relationship;
- proof the premise compounds.

## Ch4｜Home wound + Next Field collision

Do NOT spend several chapters cooling down.

Required:
- mother/sister settlement;
- one ordinary-life wound:
  > this family can never use names at night normally again;
- next-entry signal/countdown appears;
- the destination is a real address;
- Cheng Ye checks it;
- **there is no forty-floor building there—only a construction pit / announced project.**

Preferred Ch4 end:

This is the first Terminal Spine Shadow.

## Ch5｜Enter 《封门楼》

No long prep montage.

Give only preparation that produces immediate payoff.

At / through the present construction site:

Within the chapter:
- visual shock;
- one door-rule consequence;
- co-lead appears through action;
- at least one person pays a real cost.

## Ch6｜Prove this is a different novel mode

By Ch6:
- vertical rescue grammar;
- rule fair-play evidence;
- Cheng Ye/co-lead conflict;
- one visible competence payoff;
- one mistake;
- reader wants Ch7 because of situation + people, not just lore.

# 9. Future Mystery｜Hide the Explanation, Show the Corpse

Do NOT have characters explain:
> “these are failed future branches”

early.

- a completed tower where only a construction pit exists;
- a future inspection date;
- a resident list containing something impossible;
- a street layout that current city has only proposed.

Reader should form the theory before Cheng Ye can prove it.

Hard:
> **physical evidence first, terminology later.**
