# 《把一条副本规则带回现实》｜Head-Tier Framework V2
## 目标：不是一项特别强，而是没有明显短板，同时保住独占Premium

# 0. Core Position

Genre chassis:
> 都市规则求生 / 无限流 / 悬疑脑洞 / 群像成长

Unique Premium:
> **每次通关必须从副本带回一条规则，而这条规则会永久成为现实的一部分。**

Ultimate commercial emotion:
> **每赢一次，程野都更强、更有选择权，但“原来的家”也更回不去了。**

This sentence is the emotional root.

---

# 1. Full Book Contract

> **程野每次从致命规则场活着回来，都必须带一条规则回现实。**
>
> **起初，他只是想让母亲和妹妹学会在这些离谱规则下正常生活。**
>
> **后来他发现，别的幸存者也在做同样的事；更可怕的是，那些副本根本不是异世界——它们是现实在不同规则下已经失败过的未来。**
>
> **他们每带回一条规则，都在把某一个失败未来拉得更近。**

Four promises:
1. 下一场规则场怎么玩；
2. 选哪条规则回来；
3. 现实会变成什么鬼样子；
4. 这些失败未来到底为什么在逼近现在。

# 2. Four Hard Metaphysical Facts

Do not create a big system.

Only four stable facts are needed.

## Fact 1｜Permanent Return
A cleared Rule Field requires:
> choose one surviving/candidate law fragment to return.

No “take nothing” by default.

The public rule is permanent unless very late endgame conditions prove otherwise.

## Fact 2｜Returner Is Not Immune
Cheng Ye obeys the same returned public rules.

This preserves:
- fear;
- hypocrisy pressure;
- family consequences;
- opponent counterplay.

## Fact 3｜Visible Next Entry
After return:
> a countdown appears on the returner.

At zero:
> forced entry.

Later agency may allow:
- early entry;
- field choice;
- teammate synchronization;
but not cancellation for free.

## Fact 4｜Rule Scope Is Discovered, Not Explained
Early characters only observe:
> a rule works in some area and stops beyond a strange boundary.

Deep truth later:
> each Rule Field is tied to the geographic footprint of a failed-future branch; returned laws fall back onto the corresponding present area.

This naturally supports:
> building -> district -> city -> region -> world scale.

No minimap.
No radius UI.

# 3. Deep Secret / Terminal Crisis

# Layer 1｜What Reader Initially Thinks
> strange dungeons force rules into reality.

# Layer 2｜First Answer
> there are other returners.

# Layer 3｜Second Answer
> Rule Fields contain real-world places that should not exist in those forms yet.

# Layer 4｜Third Answer
> they are not copies of the present.
They are **failed future branches**.

# Layer 5｜Fourth Answer
> returning a law makes present reality more compatible with that branch.

# Layer 6｜Crisis
As enough law fragments accumulate:
> incompatible futures begin physically bleeding into present reality.

Working internal term:
> **现实重叠**

Later large crisis:
> **归一**

One coherent future branch will eventually overwrite incompatible present states.

# Deeper Endgame Truth

The Rule Field mechanism is NOT primarily a villain's game.

Preferred final logic:
> **present reality itself is unstable and is borrowing “survival laws” from failed futures to remain coherent.**

The mechanism optimizes:
> stability / survivability.

It does NOT understand:
> whether humans would actually want to live under that stable law set.

Therefore humans are the choice layer.

This creates final conflict:
> survival maximum vs a world worth living in.

The final solution must not be:
> destroy the machine.

The strongest endgame direction:
> Cheng Ye eventually stops merely importing laws from failed futures and earns the ability to create/anchor ONE law born from the living present.

Do NOT expose this “write a rule” capability before very late book.

# 4. Cheng Ye｜Protagonist Engine

Current weakness:
> practical/competent can become generic.

V2 behavioral signature:

> **遇到再离谱的超自然问题，程野第一反应永远是：这东西放进普通人的一天里，会怎么把日子过烂？**

He is not a philosopher.

Examples of thought pattern:
- other returner: “这条规则能防刺杀.”
- Cheng Ye: “急救人员进不去怎么办？”
- other returner: “能让敌人暴露位置.”
- Cheng Ye: “那我妹晚上放学还敢不敢叫名字？”

Why:
> his first return already proved that “强力规则” hits his own family first.

Secondary personality:
> he accepts the bill for his own irreversible choice.
He does not say:
> “system made me do it”
after choosing.

Humor source:
> cosmic absurdity treated as household trouble.

Not quip spam.
Not clown.

One-line protagonist without Premium:
> **“一个遇到世界末日先问今晚家里怎么过、自己选错了也认账的人。”**

# 5. Human Home Base｜The Family Store

Current text establishes:
> mother had a store that stopped operating during Cheng Ye's disappearance.

Do not waste this.

The reopened small family shop becomes:
> **the visible ordinary-life measuring instrument of the whole novel.**

Functions:
- income;
- mother agency;
- neighborhood;
- black comedy;
- rule adaptation;
- safe/unsafe ordinary people;
- recurring local faces;
- physical evidence that “life goes on”.

Examples of function, not case-of-week:
- customers learn night aliases;
- delivery systems adapt;
- a returned rule changes whether the store can open;
- future-overlap briefly replaces the store with a failed-future version;
- an old regular remembers a different history.

The shop must remain:
> human home base, NOT “规则事务所”.

This distinguishes the book from generic official-organization infinite flow.

# 7. Personal Growth｜Agency, Not RPG Stats

Do not copy “score -> power”.

But male-commercial fiction still needs visible growth.

Use three tangible ladders.

## Ladder A｜Rule-Field Access Authority

### Stage 0
forced entry.

### Stage 1
can enter early.

### Stage 2
can distinguish between two nearby candidate field openings and choose.

### Stage 3
can synchronize entry with one consenting proven teammate.

### Stage 4
can influence return anchor/branch connection within strict limits.

### Stage 5
can sacrifice major accumulated opportunity to reject/close one candidate law return.

### Endgame
native-law creation/anchoring, once.

No numeric levels.

## Ladder B｜Reality Assets
Visible possessions:
- family store reopened;
- safe back room / prep cache;
- reliable vehicle/route;
- verified field notes;
- recurring team;
- official contact;
- trusted returner network;
- later regional safe route/map.

Reader can point at what Cheng Ye has now.

## Ladder C｜Social Choice Power
Progression:
> nobody believes him
-> Fang Cen listens

-> other returners seek/contest his judgment
-> one district depends on his choices

# 9. Dungeon Quality Engine｜Critical New Core

Every Rule Field must be worth reading as a standalone mini-novel.

A field is NOT:
> “a place containing five clever rules.”

It requires eight elements.

## 1. One-line human situation
Examples:
- evacuate a building;
- keep one child alive until dawn;
- deliver something across a town;
- identify which teammate is no longer the same person;
- survive a marketplace where every transaction is irreversible.

## 2. Iconic image
One visual Reader can recall.

## 3. Dominant story mode
Rotate:
- deception;
- chase;
- escort/rescue;
- scarcity;
- social bargaining;
- investigation;
- public disaster;
- siege.

Never two consecutive fields with same dominant mode.

## 4. One core impossible law
Reader should understand the dramatic law in one sentence.

Local observed constraints may exist.
No rule encyclopedia.

## 5. Human conflict
Someone wants something besides “survive”.

## 6. Relationship change
At least one recurring relationship is permanently different after field.

## 7. Return-choice dilemma
Candidate returned rule must emerge FROM the field's lived logic.

Not random loot.

## 8. Terminal clue
Maximum ONE major future-fragment clue per field.
The field must still be good without it.

# Dungeon Kill Test
> Remove the returned-rule reward.

If the field becomes boring:
> reject the field.

This is how V2 targets 《诡舍》-level standalone arc quality.

# 10. Rule Design Standard

A high-quality returned rule must satisfy:

1. one sentence;
2. visual/social consequence;
3. obvious benefit;
4. obvious harm;
5. unexpected second-order effect;
6. can matter again 100 chapters later;
7. can collide with at least one old rule;
8. ordinary people can understand it;
9. cannot be solved simply by Cheng Ye becoming immune.

Rule selection criteria:
> story first, society second, combination third.

Not:
> “we need a hospital chapter, invent a hospital rule.”

# 13. Reality Engine｜Human First

Every major returned-rule consequence travels through this order:

> person -> relationship -> place -> small institution -> broader world.

Example:
NOT:
> “医院系统调整了.”

Instead:
> Fang Cen needs to enter one room to save one specific person and the new rule blocks her.

Then:
> show the wider hospital adaptation.

This protects against policy-montage fiction.

---

# 14. Black Comedy Engine

Comedy source:
> normal society tries to behave professionally under absurd physics.

Examples of FUNCTION:
- family invents ridiculous but practical night nicknames;
- local shop has to stop shouting customer names;
- official forms become absurdly careful;
- people exploit loopholes for petty daily reasons;
- Cheng Ye evaluates a cosmic rule through “can ambulance workers use this?”

Hard:
- no joke while deaths are treated as disposable;
- not every character is funny;
- serious field scenes can stay serious.

Comedy rests mainly in:
> aftermath / preparation / relationships.

This gives breathing room and a distinct voice.

# 15. Mystery Repayment Ladder

Never ask one question for 300 chapters.

## Ch1–20

Answer:

## Ch21–50

> why do field places look strangely familiar?

Answer:
> they correspond to real places, but not current versions.

New:
> past or future?

## Ch51–100
Answer:
> future.

But:
> multiple incompatible futures exist.

New:
> which one is real?

## Ch101–180
Answer:
> none is fixed; they are branches.

New:
> why does bringing a rule back matter?

## Ch181–260
Answer:
> each returned law increases compatibility with one branch.

New:
> who/what is selecting them?

## Ch261–380
Answer:
> not a conventional game-master; the present-world stabilization process is using failed futures as tested law sets.

New:
> why is present unstable?

## Ch381–520
Answer:
> current reality cannot remain coherent indefinitely under accumulated contradictions.

“归一” is not myth.
It is an upcoming convergence.

New:
> can another solution exist?

## Ch520+
Answer:
> importing failed laws cannot produce a truly living future forever.

Endgame objective:
> create a present-born stabilizing law instead of copying another failed world.

Every answer must:
- alter choices;
- alter a relationship;
- unlock/close an action.

# 24. Biggest Remaining Dangers

1. **Too clever**
   - rules become logic exercises.

2. **Too designed**
   - every rule obviously exists to demonstrate a social system.

3. **Too much world**
   - family/person disappears.

4. **Too much reality**
   - dungeon becomes a tax before the interesting part.

5. **Too much dungeon**
   - unique reality engine disappears.

6. **Too many rules**
   - cognitive overload.

7. **Too much mystery**
   - readers stop receiving tangible fruit.

8. **Cheng Ye too neutral**
   - Concept becomes the protagonist.

9. **Future-fragment reveal feels derivative**
   - requires unique physical consequences + rule-return causal loop.

10. **Ending becomes philosophy meeting**
   - final truth must resolve through a concrete field/choice/rescue.
