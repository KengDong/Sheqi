# 1. Reference map｜what we actually borrow

## 《没钱修什么仙？》
Role:
> DIRECT / CURRENT HOT for modern cultivation civilization.

Borrow ONLY:
1. cultivation must enter ordinary life rather than decorate it;
2. ability must be recognized through social metrics;
3. recognized ability changes school / money / work / access / teacher attitude / opportunities;
4. public revaluation should change resource allocation, not only trigger shock;
5. Ch1 pressure should be a concrete life problem before macro lore.

Key engine:
> ability
→ recognized metric
→ social revaluation
→ new resources / access
→ harder competition.

Do NOT borrow:
- exact debt / school / loan / job vocabulary;
- direct copy of finance-heavy modern cultivation;
- protagonist life setup.

---

## 《我有一个修仙世界》
Role:
> LONG-RUN CONTROL for modern cultivation + two-world structure.

Borrow ONLY:
1. modern cultivation society can remain useful hundreds of chapters later;
2. public infrastructure / school / certification / library / institution can be long-term story engines;
3. two worlds must both have independent pressure and local casts;
4. modern line does not need immediate public exposure if private compounding is already satisfying;
5. strong two-world structures accumulate four capitals:
   - resource;
   - knowledge;
   - identity;
   - relationship / institution.

Do NOT borrow:
- exact "modern knowledge / depleted resources vs traditional resource-rich world" world pair;
- free physical two-world access;
- exact cultivation-school / 天河界 configuration.

---

## 《让仙门再次伟大》
Role:
> CURRENT NEAR.

Borrow ONLY:
1. complicated world rules should appear when someone is about to gain / lose something;
2. identity should alter action permissions;
3. status revaluation should change official resources / rights;
4. current commercial writing can reveal complex systems through transaction and danger instead of encyclopedia exposition.

Do NOT use it as proof of:
- modern school;
- employment;
- modern certification systems.

---

## 《请勿高考时渡劫》
Role:
> CURRENT NEAR / emerging signal.

Borrow ONLY:
> modern institutions work best when they actively reinterpret supernatural behavior.

Use as:
> creative support, not success-causal evidence.

# 2. Dual-world reference map

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
Role:
> DIRECT / CURRENT for two-world early engine.

Borrow ONLY:
1. every cross-world contact must immediately change a present decision;
2. the same thing can be valued differently under two evaluation systems;
3. early visible gaps can be resources / methods;
4. long-run payoff should rise toward status / access / relationships;
5. large title identity promise can be delayed while small practical gains prove it.

Key engine:
> other-world contact
→ revaluation / new interpretation
→ current choice changes
→ local result
→ identity / relationship capital remains.

Do NOT borrow:
- modern vocational cultivation student;
- artificial root;
- ancient world = heavenly-root genius;
- break-world pearl;
- commodity-trader structure;
- exact school / sect dual ladder.

---

## 《我有一个修仙世界》
Dual-world function:
1. both worlds must have things happening even when no crossing occurs;
2. "advanced / backward" is weaker than "different comparative advantages";
3. structural gaps can deepen:
   - knowledge production;
   - validation;
   - access;
   - institution;
   - resource environment;
4. one world must retain exclusive bottlenecks the other cannot erase.

---

## 《集群重炮轰杀修仙者》
Role:
> CURRENT / NEAR long dual-line control.

Borrow ONLY:
> modern knowledge / organization can coexist with an independently dangerous cultivation world for extremely long serialization.

No surface copying.

---

## 《天命皆烬》
Role:
> NEAR / different-dimension dual-world support.

Borrow ONLY:
> two high-energy worlds can differ in civilization logic without one simply being primitive.

---

## 《苟在妖武乱世修仙》
Role:
> MATURE CONTROL / caution.

Learn:
> when one secondary world becomes mostly resourceized, authors often need new worlds to refresh the engine.

Use as warning:

---

## 《天师，我有一个异世界》
Role:
> MATCHED CONTROL.

Warning:

# 5. Eight functions frozen for our later framework

F1｜Immediate Proof
> first interaction must create an observable useful result quickly.

F2｜Open Slot
> first result must reveal another unknown possibility.

F3｜Agency Flip
> protagonists should actively seek the next interaction, not wait for the plot to ping them.

F4｜Persistent Residue
> each meaningful interaction leaves ability / information / relationship / status / permission that changes later interactions.

F5｜Domain Expansion
> by about Ch10, the same core engine should work in a second type of real problem.

F6｜Social Ratification
> at some point each world must alter how it treats its local protagonist because of visible results.

F7｜Delayed Ontology, Not Delayed Payoff
> explain why the living connection exists later; prove it is useful early.

F8｜Endpoint Scale Separation
> title may promise a major destiny, but early payoff should remain small and personal.

---

# 6. Seven relationship functions frozen

R1｜Independent Action Rights
> either protagonist can define what problem matters locally.

R2｜Independent Consequence Ownership
> success/failure first changes his own life.

R3｜Permission Ladder
> relationship grows by what one is allowed to do on behalf of the other.

R4｜Objective-Based Conflict
> disagreement comes from responsibilities / goals, not intelligence-vs-recklessness.

R5｜Irreversible Relationship History
> old endorsement / refusal / debt / delegation changes future scenes.

R6｜Five-Chapter Independence
> either line can remain readable for 5 chapters without the other.

R7｜Dual Social Revaluation
> each protagonist's world should eventually react to the existence / effects of the other.

---

# 7. Seven dual-world functions frozen

D1｜Immediate Decision Change
> other-world information matters only if it changes what someone does now.

D2｜Multi-dimensional Difference
> not one "advanced" world and one "backward" world.

D3｜Exclusive Bottlenecks
> each world owns conditions / institutions / relationships the other cannot directly replace.

D4｜Two Local Pressure Systems
> both worlds generate plot independently.

D5｜Capital Accumulation Beyond Resources
> knowledge + identity + relationship / institutional capital.

D6｜Gap Evolution
> early visible resource / method gap can evolve into identity / qualification / institution / historical differences.

D7｜No Merchant Collapse
> not every difference can become a tradable commodity.

# 8. Three framework directions now supported by evidence

These are not finished concepts.
They are framework emphases for AUTHOR discussion.

## FRAME A｜Fast mutual-payoff / social-revaluation type

Nearest functional references:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》
- 《九种魔药》
- relationship functions from 《泼刀行》

Reader experience emphasis:
> every few chapters the living connection produces a practical gain that one local society can measure and react to.

Early rhythm:
- fast proof;
- fast second interaction;
- by Ch10 engine has entered another life domain;
- by Ch20 both worlds have visibly revalued their local protagonist.

Strength:
- commercially clear;
- easy to feel immediate pleasure;
- title promise gets paid early.

Risk:
- can become "solve problem → shock locals → repeat";
- relationship may feel secondary to productivity.

Best if AUTHOR wants:
> strong Fanqie-like immediate payoff and obvious momentum.

---

## FRAME B｜Private co-development / relationship-compounding type

Nearest functional references:
- 《我有一个修仙世界》
- 《我的学习群里全是真大佬》
- 《从姑获鸟开始》
- 《诡秘之主》
- early-loop persistence from 《初圣》

Reader experience emphasis:
> two people first discover that the other really changes their life; a private advantage gradually becomes a deep relationship and only later spills into public status.

Early rhythm:
- first useful interaction fast;
- public exposure can be delayed;
- first 20 chapters focus on repeated mutual verification / exchange / local consequences;
- relationship permissions and trust change every few interactions.

Strength:
- strongest chance of making dual protagonists emotionally valuable;
- reduces "other protagonist = remote encyclopedia";
- information-gap and relationship engine become one thing.

Risk:
- if local stakes are weak, becomes chatroom;
- needs very strong independent casts in both worlds;
- public payoff can feel slow if private gains are not concrete.

Best if AUTHOR wants:
> readers to become attached to the pair, not only the gimmick.

---

## FRAME C｜Civilization-collision / institutional-compounding type

Nearest functional references:
- 《我有一个修仙世界》
- 《没钱修什么仙？》
- 《天命皆烬》
- 《让仙门再次伟大》
- distributed actor lessons from 《诡秘之主》

Reader experience emphasis:
> early small information gap eventually exposes that the two cultivation civilizations define "valid knowledge / legitimate talent / correct cultivation / acceptable risk" differently.

Early rhythm:
- Ch1–20 still needs small practical wins;
- Ch20–100 increasingly lets the same discovery receive different institutional reactions in two worlds;
- status / qualification / organization access becomes the compounding asset.

Strength:
- richest long-horizon ceiling;
- information gap can get deeper instead of exhausting;
- both worlds have strong reasons to remain alive.

Risk:
- easiest to become consultancy / worldbuilding fiction;
- high exposition danger;
- if characters are weak, Reader understands the system but does not care.

Best if AUTHOR wants:
> a very long novel where the dual-world premise eventually grows into two civilization systems interacting.

---

# 9. These three are NOT mutually exclusive at the feature level

But one should dominate.

A plausible layering principle:
- FRAME A can be the commercial opening behavior;
- FRAME B can be the core emotional / relationship engine;
- FRAME C can be the later expansion horizon.

However:
> if all three are given equal weight from Chapter 1, the book will become over-designed.

AUTHOR must choose:
> what should Reader primarily be waiting for in the first 20 chapters?

That answer should determine the dominant frame.

# 10. Author decision questions

Q1:
When Reader closes Ch3, which thought should be strongest?

A:
> "再连一次，看看这次又能解决什么、获得什么。"

B:
> "这两个人到底会变成什么关系？下一次他们还会不会信对方？"

C:
> "两个世界为什么会对同一件事有完全不同的答案？"

Q2:
By Ch20, which reward is most important?

A:
> both leads have visibly moved upward in local status.

B:
> both leads have crossed an irreversible trust / relationship boundary.

C:
> Reader clearly sees the first structural contradiction between the two civilizations.

Q3:
What should remain the strongest asset at Ch100?

A:
> a repeatedly useful cross-world advantage engine.

B:
> the two-protagonist relationship itself.

C:
> access to two expanding cultivation civilizations and their institutional conflict.

# 11. Current synthesis recommendation to discuss, NOT finalize

The evidence suggests a promising hierarchy for discussion:

> commercial surface = A
> emotional core = B
> long-horizon expansion = C

Meaning:
- Ch1–20 should still pay fast;
- the reason the novel remains distinctive should be the two independent protagonists and accumulated relationship history;
- the reason information gap survives after Ch100 should be deeper institutional / civilization differences.

This is NOT yet a selected framework.

Before logic design, AUTHOR should decide whether this hierarchy feels right or whether one of A / B / C should be dominant instead.
