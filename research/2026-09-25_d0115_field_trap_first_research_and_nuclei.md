# D-0115｜Field Design Reset
## 从“环境堆叠”改成“陷境吞解法”

# 0. Author Rejection

Rejected:
- environment-first catastrophe design;
- “city overlap / floors disappear” as sufficient identity;
- rules that only occasionally appear;
- danger assembled as checklist;
- Fields whose personality can be summarized only by location or disaster.

Author target:
> each Field must have a highly distinctive **陷境**;
> rules are active throughout;
> Cheng Ye repeatedly approaches death;
> Field #1 must forge his enemy-side personality;
> later revelations should explode hidden details from Field #1.

# 1. Competitive Research Takeaways

## 《十日终焉》
Author method:
- game design AND participant design are equally important;
- small rules should grow from one strict world framework;
- author's first obvious solution should be treated as the designer/judge's intended trap;
- simulate from participant -> judge -> participant again;
- choose participants whose personality/ability changes the solution.

Project translation:
> Every Field must have an adversarial structure that has already anticipated the obvious safe solution.
> Cheng Ye's personality must be necessary to the winning route.

## 《神秘复苏》
Strong horror comes from:
- one clear, embodied, non-negotiable rule threat;
- an ordinary action becomes lethally wrong;
- threat is predictable enough to reason about but not defeatable by normal violence;
- opening event also plants deeper mainline mysteries / objects.

Project translation:
> A Field needs a memorable “thing/ritual/behavior” that represents the trap.
> Environment cannot be the only antagonist.

## 《诡舍》 reader feedback
Positive:
- short/dense;
- horror + logic + story;
- human/ghost life-and-death pressure;
- reversals.

Negative:
- threat too human / negotiable reduces horror;
- arbitrary death / unplanted exit danger feels like plot kill;
- participant stupidity damages immersion.

Project translation:
> intelligent humans + inhuman enforcement;
> fair clues;
> no idiot fuel.

## 《死亡万花筒》 reader feedback
High-level takeaway:
- later doors becoming smarter / less rigid can increase pressure;
- vague trigger conditions and protagonist-only hidden solutions reduce satisfaction.

Project translation:
> threat may adapt, but evidence must remain reader-accessible.

## 《全球高考》 mixed feedback
Strength:
- high-energy set pieces and long-line foreshadowing can carry the mainline.
Weakness noted by some readers:
- individual dungeons may become less memorable than the main relationship/system line.

Project translation:
> every Sheqi Field needs its own one-sentence memory identity.

## 我会修空调 craft method
- terror should break safety layer by layer;
- weirdness works best when normal things behave in an impossible relation;
- foreshadowing should later connect suddenly into a larger truth;
- fear is pressure, payoff is release / counterattack.

Project translation:
> start normal enough for a human assumption to form;
> then make that exact assumption become dangerous.

# 2. New Definition｜What Is a Sheqi “Trap”?

A trap is NOT:
- a location;
- a monster;
- a list of rules;
- a catastrophe.

A trap is:
> **a situation where the most natural survival action feeds the thing that will later kill you.**

The Field becomes memorable when:
1. Reader understands the natural action;
2. Cheng Ye / group takes it for good reason;
3. it works once;
4. later they discover the success itself tightened the trap;
5. the real solution requires a different value/personality, not just more information.

# 3. Mandatory Field Spine

Every major Field must contain:

## A｜One ordinary desire
Examples:
- get everyone into safety;
- keep the group together;
- rescue the missing;
- share food fairly;
- get home;
- protect one room.

## B｜One survival action that looks obviously correct

## C｜One hidden conversion
The correct-looking action creates future danger.

## D｜One embodied enforcer
Not necessarily a person.
Could be:
- a bell;
- a roll call;
- a train;
- a household table;
- an elevator;
- a broadcast;
- a mirror;
- an object carried by every survivor.

The trap must have an icon.

## E｜Participants as keys
At least 3 participants must solve / worsen the trap differently because of who they are.

## F｜First solution = designed failure
Writer must find the obvious solution,
then deliberately make it the trap's bait.

## G｜True solution changes a person
The winning route must cost:
- a relationship;
- a principle;
- a permanent choice;
or reveal a flaw.

# NUCLEUS A｜《第十八条活路》
## Current strongest candidate

One-line trap:

> **这座未来城区每天早上都会根据昨夜的死法生成三条“救命规则”，幸存者必须选一条永久加入城市；每一条都真的能少死人，但它会和前面的规则结合，制造下一种更难躲的死法。程野要在十八天里活下来，而真正可怕的是——所有人每天做的“正确选择”，正在亲手把城市改造成地狱。**

## Why This Is NOT just “a rules dungeon”

The rule selection IS the trap.

Ordinary desire:
> 让今晚少死人。

Obvious correct action:
> 选择看起来保护人数最多的规则。

Hidden conversion:
> every protection permanently narrows future human freedom and interacts with previous protections.

By Night 10+:
> rules no longer kill because someone “breaks a rule”;
> they kill because two individually reasonable rules become incompatible.

The city is not suffering random rules.
It is suffering:
> **17 previous successful survival decisions.**

## Icon

Every dawn:
> three black plaques rise beside the bodies / incident site.

Each plaque contains:
> one law that would have prevented one category of last night's deaths.

People argue / vote / seize control over which becomes permanent.

Do NOT foreground bureaucratic voting.
The choice must happen under immediate human pressure.

## Why It Fits the Whole Novel

Field #1 secretly miniaturizes the entire series:
> humans repeatedly choose one survivable law;
> each law fixes something;
> accumulation builds an unlivable reality.

Later huge reveal:
> Cheng Ye did not merely survive a weird first Field.
> He already lived through a compressed model of what present reality is now doing.

This can explode hundreds of chapters later.

## R1's Role

R1 is ONE earlier chosen city law.

Possible origin:
> a night when hundreds were separated during a blackout / evacuation.
> the chosen rescue law lets a correctly bound spoken true name reveal the target.

It initially saves people.

Later:
> identity hunters weaponize it.

Cheng Ye learns mature R1 use because he lived many nights AFTER its introduction.

At exit:
> R1 can still be the law he chooses to return because he understands its mature counterplay.

But Field #1 is not “the name Field.”

## Cheng Ye Transformation

Pre-Field Cheng Ye:
> optimistic / funny / assumes most people deserve a second chance.

A human predator repeatedly uses “protective laws” to divert costs onto outsiders.

Cheng Ye catches them once.
Spare / compromise.
The person exploits another rule interaction and causes a close ally's death.

Important:
> this betrayal must be something the trap itself makes possible.
> Not generic “bad guy stabs teammate.”

From then on:
- warmth stays for allies;
- confirmed predators receive ruthless mode.

## Near-Death Generator

Every night can create a DIFFERENT high-risk manifestation from accumulated-law interaction.

The writer does NOT invent unrelated disasters.
All danger must trace back to:
> yesterday's “good” decision + an older law.

This provides:
- variety;
- fairness;
- escalation;
- thematic unity.

## Long-Term Seeds

1. black plaques resemble exit selection plaques;
2. one municipal area / Qingheli exists in this future city;
3. one law is marked as “already imported / unavailable” without explanation;
4. one plaque bears evidence of a previous chooser;
5. all city records stop after one repeated date;
6. one NPC knows a phrase / symbol later used by another Returner.

# NUCLEUS B｜《谁也不能落单》
## relationship trap

One-line trap:

> **这个未来避难区唯一公开的安全规律是“只要保持在三人以上的小组里，夜里的东西就不会碰你”；所有人于是拼命抱团。第六天他们才发现——每次一个小组成功活过夜，那个小组就会被规则永久认成“一个人”，下一夜必须与别的“人”重新组满三人。**

Why memorable:
> **抱团越成功，活人数量在规则眼里越少。**

By late Field:
- 30 humans may count as 4 “people”;
- breaking a recognized group may kill all members;
- adding strangers creates terrifying identity/relationship mergers;
- betrayal becomes literal structural violence.

R1 can exist as a minor emergency rule used to locate separated members.

Strength:
- character/relationship heavy;
- very distinct social horror;
- Cheng Ye's warmth matters;
- group trust becomes mechanically dangerous.

Weakness:
- abstract “group recognition” risk;
- requires excellent concrete manifestations to avoid AI feel.

# NUCLEUS C｜《请把死人送回家》
## ritual / urban horror

One-line trap:

> **每天凌晨，城里昨夜死去的人都会“回家”；活人必须在天亮前把每个死者送进一个仍承认他的家，否则他会沿着自己生前所有关系逐户敲门。问题是，越来越多的家为了自保开始宣布“不认识”自己的死人。**

Ordinary desire:
> close the door / deny the dead.

Obvious survival action:
> refuse recognition.

Hidden conversion:
> every denied dead person expands the number of homes/people they will visit.

Icon:
> knocking + household photo / table.

Why strong:
- ordinary “回家” desire becomes horror;
- families / strangers / institutions have distinct choices;
- identity / name rules can be a small tool without dominating;
- possible deep emotional Field characters.

Strength:
- most horror-personality;
- easy to remember in one sentence;
- strong family mirror.

Weakness:
- closer to classic ghost-story grammar;
- must distinguish from known knock/door works;
- macro Returned-Rule mechanism is less intrinsically embedded than A.

# 5. Current Recommendation

Do NOT expand all three.

First ask author to select whether the project wants Field #1's deepest identity to be:

A.
> **“每一次正确的救命选择，都让明天更难活。”**

B.
> **“越抱团，人越少。”**

C.
> **“死人一定要回家，可活人开始不认他们。”**

Current structural recommendation:
> **A has the largest long-form premium and the strongest hidden-series foreshadow.**
>
> **C has the strongest immediate horror personality.**
>
> A+C hybrid is possible ONLY if one is clearly subordinate; do not mechanically combine.
