- scene rhythm;
- character agency;
- information delivery;
- relationship logic;

## 1. Samples actually read across multiple chapters

### A. 《没钱修什么仙？》
连续观察：
- Ch1 面试
- Ch2 学校
- Ch3 体育
- Ch4 请神
- Ch5 羽书
- Ch6 自由

Why:
> 现代修真 / 学校 / 金钱 / 修炼制度，与 Sheqi 现代线功能最接近。

Key observation:
- 世界信息大量存在，但几乎总有一个当前现实载体：面试、公交、食堂、借贷、消费流水、体育课、历史课、训练；
- 白真真不是设定说明员。她先有自己的债务和借钱需求，发现张羽更穷后才改变行动；
- 修炼成本不是“列丹药/聚灵室/经脉检测清单”，而是账单、排名、补课、食补、租灵根等已经发生的生活；
- 允许普通生活细节占篇幅：通勤、汗味、吃饭、洗澡、债务、同学关系；
- 因此仙道社会看起来是“有人已经在里面生活很多年”，不是作者临时搭出来给 Reader 看。

### B. 《谁让他修仙的！》
连续观察：
- Ch1 今日宜出行，不宜作弊
- Ch2 单灵根
- Ch3 一看就是老实人
- Ch4 拿来吧你
- Ch5 我觉得第三关考验的是智慧
- Ch6 戴师兄你要冷静啊

Why:
> 研究轻松角色为什么不像句子段子机器，以及固定配角为什么不像主角工具人。

Key observation:
- 陆阳先是一个会淋雨、会没经验、会误判规则的人，幽默从具体处境长出来；
- 孟景舟、云芝、戴不凡各自有判断和位置，不围着陆阳完成信息交付；
- 笑点常来自“人物真的按自己的理解做到底 -> 现实后果扩大”，例如把考验理解成另一套问题后真的动手解决；
- 许多段落并不高密度，只是在把走路、登山、砍树、搭东西等具体过程写清楚；
- 叙述快来自事件持续变化，而不是每段都承担人设+设定+剧情+笑点。

### C. 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》
连续观察：
- Ch1 百世书
- Ch2 顺天易，逆天难
- Ch3 魔门作风
- Ch4 防不胜防
- Ch5 这个仇我记下了
并观察前期贡献点 / 功法 / 宗门资源段。

Why:
> 研究“修真味”与人物私心如何共存。

Key observation:
- 修真感首先来自场景尺度和制度：云海山峰、飞舟、记名弟子、血奉、功法传承、筑基之秘、贡献点；
- 但设定并非中立百科，而是不断改变人物利益；
- 接引师兄看似尽责，后续信息会重新解释他的行为；人物提供信息的同时仍有自己的算计；
- “法不轻授”、功法只给半部、福利背后有成本，使世界信息天然带关系张力；
- 角色不会为了给主角解释世界而暂时失去自我利益。

### D. 《全民修仙世界》｜Fanqie type-control
观察：
- Ch1 狠人
- Ch2 修仙世界
- Ch3 变化

Why:
> 只研究“全民现代修真社会”的日常落地，不作为最高文笔样本。

Key observation:
- 热水开关里要放灵石；
- 义务修仙教育、仙考、筑基直接进入普通人的生活路径；
- 修真不是单独列出的“丹符器阵清单”，而是普通生活设施已经被修真改造。

## 2. Root cause of Sheqi V7

V7已经解决：
- fragment spam;
- unclear technique unit;
- basic cultivation vocabulary;
- action continuity.

但仍然不好看，根因不在句子长度，而在：
> 叙事仍像“设计稿转正文”。

### R1｜每段都在交付功能
当前写法习惯：
> 这个段落要交代钱；
> 下个段落要证明修真；
> 下个段落要证明程野幽默；
> 下个段落要证明古法非万能。

结果：
> 每句话都正确，但 Reader 能感觉到作者在安排。

Human-prose correction:
> 一段首先要像“这个时刻真实发生了什么”。
> 功能可以附着其上，但不能让功能替代现场。

### R2｜“高密度”被误用为硬指标
Old over-application:
> important paragraph should do 2+ jobs
逐渐变成
> every paragraph must do 2+ jobs.

Correction:
> density must vary.

Some paragraphs may only:
- finish one body movement;
- show one awkward social beat;
- let a character wait / observe / miss;
- give environment texture.

Narrative speed comes from:
> scene state keeps changing,
not:
> every sentence maximizes information compression.

### R3｜世界观使用“类别词”，不是“生活物”
V7 examples:
- 聚灵室;
- 丹药;
- 功法私教;
- 经脉检测;
- 筑基术法场;
- 金丹测试间.

Individually valid.
Together they read like:
> 修真元素 checklist.

Correction:
> convert category into object / price / habit / inconvenience.

Preferred function examples:
- one used recovery pill with a bitter smell;
- training hall deposit because damaged阵砖 is expensive;
- student discount expires after enrollment;
- school-required body/meridian report;
- talisman scanner rejecting a prohibited item.

Reader should infer:
> this society is cultivation-native.

### R4｜叙述者太爱总结
V7 recurrent shape:
> this is reasonable;
> the problem is...;
> not X, but Y;
> this means...;
> he understood...

These close interpretation too quickly.

Human-prose correction:
> leave part of the conclusion inside action / dialogue / consequence.
> Reader should sometimes arrive half a beat before narrator explains.

### R5｜动作连续性被写成运动说明书
Previous correction rightly demanded body continuity.
But V7 overcorrected into:
> feet / knees / hips / shoulder-line / inner-line / turn-waist
all explicitly tracked.

Correction:
> action clarity is necessary; biomechanical completeness is not.

Use:
- one decisive body cue;
- one sensation;
- opponent consequence;
then move.

Especially cultivation action:
> qi / pressure / technique intent should be felt,
not become sports-science annotation.

## 3. Zhou Hang is the clearest character-agency failure

AUTHOR HARD FACT:
> 周航是出钱的人 / customer in Ch1.

V7 incorrectly makes him ask:
- whether Cheng Ye's qi is lagging;
- whether meridian testing was done;
- effectively diagnosing Cheng Ye.

Why this fails:
> his scene objective disappeared and he became a protagonist-assistance NPC.

Correct role engine for Ch1:
- Zhou Hang paid to expose HIS own problems / obtain HIS high-pressure data;
- he wants Cheng Ye to be a useful pressure source;
- he may react to Cheng Ye only when it affects his purchased session;
- he can be curious about an abnormal move, competitive, annoyed, interested, or request a retest;
- he should NOT take responsibility for diagnosing Cheng Ye.

Who may diagnose Cheng Ye:
- platform data;
- Coach Shao, because it is his job;
- Cheng Ye himself, because it is his body.

Rule:
> side characters may help the protagonist, but only after their own motive naturally points there.

## 4. Dialogue correction

Current V7 dialogue is often:
> question needed for exposition -> answer delivers exposition.

Human dialogue in sample chapters more often:
> actor wants something -> says something partial / biased / practical -> other actor responds from another desire.

Therefore before every dialogue line ask:
1. What does speaker want right now?
2. Why would THIS person say THIS, not just why Reader needs to know it?
3. Could speaker ignore the protagonist's problem because they have their own?
4. Does information emerge as a side effect?

If no:
> line is likely author dialogue.

## 5. Cultivation-feel correction

Do NOT prove修真 by listing:
> 炼气 / 筑基 / 金丹 / 元婴 / 丹符器阵
inside a short span.

Better layers:

### Layer A｜life
Show ordinary life already altered by cultivation.

### Layer B｜stakes
Money / school / jobs / health depend on cultivation.

### Layer C｜scale
Only when scene needs it, reveal a higher realm / impossible feat / ancient lifespan.

### Layer D｜mystique
Do not reduce every ancient technique to immediately measurable modern metrics.

Modern civilization can measure much,
but source-era encounter should retain:
> something embodied / contextual / dangerous that a graph does not fully explain.

## 6. Modern-world writing rule from 《没钱修什么仙？》

Strong pattern:
> ordinary modern thing + cultivation-specific distortion + character material interest.

Examples of FUNCTION, not content to copy:
- interview standards reveal cultivation labor pressure;
- expense history reveals training economy;
- class schedule reveals cultivation education;
- friend debt reveals social pressure.

Sheqi adaptation:
> crowdwork itself should reveal the world through a human transaction,
not through UI catalog.

## 7. Light-character writing rule from 《谁让他修仙的！》

Do NOT prewrite a punchline for Cheng Ye every two paragraphs.

Use:
> Cheng Ye makes one slightly crooked interpretation
> and ACTS on it for a while.

Let consequence produce humor.

More important:
> other people are not obligated to appreciate the joke.

Zhou Hang may ignore him.
Coach Shao may be busy.
A system may produce an inconvenient result.
Humor survives because Cheng Ye's behavior remains distinctive.

## 8. Ancient/source prose rule from 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》

Ancient cultivation scene needs:
- social danger;
- resource logic;
- technique lineage;
- hierarchy;
- people with hidden or conflicting interests.

Do NOT make Ji Changgeng's world:
> a pretty rainy courtyard used only to demonstrate a move.

Even a brief bleed-through should imply:
> he was already in the middle of HIS story.

Cheng Ye should catch only fragments of that independent story.

## 9. First-Chapter V8 rewrite implications

Do NOT patch V7 sentence-by-sentence.

Rebuild from same causal spine.

### Opening
Keep:
> Cheng Ye grabs paid combat/cultivation crowdwork.

Reduce:
> stacked UI and genre checklist.

Add:
> one or two lived details proving cultivation society.

### Zhou Hang relationship
Hard reset:
> buyer / client first, future classmate second.

He paid.
He came to expose his own weakness.
His behavior must orbit that.

### First exchange
Cheng Ye's flaw may be exposed incidentally.
Do NOT let Zhou diagnose it.
Coach/platform notices.

### Second exchange
Cheng Ye's decision to pressure his flaw remains.
But motive should arise naturally from:
- order rule;
- money;
- his own curiosity / stubbornness.

### Source overlap
Make Ji Changgeng's side feel like:
> an interrupted piece of an independent cultivation crisis,
not a demonstration video synchronized for Cheng Ye.

### Modern verification
Reduce technical defense.
Let:
- one risk consequence;
- one coach judgment;
- one system result
be enough.

### Ending
Archive should create awe / historical distance,
not look like a CV card.

## 10. New negative rule proposal｜NOT YET CANONIZED

> F30｜功能稿腔
Symptom:
> every paragraph visibly exists to deliver one planned piece of information or character proof.

> F31｜配角替作者工作
Symptom:
> side character temporarily abandons own motive to ask exactly the questions needed for protagonist/world exposition.

> F32｜修真元素清单化
Symptom:
> uses realm/pill/talisman/artifact/array nouns as proof of genre rather than showing a cultivation-native life.

> F33｜动作QA过拟合
Symptom:
> after fixing action discontinuity, prose tracks every joint and direction like coaching software.

These should enter stable COMMON_PROSE_FAILURES only after author confirms this benchmark is the right diagnosis.

## 11. Core conclusion

Current Sheqi prose does not mainly fail because it is "too AI-short" anymore.

It now fails because:
> the authorial design is too visible.

Next target:
> hide the design inside people who want things, physical places that already exist, and consequences that arrive without pausing for explanation.

The prose should feel:
> lived first, designed second.
