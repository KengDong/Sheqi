## Author feedback
> V12 did not feel meaningfully closer to 《谁让他修仙的！》.
> Humor mechanism still felt substantially different.

## Diagnosis
V12 mostly added:
- witty narrator comments;
- light quips;
- humorous comparisons.

These are sentence-level ornaments.
Deleting them does not change the story.

The stronger comedy pattern observed in the reference is:
> mistaken/sideways interpretation -> sincere elaboration -> actual action -> external consequence -> callback/reveal.

The joke occupies plot, not just wording.

Examples at a high level:
- practical/limited knowledge produces a wrong-but-reasonable interpretation;
- characters seriously plan around it;
- plan is physically executed;
- institution/other characters react;
- earlier assumption returns with new meaning.

## Sheqi natural event-comedy engine

> buyer cramped mid-order -> medical interruption -> platform settled half an order.

Function:

- establishes Cheng Ye has learned to care about classification/settlement rules;

### Engine B｜别人花钱，自己顺便测到了
Cheng Ye wants a 1200-yuan dynamic diagnostic.

First paid round:
- Zhou pays for stress testing;
- Zhou's flaw is captured;
- Cheng Ye's old 0.11s flaw is also incidentally recorded.

Cheng Ye quietly saves his own record.

This is situational humor:
> he came to earn money and accidentally receives a fragment of the expensive data he wanted.

### Engine C｜认真研究800的边界

> yes if genuinely generated by the test, no if staged.

Cheng Ye does not announce a clever plan.
He simply goes back in and stops pre-avoiding his own bottleneck.

### Engine D｜system consequence

The comedy exists in the objective result.
No narrator needs to say “工伤费”.
