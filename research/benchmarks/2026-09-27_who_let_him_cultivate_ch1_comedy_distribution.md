## Source observations
Chapter 1 uses multiple humor channels rather than dialogue quips alone:

1. Environment contradiction
- auspicious travel framing vs immediate heavy rain/mud.
- humor exists before banter.

2. Social misdescription / perspective gap
- someone calls a muddy, rain-soaked situation elegant.
- dignified framing collides with actual physical condition.

3. Expectation deflation
- grand cultivation-sect choice gets an ordinary practical reason.

4. Knowledge-gap inference
- character uses limited storybook knowledge to make a plausible-but-wrong supernatural judgment.

5. Dramatic irony
- characters discuss privileged/exam information in front of someone whose identity makes the discussion risky.

6. Rule reinterpretation
- a moral/logic test is reframed as a speed/decision test.
- the character then seriously acts on that interpretation.

7. Delayed consequence / callback
- earlier assumptions return later with a new meaning.

## Transfer rule for Sheqi
Do NOT imitate exact author voice.
Use distributed comedy through:
- world situation;
- Cheng Ye's real work history;
- money/resource perception;
- buyer-platform-coach information asymmetry;
- rule interpretation that changes action;
- callbacks;
- contrast between ordinary modern systems and impossible cultivation anomaly.
