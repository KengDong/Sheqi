# 1. Current strong-work findings

## Student identity is not valuable only because “everyone was a student”

Current / proven examples:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》 opens with high-school interview, school, competition and early jobs.
- 《刚准备高考，离婚逆袭系统来了》 uses high-school senior life and achieved 万人追捧 / 2025十二天王.
- 《我正经学生，每天只吃九种魔药》 puts “student” in the title; current public booklists record ~3w average subscription.
- Fanqie / high-martial long runners repeatedly use 武道高考 → 武大 → border / larger world as an instantly legible growth ladder.

The reusable functions of student identity:
1. public evaluation: exams / rankings / admissions;
2. fixed recurring cast: classmates / teachers / family;
3. hard countdown: graduation / entrance exam;
4. natural low-status comparison without invented occupation;
5. clear expansion ladder: class → school → city → university / institution → world;
6. broad lived-experience familiarity.

Therefore for A3:
> “student” should not be cosmetic. It should make the central premise work every day.

# 5. D distinction requirements

If A3 keeps two worlds, change at least these core dimensions:

## Crossing trigger
Not:
> personal bead / mirror / free portal.

Use:
> **a proper send-off itself creates a temporary 归门.**

## Cosmology
Not:
> unrelated second cultivation universe.

Use:
> **two halves / two successor cycles of the same failed Heaven funeral.**

## Value engine
Not:
> goods / knowledge arbitrage.

Use:
> **life / death method complementarity and causal repair.**

## Ancient-world identity
Not:
> top genius / sect-protected天骄.

Use:
> **unregistered living bridge / 送终者 / “生界来的活人”**, initially suspicious and constrained.

## Crossing reward
Not:
> free resources.

Use:
> access to the missing half of a causal chain, a return law, witness, name, or handling authority.

## Material restriction
Working hard rule:
> **physical goods cannot freely cross the 归门.**
Only things that have completed / belong to the same causal cycle can cross.

This destroys the standard two-world trader engine.

# 6. Benchmark-grounded D crossing mechanism

## Working cosmology
Long ago one Heaven funeral failed.

The complete cycle split:

World A:
> 生界 / current cultivation civilization
> strong at growth / extraction / continuation.

World B:
> 归界 / old cultivation civilization still carrying death / return laws.

They are not two random parallel universes.
They are two incomplete parts of one life-cycle.

## Why a send-off opens a door
When a dead thing in World A is properly closed:
> its remaining vitality must “return”.

But World A has lost the return-law endpoint.

So the returning causal flow naturally seeks the missing half:
> World B.

The protagonist's 万物送终 system stabilizes that flow for a brief period.

Thus:
> **送终 = crossing trigger.**

No dead thing:
> no door.

Wrong funeral:
> no door / dangerous backlash.

## Why protagonist can cross
His transmigrated soul is not natively registered in either side's life-cycle.

The lost return-law therefore can use him as a temporary bridge.

Important:
> this is not “because he is secretly the strongest bloodline”.

It is a structural consequence of being an outsider.

## Crossing limitations
Early:
- temporary;
- tied to the specific send-off;
- destination linked to the dead thing's causal counterpart / return-node;
- no arbitrary coordinates;
- no mass cargo;
- cannot stay indefinitely.

Middle:
- recurring nodes become stable;
- protagonist earns more choice.

Late:
- he can intentionally connect restored nodes.

This gives progression to crossing itself.

# 7. D repeated pleasure after removing arbitrage

Every crossing should give one or more:

1. **same death, different interpretation**
   - World A sees infrastructure failure;
   - World B remembers the original return-law.

2. **causal completion**
   - A has the corpse;
   - B has the missing endpoint / name / witness.

3. **reverse correction**
   - A keeps dead things alive too long;
   - B sometimes buries living things too early.
   - protagonist learns to distinguish.

4. **world mystery**
   - every successful crossing reveals why the Heaven cycle split.

No:
> “modern trash sells as ancient treasure”.

# 8. Title benchmark

Why current:
> 《全民修仙把天道吸干了，我来给它办丧事》

feels off:
- explains both cause and eventual solution;
- reads closer to a synopsis than a mystery;
- protagonist identity is absent;
- “吸干” pushes environmental thesis before character hook.

Current hit-title functions to learn:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》 — one contradiction / question.
- 《刚准备高考，离婚逆袭系统来了》 — universal life-stage + impossible mismatch.
- 《我正经学生，每天只吃九种魔药》 — identity + absurd repeatable behavior.
- 《从水猴子开始成神》 — concrete start + visible endpoint.
- 《我的化身正在成为最终BOSS》 — ongoing transformation + endpoint.
- 《什么叫我成了灭世级怪谈？》 — surprising identity / outcome.

Therefore A3 title should preferably foreground ONE of:
- student-stage contradiction;
- strange repeated action (送终);
- giant mystery (天道讣告);
- progression endpoint.

Do not explain all four at once.
