# Rule-Reality Field Benchmark Calibration Brief

# Goal

Do NOT design a Sheqi Field.

Build a concrete evidence base for:
> **作者到底会觉得什么样的副本/灵异事件“有味、够狠、够故事、够人物、够恐怖”，以及哪些热门写法虽然成功但不适合本项目。**

# Required Benchmark Set

Target:
> 8–12 memorable arcs/events.

Prefer across 4–6 works.

Priority pool may include, but is not limited to:
- 《诡舍》
- 《神秘复苏》
- 《十日终焉》
- 《死亡万花筒》
- 《全球高考》
- other current / classic popular Chinese horror, infinite-flow, survival, rule-fiction works if directly useful.

Do not use a title merely because it is famous.

Choose:
> specific arcs/events repeatedly remembered or discussed by readers.

# Evidence Sources

For each benchmark, seek when available:
1. reliable plot / chapter material;
2. author/editor interview or craft discussion;
3. Reader reviews / discussions;
4. negative reactions as well as praise.

Do NOT rely only on:
- synopsis;
- ranking;
- marketing copy;
- wiki-like summaries.

# For Each Arc Record

1. Work + arc/event.
2. One-sentence actual story:
   > what happened, not the abstract mechanic.
3. Concrete objective.
4. Main people and what they want.
5. Supernatural presence / threat.
6. How rules enter the story.
7. First memorable danger.
8. One iconic image / scene readers recall.
9. Midpoint recontextualization.
10. Why protagonist nearly loses / dies.
11. Human conflict / betrayal / relationship pressure.
12. Ending payoff.
13. What remains mysterious after resolution.
14. Reader praise — what they actually liked.
15. Reader criticism — what felt forced / stupid / mechanical / weak.
16. Which causal function is transferable.
17. Which skin / bridge / rule must NOT be copied.

# Cross-Arc Synthesis

After 8–12 cases, answer:

## A. Horror personality
What makes a Field feel like:
> “一件邪门的事”
rather than:
> “一套AI规则系统”?

## B. Story engine
What concrete story shapes are most bingeable?

## C. Character necessity
When do participants feel like people rather than puzzle pieces?

## D. Threat
What balance of:
- supernatural presence;
- human conflict;
- rule logic;
- physical danger;
works best?

## E. Reversal
Which reversals change the meaning of earlier scenes, rather than merely add a new condition?

## F. Protagonist competence
What makes intelligence satisfying instead of omniscient / author-cheating?

## G. Long-line foreshadow
What kinds of early details later explode emotionally?

## H. Author taste
Using existing author feedback, classify findings into:
- LIKELY FIT;
- LIKELY REJECT;
- UNCERTAIN / NEED MICRO-PROOF.

- NO character integration.

Output only:
> evidence + taste calibration.
