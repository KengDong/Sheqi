## Mission
Evaluate the latest Chapter 1 V16 as a complete commercial webnovel chapter.

This is NOT a rewrite window.
This is NOT a local-patch window.
This is an independent holistic editor / benchmark reader.

Highest question:
> If V16 were placed next to strong finished/popular cultivation webnovel openings, exactly why does it still fall short or succeed as a whole?

## Mandatory external benchmark
Deep-read multiple consecutive early chapters, not snippets.

Must include:
- 《谁让他修仙的！》 Ch1–6 minimum;
- 《没钱修什么仙？》 Ch1–4 minimum;
- 《让仙门再次伟大》 Ch1–3 minimum.

May add:

- 《我师兄实在太稳健了》;
- one current 2025–2026 successful cultivation / xianxia / modern-cultivation opening if it materially helps.

Do not imitate source wording.
Study function and reading experience.

## Required evaluation dimensions
Evaluate ALL of these together, not independently optimizing one:

1. Opening
- Does first 300 chars pull naturally?
- Is author intention visible?
- Does the chapter begin with a person/story, or with setup machinery?

2. Cheng Ye recognizability
- If his name is removed, which paragraphs are still unmistakably him?
- Does he feel like a protagonist with a distinct life/personality, or a competent normal lead?
- Are humor, money awareness, rule reading, risk-taking all part of one person?

3. Event comedy
- Which funny beats survive if witty wording is removed?
- Which are only quips?
- Does any comic setup cause later action/consequence/callback like mature light-xianxia examples?

4. Dialogue
- Does each person speak like a person with their own goal?
- Are there shorthand/ping-pong remnants?
- Is dialogue too functional?
- Does Cheng Ye sound distinctive without performing jokes?

5. Rhythm / camera
- Where does V16 stay on an action after Reader already understands?
- Where does it move too fast?
- Which exact paragraphs should be CUT / COMPRESS / HOLD / EXPAND / REORDER?
- Does the chapter feel like continuous prose or like a sequence of designed beats?

6. Cultivation texture
- Does the world feel naturally cultivated, or like modern training/gig-platform vocabulary with cultivation nouns attached?
- Is technique explanation readable without becoming a manual?

7. Payoff timing
- Does +800 feel genuinely extra?
- Is the first meaningful cultivation payoff satisfying?
- Does Ji Changgeng appear soon enough?
- Does reciprocal sight land with enough force?

8. Whole-chapter shape
- Is the chapter trying to do too much?
- Is anything redundant?
- Does every scene cause the next?
- Is there a stronger reading desire at the end than at the beginning?

## Required paragraph-level audit
Number every paragraph in V16.

For EVERY paragraph or tightly linked paragraph group, assign:
- KEEP
- CUT
- COMPRESS
- EXPAND
- REORDER
- REWRITE-FUNCTION

And explain why in 1–3 sentences.

No paragraph may be skipped.

## Required benchmark comparison
Do not say:
> “某书节奏更好。”

Instead state concrete differences, e.g.:
- benchmark spends X kind of space on character choice but skips intermediate movement;
- benchmark hides/reveals information differently;
- benchmark comedy changes external state;
- benchmark protagonist displays a specific ordinary imperfection before competence.

Use source evidence and citations in the report where possible.

## Final report structure

### A. Executive diagnosis
No more than 800 Chinese chars.
Answer:
> What are the 3–5 biggest reasons V16 is still below target?

### B. What V16 genuinely gets right
Do not erase improvements.

### C. Full paragraph audit
Every paragraph / group.

### D. Benchmark comparison
By function, not imitation.

### E. Whole-chapter rebuild priorities
Rank only the highest-impact changes:
- P0 must change;
- P1 should change;
- P2 optional.

Do NOT give 30 equal-weight suggestions.

### F. What must NOT be changed
Protect working parts from over-editing.

### G. Rewrite brief for the next writer
One page maximum.
No prose sentences.
Only scene-level and character-level instructions.

## Hard boundary
- NO rewriting V16.
- NO replacement paragraphs.
- NO new jokes.
- NO new plot mechanics.
- NO changing core canon.
- NO line polishing.
- NO “here is a better sentence.”
- NO scoring/ranking the benchmark novels.
- Judge V16 as ONE chapter, not as isolated craft dimensions.
