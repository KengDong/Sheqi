# P0 Rules

## 1. Person before explanation
The protagonist must be doing something concrete.

No opening encyclopedia.

## 2. Promise must appear on-page
The exact commercial Premium does not need full explanation,
but the Reader must experience its first undeniable form.

## 3. One human consequence
By the end of P0, the abnormal event must matter to a real person/life problem.

## 4. No future-summary prose
Do not write:
- “他不知道，这将改变……”
- “从此以后……”
- “未来的他会……”
as substitute for an actual hook.

## 5. No checklist voice
Do not force:
- character bio;
- system rules;
- world history;
- all supporting cast;
into 1200 characters.

## 6. Human prose
Prefer:
- physical action;
- specific objects;
- imperfect reactions;
- interrupted thought;
- concrete social pressure.

Avoid:
- AI summary paragraphs;
- symmetrical aphorism stacks;
- repeated “这意味着……” explanation.

# End Condition

End at a point where the next screen is wanted because:
- something just became irreversible;
- a choice is now unavoidable;
- an impossible thing has happened and changed the present;
- or a human consequence has just become clear.

Not because:
> narrator promises a bigger mystery later.
