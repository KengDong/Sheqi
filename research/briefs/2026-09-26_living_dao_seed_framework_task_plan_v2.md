## 1C｜Dual-protagonist / persistent-relationship scout

# Phase 6｜Early Reader / Red-Team mini gate

Before long-horizon work, run:
- one structural editor;
- two clean-room premise/early-engine readers.

They do NOT rank against other Sheqi concepts.

They only answer:
- do I understand the premise?
- do I want the second / third interaction?
- which line drags?
- does one protagonist feel like a tool?
- where does information-gap pleasure repeat?

If targeted issues:
> revise Gate 5 before Gate 6.
