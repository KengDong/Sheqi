# D-0096B｜《把一条副本规则带回现实》Two-Engine Hit-Scale Architecture Brief

status: READY
date: 2026-09-24

Mission:
> Do NOT write prose. Build the next 100 chapters around two equal engines: Dungeon Survival and Reality Transformation.

Highest question:
> Can this become a 1–3M-word rule-survival/social-transformation novel instead of “new dungeon -> new rule -> show effects -> repeat”?

Must lock:
1. one-sentence next-dungeon entry/cadence mechanism;
2. what can be prepared/carried;
3. whether/how old returned rules matter in future dungeons;
4. per-arc rule cognitive budget (1–3 focal rules);
5. recurring survivor team;
6. first evidence of other returners;
7. protagonist permanent growth that is not a second RPG system;
8. reality social surfaces driven by protagonist needs, not checklist montage.

Required architecture:
- Ch5–12 first reality-aftershock/preparation;
- Ch13–25 second dungeon;
- Ch26–45 Rule #1 × Rule #2 reality collision;
- Ch46–70 competing-returner dungeon;
- Ch71–100 first city-scale rule crisis.

Required outputs:
- 20/50/100-ch permanent-state ledger;
- rule interaction matrix limited to focal rules;
- social-surface map;
- recurring cast arcs;
- competing-returner/faction logic;
- macro mystery “answer half/open a door” ladder;
- Case-Supply/Reward-Sameness stress test;
- P20/P50 kill conditions.

Hard:
- no rule encyclopedia;
- no paragraph-long legal clauses;
- no dungeon-of-week;
- no protagonist monopoly over public rules;
- no next rule whose only value is novelty.
