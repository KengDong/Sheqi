# R2 Revival Opening Arc Card｜《万人炼我一法》

Frozen Concept:
> A cultivation method improves when real users practice it and validate effective changes; protagonist permanently inherits proven optimizations.

Prior proof:
- Reservoir 42;
- Reward Sameness STRONG PASS;
- Commercial Compounding VERY STRONG;
- Qidian shelf #4 / Fanqie shelf #4.

Prior loss:
> **HUMAN-LAYER GAP NOT CLEARED.**

Protagonist core candidate:
> a low-level cultivator who could only afford the cheapest basic method and wants to make a method ordinary people can actually practice, not a perfect art only geniuses can use.

Opening Arc mission:
> Prove this is a story about a person and other people, not a cultivation software project.

By first mini-climax:
- one real person practices the method for a personal reason;
- an unexpected valid change appears;
- protagonist benefits materially/power-wise;
- the user's life/relationship matters beyond being "feedback";
- protagonist must choose between faster propagation and a human/safety cost.

Mini-climax:
> first public or personal proof that a change made by an ordinary practitioner can overturn the expected cultivation result.

After payoff:
> Reader should want both the next evolution AND what happens to the people connected to the method.

Hard watch:
- version numbers / software language;
- passive protagonist;
- user-as-data;
- product-manager prose.
