# 1. Four P0 changes only

## P0-A｜Compress the engineering-debug middle by 30–40%

Target zone begins around:
> “听完这句，周航低头把刚才两人的轨迹叠到一起。”

and extends through the sequence where Zhou:
- opens multiple curves;
- deletes his best scores;
- closes map layers;
- compares failed trajectories;
- explains why the first / second reproduction failed.

Current problem:
> Writer proves Zhou has understood the problem several times.

Reader only needs to understand ONCE:
> Zhou has stopped caring about his overall test performance and is now seriously trying to force Cheng Ye's transition fault out.

Keep:
- Zhou notices that speed is not the key;
- Zhou identifies the timing / retreat-route problem;
- Zhou independently proposes changing the pressure method;
- 1 failed reproduction;
- 1 corrected reproduction.

Compress / cut:
- repeated screen manipulation;
- repeated graph-layer operations;
- repeated “this failure was because X, next failure was because Y” explanation;
- any technical step whose only purpose is to prove Zhou is analytical.

Hard rule:
> After Reader understands Zhou has inherited the objective, every additional technical sentence must cause a visible action or consequence within 1–2 paragraphs.

---

## P0-B｜Make Zhou's inherited logic change the VALUE SYSTEM of the scene

This is the biggest required improvement.

Current version:
> Zhou understands Cheng Ye's problem and optimizes the reproduction.

V17.1 target:
> Zhou begins judging success and failure by whether HE can force Cheng Ye's fault out.

This must be visible through behavior.

Required functional beat:

At some point after Zhou learns why Cheng Ye chose him:
1. Zhou gets an objectively good attacking / scoring opportunity OR completes a technically strong exchange.
2. Cheng Ye still escapes the dangerous transition early.
3. Under ordinary freshman-test logic, Zhou did well.
4. Zhou treats it as a failed attempt because:
   > the fault still did not appear cleanly.
5. This proves that Cheng Ye's crooked objective has spread to Zhou.

Do NOT make Zhou say a polished line explaining the theme.
Let his next action reveal it.

Examples of acceptable FUNCTION:
- ignores / dismisses a high score because Cheng Ye escaped too early;
- asks to rerun the transition despite his own result being good;
- resets position because “that one doesn't count” for the objective he now cares about;
- voluntarily gives up a cleaner scoring route to preserve the exact pressure condition.

This is the event-comedy upgrade:
> the second person now actively makes the situation stranger.

## P0-C｜Turn tester escalation into a clear visual callback line

Tester is already correct in V17.
Do NOT give the tester more jokes.

Instead make the tester's behavior form a visible progression.

Required progression:

### Stage 1
Routine position / ordinary supervision.

### Stage 2
After Cheng Ye requests complete-pressure review:
> tester pays closer attention / checks safety parameters.

### Stage 3
After Zhou starts actively trying to force the fault:
> tester no longer returns to routine posture.
> tester moves physically closer / keeps the stop device ready.

### Stage 4
Before the final reproduction:
> tester's thumb / hand is already on the stop control.

### Stage 5
Yellow line / real threshold is crossed:
> immediate intervention.

Reader should be able to notice, without anyone explaining:
> the professional adult has gradually gone from “ordinary freshman test” to “I am standing right here because of these two.”

Do NOT write:
- “你们两个真能折腾”;
- “你们疯了吧”;
- comic commentary from tester.

The behavior is the joke.

## P0-D｜Fix the ancient-scene time problem

Current problem:
Cheng Ye enters the 0.11s bottleneck, Zhou's pressure is already arriving, but Cheng Ye then watches too many seconds of ancient action before returning to the modern exchange.

This risks making Zhou's hand feel frozen in the air.

Revision target:

### First ancient glimpse = ONLY the decisive movement fragment

When rain first overlays:
- Ji Changgeng is ALREADY near / inside the third-movement ending;
- pursuer's attack is ALREADY arriving;
- Cheng Ye recognizes the same bottleneck;
- Ji does not wait;
- Ji forces unfinished momentum into the fourth movement;
- leg/body cost;
- immediate evade.

This first glimpse must feel almost instantaneous.

Cheng Ye only extracts:
> 他没等那一下。

Then Cheng Ye immediately copies once in the modern exchange.

### After modern safety stop
The rain / ancient scene remains.

NOW allow:
- Ji reaches / braces at the pillar;
- checks the wooden case;
- evaluates pursuit / escape;
- notices the impossible presence;
- turns;
- sees Cheng Ye.

This solves two things:
1. no modern-time freeze;
2. reciprocal sight gets breathing room after the action climax.

Do NOT explain time dilation.
Do NOT add a mechanism theory.

# 3. Character separation

## Cheng Ye

Preserve:
- wants usable evidence, not reassurance;
- accepts bounded risk on himself;
- reads the legal boundary literally;
- chose Zhou because Zhou is technically reliable;
- serious-mode switch after rain.

His engine:
> “问题是我的，我愿意拿自己去试清楚。”

Do NOT turn him into:
- a joke machine;
- a loophole clown;
- a testing engineer who only stares at screens.

## Zhou Hang

Strengthen difference from Cheng Ye.

His engine after reveal:
> “你既然觉得我有本事把这个问题逼出来，那我就非把它逼出来不可。”

His motivation is:
- competence;
- training pride;
- competitive recognition.

Not:
- pure helpfulness;
- data fascination;
- identical rationality to Cheng Ye.

The revision fails if Cheng Ye and Zhou both read like two algorithm engineers debugging the same system.

## Tester

Engine:
> valid data + no real injury.

No extra personality gimmick required.
