# 2. Hard canon for this draft

## Modern side

- Cheng Ye has already been admitted to 临江修真大学.
- This is summer before freshman enrollment.
- The scene is a free / school-organized freshman baseline movement screening.
- It is ordinary modern cultivation infrastructure, not a high-stakes entrance exam.
- Freshmen naturally hope their first official baseline result goes smoothly.
- They are NOT cheating or deliberately hiding abnormalities.
- Cheng Ye has a long-standing high-pressure transition problem in 《流云步》.
- Reader-facing naming:
  > 第三式·掠云 -> 第四式·回风
- Under the relevant pressure, Cheng Ye loses about 0.11 seconds at the transition.
- He has experienced this issue before and wants usable evidence, not vague reassurance.
- The safety logic is competent and correctly protects students.
- It interrupts / reduces pressure when Cheng Ye's instability begins to appear.
- Cheng Ye is frustrated because it protects him BEFORE the useful part of the fault is fully reproduced.
- There exists a legitimate, monitored way to complete the pressure segment after explicit confirmation.
- Cheng Ye seriously checks and uses that allowed option.
- Zhou Hang is Cheng Ye's same-cohort peer / future university classmate.
- Zhou is well trained and takes his own test seriously.
- Cheng Ye chose Zhou because Zhou's pressure is stable / technically good enough to expose the fault.
- Zhou initially does not understand this.
- After learning it, Zhou voluntarily becomes more serious about forcing the fault out.
- The tester / safety staff is competent and actively monitors them.
- Old paid-sparring / 11.5 orders / 320 / 800 / exact 1200-yuan baggage is NOT part of this draft.

## Ancient side

- The overlap happens only after Cheng Ye himself pushes into the exact 0.11s bottleneck.
- Cheng Ye first hears rain while the modern hall still exists.
- A four-thousand-year-old source-era scene overlays the modern scene rather than replacing it cleanly.
- Ji Changgeng is a young living person in his own immediate crisis.
- He is injured and being pursued.
- He carries / protects an important wooden case.
- He is using the same 《流云步》 lineage.
- He reaches the SAME third-to-fourth movement bottleneck.
- Safe completion is too slow for his life-or-death situation.
- He uses a dangerous transition:
  > he does not wait for the previous movement to fully settle before forcing the remaining momentum / spiritual flow into the next movement.
- He pays a clear leg/body cost.
- He survives the immediate attack because of that choice.
- Cheng Ye does NOT instantly understand the whole principle.
- Cheng Ye only recognizes:
  > this person has shown a route that does not wait for the missing 0.11s.
- Because Zhou's pressure is already arriving, Cheng Ye tries it once.
- Cheng Ye also pays a physical cost.
- The modern equipment may visibly register an abnormal / unrecorded movement, but do not stop to explain databases or awards.
- The true chapter climax is NOT “Cheng Ye learned an ancient move.”
- The true chapter climax is:
  > Ji Changgeng turns and sees Cheng Ye back.

End at or immediately after reciprocal sight.
Historical archive verification belongs later unless explicitly re-approved.

# 3. Chapter emotional curve

The emotional curve should be:

1. **Odd**
   > Why is Cheng Ye unhappy that the safety system protected him?

2. **Funny through logic**
   > He genuinely wants the fault to appear completely.

3. **Relationship comedy**
   > Zhou realizes he was selected as the most reliable person to force Cheng Ye's fault out.

4. **Escalating event**
   > Zhou accepts that as a challenge / recognition and starts helping force it out.

5. **Third-party pressure**
   > The tester now has to manage two freshmen who are both technically cooperative but are jointly driving toward a fault condition.

6. **Payoff**
   > The 0.11s finally appears.

7. **Hard tonal turn**
   > Rain. Blood. Pursuit. Same movement problem, but the ancient person will die if he cannot solve it.

8. **Action insight**
   > Cheng Ye copies the dangerous route once.

9. **Ontological reversal**
   > The ancient person sees him.

Do NOT keep making jokes after the rain becomes clearly life-or-death.
Humor frequency should drop sharply.

# 4. Required pre-rain Reader rewards

Before Ji Changgeng fully enters, the modern half must contain AT LEAST SIX distinct Reader rewards.

Do not interpret this as “write six punchlines.”

At least THREE of the six must remain interesting if every witty sentence is removed.

Recommended reward ladder:

## Reward 1｜Protection succeeds, Cheng Ye is dissatisfied
The safety mechanism correctly cuts / lowers the dangerous segment.

Everyone's default interpretation:
> good, the system caught it.

Cheng Ye's visible first concern:
> did the useful fault data survive, or did protection cut it too early?

Reader reaction:
> Why is he disappointed?

Function:
- immediate curiosity;
- Cheng Ye personality;
- modern cultivation texture;
- first comedy mismatch.

Do NOT begin with registration, policy, price, or biography.

## Reward 2｜The “wrong” goal becomes clear
The tester explains the minimum:
> the system did exactly what it should.

Cheng Ye is not arguing that safety is bad.
He wants to know:
> whether an allowed monitored mode can let the pressure segment finish far enough to capture the real transition fault.

Reader reaction:
> Everyone else wants a clean test; he wants a useful failure.

Important:
- make this reasonable;
- do not make him reckless;
- do not give a paragraph explaining that he is practical.

## Reward 3｜Rule-reading becomes action
Cheng Ye checks the actual rule / option and applies for the complete monitored pressure segment.

This should be quick.

The humor is NOT:
> he found a clever loophole.

The humor is:
> he is seriously using a normal safety rule for the unusual purpose of making his own known problem show itself more completely.

The tester should be able to accept the logic even while becoming more alert.

## Reward 4｜Zhou learns why he was chosen
Before this, Zhou can reasonably think:
> Cheng Ye picked / paired toward him because Cheng Ye wanted a strong opponent, a competitive test, or a good result.

The reveal:
> Cheng Ye watched Zhou's movement quality and specifically chose him because Zhou produces reliable pressure in the relevant direction / rhythm.

Critical effect:
> Zhou realizes he was not selected as “someone Cheng Ye wants to beat,” but as “someone Cheng Ye trusts to force his defect out.”

This should create mixed pride + competitive irritation.

Do not have narrator name the emotions.
Let Zhou's next decision reveal them.

## Reward 5｜Zhou takes over the crooked logic
This is mandatory.

Zhou must NOT merely say:
> fine, I'll help.

He should begin THINKING about the problem himself.

Functionally, he should notice / ask / test:
- when Cheng Ye normally retreats;
- which pressure causes the third-to-fourth transition;
- why the first run failed to expose it;
- what he can do differently without breaking the monitored rules.

This is the equivalent of:
> the second character catches the first character's logic and makes the event bigger.

This is the most important lesson to execute.

After this beat:
> the scene is no longer Cheng Ye doing something odd while Zhou watches.
> They are now jointly manufacturing a controlled failure condition for different personal reasons.

## Reward 6｜Tester becomes the funniest person without telling jokes
The tester should NOT become a comic straight man.

Use behavior.

Possible functional progression:
- initially watches routinely;
- after Cheng Ye requests the complete segment, attention sharpens;
- after Zhou starts deliberately reproducing the fault condition, tester changes position / readiness;
- before the final run, tester is physically closer to the stop control / intervention line.

The reader should understand:
> the professional adult is now taking these two much more seriously.

No quip needed.

## Optional Reward 7｜Literal compliance callback
The tester gives a concrete safety boundary:
> a clear stop condition, allowed range, or intervention threshold.

Both freshmen seriously acknowledge it.

Then they proceed to use the FULL allowed range.

The joke:
> they are not disobedient at all.
> They are obeying the boundary with uncomfortable precision.

Do not make them intentionally endanger themselves beyond the allowed condition.

## Optional Reward 8｜Emotion mismatch at success
When the 0.11s finally appears:
- Cheng Ye gets the “finally” moment;
- Zhou gets the “I actually forced it out” moment;
- the tester gets the opposite reaction: this is exactly the point where vigilance peaks.

Three people see the same event and emotionally read it differently.

Then rain arrives before this comedy can settle into another round.

# 5. Modern-half scene construction

## 5.1 Cold open

Start at or just before the FIRST protective interruption.

Good opening state:
- Cheng Ye is already in motion;
- Zhou's pressure is real;
- the safety logic intervenes;
- the test stops / changes;
- Cheng Ye looks at what got cut.

By roughly the first 300–450 Chinese characters, Reader should know:
- this is a freshman baseline movement test;
- Cheng Ye's reaction is unusual;
- there is a real physical issue, not a prank.

Do NOT frontload:
- full school background;
- how many days Zhou trained;
- entire test rules;
- Cheng Ye family finances;
- institutional history.

## 5.2 First pause

This pause exists to:
- reveal the 0.11s issue;
- reveal Cheng Ye's actual goal;
- set the legal monitored option;
- seed Zhou's misunderstanding.

Keep it active:
- screen / playback / body position / test line;
- characters must be doing something while talking.

Avoid:
> everyone standing still while the tester explains the world.

## 5.3 Zhou reveal

This must happen before the final high-pressure run.

Important:
> do not save it as a late “gotcha.”

The reveal should create the second half of the modern engine.

Zhou learns why Cheng Ye chose him.
Then Zhou makes a new decision.

The draft fails if:
> reveal -> explanation -> Zhou passively agrees -> next round.

The draft passes if:
> reveal -> Zhou reinterprets the first round -> pride/competition changes his method -> new pressure pattern emerges.

## 5.4 Second / final pressure sequence

Do not write a generic “they fought harder.”

Show 2–3 specific functional adjustments, not a long choreography.

For example, functionally:
- Zhou stops chasing broad advantage and instead keeps returning pressure toward the transition that makes Cheng Ye want to retreat;
- Cheng Ye deliberately refuses the usual half-step exit once;
- the tester watches the relevant body / data threshold;
- each participant is now pursuing a different goal inside the same exchange.

The reader should always know:
- where Cheng Ye is;
- what Zhou is trying to force;
- what Cheng Ye normally does;
- what Cheng Ye does differently this time;
- why the 0.11s appears.

# 6. Cheng Ye writing rules

Cheng Ye must be recognizable from behavior.

## Must show
- he wants useful evidence, not reassurance;
- he reads the rule literally but not maliciously;
- he accepts bounded risk on himself;
- he notices competence in Zhou;
- he can explain only what another person needs to act;
- he enjoys when a test becomes useful;
- once ancient danger appears, his tone tightens immediately.

## Do not show by narration
Do NOT write equivalents of:
- Cheng Ye was practical.
- Cheng Ye was good at using rules.
- Cheng Ye always liked useful data.
- Cheng Ye was different from other freshmen.
- Cheng Ye had already calculated everything.

Reader must infer these.

## Humor
One light turn per conversational beat is enough.

He is NOT:
- Lu Yang with a new name;
- a loophole comedian;
- a permanent deadpan joke machine;
- someone who treats Zhou as an object.

His choice of Zhou should carry genuine respect:
> he chose Zhou because Zhou is technically reliable.

That matters to the relationship.

---

# 7. Zhou Hang writing rules

Zhou needs his own engine.

## Initial state
He cares about:
> doing his own freshman test well.

He is trained enough to notice movement quality.

## Mid-scene shift
He discovers:
> Cheng Ye selected him because Zhou looked most capable of forcing the fault out.

The writer should let Zhou experience this as:
- a strange form of recognition;
- a challenge to his competence;
- something he personally wants to prove.

## Mandatory independent action
Zhou must make at least ONE decision Cheng Ye did not explicitly tell him to make.

Examples of FUNCTION, not required wording:
- changes pressure rhythm after noticing Cheng Ye's habitual retreat;
- proposes repeating the specific transition rather than broad sparring;
- deliberately gives Cheng Ye the same difficult angle more cleanly;
- refuses to count an earlier near-miss as “good enough.”

Do not make Zhou:
- arrogant rich kid;
- customer;
- helper NPC;
- exposition mouth;
- instant best friend.

Goal:
> by the end of the modern half, Reader can imagine wanting to see Cheng Ye and Zhou interact again.

---

# 8. Tester writing rules

The tester is the third active force.

## Goal
> get valid baseline data without allowing a freshman to cross a real injury threshold.

## Must do
- correctly back / trigger the first safety protection;
- explain only the minimum needed rule;
- approve the legitimate monitored option if conditions are met;
- become increasingly alert as Zhou and Cheng Ye jointly narrow onto the fault;
- retain authority to stop the test.

## Comedy method
Behavioral escalation, not witty lines.

Strong image:
> a professional who began the test in routine mode and gradually ends up watching these two as if they have turned a harmless freshman screen into a live engineering fault-reproduction session.

Do not make the tester:
- stupid;
- negligent;
- constantly shocked;
- an audience surrogate who says “you two are crazy.”

# 9. The 0.11s must become a story object

Do not treat 0.11s as a statistic mentioned once.

Before rain, Reader should understand it functionally:

> third movement ends;
> fourth movement should begin;
> Cheng Ye's safe habit is to create distance / wait;
> under strong continuous pressure, that delay becomes a real opening.

Do NOT repeatedly show “0.11” on screens.

One precise data appearance is enough.

After that, embody it:
- waiting;
- missing half a beat;
- needing to retreat;
- pressure arriving before the next movement is ready.

The reader should FEEL what 0.11s means before Ji appears.

That is what allows ancient resonance to hit hard.

# 10. Rain / ancient-world entry

The tonal shift must be immediate but not melodramatic.

## Entry
- Cheng Ye is already trapped in the exact transition;
- Zhou's pressure is still physically present;
- then rain enters his hearing;
- the modern hall does not vanish;
- an ancient courtyard / eaves / rain / blood overlays part of it.

Do not explain:
- time travel;
- resonance;
- hallucination;
- past-life memory;
- mechanism.

Cheng Ye should not pause long enough to theorize.

## Ji Changgeng first impression
First impression must be:
> young person trying to survive.

Not:
> legendary historical cultivator.

Show:
- injury;
- pursuit;
- wooden case;
- immediate decision;
- same movement bottleneck.

The reader should recognize the movement connection BEFORE receiving biography.

# 11. Ji's dangerous movement solution

This must be clear but brief.

Reader only needs to understand:

1. Ji reaches the same transition.
2. If he uses the safe / modern-like completion, he is too slow.
3. He forces the unfinished momentum / spiritual flow of the third movement directly into the fourth.
4. His leg/body visibly pays for it.
5. He gains the missing half-beat and escapes the immediate strike.

Do NOT:
- explain meridian theory in detail;
- label it a superior ancient method;
- have Cheng Ye instantly derive a complete principle;
- repeat the same mechanics three times.

Camera rule:
> one cause -> one dangerous action -> one visible cost -> one survival result.

---

# 12. Cheng Ye copies once

This should be fast.

Cheng Ye's thought is NOT:
> I have solved my five-year problem.

It is:
> he did not wait.

Zhou's pressure is already coming.
Cheng Ye tries the route once.

Result:
- fourth movement begins earlier;
- body / leg pain appears;
- modern equipment registers something abnormal;
- he gets through the immediate exchange.

The cost protects the setting:
> ancient choice is not simply better.

Do not stop for celebration.

---

# 13. Reciprocal sight

This is the chapter's largest beat.

Before this:
> Cheng Ye can still interpret the ancient scene as some kind of image / echo / impossible perception.

Then:
- Ji survives / clears his immediate threat enough to check the wooden case or surroundings;
- Ji notices something;
- he turns;
- his gaze lands on Cheng Ye;
- this must be spatially specific enough that coincidence is hard to believe;
- Ji reacts as if HE is seeing something impossible too.

Possible final function:
> a short question / lip movement equivalent to “你是谁？”

Do not explain after it.

No archive.
No database.
No historical achievement card.
No narrator conclusion about what this means.

End.

# 14. Comedy-density hard gates

Before delivering the draft, verify:

Before rain:

## Gate B｜Three event laughs
At least 3 rewards remain interesting if all witty phrasing is removed.

Required candidates:
- safety succeeds -> Cheng Ye dissatisfied;
- Zhou learns he was chosen as fault-forcing pressure;
- Zhou adopts the goal and makes the situation bigger;
- tester's professional behavior escalates.

## Gate C｜Second-character inheritance
At least one “crooked but valid” Cheng Ye logic must be inherited and extended by Zhou.

If only Cheng Ye is funny:
> FAIL.

## Gate D｜Callback
At least one early comic premise must return as a later consequence.

Preferred:
> safety boundary / tester vigilance introduced early -> becomes physically relevant during final pressure run.

## Gate E｜No joke stacking
No three consecutive paragraphs exist only to deliver jokes.

Every comic beat must also advance at least one of:
- goal;
- relationship;
- pressure;
- world texture;
- next decision.

# 15. Prose rhythm

Apply CURRENT_PROSE_STANDARD strictly.

Especially:

- continuous storytelling, not subtitle cards;
- default paragraphs 1–3 linked sentences;
- no 1–4 character normal dialogue ping-pong;
- no “少贫 / 查 / 行 / 对 / 刚才” standalone ordinary replies;

> tighter, more sensory, less joking.
