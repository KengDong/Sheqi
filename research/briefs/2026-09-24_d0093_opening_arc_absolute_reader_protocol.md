# First Principle

> **Do not finish a novel just because you are evaluating it.**

You are a normal target-platform reader.

If at any point you genuinely would leave this book on a real platform:
> STOP READING IMMEDIATELY.

Do not inspect later chapters to be fair.
Do not finish the mini-climax after you have already lost the desire to continue.

The exact abandonment point is higher-value evidence than a complete review.

# HARD INPUT BOUNDARY

Read only:
1. this protocol;

- any other anonymous arc;
- private mapping;
- project titles;

# Reading Procedure

Start at page 1.

## If you would abandon before the end
STOP.

Record:
1. exact chapter / approximate sentence or event where you would leave;
2. the last moment you were genuinely interested;
3. what changed between interest and abandonment;
4. what you had been waiting for that failed to arrive;
5. whether the problem feels local/executional or fundamental to what the story is offering.

Do not read later material.

## If you naturally reach the mini-climax
Continue through the settlement beat, then answer:

1. **Would you voluntarily click the next chapter after this Opening Arc?**
   - YES
   - MAYBE
   - NO

2. **What are you now specifically waiting to happen?**
   - one concrete event/desire, not “see what happens”.

3. **What are you following most?**
   - PERSON
   - RELATIONSHIP/GROUP
   - WORLD/LIFE
   - CONCEPT/MECHANISM
   - MIXED

4. **Which non-protagonist do you most want to see again?**
   - if none, say NONE.

5. **What permanent thing now exists that did not exist on page 1?**
   - asset / body change / relationship / obligation / status / home / rule / institution / etc.

6. **Did the world feel larger by the mini-climax?**
   - YES / PARTLY / NO
   - explain what new social/scene layer became real.

7. **Was the mini-climax satisfying?**
   - YES / PARTLY / NO
   - say exactly what got paid off.

8. **After the payoff, did desire rise, stay flat, or fall?**
   - RISE / FLAT / FALL

9. **What is the clearest repetition risk if the novel continued?**
   - describe, do not repair.

10. **What scene/image/person remains in memory without rereading?**

11. **Any strong AI / engineered / checklist feeling?**
   - identify exact pattern if present.
