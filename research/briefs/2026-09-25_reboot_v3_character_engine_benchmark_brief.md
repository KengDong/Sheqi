# REBOOT-V3 Character Engine Benchmark Brief
## 先研究“人物如何自己生戏”，再允许设计角色

# Goal

For the four ACTIVE candidates, identify:
> **commercially proven character/relationship engines that make ordinary chapters readable and let recurring people generate story independently of the protagonist's next task.**

This is NOT:
- a character design task;
- a naming task;
- a cast list task;
- a “copy popular character archetypes” task.

# ACTIVE Candidates

1. A01 《怪物死了，地盘还在》
2. A02 《末日先修水电》
3. A04 《冒险者公会救援队》
4. B10 《灵气复苏，我开二手装备店》

# Research Standard

Use:
> current/recent successful works + strong established controls where directly useful.

Prefer 2024–2026 for market relevance.

Total sample:
> about 8–12 books, with overlap allowed across candidate problems.

For each relevant benchmark record:

1. work/platform/date/status;
2. protagonist's recurring behavioral scene engine;
3. 3–6 recurring relationship roles;
4. what each recurring person wants independently;
5. how they act when protagonist is absent;
6. what old relationship memory changes later scenes;
7. how ordinary chapters stay readable without major plot novelty;
8. how humor/emotion arises from recurring relationships;
9. what makes characters memorable beyond traits;
10. what readers praise about cast/chemistry;
11. what readers complain about (NPC-ization, harem/card collecting, repetitive banter, etc.);
12. first-time-author execution difficulty;
13. AI-assisted production risk.

# Candidate-Specific Questions

## A01｜Territory / tenants / debt
Study:
- how property/community/organization fiction keeps residents from becoming management units;
- how ownership creates recurring human conflict;
- how people gain leverage over the protagonist.

Need functions, not skins.

## A02｜Infrastructure / community
Study:
- how survival/community fiction makes ordinary residents independent;
- how professional competence interacts with family, dignity, conflict and authority;
- how to avoid “all residents are stupid obstacles until protagonist fixes them.”

## A04｜Rescue profession
Study:
- team chemistry under danger;
- survivors who do not become automatic grateful allies;
- guilt, career pride, family, fear and professional disagreement;
- how old incidents return through people rather than only maps/problems.

## B10｜Shop / customers / old equipment
Study:
- fixed shop/counterparty cast;
- customers who become people rather than transaction cards;
- objects carrying relationship history;
- how shop fiction generates scenes without “new product of the week.”

# Benchmark Causality

For every proposed transferable lesson, answer:
- Negative control;
- Ablation;
- Reader-level mechanism;
- Compounding;
- Uniqueness;
- Function vs Form.
