# REBOOT-V4 Market Desire Map
## 先回答“读者想要什么”，再回答“怎么写”

# Research question

> For 2025–2026 current Chinese male web-fiction readers, what PRIMARY READER FANTASIES / emotional experiences are producing strong demand, especially for unknown / new-generation authors?

Do NOT begin from:
- engine;
- AI production convenience;
- state compounding;
- current Sheqi candidates.

# Platforms
- Qidian male
- Fanqie male

# Sample design

Use a broad but bounded current sample.

At minimum:
- 10–15 current/recent Qidian strong works;
- 10–15 current/recent Fanqie strong works;
- strong newcomer / low-author-credit cases;
- mature-author controls;
- matched ordinary/weaker same-category controls where accessible.

Prefer current 2025–2026 evidence.
Use older controls only when necessary.

# Unit of analysis

For each work separate:

## A. PRIMARY READER FANTASY
What does the reader repeatedly want to FEEL / EXPERIENCE?

## B. DELIVERY VEHICLE
What genre skin / job / system / organization delivers it?

Never merge these two.

# For each strong work

Record:
1. title;
2. platform;
3. author track;
4. current/recent measurable performance evidence;
5. category label;
6. PRIMARY READER FANTASY;
7. secondary fantasies;
8. protagonist fantasy;
9. delivery vehicle;
10. first-screen / Ch1 immediate pleasure;
11. repeated scene pleasure;
12. social/status/emotional reward;
13. what novelty becomes ordinary after Ch20;
14. what still keeps working after novelty fades;
15. breakout differentiator;
16. reader praise;
17. reader complaints;
18. what same-category ordinary books often imitate incorrectly.

# Matched-control requirement

For every major fantasy family:
> compare strong hit vs at least one ordinary/weaker same-category example where evidence exists.

Ask:
- what both have;
- what only hit executes better;
- what hit fantasy is stronger;
- what is just genre baseline;
- what is execution premium.

# Fantasy-family map

At minimum test:
- power growth / cultivation;
- urban supernatural / high martial arts;
- horror / weird / forbidden mystery;
- apocalypse / survival;
- game invasion / lord / national survival;
- historical agency / rebirth / rebellion / strategy;
- wealth / ownership / business / status reversal;
- collection / evolution / pet / equipment growth;
- romance / harem / attachment relevant to male readership;
- food / cozy / family / period-life;
- professional fiction;
- comedy / social embarrassment / public reaction.

Do not assume all are equally large.

# Critical questions

1. Which fantasies show repeated current demand across multiple hits?
2. Which are especially friendly to unknown/new authors?
3. Which fantasies are high-demand but execution-heavy?
4. Which low-stakes / mundane subjects work, and what pleasure makes them work?
5. When a profession title succeeds, what fantasy is it actually carrying?
6. Which “clever” concept types have weak fantasy demand despite good structure?
7. What does Qidian reward differently from Fanqie at the fantasy level?
8. Which current reader fantasies are underserved enough for an original Premium?
