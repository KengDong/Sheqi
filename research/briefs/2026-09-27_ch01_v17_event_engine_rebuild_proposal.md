# 0. Highest decision

Do NOT continue local-patching V16 around the paid sparring order.

The next version should preserve the already-working character / dual-world / payoff logic, but rebuild the opening event so that:

> Cheng Ye's personality itself twists an ordinary modern-cultivation event into something increasingly unusual, and that choice personally opens the dual-world premise.

Target reading experience:

> The event is interesting even with all witty wording removed.

Reference function to learn from strong light-xianxia openings:

> serious character judgment -> real action -> scene changes -> other people react -> consequence grows -> later callback.

Do NOT imitate any benchmark scene, exam structure, character configuration, or signature joke.

---

# 1. Core things to preserve from V16

These are NOT defects and must not be discarded during rebuild:

1. Cheng Ye has a five-year `掠云 -> 回风` transition delay around 0.11s.
2. He cannot casually afford a full dynamic diagnostic; 1200 yuan is a meaningful but not life-or-death resource threshold.
3. He has already been collecting his own abnormal-motion records instead of only complaining vaguely.
4. He is willing to accept a bounded cost on himself if the rule/environment makes the test worthwhile.
5. The anomaly must intersect HIS choice. It may not randomly save him.
6. Ji Changgeng first appears as a living young man under immediate pressure, not as a historical celebrity card.
7. The ancient movement is not simply superior. It trades bodily safety for time.
8. Cheng Ye copies the movement under real pressure and pays a physical cost.
9. Modern instrumentation really records the movement.
10. The dual-world mechanism upgrades from one-way observation to reciprocal sight.
11. The historical record later proves that Ji Changgeng existed thousands of years ago.
12. Cheng Ye is genuinely happy about money; he does not perform indifference.
13. Zhou Hang may remain as a peer, but not as a temporary buyer/tool.

# 2. Rebuild the opening event

## Recommended event shell

Use a normal, public, modern-cultivation freshman event before enrollment, such as:

> freshman pre-enrollment movement / body adaptation testing day

This is NOT a high-stakes entrance exam.
This is NOT a face-slapping talent test.
This is NOT a disguised paid-sparring order.

Its normal social purpose:

- freshmen get baseline movement / body data;
- schools or partner facilities identify risks before formal training;
- students naturally care about whether their results look good;
- the service is free or heavily subsidized, which matters to Cheng Ye.

## The crooked-but-valid goal difference

Most students:
> want to produce their best / cleanest result.

Cheng Ye:
> wants to use the free high-load environment to reliably expose the five-year 0.11s problem.

This difference must be visible through action, not explained as a personality thesis.

Key requirement:

> Even if every witty sentence is removed, “everyone is trying not to show an abnormality, while Cheng Ye is trying to make his own abnormality show up under a legal test condition” should still be funny / interesting.

# 3. Event-comedy engine

The chapter needs ONE real event-comedy chain, not a pile of quips.

Recommended functional chain:

1. **Normal premise**
   - freshmen are trying to make their test results clean.
   - the test system is designed to protect students and reduce pressure when instability appears.

2. **Cheng Ye's serious judgment**
   - he is here partly because a free standardized load is more useful than another vague self-test.
   - if the system keeps protecting him before the 0.11s appears, today's most valuable opportunity is wasted.

3. **First action**
   - he attempts the test normally / within allowed settings.

4. **System reaction**
   - safety logic lowers the later-stage load or terminates the instability before the exact problem is exposed.

5. **Scene consequence**
   - everyone else would regard that as good protection.
   - Cheng Ye regards it as an incomplete test.

6. **Second judgment**
   - he reads the actual rules / available test modes.
   - identifies one legal high-pressure / complete-load option with explicit personal confirmation and safety monitoring.

7. **Second action**
   - he chooses it.
   - no narrator speech about how smart / practical he is.

8. **Other-person reaction**
   - Zhou Hang / coach / operator does not become a joke machine.
   - they react as reasonable people seeing someone use a normal rule for an unusual but defensible purpose.

9. **Escalation**
   - Cheng Ye removes the usual half-step of safety.
   - the old delay finally appears under the exact pressure he wanted.

10. **Dual-world collision**
    - the rain / courtyard / Ji Changgeng arrives at the body's most relevant moment.

The comedy is:
> the safety system is correctly protecting a freshman; Cheng Ye is correctly trying to stop it from protecting him too early.

Both sides are reasonable.
That tension itself should carry the scene.

# 4. Cheng Ye recognizability target

The next draft must make Cheng Ye recognizable by behavior even if his name is removed.

## He should be the kind of person who:

- notices what the rule literally allows;
- cares about a concrete resource difference;
- tests a slightly unconventional route himself;
- accepts a bounded cost on himself;
- updates fast when evidence appears;
- does not need to win an argument before acting;
- becomes more serious when danger becomes real.

## Do NOT manufacture recognizability through:

- clever aphorisms;
- constant dry jokes;
- narrator praise;
- repeated “he had already calculated...” explanations;
- making every NPC slower than him;
- turning him into a loophole clown.

The chapter should contain at least one beat where:

> Cheng Ye's strange-looking action makes complete sense only after the reader understands his resource constraint + old injury + rule-reading habit.

# 5. Zhou Hang redesign

Strong recommendation:
> keep Zhou Hang as a same-cohort peer, not a buyer.

Possible function:

- same university freshman;
- noticeably better private-training conditions;
- wants a clean / high-quality result like a normal student;
- sees Cheng Ye deliberately push toward an abnormal result;
- initially does not understand why;
- later becomes a useful human reference point for “what normal students would do.”

Important:
- no hostility required;
- no rich-kid caricature;
- no face-slap;
- no need for him to explain the system;
- he should have at least one decision of his own.

The goal is to create a relationship that can survive past Chapter 1.

# 6. Suggested chapter beat map

This is a FUNCTION map, not prose and not a mandatory scene-by-scene script.

## Beat A｜Immediate oddity
- normal freshman testing context established quickly;
- Cheng Ye's goal is visibly different from everyone else's;
- no long platform / order field setup.

Reader question:
> Why is this guy trying to make his own problem show up?

## Beat B｜First failed attempt
- test safety logic prevents a clean exposure of the 0.11s delay;
- this is objectively good protection;
- to Cheng Ye it means the free opportunity is being wasted.

Reader reward:
> character logic becomes funny through the situation.

## Beat C｜Rule-reading choice
- Cheng Ye finds / confirms a legal complete-load option;
- safety monitoring remains active;
- he personally selects it.

This is the chapter's key agency hinge.

## Beat D｜Real pressure
- Zhou Hang / operator / coach responds;
- Cheng Ye removes his usual half-step safety behavior;
- the known weakness finally appears.

No extra narrator paragraph justifying why this is rational.

## Beat E｜Rain overlaps
- modern training hall remains visible;
- rain, stone courtyard, old eaves, blood, wooden case appear through / over it;
- no lore explanation.

## Beat F｜Ji Changgeng as a living person
- injured;
- being pursued;
- protecting / checking the wooden case;
- uses the same movement system;
- reaches the same transition problem.

## Beat G｜Ancient solution
- modern method wants the previous movement to settle before the next begins;
- Ji Changgeng has no time;
- he forces unfinished momentum / spiritual flow into the next move;
- right leg visibly pays the price;
- he survives the immediate strike.

Key reading effect:
> not “ancient secret is better,” but “this person is using a dangerous answer because the sword will kill him before the safe answer finishes.”

## Beat H｜Cheng Ye copies
- he does NOT instantly diagnose his five-year problem;
- he only recognizes a viable route he has never seen;
- he tries it because modern danger is already on him;
- physical pain + earlier completion happen together.

## Beat I｜Small payoff
- modern system records the unregistered movement;
- base completion / normal result appears;
- 800-yuan anomaly / sample reward may remain.

Important:
- 800 is a real pleasant surprise;
- it is not the chapter's largest revelation;
- Cheng Ye should genuinely enjoy it.

## Beat J｜Bigger reversal
- the rain does not disappear;
- Ji Changgeng notices something impossible;
- turns;
- looks directly at Cheng Ye;
- asks / mouths: “你是谁？”

This is the actual chapter climax.

## Beat K｜Minimal modern verification
Use only the minimum evidence required to make the impossible fact harder, not softer.

Recommended sufficient proof:
1. modern device really recorded the movement;
2. historical movement lineage contains an early matching form;
3. repaired historical image shows the same young man / same eyebrow scar;
4. minimal identity confirmation: Ji Changgeng, 4,100+ years ago, early co-shaper of `流云步`.

Do NOT dump:
- full later cultivation achievement;
- full lifespan;
- multiple biography rows;
- long theory debate about hallucination / secret realm / past-life / archive error.

## Beat L｜End
End on the contradiction:

> the archive says this is a man from thousands of years ago;
> less than a minute ago, that man looked at Cheng Ye and asked who he was.

No second explanatory conclusion after this.

# 7. 800-yuan handling

Recommendation:
> demote from headline-level hook to character-level reward.

Why:

- the real first-chapter fantasy is reciprocal contact with a living person from 4,100 years ago;
- 800 is useful because Cheng Ye cares about money and training access;
- letting him be visibly pleased makes him more human;
- the pleasure then being instantly overwhelmed by Ji Changgeng seeing him creates a strong scale jump.

Avoid:
- title that explicitly promises 800 if the body is trying to make it a surprise;
- pre-announce an 800 reward pool;
- make Cheng Ye take the high-load mode because of 800;
- post-award explanation paragraph proving why 800 is exciting.

# 8. Camera / rhythm rules for this rewrite

## Stay longer on:
- Cheng Ye making the unusual choice;
- the safety system frustrating his purpose;
- other people realizing what he is trying to do;
- Ji Changgeng's escape problem;
- reciprocal sight.

## Skip / compress:
- routine registration;
- complete rules recitation;
- every intermediate body movement;
- repeated proof that testing is legitimate;
- repeated proof that Cheng Ye is rational;
- repeated history-database steps.

## Hard test
For every paragraph:
> after the reader understands the current action/result, does the paragraph continue filming it anyway?

If yes:
> compress unless relationship or consequence is still changing.

# 9. Modern-cultivation texture

Do not solve “life texture” by adding more system names.

Use existing stakes:
- free vs 1200 diagnostic;
- private training access;
- standardized freshman screening;
- injury risk;
- university preparation;
- data that later matters to doctors / training.

The society should feel modern because:
> cultivation has institutions, costs, defaults, safety rules, and unequal access.

Not because:
> every noun gets a platform / database / smart-device prefix.

3. Cheng Ye causes the escalation through a choice.

5. The 0.11s problem matters before the anomaly.

7. The ancient movement has a cost.
8. Cheng Ye does not instantly understand everything.

10. Reciprocal sight is the largest escalation.
11. Historical verification is minimal but decisive.
