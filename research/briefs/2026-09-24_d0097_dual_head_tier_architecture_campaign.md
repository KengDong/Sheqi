# D-0097｜Dual Head-Tier Architecture Campaign
## 《劫痕道途》 + 《把一条副本规则带回现实》

# Author Trigger

Author judgment:
> 两本Opening好看，但真正摊开以后仍显得“小家子气”；需要从第一性原理把架构提升到起点/番茄头部爆款尺度，再考虑继续正文。

# Core Rule

Do NOT solve scale by:
- bigger maps;
- more factions;
- more lore;
- more scars;
- more rules;
- more dungeons;
- more villains.

Scale must come from:
> **one core Premium activating multiple autonomous systems whose consequences persist and later collide.**

A head-tier long-form should be able to change story mode while preserving the same Premium.

# Campaign Stages

## Stage 1｜Head-Tier Architecture Benchmarks
Two isolated lanes.

### Qidian Lane
Main benchmark:
> 《没钱修什么仙？》

Secondary structural contrast:
> 《玄鉴仙族》 + current Qidian top-field evidence.

Question:
> why can a familiar cultivation chassis produce 100/300/700+ chapter expansion without feeling like a bigger map version of the same story?

### Fanqie Lane
Benchmarks:
> 《十日终焉》
> 《时停起手，邪神也得给我跪下！》
> 《全球死考：刚下刑场你让我去考试》

Question:
> how do rule/infinite/high-concept hits rotate modes, rewards, relationships and scale without becoming Case-of-Week?

No candidate-specific fixing in benchmark lanes.

## Stage 2｜Independent Architecture Forge
After BOTH benchmark lanes are DONE:

- Scar Hit-Scale Architect
- Rule-Reality Hit-Scale Architect

Each creates:
> Ch1–100 + Ch100–300 + 300+ macro architecture.

No prose.

## Stage 3｜Independent Red Team
After corresponding architecture forge is DONE:

- Scar Scale Red Team
- Rule-Reality Scale Red Team

Mission:
> try to kill the architecture.

Test:
- repetition;
- fake scale;
- Case Supply;
- reward sameness;
- character flattening;
- mystery exhaustion;
- world inflation;
- mother-market erosion;
- whether the Premium is actually stronger at Ch100/300 than Ch10.

## Stage 4｜Total Editor Merge
Only after:
- two benchmark reports;
- two architectures;
- two red-team reports.

Then editor decides:
- architecture PASS;
- revision;
- HOLD/STOP.

No prose before Stage 4.

# Shared Head-Tier Requirements

Every architecture must answer:

## Ch20
What permanent fruits exist across:
- power;
- money/resource;
- status;
- relationship;
- knowledge;
- organization/life state?

## Ch50
Which Ch10 assets/people/rules now produce consequences without being reintroduced?

## Ch100
Has the book entered a larger SOCIAL SYSTEM, not just a larger map?

## Ch300
Has story mode changed at least twice while Premium remains central?

## 1–3M words
Can old assets, relationships, institutions and rules create stories without constant fresh-case supply?
