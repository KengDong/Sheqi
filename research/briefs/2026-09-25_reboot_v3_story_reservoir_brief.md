# Task

For each ACTIVE candidate:
> generate 30–50 genuinely different Situation Seeds.

Do NOT order chapters.

Each seed should answer:
- what concrete situation happens;
- who wants what;
- what makes it worth reading;
- what old state/asset/person can matter;
- what changes after the scene.

# Required Stress Tests

## 1. Reward Sameness
After 15–20 seeds:
> are rewards still meaningfully different?

## 2. No-Fresh-Gimmick
Can 10+ good scenes exist without:
- new ability;
- new monster species;
- new world;
- new customer type;
- new ruleset?

## 3. Old-State Compounding
How many seeds reuse:
- old people;
- old assets;
- old debts;
- old locations;
- old failures;
- old reputation?

## 4. Big / Medium / Small Scene Ecology
Need all three:
- large public/high-stakes;
- medium conflict/progression;
- small relationship/ordinary-life.

## 5. Recurring Human Layer
At least some situations must be generated by:
> recurring people choosing things,
not only by the external engine producing a new task.

## 6. Ordinary-Chapter Test
For each candidate:
> list 10 plausible ordinary chapters that do not depend on a major novelty reveal.

If impossible:
> strong production-risk warning.

## 7. AI-Native Production Check
For each:
- state compressibility;
- hallucination blast radius;
- human-only taste burden;
- likely draft-to-publish gap;
- where AI would become repetitive/generic.
