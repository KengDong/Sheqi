# R4 Revival Opening Arc Card｜《把一条副本规则带回现实》

Frozen Concept:
> after clearing a rule-survival instance, protagonist must permanently bring one rule from that instance into reality. The rule applies for real and can later combine with old rules.

Example first rule:
> **天黑后不能说真名。**

Prior proof:
- Reservoir 31;
- Premium Survival PASS;
- Commercial Compounding STRONG.

Prior warnings:
- Reward Sameness WARNING;
- Case Supply MEDIUM-HIGH;
- cognitive load can grow quickly.

Opening Arc mission:
> Prove one carried rule can generate enough life, relationship and survival story before the novel asks for another rule.

First mini-climax should stay mostly with the first rule.

Need:
- the rule intrudes into normal life immediately;
- it harms/complicates someone protagonist cares about;
- protagonist discovers one usable advantage;
- someone else has a different interest in the rule;
- a conflict is settled using the rule in a way that permanently changes home/relationship/safety.

After payoff:
> Reader wants to see the consequences of living under this rule, not merely "what is rule #2?"

Hard watch:
- rule encyclopedia;
- immediate second-rule addiction;
- arbitrary loopholes;
- explanation-heavy logic puzzle prose.
