# WHY

The previous round over-optimized structural safety and allowed concepts that were:
- market-adjacent;
- long-form viable;
- anti-treadmill;
but visibly weaker at title / premise / desire level than the real hit references already collected.

AUTHOR has explicitly rejected this workflow.

# NEW HIGHEST RULE

> **No new concept may enter structural design before AUTHOR sees it side-by-side with real hit references.**

The test is not:
> “Is this structurally valid?”

The first test is:
> **“On the same shelf, does this even deserve to stand next to the references?”**

# FIRST-PASS OUTPUT FORMAT

For every new raw idea, show AUTHOR only:

1. **Reference(s)** — 1–3 nearest real hit works.
2. **Why the reference is clickable** — one plain-Chinese sentence.
3. **Candidate title**.
4. **Candidate one-line hook**.
5. **Candidate 80–150 Chinese-character premise**.
6. **What concrete desire / mystery / target remains after the first payoff**.
7. **Why this is not just a weaker sanitized version of the reference**.

No Ch20.
No Ch100.
No 14-item concept sheet.
No relationship-engine audit.
No anti-treadmill essay.

# AUTHOR DECISION

AUTHOR can answer:
- 推进
- 可能
- 砍

Only **推进** may enter full Concept architecture.

“可能” stays in parking lot.
“砍” stops immediately.

AI may not rescue a rejected idea by adding more explanation.

# HARD BENCHMARK GATE

Before presenting a candidate:
- compare its title against the real reference title;
- compare its hook against the real reference promise;
- compare its premise against the real reference premise;
- remove editor-facing defensive language such as:
  - “不是技能槽”
  - “不会全员下跪”
  - “不是任务流”
  unless the phrase itself is genuinely consumer-facing.

If the candidate's appeal only becomes visible after explaining long-form structure:
> **DO NOT PRESENT AS A VIABLE IDEA.**

# IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Copyright / originality still applies:
- do not copy names;
- do not copy character combinations;
- do not copy concrete opening events;
- do not rename a hit plot.

But:
> **strong premise geometry, scale, immediacy, concrete long-term magnet, and commercial directness are allowed to be benchmarked.**

“Do not copy” must never again be interpreted as:
> “make the premise weaker and more abstract.”
