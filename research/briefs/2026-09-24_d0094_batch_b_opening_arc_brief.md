# Goal

Give every newly qualified candidate enough real prose to prove whether its root engine can become a novel.

Do not optimize for candidate reduction.

# Arc Length

Natural target:
> **3—5 chapters / approximately 8,000—15,000 Chinese characters**

Do not force equal chapter counts.

Stop only after:
> first natural mini-climax / first meaningful settlement.

# Required reader experience by the end

Not a checklist to mechanically insert, but the arc should naturally contain:
1. core promise on-page;
2. protagonist concrete desire;
3. first meaningful fruit;
4. fruit creates cost;
5. at least one relationship changes;
6. world/social interface expands;
7. pressure learns/intensifies;
8. protagonist makes a real choice;
9. first mini-climax/payoff lands;
10. a specific larger desire remains after payoff.
