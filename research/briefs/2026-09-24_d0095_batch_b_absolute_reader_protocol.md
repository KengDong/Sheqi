# First Principle

> **Do not finish a novel just because you are evaluating it.**

Treat the assigned text exactly like an unfamiliar Qidian/Fanqie opening.

If at any point you genuinely would leave on a real platform:
> STOP READING IMMEDIATELY.

Do not inspect later chapters for fairness.

Read only:
1. this protocol;
2. the one anonymous Opening Arc assigned in CURRENT.

Do NOT read:
- any other anonymous arc;
- private mapping;
- real title;
- winner / revival status;
- Reservoir;
- Shelf;

# If you would abandon

STOP and record:
1. exact chapter / approximate sentence or event;
2. last genuinely interesting moment;
3. what changed;
4. what you were waiting for that failed to arrive;
5. local execution issue vs fundamental lack of desire.

Do not read later material.

# If you naturally finish the mini-climax

Answer:

1. Would you voluntarily click the next chapter? YES / MAYBE / NO
2. What specifically are you waiting to happen next?
3. What are you following most?
   - PERSON
   - RELATIONSHIP/GROUP
   - WORLD/LIFE
   - CONCEPT/MECHANISM
   - MIXED
4. Which non-protagonist do you most want to see again? If none, NONE.
5. What permanent thing exists now that did not exist on page 1?
6. Did the world feel larger? YES / PARTLY / NO.
7. Was the mini-climax satisfying? YES / PARTLY / NO.
8. After payoff, desire: RISE / FLAT / FALL.
9. Clearest repetition risk.
10. Strongest remembered scene/image/person without rereading.
11. Any strong AI / engineered / checklist feeling? Identify exact pattern.
