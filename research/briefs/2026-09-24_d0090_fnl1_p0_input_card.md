# FNL1 P0 Input Card
## 《养一条会跟我走的灵脉》

status: frozen-input
writer_visibility: FNL1 only

# Book Desire Thesis

A bottom-tier itinerant cultivator who has spent his life being told “this place is not yours” finds an abandoned dying spirit vein.

He does not ultimately want a giant sect.

He wants:
> **a place where he and the people he cares about cannot be thrown out simply because they lack money, status or permission.**

The Premium:
> he nurtures the dead vein until it can move and follow him.

Eventually land, fields, people and a home can grow around a resource that moves.

# Protagonist Lens

At a new place, he instinctively notices:
- water;
- roads;
- what can be carried;
- what cannot;
- who would be left behind if they had to leave tonight.

Do not state this as a personality summary.
Show it through action.

# P0 Promise

By the end of the opening slice:
> the Reader must encounter the first undeniable sign that the abandoned spirit vein is following him / choosing to move with him.

Before or simultaneously, Reader must understand:
> why this matters to this specific person.

# Human Consequence

The miracle must immediately change a present problem such as:
- where he can stay;
- what he can carry;
- who is affected;
- what obligation now exists.

Choose naturally.
Do not build a whole settlement in P0.

# Tone

Qidian-first.

Readable, concrete, lived-in cultivation world.
Can contain quiet humor or embarrassment.
Do not make the vein a cute pet.
Do not make the protagonist a chosen-one prince.

# Hard No

- no system panel;
- no spirit-vein pet voice;
- no giant lore dump;
- no copied school/finance/array/alchemy scenes;
- no “mobile sect” explanation speech;
- no chapter roadmap.
