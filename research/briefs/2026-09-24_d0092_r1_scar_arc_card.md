# R1 Revival Opening Arc Card｜《劫痕道途》

Frozen Concept:
> Zhou Ye's body carries old tribulation scars; the costs others once externalized have become permanent structures he can learn to use. Power and physical debt are the same thing.

Prior proof:
- Reservoir 38;
- Case Supply LOW;
- Premium Survival PASS;
- Hit Gap protagonist/opponent/emotion PASS.

Prior loss:
> Shelf Conversion friction only.

Protagonist core:
> Zhou Ye instinctively asks of every power: **"这笔账最后落到谁身上？"**

Human flaw:
> he confuses accepting help with transferring his cost to others.

Opening Arc mission:
> Make the abstract "scar = cultivation structure" immediately concrete, bodily and emotionally legible.

By first mini-climax, Reader should experience:
- one scar is genuinely useful;
- using it creates a cost that remains;
- a person close to him has a reason to oppose/support his choice;
- a "clean" cultivation route or authority provides a credible alternative;
- Zhou Ye makes a choice that defines why his path is his.

Mini-climax:
> first meaningful win/settlement where the very cost others rejected becomes the reason Zhou Ye can do something they cannot.

After payoff:
> Reader wants to see how he can build a cultivation path out of a body that cannot simply be healed clean.

Hard watch:
- pain-porn;
- body-stat manual;
- vague philosophical "cost" language;
- another-scar-as-another-skill repetition.
