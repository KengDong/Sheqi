## 《把整座城市当成超能力》

status: frozen-input
writer_visibility: FNL2 only

# Book Desire Thesis

A low-level frontline city emergency/maintenance worker discovers that registered public infrastructure can briefly become supernatural under disaster conditions.

His deepest instinct:
> **when everyone looks at the big system, he looks at the people who keep it working and the people who get hurt when it fails.**

The growth fantasy:
> one lamp -> one street -> one district -> one city.

But the city is never a toy.
People live inside every system he uses.

# Protagonist Lens

In ordinary situations he notices:
- broken lights;
- blocked exits;
- trapped elevators;
- real maintenance state;
- the worker who knows what the dashboard does not.

Do not tell the Reader he is responsible.
Show him acting like this before/while the supernatural appears.

# P0 Promise

By the end:
> one familiar city facility must perform an impossible, visual action.

Preferred function:
> something as immediately legible as a row of streetlights all turning toward the same monster.

Exact surface execution is yours; do not turn it into a technical lecture.

# Human Consequence

The impossible action must immediately matter to:
- a resident;
- coworker;
- passerby;
- patient;
- trapped person;
or another concrete human presence.

The city must feel inhabited.

# Tone

Fanqie-first, Qidian viable.

Fast, visual, low jargon.
Frontline reality before “city administrator” fantasy.

# Hard No

- no infrastructure encyclopedia;
- no abstract permission table;
- no cyberpunk dashboard dump;
- no remote god-mode protagonist;
- no facility catalogue;
- no copied characters/scenes from urban-supernatural competitors.
