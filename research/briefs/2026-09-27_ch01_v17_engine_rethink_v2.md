# Proposed two-stage engine

## Stage A｜Modern social misread

Container:
> freshman movement/adaptation test, preferably paired pressure/calibration.

Cheng Ye's private objective:
> expose a real recurring movement bottleneck under standardized pressure.

Peer's independent objective:
> prove/verify his own training under the same standardized environment.

Neither fully understands the other's objective.

Because of that:
- peer increases seriousness/pressure for his own reason;
- Cheng Ye welcomes it for his own reason;
- each interprets the other's response through his own goal;
- the exchange escalates;
- safety/staff interrupts.

Reveal:
> they were not competing / cooperating toward the same result at all.

This should be a complete amusing mini-story before any ancient-world premise.

## Stage B｜Dual-world reversal

The flagged abnormality gives a natural reason for one supervised repeat/continuation.

At the exact bottleneck:
- source-world overlap;
- Ji Changgeng has the same movement problem under life-or-death pressure;
- Ji uses a dangerous answer;
- Cheng Ye copies once.

Modern instrumentation records it.

Then:
> Ji sees Cheng Ye.

This reclassifies the source scene from:
> strange observation
to:
> reciprocal contact with a living person.

Minimal historical verification follows.
