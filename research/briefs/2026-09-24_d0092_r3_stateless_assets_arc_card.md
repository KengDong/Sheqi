# R3 Revival Opening Arc Card｜《无身份资产》

Frozen Premium:
> on "missing days", everyone can see the protagonist but cannot map him back to his original identity; identity systems fail with him.

Commercial engine:
> he deliberately accumulates assets usable even when no system recognizes who he is: cash, physical keys, fixed places, pre-agreed protocols, trusted people, paper processes, identity-independent business continuity.

Prior proof:
- Reservoir 37;
- Reward Sameness PASS;
- Premium Survival STRONG PASS;
- Case Dependency LOW-MEDIUM.

Prior loss:
> first-bite mass-market / obvious爽感 weaker than top five, not structural failure.

Opening Arc mission:
> Make "the world does not recognize me, but what I built still works" feel like a commercial fantasy rather than an abstract identity essay.

Need:
- first identity-loss day creates immediate concrete humiliation/danger;
- protagonist cannot solve it through explanation or a hidden tech fix;
- one physical or relational asset genuinely works;
- a close person seeing him as a stranger must matter emotionally;
- first mini-climax proves an asset/protocol can carry him through a real problem while identity remains broken.

After payoff:
> Reader wants to see what he will build before the next identity-loss day.

Hard watch:
- missing-person case-of-week;
- procedural identity bureaucracy;
- philosophical monologues;
- magical restoration of recognition.
