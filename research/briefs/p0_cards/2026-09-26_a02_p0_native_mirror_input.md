# P0 Input Card｜A02《末日先修水电》

## Native Card｜READ FIRST
Title:
> 《末日先修水电》

Shelf promise:
> 别人抢粮抢枪时，他先抢修水泵、配电和门禁——只要一栋楼重新出水亮灯，就能让一群人真正活下来。

Protagonist:
> 林湛（provisional）

Core behavior:
> 把“还能撑多久”翻成具体维修/负载/维护选择，但不把专业正确等同于社会上自动正确。

Recurring relationship seeds:
- 孙雯：居民分配/自治立场，关心谁承担今天的不便；
- 赵梁：第二个懂技术的人，能独立判断，不是主角的捧哏。

Near contract:
> Ch1–3内，第一栋楼最关键的水泵/应急电真实恢复，而且不是“今晚亮一下”，而是留下维护、分配和谁被优先/延后的永久状态。

P0 must show:
- physical lack is visible;
- technical action has human consequence;
- at least one other person has a reasonable competing priority;
- 林湛 makes a choice with cost;
- premise works on-page, not as future plan.

Do not:
- dump engineering terminology;
- make residents stupid so protagonist can lecture;
- solve everything in one switch flip;
- add instant-build/engineering cheat.

## Mirror Function Card｜READ ONLY AFTER NATIVE FREEZE
Function benchmarks:
> fast profession/survival openings: concrete problem -> active diagnosis -> decision -> visible result -> changed social state.

Mirror only:
1. open on a felt consequence (dry tap/dark corridor/failed lift/medical load), not apocalypse exposition;
2. force protagonist into action immediately;
3. premise-in-action before full system explanation;
4. visible partial payoff inside the screen;
5. relationship conflict enters before or with payoff;
6. end on “who gets the restored resource / who maintains it”, not another disaster teaser.

Cadence target:
- first quarter: Reader knows exactly what is failing and why it matters tonight;
- middle: 林湛 commits to one repair/priority;
- second half: something physically works or almost works;
- final beat: restoration creates a harder human choice.

Forbidden:
- benchmark wording/scene copying;
- miracle engineering;
- “everyone doubts him, then all are shocked” template;
- living-author voice imitation.
