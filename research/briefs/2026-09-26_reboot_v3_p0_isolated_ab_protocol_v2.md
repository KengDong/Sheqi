## Why separate windows
Same-file or same-window phase boundaries are insufficient because prior tool output remains in model context.

Therefore:
> Native and Mirror MUST be different roles/windows.

- recurring relationship seed;
- Near contract.

Mirror may NOT copy:

- scene;

- character mapping;
- signature conflict;
- joke;
- reveal;
- living-author voice.
