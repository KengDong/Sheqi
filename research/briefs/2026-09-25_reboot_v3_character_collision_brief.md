# Task

For each ACTIVE candidate create a compact character engine:

## A. Protagonist
Define:
1. provisional name;
2. concrete current desire;
3. non-gimmick recurring behavior engine;
4. decision rule under pressure;
5. flaw / cost;
6. social style;
7. what protagonist does on an ordinary day with no new task;
8. why Reader could describe them without mentioning system/power/occupation premise.

Do NOT use generic labels as the answer.

## B. 3–5 Recurring Core People

For each:
1. provisional name / role;
2. independent want;
3. recurring behavior;
4. offscreen action;
5. leverage over protagonist;
6. how protagonist can hurt/help them;
7. one horizontal relationship with another core person;
8. old-state memory;
9. what would make them oppose protagonist;
10. what they may do that protagonist dislikes but cannot simply forbid.

## C. Relationship Network

Must contain:
- at least two meaningful non-protagonist relationships;
- at least one alliance that can split;
- at least one conflict that can improve into respect/dependence;
- at least one person whose leverage grows when protagonist succeeds.

## D. 10–15 Character Collision Events

At least half must NOT start with:
- new task;
- new monster;
- new emergency;
- new customer;
- new equipment;
- new location;
- new system rule.

Each event records:
- who wants what;
- why desires conflict;
- why scene is worth reading;
- what old state matters;
- permanent relationship delta.

## E. Protagonist-Absent Simulation

At least 3 mini-scenarios:
> protagonist disappears for 24 hours.

Show:
- who acts;
- what changes;
- what new problem/opportunity exists when protagonist returns.

## F. Ordinary-Chapter Test

At least 5 chapter-sized situations using only:
> old people + old location + old relationship state.

No novelty reveal.

## G. Anti-AI Self-Attack

For each candidate identify:
- where voices may converge;
- where gratitude shortcut may appear;
- where strawman conflict may appear;
- where cast becomes spreadsheet;
- where relationship resets may occur;
- how to prevent it structurally.
