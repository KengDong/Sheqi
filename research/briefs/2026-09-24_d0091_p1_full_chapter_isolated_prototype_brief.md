# D-0091｜Three-Way Isolated P1 Full-Chapter Prototype

status: active
date: 2026-09-24
phase: P1
scope: one complete Chapter 1 per finalist

# Mission

For exactly one assigned finalist:
> write one complete, real Chapter 1.

The chapter must begin from the already validated P0 material/function,
but you are not required to preserve every sentence.

You may:
- retain;
- compress;
- rewrite;
- extend;
the P0 so the chapter reads as one natural chapter.

# Highest Goal

> **A stranger who voluntarily continued after the first screen still wants Chapter 2 after the full chapter.**

# Input Boundary

Read only:
1. this shared brief;
2. your candidate-specific P1 card;
3. your own candidate's P0 First Screen.

Do NOT read:
- other finalist prose/cards;
- Fresh Reader report;
- private mapping;
- D-0089 competitor reports;
- other writers' selfchecks;
- old C01/C12 prose;
- unrelated Canon/Outline.

Reason:
> candidate-specific P1 card already distills the evidence gap without exposing comparative results.

# Chapter Length

Target:
> **2500—4000 Chinese characters**

Do not pad to hit word count.
A shorter complete chapter is preferable to filler.

# Core Rule

The P0 Premium must remain,
but the rest of Chapter 1 must prove:
> **the novel has somewhere to go after the first-screen trick.**

The second half must not simply repeat/escalate the same spectacle.

# Chapter-End Pull

End because:
- a concrete next problem becomes unavoidable;
- a relationship/obligation changes;
- a new choice opens;
- the first reward creates a cost;
- or the protagonist takes an action that naturally demands Chapter 2.

Do not end with narrator promises.

# Human Prose

Keep:
- physical action;
- lived objects;
- imperfect reactions;
- local social pressure;
- specific human behavior.

Avoid:
- mechanism lecture;
- system-manual paragraphs;
- "this means..." summary;
- future roadmap;
- artificial aphorism stacks.

# Hard

- no Ch2;
- no Concept mutation;
- no second core system;
- no cross-finalist reading;
- no benchmark imitation;
- no Primary choice;
- no broad research.

# Output

Candidate-specific path in CURRENT.

Also:
- selfcheck;
- CURRENT DONE;
- history;
- Git commit;
then STOP.
