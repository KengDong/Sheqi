# 0. Author core

新生免费摸底，别人都怕测试时出问题，程野却嫌安全机制太负责，自己的老毛病刚露头就被保护掉了。

于是他认真研究规则，申请把压力测完整；原本只想做好自己成绩的周航发现这家伙主动挑自己，居然是为了让自己把他的毛病逼出来。

两个人较上了劲——一个非要把自己的问题测出来，一个非要证明自己真能把它逼出来，连负责保护新生的测试员都不得不盯紧他们。

直到程野真的卡在那0.11秒里，他听见了雨声。

四千年前，一个正在被人追杀的年轻人，也恰好卡在同一式上。那个人用伤一条腿的代价跨了过去，程野照着试了一次。

然后他发现，自己不只是看见了对方——对方也看见了他。

# 1. Why this engine is stronger

## A. The test has three aligned-but-conflicting goals

### Cheng Ye
He wants:
> his real old problem to appear completely under safe, legal pressure.

### Zhou Hang
He initially wants:
> a good, complete freshman test result for himself.

After discovering Cheng Ye deliberately chose him as the pressure source:
> pride / competitiveness activates.
> If Cheng Ye thinks Zhou can force the fault out, Zhou wants to prove he actually can.

### Tester / safety staff
Wants:
> valid data without a freshman getting hurt.

Therefore:
- no actor is stupid;
- no actor exists only to explain rules;
- each action naturally raises pressure on the others.

## B. “Others are afraid of problems” is emotional/social, not logical concealment

Do NOT rewrite this as:
> everyone deliberately hides abnormalities.

Correct reading:
- freshmen naturally hope their first official baseline test goes smoothly;
- an abnormal result can mean anxiety, follow-up, training restrictions, embarrassment, or uncertainty;
- they are emotionally reluctant to have problems appear even though the screening exists to detect them.

Cheng Ye differs because:
> he already knows a problem exists and is frustrated when safety logic prevents him from reproducing it fully.

This contrast is natural.

## C. Safety logic is the first comedy engine

The safety system is competent.
It detects the beginning of instability and protects Cheng Ye.

For an ordinary freshman:
> good.

For Cheng Ye:
> frustrating, because the exact fault disappears before it can be captured.

The humor comes from:
> the system succeeds at exactly the wrong moment for Cheng Ye's personal objective.

No witty system message is needed.

## D. Rule-reading is a hinge, not the whole chapter

Cheng Ye seriously checks:
> what the test actually allows after a protective interruption.

He finds a legitimate monitored way to complete the pressure segment.

This displays:
- practical intelligence;
- rule-reading;
- willingness to accept bounded risk.

But do NOT make the chapter about menus, loopholes, or procedural debate.

## E. Zhou's reveal does not end the engine — it upgrades it

Critical correction over V3:

When Zhou learns:
> Cheng Ye deliberately chose him because he looked capable of forcing the fault out,

the misunderstanding does NOT simply resolve.

It creates a new motive:
> Zhou now wants to prove Cheng Ye judged him correctly.

Thus the relationship progression is:

1. Zhou wants his own good result.
2. Cheng Ye chooses Zhou for pressure.
3. Zhou realizes why.
4. Zhou takes that as a challenge / recognition.
5. Both knowingly push the same exchange for different but compatible goals.

This is much stronger than:
> misunderstanding -> explanation -> cooperative retest.

The reveal itself creates the next escalation.

## F. Tester becomes the third active force

The tester is not a rule dispenser.

The tester sees:
- one student actively asking to reproduce his own abnormality;
- the other student getting increasingly serious about forcing it out;
- both still technically within monitored conditions.

Therefore the tester must:
> pay closer attention / tighten safety readiness / decide when enough is enough.

This adds live scene pressure.

The test no longer feels like:
> Cheng Ye + NPC system.

It becomes:
> Cheng Ye vs his own problem,
> Zhou vs Cheng Ye's confidence in him,
> tester vs unnecessary risk.

# 2. Core escalation chain

1. freshman free baseline movement screening;
2. Cheng Ye's old problem begins to appear;
3. safety mechanism intervenes too early;
4. Cheng Ye is dissatisfied because the useful part vanished;
5. he seriously checks the rules;
6. he applies for / chooses the allowed monitored complete-pressure segment;
7. Zhou learns Cheng Ye picked him because Zhou looked capable of forcing the problem out;
8. Zhou's motive changes from “finish my own test well” to “then I'll really force it out”;
9. Cheng Ye welcomes the pressure;
10. tester becomes increasingly alert;
11. both push the exchange harder but for intelligible reasons;
12. Cheng Ye finally enters the exact 0.11s bottleneck;
13. rain appears;
14. Ji Changgeng, in a completely independent life-or-death crisis, reaches the same movement bottleneck;
15. Ji uses a dangerous route that costs his leg/body to save the half-beat;
16. Cheng Ye copies once under current pressure;
17. modern movement succeeds / instrumentation records it / bodily cost appears;
18. Cheng Ye initially only sees the ancient person;
19. Ji finishes/checks his immediate survival objective;
20. Ji turns and sees Cheng Ye back.

# 3. The two real chapter reversals

## Reversal 1｜Modern relationship
Before:
> Zhou thinks he is simply doing his own test against a serious peer.

After:
> he learns Cheng Ye specifically selected him as the person most likely to expose Cheng Ye's hidden fault.

Effect:
> not embarrassment resolution, but competitive escalation.

## Reversal 2｜Dual-world ontology
Before:
> Cheng Ye can still classify the ancient scene as echo / hallucination / residue / unknown overlay.

After:
> Ji reacts to Cheng Ye.

Effect:
> the “image” becomes a living actor on the other side.

Reversal 2 must be much larger than Reversal 1.

# 4. Opening target

Do NOT open with:
- diagnostic price;
- 0.11s explanation;
- school policy;
- full testing rules;
- Zhou biography;
- money arithmetic.

Open at or just before:
> Cheng Ye's first test is being safely interrupted.

Reader should first see:
> everyone else regards the protection as normal;
> Cheng Ye is dissatisfied.

Immediate question:
> why is he annoyed that the test protected him?

Then reveal his prior problem through the next action / conversation.

# 5. Cheng Ye first-chapter personality

Must be visible through behavior:

- he already knows his body has a repeatable problem;
- he does not want reassurance; he wants usable evidence;
- safety protection does not make him reckless, but he wants the legal boundary pushed far enough to learn something;
- he reads rules seriously;
- he picks Zhou because Zhou's pressure looks reliable;
- he accepts bounded cost on himself;
- he likes useful data more than vague comfort;
- when ancient danger appears, humor drops immediately.

Do NOT summarize:
> “程野很实用 / 很会钻规则 / 很大胆.”

# 6. Zhou Hang

Keep as same-cohort peer.

Initial motive:
> produce a good complete result for himself.

Personality requirement:
> enough pride / competitiveness to care when he realizes Cheng Ye chose him as the most reliable pressure source.

Do not make him:
- arrogant rich kid;
- buyer;
- helper;
- explainer;
- idiot.

He must make an independent decision:
> after learning Cheng Ye's purpose, he voluntarily agrees to / intensifies the legitimate pressure within the test.

That choice is what earns him a continuing relationship role.

# 7. Tester

Must be competent.

Tester function:
- recognizes first instability;
- triggers/backs safety protection;
- explains only the minimum required rule;
- allows the legitimate monitored completion;
- actively watches the escalation;
- stops if real injury threshold is crossed.

The tester should feel like:
> someone doing a professional job while two freshmen are making the job unexpectedly difficult.

Do not turn tester into:
- comic straight man;
- lore lecturer;
- negligent adult.

# 8. 0.11s

KEEP.

Reveal timing:
> after / around the first protective interruption.

Why it works:
- concrete;
- visually/actionably tied to the movement;
- gives Cheng Ye a specific goal;
- creates exact resonance with Ji.

Do not over-measure it repeatedly.
One precise appearance is enough.

# 9. Ancient collision

Ji's scene must already be happening.

He is:
- injured;
- pursued;
- protecting/checking something important;
- trapped at the same movement transition.

He does NOT exist to show Cheng Ye a move.

His choice:
> safe completion is too slow for survival.

He pays:
> serious leg/body cost.

Cheng Ye copies:
> because he is already trapped in the same time problem.

He does not instantly understand the whole principle.

# 10. Money / old elements

DROP from required Chapter 1:
- paid sparring;
- 800 reward;
- 11.5 orders;
- exact 1200;
- title around money.

Do not restore them because earlier drafts used them.

Resource texture can come from:
> free freshman screening is valuable access.

That is enough unless prose naturally needs more.

# 11. Pacing principle

Do not rush Ji merely by character count.

The modern event is allowed to breathe because it now contains:
- curiosity;
- character;
- relationship;
- comedy;
- escalation;
- conflict.

But no waiting-room exposition.

Stay on:
- safety protection frustrating Cheng Ye;
- Cheng Ye asking to complete the pressure;
- Zhou realizing why he was chosen;
- Zhou deciding to really force the problem out;
- tester becoming alert;
- exact 0.11 bottleneck;
- Ji's dangerous solution;
- reciprocal sight.

Skip/compress:
- registration;
- test brochure;
- routine warmup;
- repeated rule confirmation;
- repeated technical explanation;
- database process.

# 12. Chapter memory target

Reader should remember the modern half as:

> 新生摸底时，系统刚发现程野不对就把他保护掉了，他反而嫌保护得太早，认真申请把压力测完。周航这才发现，程野一开始主动挑自己根本不是为了赢，而是觉得自己够强，能把他的毛病逼出来。周航一听反而较上劲了，非要真给他逼出来。

Reader should remember the dual-world half as:

> 程野真卡进那0.11秒时，看见四千年前另一个正在逃命的人也卡在同一式上。那人拿伤腿换了半拍，程野照着试了一次。随后他发现，那个人也看见了他。

If those two memories land:
> the engine works.

# 13. Hard bans

- do not explain all motives before action;
- do not turn “afraid of problems” into deliberate hiding/cheating;
- do not end the modern engine when misunderstanding is clarified;
- do not make Zhou stay passive after reveal;
- do not make rule-reading the chapter's main spectacle;
- do not make the safety system antagonistic;
- do not preserve old rewards by nostalgia;
- do not turn tester into joke machine;
- do not over-explain technique;
- do not let Ji appear as a historical celebrity card;
- do not resolve the anomaly before reciprocal sight.
