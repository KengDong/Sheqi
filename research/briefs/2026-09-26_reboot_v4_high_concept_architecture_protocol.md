# PURPOSE

AUTHOR has explicitly marked all 12 high-concept raw ideas as:
> **推进 / 每一部都想看**

The next stage is NOT to reinvent them.

The next stage is:
> **prove each strong premise can support a compelling long novel without weakening the click magnet.**

# HIGHEST RULE

> **Structure serves the hook. The hook does not serve structure.**

Frozen for each idea:
1. working title;
2. one-line hook;
3. central premise;
4. long-range magnet.

These may only be clarified for logic.
They may NOT be weakened, generalized, sanitized, or replaced by abstract language.

Forbidden examples:
- “灭世级目标” → “逐渐变强”
- “九十九次死亡” → “有限重开”
- “整座东海成为本命法宝” → “水域资源成长”
- “七个S级都是我” → “多身份玩法”
- “怪谈在拼回我” → “主角逐渐异常化”

# PER CONCEPT OUTPUT

## 1. Promise Lock
- frozen title
- frozen hook
- what exact fantasy must remain intact

## 2. Ch1–3
Concrete:
- opening pressure;
- first irreversible choice;
- first visible payoff;
- first larger question.

The title promise must already be working by Ch3.

## 3. Why protagonist cannot simply walk away
Give one strong personal reason and one external pressure.

## 4. Long Magnet
Define one concrete thing the reader can keep waiting for:
- identity truth;
- countdown;
- final place;
- final form;
- fixed number;
- named enemy;
- specific transformation;
- world-scale mystery.

No abstract “become stronger / more famous”.

## 5. Escalation Ladder
Give 4–6 named stages from opening to 100+ chapters.
Each stage must visibly intensify the original title promise.

## 6. Ch20 State
3–5 concrete things that exist and matter by then.
Do not use this as the creative center.

## 7. Ch100 Proof
Explain why chapter 100 is a bigger version of the same book, not a different genre.

## 8. Character Engine
- protagonist's non-generic desire;
- one fear / temptation;
- 2 core relationships with independent desires;
- how those relationships directly collide with the central premise.

## 9. Opposition
Define recurring pressure sources.
Avoid disposable idiot antagonists.

## 10. Treadmill Risk
Name the most likely repetitive failure mode.
Fix it without weakening the premise.

## 11. Benchmark Backcheck
Compare only to the named real references:
- does the title still hit?
- does the first payoff arrive as fast?
- is the long magnet at least as concrete?
- did structure accidentally make the premise smaller?

No scores.
No rankings.
No winner.

## 12. Biggest Unverified Assumption
One sentence.

# RESULTS

Allowed:
- STRUCTURALLY VIABLE
- REVISE WITHOUT WEAKENING THE HOOK
- STRUCTURAL BREAK

A concept may only receive STRUCTURAL BREAK if the title promise itself cannot sustain the novel without contradiction.

Do NOT downgrade simply because execution is difficult.

# FORBIDDEN
- new concept generation;
- changing mother genre;
- weakening title for realism;
- prose;
- chapter-by-chapter outline;
- cross-lane ranking;
- final winner selection;
- web research;
- old Sheqi candidates.

# AFTER

A separate editor will compare the architecture back to the frozen raw ideas and verify:
> **Did we preserve the thing AUTHOR actually wanted to read?**
