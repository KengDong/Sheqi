## 0. Highest change

The new opening allows the early story to separate four jobs that V17 tried to solve in one sparring scene:

1. **campus pleasure** — modern cultivation university life is enjoyable by itself;
2. **title payoff** — the class binds Dao Seeds; Cheng binds a living other-world person;
3. **mechanic proof** — later prove what this abnormal seed can do;
4. **relationship engine** — Cheng / Ji become two independent actors rather than tutorial giver + recipient.

This means:
> Ch1 no longer needs to prove the entire Living Dao Seed mechanic.

The rule from the early-loop benchmark applies:

> Delay ontology, NOT payoff.

Reader can get a huge payoff in Ch1:
> “The thing on Cheng’s Dao Seed interface is an actual living person, and that person can perceive him.”

The explanation can remain incomplete.

Function:

Desired end-state:
> Ji Changgeng perceives Cheng back.

Ch1 ends on contact, not explanation.

# 4. Modern mainline after the new opening

Modern cultivation benchmark says the world must run through:

> ability
> → recognized metric
> → credential / rank
> → resource / access
> → social revaluation
> → larger opportunity / obligation.

Therefore the campus cannot be decorative.

Early university lines should repeatedly use:
- courses;
- practical training;
- military training;
- Dao Seed track choice;
- credits / certifications;
- labs / mentors;
- campus jobs or projects;
- competitions;
- internships / public institutions later.

The Dao Seed primary choice should alter which of these doors are easy or hard to enter.

Cheng’s abnormal seed should produce BOTH:
- opportunity;
- administrative / training mismatch.

Do not make “special seed” equal automatic VIP treatment.

# 6. Ji Changgeng after Ch1

Relationship benchmark imposes a hard test:

> Remove Cheng from Ji’s next five chapters. Does Ji still have a story?

Answer must become YES.

Ji requires:
- a local objective unrelated to helping Cheng;
- people who only know / trust / hate / need Ji;
- a resource / responsibility Cheng cannot command;
- consequences Ji owns;
- decisions Cheng may disagree with.

The wooden case / pursuit can remain as an initial engine only if it opens:
> Ji’s own network, obligation and next decision.

Do not make it a mystery box whose only purpose is to reward Cheng.

# 7. Living Dao Seed multiplicity

Normal Dao Seed multiplicity:
- multi-candidate initial choice;
- later branch development is normal in society.

Cheng’s anomaly should rhyme with the normal system instead of replacing it with a private game UI.

Provisional model:

## Primary anchor
- Ji Changgeng;
- deepest channel;
- non-replaceable under ordinary conditions;
- relationship accumulates trust / obligations / permissions.

## Later branch anchor slots
- unlock rarely through normal Dao Seed growth stages;
- present a SMALL candidate set;
- candidates are living people, not skills;
- choosing one means giving up another route for a meaningful period;
- each chosen person owns an independent local life.

Hard anti-collection rule:
> New person ≠ new power card.

Each new anchor must add at least one:
- new social network;
- new institution;
- new knowledge-production method;
- new responsibility;
- new conflict with an existing anchor’s priorities.

Do not exceed cast bandwidth merely because the mechanic permits it.

# 8. Candidate-selection engine for later anchors

A useful long-run rule to test:

> candidates emerge through “same problem / same question” resonance rather than raw strength ranking.

Why:
- prevents SSR-hunting;
- allows doctor / artisan / clerk / cultivator / guard / researcher / merchant etc.;
- keeps the Reader asking “why THIS person?”;
- makes each branch choice a story decision.

Do not freeze this as canon yet.

The candidate decision must have:
- present benefit;
- missing information;
- opportunity cost;
- relationship consequence.

# 12. Long-run two-world growth

Dual-world benchmark warning:

> price differences decay; identity / institution / knowledge / relationship gaps compound.

Therefore long-run cross-world escalation should move roughly:

### Early
- technique / immediate survival / small practical knowledge.

### Middle
- validation methods;
- education / training systems;
- social identities;
- institution access;
- scarce conditions one side cannot reproduce.

### Later
- who has authority to define truth / orthodoxy;
- organizations;
- political / economic / sect responsibilities;
- historical consequences;
- two worlds making different demands on the same relationship.

Avoid:
> ancient world becomes material warehouse;
> modern world becomes answer key.

Both worlds must remain capable of surprising the other.
