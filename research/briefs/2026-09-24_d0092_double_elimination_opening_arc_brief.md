# What the Arc Must Contain

Not as a checklist to force into chapters, but by the end the Reader should have experienced:

1. opening promise;
2. protagonist's concrete desire;
3. first meaningful fruit;
4. fruit creates consequence/cost;
5. at least one relationship changes;
6. world/social interface expands beyond the opening room/scene;
7. pressure learns or intensifies;
8. protagonist makes a choice;
9. first mini-climax/payoff;
10. after payoff, a specific larger desire remains.

# Comparison Rule

No candidate is eliminated for:
- being third;
- being slower;
- losing a forced top-two question;
- having a different platform rhythm.

Candidate may stop early only for catastrophic failure:
- prose becomes unreadable or explanation manual;
- core promise cannot be delivered without Concept mutation;
- natural arc collapses before mini-climax;
- same prior structural failure clearly reappears and dominates actual reading.

Otherwise:
> finish the mini-climax.
