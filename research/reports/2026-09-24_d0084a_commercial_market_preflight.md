# 4. Immediate correction to current R-candidates

未经最终研究，不做最终判决。

但必须重新核验：

## R1 Future Holdings
- strong novelty;
- commercial chassis currently ambiguous;
- must prove a mass familiar desire loop beyond relationship/ethics.

## R2 Second Autobiography
- very strong character engine;
- high risk of high-concept social SF / relationship ethics niche;
- must prove repeatable commercial reward.

## R3 Borrowed Tribulations
- previously HOLD for runway;
- commercially deserves re-open because cultivation is a massive familiar chassis;
- exact question: can we build longform growth without returning to case-of-week risk?

## R7 Dead People Still Work
- strong one-line click and current AI/job anxiety;
- must prove it is not only a Black-Mirror-like concept;
- needs visible protagonist growth / reward loop.

## R8 Missing Day List
- closest current candidate to proven suspense/urban-anomaly/规则系 market;
- must test whether external suspense / power / survival / recontextualization firepower is sufficient without destroying its relationship engine.

# 5. D-0084A mission

Do not ask:
> which current book is smartest?

Ask:
> **which large reader markets repeatedly produce hits, what repeated fantasy/reward is being bought, and how much originality can be layered on without losing legibility?**
