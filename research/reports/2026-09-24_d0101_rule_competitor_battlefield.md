# D-0101｜《把一条副本规则带回现实》全面竞品战场
## 目标：不是“有一两项比热门强”，而是建立足以进入头部竞争的完整发动机组合

# 0. Executive Conclusion

当前《规则》的真正优势已经很清楚：

> **“副本里的规则不是一次性解谜条件，而是永久进入现实、改变所有人的公共法则。”**

这一点相较大量：
- 遵守规则；
- 找规则漏洞；
- 修改规则；
- 获得规则能力；
- 一关一关通副本；

具有明显的高层差异。

但当前版本仍然“窄”的原因也很明确：

> **我们已经把最独特的发动机做得很强，却没有把同Reader池头部小说的其他发动机全部补齐。**

尤其缺口集中在：
1. 副本本身的独立故事质量；
2. 主角人格记忆度；
3. 前50章个人成长的可见度；
4. 强固定群像；
5. 资源/社会地位果实；
6. 标志性长期对手；
7. 终极主线的分段兑现；
8. 谜团回答速度；
9. 300章以上故事模式接力；
10. 结局方向与中期每次选择的因果统一。

因此D-0101不再把“Rule-Reality”当成：
> 一个高Concept + 一堆应用场景。

而必须把它做成：
> **无限求生 + 都市现实改写 + 群像 + 可见成长 + 长线谜团 + 世界危机 + 黑色幽默 + 不可逆选择**
的复合商业母盘。

# 2. S-Tier Direct Competition Pool

# S1｜《十日终焉》
Current evidence:
- 320.1万字;
- 1496章;
- 2024番茄年度巅峰榜TOP1 in official synopsis;
- official synopsis explicitly reveals:
  ordinary day -> death games -> supernatural awakening -> “造神之地” -> 湮灭.

Most important benchmark:
> **它及时停止让“下一场游戏”成为唯一发动机。**

Early directory already shows engine expansion:
- first game/lying;
- new targets;
- new teammates;
- “回响”;
- contracts;
- factions;
- rebellion;
- organizations;
- disasters;
- identity/history.

What it is strongest at:
1. macro recontextualization;
2. ensemble;
3. repeated reader-model upgrades;
4. characters becoming stronger retention than games;
5. philosophical/value conflict arriving AFTER survival attachment;
6. game formats vary without requiring a new power reward each time.

Where Rule-Reality can beat it:
> **our victories permanently rewrite ordinary reality.**
Its game consequences mainly alter the closed “终焉” ecosystem; ours can make first-chapter family life, police, housing, commerce, language and cities permanently mutate.

Requirement:
> do not waste this edge by making reality interludes feel like aftermath.

# S2｜《诡舍》
Current evidence:
- ~213万字;
- ~1040章;
- forced periodic entry through blood doors;
- macro secret: 诡舍并非表面上的诅咒.

Most important benchmark:
> **每个血门本身有“恐怖短篇小说”的完成度。**

Opening:
- driverless bus;
- black house;
- fog;
- first blood door;
- explicit countdown.

Directory proves strong handoff grammar:
> Ch13 “结束”
> -> Ch14 “拼图碎片”
> -> Ch15 “第二封信”
> -> Ch16 “第二扇血门”.

This is nearly textbook:
> payoff -> permanent clue -> next instruction -> next concrete door.

What it is strongest at:
1. atmosphere;
2. unit-story horror completeness;
3. concrete hub/home base;
4. forced cadence;
5. physical collectible clue;
6. predictable but addictive “next door” rhythm.

Where Rule-Reality can beat it:
> blood-door stories mostly return to a hub; our return changes the hub/world itself.

Requirement:
> our second/third rule fields must be enjoyable even if the “bring rule back” mechanic were removed.
If dungeon is only a delivery vehicle for new public rules:
> we lose to 《诡舍》 on the actual infinite-flow food.

# S3｜《全球死考》
Current evidence:
- ~81万字;
- official page currently surfaces ~112.9万人在读;
- explicit “death exam” premise;
- explicit score reward ladder:
  score -> money -> genetic/power rewards -> much larger endpoint.

Strongest benchmark:
> **Reader never needs to ask “我追十场考试到底拿什么？”**

It has:
- score;
- money;
- power;
- clear endpoint hint;
- global participation.

What it is strongest at:
1. unknown-reader premise clarity;
2. tangible growth;
3. reward visibility;
4. exam-by-exam next-rung pull;
5. action/survival scale.

Where Rule-Reality can beat it:
> our reward is not a private number; it permanently alters everybody's life and generates much deeper old-state compounding.

Current Rule gap:
> Cheng Ye's personal reward ladder remains less viscerally visible.

Requirement:
> solve personal agency + asset/status progress without turning the book into a score system.

# S4｜《灵境行者》
Official Qidian evidence:
- ten-wan average subscription honor;
- repeatedly top monthly-ticket rankings;
- dungeon/role/organization chassis.

Strongest benchmark:
> **副本、职业成长、道具资源、官方/民间组织、现实身份同时存在。**

What it is strongest at:
1. paid-chapter growth fruits;
2. class/profession identity;
3. resource and item economy;
4. organizations;
5. real-world and dungeon coupling;
6. protagonist social/humorous surface.

Where Rule-Reality can beat it:
> returned rules can change public reality more deeply than a conventional “dungeon rewards strengthen player” chassis.

Current Rule gap:
- item/resource economy weak;
- protagonist's own power/status ladder too soft;
- organization layer delayed.

Requirement:
> add tangible assets/status without copying RPG professions.

# S5｜《神秘复苏》
Not the closest infinite-flow structure, but structurally essential.

Official Qidian honor:
- 10k+ average-subscription badge;
- major long-term readership/history.

Strongest benchmark:
> **using power advances the problem.**

Ghost rules:
- must be understood;
- cannot simply be stat-checked;
- using supernatural power carries escalating cost;
- urban reality and institutions become increasingly involved.

What it is strongest at:
1. threat credibility;
2. power-as-cost;
3. reality never becomes safe filler;
4. city-scale consequence;
5. protagonist identity/behavior;
6. “understand the law to survive” tension.

Where Rule-Reality can beat it:
> our rules can also create positive social utility, black comedy and civilization design—not only horror pressure.

Requirement:
> every beneficial returned rule must have a cost that creates story.
No free “good public rule”.

# 4. Current/Market-Saturation Signals

Current “规则怪谈” shelf is highly saturated with titles such as:
- “我编造规则…”
- “我能合理利用规则”
- “你管这叫遵守规则？”
- “我的手册是空白的”
- “我的推理能杀人”
- “我能完美利用隐藏规则”

These commonly sell:
> protagonist smarter than rules / exploits rules / modifies rules.

This is evidence that:
> **“我会玩规则” is no longer sufficient differentiation.**

Rule-Reality's title/premise avoids the most saturated promise:
> it sells “规则永久进入现实”.

Do NOT dilute this by making Cheng Ye primarily a rule-hacking genius.

# 7. Key Diagnosis

Current book is NOT too small because the world is small.

It feels incomplete because:
> **our unique engines are already head-tier, but common commercial support engines are underpowered.**

Already strong:
1. core premise;
2. reality permanence;
3. old-rule compounding;
4. family consequence;
5. irreversible choice;
6. dual-engine potential;
7. terminal future-fragment concept.

Underpowered:
1. dungeon quality;
2. MC identity;
3. personal growth;
4. ensemble;
5. resource/status;
6. rival;
7. mystery payment rhythm;
8. standalone arc diversity;
9. signature imagery.

# 8. Target-To-Exceed Strategy

Do NOT try to beat each book at its own specialty.

That is impossible/unnecessary.

Aim for a combination no benchmark currently dominates simultaneously:

> **《诡舍》的副本短篇完成度**
> + **《十日终焉》的群像/谜团换挡**
> + **《全球死考》的可见奔头**
> + **《灵境行者》的成长/现实组织果实**
> + **《神秘复苏》的规则威胁与代价**
> + **《天才俱乐部》的谜团返钱**
> + **《异兽迷城》的关系卷入真相**
> + **Rule-Reality独有的“胜利永久重写现实”**

The differentiation must remain:
> **every victory makes home harder to return to.**

That is the book's premium emotional engine.

# 9. Required V2 Additions

## Engine 1｜Dungeon Quality Engine
Every major rule field must independently work as a memorable story.

Required:
- one-line situation;
- iconic visual;
- one human conflict;
- one rule-based impossible pressure;
- one non-rule emotional choice;
- one result that changes recurring cast;
- candidate return rule that arises from field logic;
- one terminal-spine physical clue at most.

If removing the “return rule” reward makes the dungeon boring:
> FAIL.

## Engine 2｜Four-Layer Reward Stack
Every major arc should rotate among:
1. survival/intellectual win;
2. personal agency/access growth;
3. material/status/social fruit;
4. reality/world-state change.

Never require all four every arc.
Never pay three consecutive arcs only with “new rule”.

## Engine 3｜Recurring Human Core
By Ch50:
- family;
- Fang Cen;
- 2 recurring returners;
must each have own desire and ability to cause plot.

By Ch100:
> a stable 4–6 person human web.

## Engine 4｜Iconic Rival
Need one recurring returner whose rule-choice philosophy comes from a personal wound.

They should sometimes be right.

Their choices must create better short-term outcomes than Cheng Ye's, so the conflict is real.

## Engine 5｜Mystery Repayment Ladder
Every 30–60 chapters:
> answer a concrete question.

Question ladder:
- are there other returners?
- are fields related to reality?
- are they future fragments?
- are futures fixed?
- does choosing a rule increase branch weight?
- why does reality require return?
- what is “归一”?
- can the process be interrupted?

No 300-ch “just more clues.”

## Engine 6｜Reality Economy
Not a full finance system.

Natural assets:
- safe apartments;
- prep base;
- rule-compatible routes;
- verified field intelligence;
- emergency gear;
- paid expertise;
- property/insurance/logistics effects.

Give Cheng Ye visible life improvement and cost.

## Engine 7｜Macro-Personal Coupling
Terminal truth must threaten:
- mother;
- sister;
- Fang Cen;
- teammates

through incompatible future histories.

No abstract civilization-only ending.

## Engine 8｜Signature Imagery
The novel needs recurring images:
- black wooden rule tag turning to ash;
- invisible line after a true name;
- countdown on wrist;
- ordinary city street briefly becoming its failed-future version;
- two incompatible versions of the same building overlapping.

Images make macro lore memorable.

# 10. V2 Commercial Promise

The strongest candidate formulation is:

> **程野每次从致命规则场活着回来，都必须带一条规则回现实。**
>
> **起初他只想让家里人学会在这些规则下活下去。**
>
> **后来他才发现，那些副本并不是另一个世界——而是现实在不同规则下已经失败过的未来。**
>
> **每带回一条规则，他们都在把某个失败未来拉得更近。**

This gives:
- premise;
- person;
- life;
- mystery;
- terminal crisis;
without explaining the system.

# 11. Final Benchmark Standard

Before serious P20:
the book must prove at least:

1. One field rivals direct competitors as a standalone mini-novel.
2. Cheng Ye can be remembered without mentioning rules.
3. One recurring teammate is wanted for personality, not usefulness.
4. One permanent material/status fruit is visible.
5. One old rule unexpectedly compounds.
6. One mystery question is concretely answered.
7. One new answer enlarges the terminal crisis.
8. Reality chapters are wanted, not tolerated between fields.
9. No new returned rule for 30–40 chapters remains entertaining.
10. Reader can describe the future book as larger than “more dungeons.”
