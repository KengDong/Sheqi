# D-0101｜《把一条副本规则带回现实》同池热门梯队 + 全栈差距矩阵

# 0. Executive Conclusion

The candidate has a genuinely differentiating core:

> **副本规则不是一次性解谜条件，而是通关后永久进入现实、影响所有人。**

This is stronger than many ordinary rule/infinite premises on:
- old-state compounding;
- reality consequence;
- reward-as-cost;
- public-world transformation.

But the current package is NARROW.

It is not yet head-tier because its strengths cluster in:
> Concept / reality consequence / future mystery.

It is weaker or unproven in:
> protagonist memorability / recurring ensemble / iconic hub / tangible personal growth / resource economy / field variety / long-term antagonist / emotional breadth / serial reward cadence.

The correct goal is NOT:
> be #1 at every flavor.

The correct head-tier goal is:

> **No fatal weakness across the full commercial stack + 4–5 axes where this book is unmistakably better/different.**

Target lead axes:
1. Reality Permanence
2. Rule-Choice Externality
3. Dungeon ↔ Reality Mirror
4. Old-Rule Compounding
5. Terminal future-selection spine

# 3. Diagnosis｜Why Current Candidate Feels Narrow

It has a very strong vertical column:
> Rule -> Reality Consequence -> Social Adaptation -> Bigger Rule Mystery.

But top books feel “wide” because they have MULTIPLE horizontal pleasures running at once:

- person;
- team;
- gear/resource;
- power;
- hub;
- rival;
- social status;
- mystery;
- emotional relationship;
- changing world.

Current Rule-Reality lacks enough of those horizontal ledgers.

So the fix is NOT “more rules”.

The fix is:
> **make ONE returned rule feed more durable ledgers.**

# 5. “全面” Does NOT Mean Every Flavor

Do NOT try to simultaneously become:
- the scariest;
- funniest;
- most combat-heavy;
- deepest;
- most romantic;
- most puzzle-dense.

That produces a swollen book.

Full-stack target:

## Minimum head-tier floor
Must not be weak in:
- shelf;
- MC;
- first payoff;
- recurring cast;
- growth;
- hub/assets;
- field quality;
- macro mystery;
- terminal crisis;
- long-run mode change.

## Chosen lead axes
Must aim to BEAT typical peers in:
1. returned rules permanently alter ordinary reality;
2. victory reward is also a public externality;
3. dungeon and reality are two sides of the same place/future;
4. first rule remains relevant hundreds of chapters later;
5. the final crisis is generated by all previous reward choices.

# 6. Architecture Reforge Requirements

The V2 architecture must add:

1. Iconic Reality Hub generated by R1.
2. Tangible prep/loadout economy.
3. Personal authority ladder with clear rungs.
4. 3–5 recurring returners with incompatible human motives.
5. One long-term human antagonist/rival.
6. Field-mode rotation charter.
7. Dungeon↔Reality mirror rule.
8. Failed-future terminal spine with partial-answer cadence.
9. Reality/base assets that physically accumulate.
10. Chapter/arc reward cadence.
11. First-20-ch breakout package.
12. Qidian/Fanqie dual-surface plan.

- chapter catalogue/free chapter = medium for early-function inference;
- synopsis = packaging evidence, not full-prose evidence;
