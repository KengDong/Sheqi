# D-0101｜《把一条副本规则带回现实》Full Competitive Battlefield
## 不只对标“规则怪谈”，而是对标四个Reader欲望池的头部交集

# 0. Conclusion First

Current candidate is NOT broadly head-tier yet.

It already has potential HEAD-TIER / LEADING functions in:
- one-line premise;
- irreversible rule choice;
- rule permanently contaminating ordinary reality;
- old-rule compounding;
- family/public-life collision;
- future-fragment terminal-spine potential.

But it is currently behind proven head-tier works in several full-book capabilities:
- protagonist signature;
- recurring cast depth;
- dungeon/field supply proven quality;
- tangible personal growth;
- iconic antagonist pressure;
- long-range mystery payment;
- multiple independent reward currencies;
- mode variety proven in prose.

Therefore:
> **do not optimize it as a niche rules book.**
> Optimize it as a hybrid that must compete simultaneously with top infinite-survival, reality-horror, urban-supernatural, and terminal-mystery books.

# 1. Competitive Reader Pools

## Pool A｜Infinite Survival / Puzzle / Rules
Reader appetite:
- enter impossible place;
- understand danger;
- solve/cheat rule;
- recurring survivors;
- escalating meta mystery.

Direct benchmarks:
- 《十日终焉》
- 《诡舍》
- 《天官赐邪》
- 《灵境行者》
- 《惊悚乐园》 (classic function benchmark)

## Pool B｜Rules Invade Reality
Reader appetite:
- supernatural laws are real;
- ordinary society adapts;
- power always carries cost;
- city/world gradually changes.

Benchmarks:
- 《神秘复苏》
- 《怪谈游戏设计师》
- 《我不是戏神》 (macro reality transformation)
- 《异兽迷城》 (hidden-world reality + terminal truth)

## Pool C｜Visible Commercial Growth
Reader appetite:
- protagonist becomes materially more capable;
- next rung is obvious;
- reward arrives quickly.

Benchmarks:
- 《灵境行者》
- 《时停起手，邪神也得给我跪下！》
- 《全球死考》

## Pool D｜Character / Ensemble / Terminal Mystery
Reader appetite:
- remember the people;
- care when they separate/die/betray;
- one giant secret keeps recontextualizing earlier arcs.

Benchmarks:
- 《十日终焉》
- 《我不是戏神》
- 《异兽迷城》
- 《诡舍》

# 2. S-Tier Direct / Near-Direct Benchmarks

## S1｜《十日终焉》

Current evidence:
- Fanqie official page: complete, 320.1万字, 1496 chapters;
- official synopsis exposes several recontextualization layers:
  ordinary day -> death games -> supernatural awakening -> god-making place -> annihilation.
- 2024 Fanqie annual peak TOP1 in official synopsis metadata.

What it owns:
1. first-room immediate danger;
2. early “this is much bigger” signal;
3. large recurring ensemble;
4. games as character revelation, not only puzzles;
5. macro mystery that repeatedly changes model of the world;
6. later personal supernatural growth;
7. distinct voices / memorable recurring people.

Transferable function:
> every game should pay character + relationship + macro truth, not merely survival.

Our current advantage:
> returned rules permanently alter reality; stronger external-world residue.

Our current deficit:
> no proof yet that Rule Fields themselves can match its game quality / cast chemistry / mystery cadence.

## S2｜《诡舍》

Current official evidence:
- Fanqie official: complete, ~213万字, 1041 chapters;
- current search cache shows ~68万+ reading;
- first 13 chapters complete the first horror event;
- Ch14 immediately leaves “拼图碎片”; Ch15 “第二封信”; Ch16 enters second blood door.

What it owns:
1. extremely fast horror contract;
2. visible base: 诡舍;
3. forced cadence: blood doors;
4. one door = one strong story ecology;
5. terminal secret: the inn itself is not what it appears;
6. recurring survivors / safe base between horror arcs.

Transferable:
> dungeon cadence works because there is a persistent base + persistent mystery + persistent people.

Our advantage:
> our reality world itself changes permanently; intermission can be stronger than “rest at base.”

Our deficit:
> we have not proven a Rule Field can sustain a 30–50 chapter arc with its own memorable ecology.

## S3｜《灵境行者》

Official evidence:
- Qidian honor: ten-wan average subscription in 2026;
- official-facing data: ~418万字 / 1050 chapters;
- tags include original instances, system/progression, infinite flow, urban reality.
- protagonist is socially recognizable / talkative rather than neutral.

What it owns:
1. dungeon + reality organization are both active;
2. very clear personal progression;
3. profession/skill rewards;
4. real-world factions/status;
5. relationship and romantic/social chemistry;
6. instance clears change outside status/resources.

This is probably our MOST IMPORTANT structural benchmark.

Our advantage:
> our returned rule alters public physics, so reality consequences can be more novel.

Our deficit:
> Cheng Ye personal progression is much softer; world may become more interesting than the protagonist.

## S4｜《天官赐邪》

Current platform-facing evidence:
- 2026-09 current source: ~269万字 / ~1290 chapters;
- tags: original instances / survival / myth / infinite;
- core loop: 福地 -> worship/select divine gift -> survive -> carry growth.

What it owns:
1. very compact instance contract;
2. strong Chinese folk/myth surface;
3. tangible reward every cycle;
4. roguelike/random choice freshness;
5. horror and power food simultaneously.

Our advantage:
> rule choice has larger permanent reality consequence.

Our deficit:
> we intentionally weakened “loot/power” and may underfeed readers seeking personal gain.

## S5｜《怪谈游戏设计师》

Official Qidian honor:
- first subscription >10k;
- average subscription >10k;
- 500k+ collections historically;
- platform-facing text ~208万字 / 960 chapters.

What it owns:
1. title/synopsis one-line identity;
2. protagonist profession is itself content generator;
3. “my creations become reality” couples case supply to protagonist identity;
4. reality and horror world increasingly merge;
5. strong first-chapter domestic abnormality.

Our advantage:
> our rule import creates explicit irreversible choices.

Our deficit:
> Cheng Ye currently has no equivalent “profession/personality = content engine.”

# 3. A-Tier Adjacent Benchmarks

## A1｜《神秘复苏》

Official Qidian:
- >10k average subscription badge;
- huge collection/recommendation history.

Benchmark function:
> rules are terrifying because they are consistent, unknowable and costly.
> personal power is acquired from the same thing that may kill you.

What we must learn:
- rule-based horror must keep teeth;
- do not let returned rules become cute city gimmicks;
- “using the rule” must always risk something.

Our advantage:
> public-rule reality transformation + dungeon source.

Our deficit:
> current R1 is dangerous but later architecture risks normalizing rules too quickly.

## A2｜《我不是戏神》

Fanqie official:
- complete;
- ~400万字;
- ~168万 current readers in current cache.

Benchmark function:
> giant world crisis + highly theatrical protagonist/world identity + long group/organization arcs.

What we must learn:
- macro threat needs iconic imagery and personal embodiment;
- not enough to say “worlds overlap.”

Our deficit:
> “failed futures” is conceptually strong but currently lacks one iconic recurring visual/object.

## A3｜《异兽迷城》

Fanqie official:
- complete ~296万字 / 1322 chapters;
- current cache ~47–59万 readers.

Benchmark function:

> protagonist's daily life and world truth are the same plot.

Transfer:
> macro secret should reach into Cheng Ye's mother/sister, not remain city cosmology.

## A4｜《时停起手，邪神也得给我跪下！》

Fanqie official current:

- ~93万 current readers.

Benchmark function:

## A5｜《全球死考》

Current Fanqie evidence:

Benchmark function:

## A6｜《惊悚乐园》｜Classic Functional Benchmark

Qidian historical:
- >10k average subscription badge;
- ~489万字 in platform lists.

Function:

- humor + serious instances can coexist;
- instance variety can be huge when protagonist itself remains stable and memorable.

Transfer:

# 6. Why It Currently Feels Narrow

The candidate has an unusually strong UNIQUE MECHANIC, but the other food groups are underdeveloped.

Current mental model:
> rule -> consequence -> next rule.

Head-tier mental model needs:
> **person + rule + team + asset + growth + rival + world + mystery + terminal pressure** all moving.

The candidate cannot be “the book with the best rule gimmick.”

It must become:
> **the book where every rule changes people, property, relationships, power, the city, and the future—while the same recurring cast keeps paying for those changes.**

# 7. The Eight Gaps V2 Must Close

## Gap 1｜Cheng Ye is not iconic enough
Need:
- repeatable behavior;
- black-comedy interaction style;
- one flaw;
- one selfish/private desire beyond family safety.

## Gap 2｜Rule Fields themselves are not proven head-tier
Need a Field Design OS:
- human desire;
- spatial/ecological identity;
- simple surface rule;
- hidden second interpretation;
- permanent recurring character;
- return choice;
- future-fragment clue.

## Gap 3｜Growth is too abstract
Access Authority alone needs stronger embodied/tangible manifestations.

## Gap 4｜Team is too thin
Need 3–5 recurring humans with incompatible rule preferences and useful competencies.

## Gap 5｜Rules can become societal demonstrations
Reality arcs must begin from named humans, not “schools/hospitals/platforms adapted.”

## Gap 6｜Terminal spine lacks icon
“failed future” must have recurring physical evidence.

## Gap 7｜No iconic rival yet
Need a returner whose choices are attractive enough that readers may sometimes agree with them.

## Gap 8｜Rule supply remains high-risk
Need long arcs where:
- no new rule;
- old rule gains new meaning;
- future mystery pays;
- relationships/asset growth carry retention.

# 8. Competitive Target

Do NOT aim for:
> “our rule mechanic is fresher than 《诡舍》.”

Aim for:
- 《十日终焉》 level macro pull / cast desire;
- 《诡舍》 level forced-entry clarity and field-story ecology;
- 《灵境行者》 level protagonist growth + reality organization;
- 《神秘复苏》 level rule danger/cost;
- 《惊悚乐园》/《时停》 level protagonist recognizability;
- 《异兽迷城》 level personal-family tie to final truth;
- while preserving our distinctive advantage:
  > **the winner's rule permanently rewrites ordinary reality.**

That combination is the actual breakout target.
