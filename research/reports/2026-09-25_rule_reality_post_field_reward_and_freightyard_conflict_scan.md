# Purpose

Author identified two issues:
1. Cheng Ye reaches Xiaoman at the freight yard and exits too easily, wasting the first real-world confrontation.
2. The protagonist currently gains little personally from surviving a Rule Field, which may weaken early progression/satisfaction.

This note does NOT change canon.

# Competitive Pattern Takeaway

Strong popular progression fiction typically gives one or more of:
- immediate competence proof;
- a tangible new capability;
- a cost / liability attached to power;
- cross-arc utility;
- a visible future progression horizon.

For this project:
> do NOT copy numeric stats, skill trees, or one new combat skill per Field.

That would dilute:
> Field -> choose Returned Rule -> reality changes.

# Freight-Yard Diagnosis

Current weak shape:
> find sister -> say leave -> collector calls her name -> chase.

Problems:
- collectors have little resistance before supernatural escalation;
- Cheng Ye does not visibly show what 18 days in a deadly Field changed in him;
- little evidence / leverage is gained;
- Cheng Ye's personality appears after the premise instead of inside the rescue.

# Preferred Redesign Function

The freight-yard scene should prove:
> **Field survival changed Cheng Ye's way of acting before it gave him conventional power.**

Beat target:
1. Cheng Ye enters and first reads exits / people / who controls Xiaoman's phone/bag/contract.
2. He asks for amount / contract / responsible party instead of instantly starting a fight.
3. He quietly preserves one useful piece of evidence: face, company, voice, transaction or document.
4. collector physically blocks / grabs / threatens;
5. Cheng Ye makes ONE fast, grounded, non-martial-arts dominance move to free Xiaoman / create a chokepoint / regain an item;
6. he does NOT beat three men like an action hero;
7. collector then discovers / deploys R1 true-name tracking;
8. mundane confrontation escalates into the book's unique supernatural conflict.

Payoff:
- Cheng Ye looks changed by survival;
- the collectors remain dangerous;
- R1 remains the decisive escalation;
- later police investigation has a concrete evidence bridge.

# Post-Field Gain Options

## Option A｜Conventional Physical Buff
Examples:
- strength;
- speed;
- healing;
- pain resistance as supernatural stat.

Verdict:
> REJECT as primary route.

Reason:
- generic;
- pushes story toward high-combat escalation;
- competes with Rule-return engine;
- encourages every Field to pay another stat.

A small believable survival-conditioning effect may exist in behavior, not UI/stat form.

## Option B｜One New Power From Every Field
Verdict:
> REJECT.

Reason:
> quickly becomes a skill list and makes Returned Rules feel secondary.

## Option C｜Access Authority
Existing architecture already supports this.

Examples over long progression:
- next-entry countdown / destination;
- earlier voluntary entry;
- distinguish nearby Field families;
- choose between openings;
- synchronize one consenting teammate;
- later reject one Returned Rule at major cost.

Verdict:
> KEEP and make more reader-visible.

This is progression over the Field system itself rather than combat power.

## Option D｜Single Deepening Returner Faculty
Working concept:
> a Returner gradually retains / detects contradictions when Field-reality overlap occurs.

Possible early manifestation:
- notices one impossible mismatch others fail to retain;
- later holds both versions of a place/event in memory;
- much later anchors one agreed fact/person briefly through overlap.

Verdict:
> KEEP as slow-burn secondary progression.

Hard:
- one faculty only;
- no skill list;
- no level UI;
- not a Ch1 combat solution.

## Option E｜Carrier Feedback / Rule Resonance
Possible concept:
> the person who returned a rule perceives its activation more clearly than ordinary listeners.

Potential benefit:
- gives Cheng Ye a small unique relationship to the rule he imported;
- visually reinforces responsibility;
- can make early rule use more satisfying.

Risk:
- can become a hidden rule-analysis superpower;
- may trivialize mysteries;
- creates a new mechanic not yet needed.

Verdict:
> TEST ONLY, NOT ADOPTED.

If used, limit to:
> clearer feedback that a personally Returned Rule has fired;
NOT:
> automatic knowledge of hidden conditions / counters / future results.

# Recommended Growth Stack

## Immediate
> **competence gain, not power gain.**

Show that 18 days in Field changed how Cheng Ye handles danger.

## First personal supernatural payoff
> **Access Authority Level 1** already on page:
> next entry gives private countdown + destination.

Make this feel like a consequential privilege, not incidental UI.

## Mid/long-term
> one deepening contradiction-memory / overlap faculty.

## Arc rewards rotate
A Field may pay in:
- Returned Rule;
- relationship;
- status;
- prep asset;
- future clue;
- Access Authority;
- prevention of worse rule;
- one persistent object.

Do not pay every arc mainly in personal strength.

# Core Recommendation

> **Do give Cheng Ye personal progression.**
>
> **Do NOT give him a standard combat buff after every Field.**

Best identity:
> he becomes increasingly able to choose, prepare for, survive and eventually refuse Fields;
not:
> he becomes stronger because each Field gave him another ability.
