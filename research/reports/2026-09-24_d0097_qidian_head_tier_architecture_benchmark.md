# D-0097Q｜Qidian Head-Tier Architecture Benchmark

# Executive Conclusion

Qidian head-tier long-form scale is not created by “more lore” or “more maps”.

The strongest transferable function is:

> **a familiar life/cultivation chassis + a small number of universal conversion systems + permanent state change.**

A head-tier scene often moves 3–6 ledgers at once:
- power;
- money/resource;
- status/rank;
- relationship;
- institution;
- future option.

The book feels large before the map is large because:
> **ordinary life already touches a large system.**

《没钱修什么仙？》 is the clearest example.
《玄鉴仙族》 proves a different route:
> scale can come from generations, family memory, land, inheritance and irreversible succession rather than modern institutions.

# 1. Evidence

## 《没钱修什么仙？》
Evidence:
- FULL-TEXT clean-zone deepread through opening + multiple verified mid arcs from user-provided source;
- official Qidian honor page confirms ten-wan average subscription on 2026-04-01 and monthly-ticket top 5 placements in 2026-06/07/08.

Key transfer:
> school / work / money / cultivation / medicine / rank are not separate subplots; they are one economy.

## 《玄鉴仙族》
Evidence:
- OFFICIAL/CATALOGUE level for this pass;
- official Qidian-facing pages describe a small family receiving cultivation inheritance and opening a long family era;
- current Qidian front-page evidence places it at/near the top field;
- current platform materials identify family/generation/group structure as the core.

Do NOT claim a fresh full-text read here.

# 2. Why 《没钱》 Feels Large Immediately

The opening does not explain the civilization first.

It makes a familiar action economically dense:
> interview -> school -> debt -> body -> cultivation -> family -> future.

The reader learns systems because Zhang Yu needs something now.

This produces:
> **Problem First, Knowledge Second.**

World information has price attached.

Examples of functions:
- rank changes treatment;
- money changes cultivation speed;
- cultivation changes work options;
- work changes money;
- debt changes family;
- school changes future status.

No isolated lore.

# 3. Multi-System Bus

A head-tier scene should usually have:

> immediate goal
> + one status/resource consequence
> + one relationship consequence
> + one new world implication
> + one next desire.

Not every scene needs all five.
But if ten consecutive scenes only move one ledger:
> the book feels narrow.

《没钱》 repeatedly uses:
- class;
- exam;
- job;
- competition;
- rent;
- treatment

as “buses” that naturally carry multiple systems.

# 4. Tangible Conversion Layer

A large long-form needs at least one reader-legible conversion chain.

《没钱》:
> money -> cultivation -> rank -> education/work -> more money/options.

This makes every small number meaningful.

Transferable requirement:
> the candidate must have at least one universal value bridge that links multiple parts of life.

Examples of allowed functions:
- qualification;
- score;
- cultivation realm;
- money/resource;
- territory;
- reputation;
- rule access.

Avoid ten unrelated currencies.

# 5. Book Contract Exposure

A Qidian opening does not need full lore.

By ~10k–20k words the reader should know:

### Near
What must happen now?

### Mid
What concrete ladder exists?
- exam;
- realm;
- job;
- debt;
- rank;
- family stage.

### Far
What does this book become if it succeeds?

The far horizon can be one shadow, not an explanation.

Anti-sitcom:
> first mini-climax must hand off a visible rung, not only “bigger mystery remains”.

# 6. 20 / 50 / 100 / 300 Chapter Requirements

## Ch20
Reader can name at least 3 permanent gains/costs across different ledgers.

## Ch50
At least one Ch10 asset/person/constraint now creates a new problem or advantage without reintroduction.

## Ch100
Protagonist has entered a larger SOCIAL SYSTEM:
- not just another city;
- not just stronger enemies.

Examples of functions:
- from student to industry;
- from household to clan/territory;
- from individual worker to institution/market.

## Ch300
At least two genuine story-mode changes have happened.
The original Premium is more important, not less.

# 7. What 《玄鉴仙族》 Proves

《没钱》 shows “institutional coupling”.

《玄鉴》 shows “time coupling”.

Family structure creates automatic scale because:
- people age;
- children inherit;
- land remains;
- enemies remember;
- marriages/alliances alter future generations;
- one person's choice becomes family history.

Transferable principle:
> **a durable container can be a person, family, team, organization, territory or public rule.**

If the protagonist is the only durable container:
> scale is fragile.

# 8. Permanent State > Episode Quality

A beautiful arc is insufficient if it leaves only:
> memory + clue.

Qidian paid retention benefits from visible residue:
- new skill;
- new qualification;
- new property;
- new debt;
- changed relationship;
- new office/role;
- new social access;
- irreversible public reputation.

A payoff should alter future chapter options.

# 9. Character Desire During System Expansion

As systems grow, desire must remain concrete.

Bad:
> “change the cultivation civilization.”

Good progression:
> pass test -> keep job -> afford treatment -> build method -> win qualification -> protect team -> enter larger market.

Macro scale must be entered through personal need.

# 10. Qidian Head-Tier Architecture Requirements v1

PASS only if:

1. Familiar mother-market remains legible.
2. Premium appears in ordinary life, not only special events.
3. At least one universal conversion layer connects 4+ systems.
4. Ch20 leaves 3+ permanent fruits/costs.
5. Ch50 proves old-state compounding.
6. Ch100 enters a larger social system.
7. Ch300 changes story mode twice.
8. At least 2 non-MC recurring people have independent goals.
9. First mini-climax hands off a tangible next rung.
10. Power/resource/status are visible, not hidden under mystery.
11. World knowledge enters because somebody needs it.
12. A local scene can imply a much larger world.
13. The Premium is more valuable at high scale.
14. The architecture can run significant spans without fresh-case supply.

# 11. Kill Signals

- one incident = one system only;
- “bigger map” used as scale;
- every 20 chapters require a new gimmick;
- the protagonist's life outside the Premium is empty;
- rewards are mostly clues;
- relationships reset after arcs;
- status never changes how others behave;
- ordinary chapters cannot carry the premise.

# 12. Book Contract Gate

After Opening Arc a reader should answer:

> What do I expect to see get bigger?

At least 3:
- protagonist power;
- material life/resources;
- social position;
- key relationship;
- organization/territory;
- macro question.

If the answer is mainly:
> “I want to know the mystery,”

Qidian long-run architecture is underbuilt.
