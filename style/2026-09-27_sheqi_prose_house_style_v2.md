# Sheqi 正文 House Style V2
date: 2026-09-27
scope: all formal prose drafts
status: HARD STYLE GUARDRAIL / supersedes V1 for delivery QA

# 0. Core principle
Fast prose is NOT fragmented prose.

Every paragraph should carry a complete narrative beat:
> stimulus → reaction / judgment → action / consequence

Do not split one complete thought into several tiny paragraphs merely to create visual speed.

The target is:
> natural spoken-read rhythm + clear causal movement + selective emphasis.

# 1. Semantic-beat rule
Before every paragraph break, ask:
> Did the thought / action / exchange actually finish here?

If no:
> do not break the paragraph.

Bad:
> “哪学的？”
>
> “刚才。”
>
> 邵教练抬头。
>
> “我问你从哪学的。”
>
> “我也没答错。”

Preferred:
> “哪学的？”
>
> 程野看了眼方才雨院出现的位置，“就刚才。你要问跟谁学的，我也正想知道。”

Or:
> “哪学的？”
>
> “刚才现学的。”程野说完，见邵教练明显不信，又补了一句，“别这么看我，我现在比你更想知道老师是谁。”

The complete exchange is more important than punchy fragments.

# 2. Short-line budget
HARD:
- narrative sentence under 6 Chinese characters should almost never stand alone;
- dialogue under 4 Chinese characters should not stand alone unless emotional force genuinely depends on brevity;
- in any 300 Chinese characters, no more than 2 standalone short lines under 8 characters;
- never use 3 consecutive short turns under 8 characters in ordinary conversation.

Allowed rare uses:
> “跑！”
> “谁？”
> “停手！”

These are pressure beats, not default prose rhythm.

# 3. Dialogue-completion rule
Short replies such as:
> 刚才。
> 少贫。
> 查。
> 对。
> 行。
> 不信。

are usually drafting shorthand, not finished prose.

Before delivery, expand or integrate them when the conversational intent is richer.

Bad:
> “少贫。”
> “查。”

Preferred:
> 邵教练皱了下眉：“先别贫，调记录查一遍。”

Bad:
> “刚才。”

Preferred:
> “刚才现学的。”程野说。

Dialogue may be short if the CHARACTER is deliberately shutting conversation down.
Otherwise, let the line carry attitude + information.

# 4. Paragraph composition
Most prose paragraphs should contain 1-3 linked sentences.

Good paragraph shapes:
A.
> observation + judgment

B.
> action + physical consequence

C.
> dialogue + reaction beat

D.
> sensory detail + interpretation

Avoid:
> one fact per paragraph
> one verb per paragraph
> one reaction per paragraph

unless deliberately slowing for a major reveal.

# 5. Sentence-length variation
Do not optimize toward uniformly short prose.

Desired rhythm:
- short sentence: impact / turn / joke;
- medium sentence: default storytelling;
- long sentence: continuous action / layered observation / causal explanation.

A paragraph should often mix lengths.

Bad rhythm:
> 他抬头。
> 对方没动。
> 雨还在下。
> 他觉得不对。

Better:
> 程野抬头时，对方还站在雨里没动。那场雨明明隔着另一个地方，他却莫名觉得，那个人是在等他先开口。

# 6. Punch-line restraint
A punch line only works when surrounded by normal prose.

If every exchange tries to end on a joke / sting / quote-worthy line:
> character becomes scripted.

Cheng Ye rule:
- one light turn per conversational beat is enough;
- after the joke lands, continue naturally;
- do not give every interlocutor a matching punch line.

# 7. Cheng Ye voice
Personality should appear through:
- what detail he notices first;
- how he interprets rules;
- what risk he accepts;
- how he reframes embarrassment;
- how he answers people.

Not through constant jokes.

Voice formula:
> practical observation → slightly crooked but reasonable conclusion → action

Example:
> 平台既然给极限样本加钱，保险也已经生效，他要还一直绕着弱点走，多少有点对不起这份保费。

This is stronger than stacking:
> 八百。
> 保险。
> 值得。
> 干。

# 8. Dialogue texture
Real conversation often includes:
- partial answers;
- clarification;
- interruption;
- physical behavior;
- implication.

But "realistic" does not mean transcript-like filler.

Use:
> “你这一下总慢？”
> “第三转，老毛病。”程野活动了一下脚腕，“准得很，比我闹钟还守时。”

This carries information and personality in one unit.

# 9. Action continuity
Keep linked movement in the same sentence / paragraph when possible.

Bad:
> 他扣步。
> 压身。
> 切线。
> 转身。
> 拍中。

Preferred:
> 他右脚内扣，重心随之压低，借着错身的势头从周航掌侧切过去，转腰时右手已经顺势拍上了对方后背的感应区。

Short action fragments are reserved for:
- panic;
- impact;
- an intentionally slowed decisive instant.

# 10. Suspense rhythm
Suspense should come from:
- information arriving in the wrong place;
- expectation being contradicted;
- sensory detail becoming impossible;
- character realizing consequence.

Not from line breaks alone.

Bad:
> 雨。
> 不对。
> 这里没有雨。

Preferred:
> 程野先听见了雨声。可训练馆顶棚封得严严实实，场内连风都没有。

# 11. AI contrast-pattern guard
Ration:
- 不是X，是Y
- 没有X，反而Y
- 并非X，而是Y
- 与其说X，不如说Y

Use them when contrast itself matters.
Do not use them as default sentence engine.

# 12. Reaction specificity
Avoid generic reactions:
> 愣了
> 沉默
> 眼神一变
> 心里一动

Replace with observable behavior or specific thought.

Bad:
> 周航愣了一下。

Better:
> 周航已经摆好的起手式停了半拍，随后笑了一声。

Bad:
> 邵教练沉默两秒。

Better:
> 邵教练盯着他看了两秒，像是在判断这句话到底算解释还是胡扯。

# 13. Read-aloud pass
Mandatory before delivery:
Read every paragraph internally as spoken Chinese.

Flag if:
- breath stops unnaturally every few characters;
- many sentences begin with subject name + verb;
- dialogue resembles screenplay cue cards;
- emphasis relies on paragraph breaks;
- a paragraph sounds like a slogan.

Question:
> Would a competent human storyteller naturally read it this way?

If not, rewrite.

# 14. Compression pass
After first draft:
- merge adjacent fragments that express one thought;
- remove repeated explanation;
- delete narrator paraphrase if dialogue/action already showed it;
- preserve only one strongest line among two lines doing same job.

Compression means:
> fewer units, richer units.
NOT:
> shorter sentences.

# 15. Expansion pass
If prose feels skeletal, do NOT add lore.
Add one of:
- physical transition;
- character interpretation;
- relational response;
- sensory continuity.

This creates texture without slowing premise.

# 16. Formal chapter pre-delivery gate
Every formal chapter must pass all:

A. Fragment scan
- no single-character narrative paragraph;
- no ordinary 1-3 word dialogue ping-pong;
- no 3+ consecutive short paragraphs unless deliberate high-pressure beat.

B. Semantic beat scan
- every paragraph break marks a real beat boundary.

C. Dialogue scan
- no drafting-shorthand lines like “查。”“刚才。”“少贫。” unless deliberately justified.

D. Rhythm scan
- sentence lengths visibly vary;
- normal prose exists between impact lines.

E. Voice scan
- Cheng Ye personality emerges from choices and interpretation, not joke density.

F. Action scan
- body movement is causally continuous.

G. AI-pattern scan
- repeated contrast constructions and generic reactions rewritten.

H. Read-aloud scan
- page reads like prose, not subtitle cards.

# 17. Author-review responsibility
The author should judge:
- plot;
- pleasure;
- character preference;
- emotional effect.

The author should NOT have to catch:
- fragment spam;
- drafting-shorthand dialogue;
- repetitive cadence;
- body-action discontinuity;
- generic AI reaction phrasing.

Those are writer-side QA failures and must be removed before review.
