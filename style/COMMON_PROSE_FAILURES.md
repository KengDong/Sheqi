# Sheqi｜COMMON PROSE FAILURES
status: SHARED NEGATIVE EXAMPLE CATALOG
scope: formal fiction prose

Purpose:
> prevent repeated author feedback from being rediscovered chapter by chapter.

# F01｜字幕卡式碎句
Symptom:
> 刚才。
> 少贫。
> 查。
> 很轻。
> 不对。

Why bad:
- one semantic thought is artificially split;
- pacing comes from whitespace rather than narrative;
- reads like AI-generated subtitles.

Fix:
> merge into complete thought / exchange.

# F02｜一问一答台词卡
Symptom:
> “哪学的？”
> “刚才。”
> “哪？”
> “不知道。”

Why bad:
> dialogue becomes screenplay cue cards.

Fix:
> one answer should often carry information + attitude + subtext.

# F03｜把“短”误当“快”
Symptom:
> every sentence 3-10 chars.

Why bad:
> no sentence-length contrast, so emphasis loses force.

Fix:
> medium sentences carry story; short lines reserved for impact.

# F04｜不是X，是Y过载
Symptom:
> 不是错觉。
> 是真的。
> 不是看这边。
> 是看他。

Fix:
> use one clean contrast sentence, or dramatize the contradiction.

# F05｜AI通用反应
Watch:
- 愣了一下;
- 眼神一变;
- 沉默两秒;
- 心里一动;
- 嘴角勾起;
- 瞳孔一缩.

Fix:
> specific physical behavior / decision / thought.

# F06｜身体部件瞬移
Symptom:
> 脚切进去 -> 下一句手已经在对方背后.

Fix:
> preserve torso turn, body line, opponent movement, momentum.

# F07｜作者替设定辩护
Symptom:
> 这并不是古法更强……
> 现代体系当然不是落后的……

Fix:
> demonstrate tradeoff in scene; let characters infer.

# F08｜产品经理式赛博修真
Symptom:
> 多维功法适配矩阵 / 神经云端灵能模型 / 源流核验接口.

Fix:
> use words a real person would use:
> 平台 / 保险 / 订单 / 动作分析 / 历史版本 / 训练馆 / 课程 / 档案.

# F09｜为幽默而幽默
Symptom:
> every two paragraphs ends with a punch line.

Why bad:
> Cheng Ye becomes scripted comedian rather than a person.

Fix:
> humor must grow from rule-reading, practical incentives, relationships, or absurd reality.

# F10｜所有人都配合主角抛梗
Symptom:
> every NPC gives perfect setup and perfect comeback.

Fix:
> other characters have their own conversational rhythm and goals.
> Sometimes they ignore, misunderstand, push back, or move on.

# F11｜旁白先解释、人物再重复
Symptom:
> narrator explains danger, coach explains same danger, protagonist summarizes again.

Fix:
> retain the strongest carrier once.

# F12｜一段一个事实
Symptom:
> 年龄一段。
> 身份一段。
> 学校一段。
> 钱一段。

Fix:
> bind details to active scene and one semantic beat.

# F13｜悬念靠换行
Symptom:
> 雨。
> 他听见了雨。
> 可这里没有雨。

Fix:
> impossible sensory fact + immediate interpretation in flowing prose.

# F14｜名字/主语开头机械重复
Symptom:
> 程野……
> 程野……
> 程野……
> 周航……

Fix:
> vary syntax naturally; do not force variety if it damages clarity.

# F15｜精修变成堆辞藻
Symptom:
> every object gains metaphor; every movement becomes poetic.

Fix:
> polish = precision + rhythm + voice, not ornament.

# F16｜“真人感”误解成废话
Symptom:
> lots of 嗯、啊、那个、你知道吧.

Fix:
> real dialogue is selective, not transcript literalism.

# F17｜古界和现代说话一个味
Symptom:
> ancient actors use platform/甲方/互联网式 turns without cause.

Fix:
> separate diction, etiquette, assumptions and naming systems.

# F18｜轻松角色遇险仍然贫嘴
Symptom:
> genuine horror/danger arrives; Cheng Ye still tries to land jokes.

Fix:
> when stakes become real, humor drops and observation sharpens.

# F19｜贫穷写成苦情
Symptom:
> character constantly self-pities for money.

Fix:
> Cheng Ye baseline:
> 活得下去，卷不起.
> Money is access/training efficiency, not misery pornography.

# F20｜外挂替主角做决定
Symptom:
> anomaly randomly rescues him.

Fix:
> protagonist makes a meaningful choice first; anomaly intersects that choice.

# F21｜每章重新证明性格
Symptom:
> writer inserts the same kind of joke every chapter to remind reader protagonist is funny.

Fix:
> personality should recur as decision pattern, not repeated surface gag.

# F22｜人物为了设定变聪明/变蠢
Fix:
> keep stable decision logic; new information changes decisions, not author convenience.

# F23｜所有句子都追求“金句感”
Fix:
> most prose should disappear into reading.
> Only a few lines deserve to stand out.

# F24｜修稿只替换词，不重组语义
Symptom:
> “把退路全吃掉”换成“没有退路”，但 paragraph structure remains synthetic.

Fix:
> if rhythm is wrong, rebuild the semantic unit, not just synonyms.

# F25｜作者成为第一轮QA
Failure:
> author repeatedly catches fragments, stock reactions, continuity errors.

Rule:
> these are writer-side failures.
> do not deliver until self-QA passes.

# Delivery question
Before sending chapter:
> 如果作者完全不看剧情，只看文笔，他还会不会马上抓到低级机械问题？

If yes:
> not ready.


# F26｜叙事单位过薄
Symptom:
> 一个段落只完成一个很小的事实：
> 看见 / 点头 / 回答 / 转身 / 再看。

Why bad:
> 章节看起来“快”，实际信息推进慢；大量换行在制造假速度。

Fix:
> 合并成一个完整微型因果：
> 刺激/观察 -> 判断 -> 动作/回答 -> 反馈。

# F27｜笑点只存在于句子，不存在于行为
Symptom:
> 主角说了很多俏皮话，但决定方式和普通人完全一样。

Fix:
> 让幽默来自他如何理解规则、如何真的去试，以及别人如何回应。

# F28｜世界观脱离现实利益
Symptom:
> 为了介绍现代修真制度而介绍制度。

Fix:
> 信息优先附着在钱、分数、训练、工作、资格、平台审核和资源分配上。

# F29｜作者内部术语直接扔给Reader
Symptom:
> “第三转 / 第四转”之类词作者自己知道含义，但读者无法立刻判断单位。

Fix:
> 先用通用可读单位：
> 第三式 / 第四式 / 第三式接第四式。
> 再用动作词说明：换向、内扣、回撤、压身。

Rule:
> reader-facing clarity beats internally elegant jargon.
