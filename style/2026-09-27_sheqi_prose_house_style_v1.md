# Sheqi 正文 House Style V1
date: 2026-09-27
scope: all formal prose drafts
status: HARD STYLE GUARDRAIL

# 0. Purpose
Prevent AI-like webnovel prose artifacts from reaching author review.

Primary target:
- natural modern Chinese narrative;
- fast without being chopped;
- humorous without stand-up scripting;
- readable Fanqie pacing without micro-fragment spam.

# 1. Standalone micro-line rule
HARD:
- Do not use a single Chinese character as a standalone narrative paragraph.
- Do not use a 1-2 character standalone narrative paragraph unless it is:
  1. actual dialogue,
  2. sound effect,
  3. a deliberate once-per-chapter shock beat.

Bad:
> 接。
> 稳。
> 对。
> 跑。
> 杀。

Preferred:
> 程野手指已经点了下去。
> 这一单得抢。
> 他没犹豫，直接接单。

# 2. Fragmentation cap
Avoid repeated artificial fragments:
> 下一秒。
> 然后。
> 不是。
> 是。
> 很轻。
> 很快。
> 没有。
> 不对。

Rule:
- no more than 1 isolated fragment sequence in any 500 Chinese characters;
- never use 3+ consecutive micro-paragraphs for ordinary action;
- if the sentence is logically connected, keep it in one flowing sentence / paragraph.

# 3. "不是X，是Y" AI rhythm guard
This construction is allowed but heavily rationed.

Avoid:
> 不是错觉。
> 是真的。
> 不是朝这边。
> 是看着他。

Preferred:
> 那道视线并不是扫向这边，而是准确落在他脸上。

Use at most once per major scene unless the contrast is genuinely the dramatic point.

# 4. Action continuity
Action must preserve physical causality.

Bad:
> 他一脚切入。
> 下一秒。
> 手掌按在对方背后。

Good:
> 他借着切入的势头错身绕后，右掌顺势拍中背后的感应区。

Before delivery, every combat exchange must answer:
- where is the body;
- what created the next movement;
- what did the opponent do;
- why did the hit land.

# 5. Humor rule｜Cheng Ye
Humor must come from:
- rule reading;
- practical self-interest;
- literal interpretation;
- quick reply;
- calm handling of absurdity.

Avoid:
- joke every 2-3 paragraphs;
- meme stacks;
- narrator announcing that he is funny;
- "golden line" written for screenshot value.

Preferred:
> “谁教的？”
> 程野看了眼已经消失的雨院。
> “老师下课有点快，名字没来得及问。”

The joke should still carry plot information.

# 6. Dialogue naturalness
Dialogue should not exist only to explain setting.

For each dialogue line, at least one must be true:
- reveals attitude;
- changes action;
- creates relationship;
- creates conflict;
- gives necessary information naturally.

Avoid Q&A exposition ladders:
> “这是什么？”
> “这是……”
> “为什么？”
> “因为……”

# 7. Exposition vocabulary guard
Prefer familiar contemporary vocabulary in modern line.

Use:
- 保险
- 平台
- 动作分析
- 历史版本
- 训练馆
- 课程
- 档案

Avoid invented product-manager vocabulary unless necessary:
- XX矩阵
- XX源流核验系统
- 多维灵能数据模型
- 神经云端功法接口

Cyber-cultivation should feel like a real society, not a UI concept deck.

# 8. Modern / ancient voice separation
Modern:
- simple contemporary names;
- contemporary speech;
- familiar institutions / work language.

Ancient:
- older naming texture;
- different social vocabulary;
- no modern internet phrasing unless intentionally taught by Cheng Ye.

Names should classify era at a glance.

# 9. Paragraph rhythm
Target distribution:
- majority paragraphs: 15-60 Chinese characters;
- occasional 5-15 character paragraph for emphasis;
- standalone 1-4 character narrative line: extremely rare.

Do not make the whole chapter:
> one sentence
> one paragraph
> one sentence
> one paragraph

Fast pacing comes from information/action turnover, not visual chopping alone.

# 10. Author-explanation guard
Avoid lines that sound like the author defending the setting:
> 这不是古法更高级。
> 现代文明并不愚蠢。
> 这只是支路。

Instead dramatize through:
- coach criticism;
- failure risk;
- different users;
- platform data;
- later consequences.

Character can summarize naturally after evidence:
> “不是学校没教，是学校不敢拿这招教一屋子人。”

# 11. AI phrase watchlist
Before author review, scan mentally / textually for overused patterns:
- 下一秒
- 就在这一刻
- 不是……而是……
- 很轻
- 很慢
- 很快
- 瞬间
- 猛地
- 突兀
- 准确落在
- 他愣了一下
- 他沉默了一下
- 眼神一变
- 嘴角
- 心里一动
- 脑海里第一个念头

These are not banned, but repeated use must be rewritten.

# 12. Chapter delivery gate
Before showing any formal chapter to author, writer must self-check:

A. Micro-line check
- no stray one-character narrative paragraph;
- no artificial fragment chain.

B. Voice check
- Cheng Ye has at least 2 dialogue / action beats that reveal personality;
- humor affects behavior, not decoration.

C. Physical continuity
- combat / movement can be visualized without teleporting body parts.

D. World-language check
- modern terms sound like real life;
- ancient terms sound era-distinct.

E. Explanation check
- no paragraph exists only to prove the setting is logical;
- logic is shown through people / action / consequence.

F. Hook check
- chapter contains both payoff and next desire;
- not mystery-only.

If any fails:
> revise before author sees it.

# 13. Specific rule from author feedback
The author should not have to repeatedly catch low-level prose artifacts.

Therefore:
> style QA is the writer's responsibility, not the author's.

Formal prose presented for review must already have passed this house-style gate.
