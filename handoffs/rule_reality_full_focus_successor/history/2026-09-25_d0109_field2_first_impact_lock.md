The card freezes only first-impact functions, not full plot/canon.

### First-impact sequence
- real unfinished construction project;
- transition to a completed, lived-in future residential tower;
- ordinary-life details make the impossible place feel real;
- a building signal triggers hundreds of doors closing in sequence;
- one specific relationship is split by an ordinary domestic door;
- rule is shown before it is explained.

### Cheng Ye proof
- one interpretation must prove incomplete/wrong;
- his prebuilt fallback prevents catastrophe;
- reader reassurance comes from robust planning, not omniscience;
- one short command scene makes panic executable.

### Co-lead proof
- enters through action;
- wins one “act now” decision;
- is not introduced as a function card or admirer.

### Naming guardrail
- 《封门楼》 remains internal codename;
- prose uses a believable ordinary project/building name;
- internal R&D labels stay backstage until naturally earned by characters/public usage.

### Memory target
Cold reader should later remember at least two:
- the not-yet-built but already inhabited tower;
- mass door closure;
- a concrete human relationship split by a door;
- Cheng Ye's fallback after a wrong assumption;
- co-lead decisive rescue;
- sense of a larger impossible city.
