## Main pool-only finding
Strong works do not make status a title counter.

They make it a **permission matrix**:
- who may command;
- who may punish;
- who must use due process;
- what protagonist can decide;
- who protagonist can protect;
- which property / residence / marriage / lineage / office assets remain.

The repeatable loop is:
identity pressure → read rules → action → other actors recalculate permission/cost → durable status/autonomy asset remains → old hierarchy returns at a higher level.
