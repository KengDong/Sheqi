# PROTOTYPE A｜Freight Yard

Target length:
> 1800–3000 Chinese characters.

Purpose:
> prove enemy-mode Cheng Ye + Rule Experience Gap + sibling-mode switch.

Must achieve all:

## A1｜Enemy Mode
Cheng Ye should be:
- cold;
- cautious;
- ruthless toward a confirmed predator;
- unemotional;
- focused on removing options rather than winning an argument.

Do NOT:
- make him yell;
- give him a revenge speech;
- make him sadistic;
- solve mainly by fighting.

## A2｜Rule Experience Gap
Reality is encountering R1 for the first time.
Cheng Ye has survived its mature Field ecology.

His tactic must use:
> a non-obvious R1-specific interaction / preparation / ordinary tool combination

that an ordinary cautious adult would NOT naturally design without mature rule experience.

Hard:
- must obey the current R1 mechanic lock;
- no invented hidden powers;
- no prerecorded/synthetic voice edge case unless current lock supports it;
- no “author says clever” explanation without visible payoff.

## A3｜Commercial Intelligence Spectacle
At least one:
- enemy thinks the game is A, Cheng Ye is playing B;
- weird prep pays off;
- cheap ordinary object becomes powerful because of R1 knowledge;
- Cheng Ye rejects an apparently clever move because he knows its mature-rule failure mode;
- one plan layer is damaged and a second layer survives.

## A4｜Enemy Learns
Wei Pingzhou / hostile side must learn something during the confrontation.

The scene cannot end with:
> veteran effortlessly farms idiots.

Required turn:
> Cheng Ye realizes the enemy has started learning R1.

This should materially increase danger.

## A5｜Sibling Switch
After immediate danger drops, Cheng Ye must switch naturally into:
- reliable brother;
- warm;
- lightly funny / teasing;
- not solemn.

Reader target:
> **“还好我是他这边的。”**

Xiaoman must still:
- have agency;
- talk back;
- not become a rescued doll.

# PROTOTYPE B｜Home Breakfast

Target length:
> 1000–1800 Chinese characters.

Timeline:
> after the first reality crisis, before next Field entry / countdown tension fully takes over.

Purpose:
> prove Cheng Ye is enjoyable when nobody is attacking him.

Must contain:
- Xiaoman / Cheng Ye real sibling rhythm;
- at least one genuinely light/funny exchange;
- one concrete caring action from Cheng Ye that is NOT announced as sacrifice;
- Xiaoman teasing / pushing back;
- Cheng Ye able to be the butt of a joke;
- one faint shadow of his Field experience, but it must NOT dominate the scene.

Reader target:
> **Reader would want another family scene even if no rule puzzle happened in it.**

Hard:
- no exposition breakfast;
- no thesis dialogue;
- no “你一定要保护好自己” speeches;
- no artificial warm-family montage;
- no polished quip every paragraph.
