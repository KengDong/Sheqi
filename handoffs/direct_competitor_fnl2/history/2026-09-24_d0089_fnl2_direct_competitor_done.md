## Main finding

Strong urban-supernatural serials do not keep readers by endlessly introducing new ability nouns.

They rapidly migrate reader desire:
> spectacle -> protagonist -> relationship -> organization -> social/world consequence.

For FNL2, the danger is:
> “路灯 -> 消防栓 -> 地铁 -> 电网” becoming a facility inventory.

The counter-function is:
- new facility reopens old story;
- permission changes relationships;
- people carry city knowledge;
- opponents attack dependence / trust / redundancy;
- reward types rotate;
- scenes add society interfaces.
