## Highest correction

The strongest evidence-backed correction is:

> **Genre / profession / system / organization / shop / school / lord / fourth-disaster / romance label is not automatically the Reader Fantasy.**

Matched controls repeatedly show that the same Delivery Vehicle can produce:
- a current hit;
- and another work with very low current readership.

Examples researched include:
- fourth-disaster;
- confession / reverse-chase;
- shenhao / infinite money;
- simulator;
- family cultivation;
- historical transmigration.

Therefore the correct order is:

1. identify what the reader wants to repeatedly FEEL / EXPERIENCE;
2. then choose a Delivery Vehicle;
3. only after the AUTHOR chooses WANT / MAYBE / NO should concept work restart.

## Repeated current fantasy families found

The report maps, without ranking:

- visible power growth / mastery;
- rule resistance / control of fate;
- status reversal / public recognition;
- ownership / territory / home / enterprise;
- forbidden mystery / hidden truth;
- survival → threat control;
- historical agency;
- wealth → access / autonomy;
- collection / raising / evolution;
- attachment / being chosen;
- everyday prosperity / sensory comfort / home;
- professional competence / being needed;
- comedy / social reversal / public reaction;
- belonging / legacy / “we”.

These are not declared equally large and are not presented as a ranking.

## Platform-level correction

### Qidian
Current strong works more often allow the same Fantasy to accumulate through:
- identity;
- relationships;
- world level;
- family / institution;
- long-term reputation;
- historical or mystery depth.

### Fanqie
Current packaging more often makes the Fantasy legible immediately:
- who I will become;
- what I will own;
- who will need me;
- who will choose me;
- how I will avoid death;
- how other people / the world will react.

Both platforms still converge on:

> **a legible fantasy promise + repeated delivery.**

## Newcomer evidence

New-author / new-generation evidence was explicitly separated from mature-author controls.

Important source set:
- Qidian 2025 Twelve Kings, including multiple 95后 / 00后;
- first-book / low-author-credit cases;
- current Fanqie high-reader-count works not dependent on legacy大神 recognition.

The evidence does not select one genre.

It shows that unknown authors can break out with very different Vehicles when the underlying Reader Fantasy is:
- easy to understand;
- visible early;
- repeatedly delivered.

---

## Low-stakes finding

A major correction is that:

> **low stakes does not mean low desire.**

Current Qidian evidence from `《1984：从川菜馆开始》` shows strong demand for:
- sensory satisfaction;
- craft dignity;
- repeat customers / social recognition;
- visible life improvement;
- home / community;
- pace autonomy.

This prevents REBOOT-V4 from assuming that only danger, mystery, or combat can produce commercial desire.

---

## Profession finding

Profession is usually a Delivery Vehicle.

Current successful professional / occupational framing more often carries:
- competence;
- being needed;
- access to a closed world;
- responsibility;
- social recognition;
- identity growth.

A profession that only produces:
> new case → solve → get paid → reset

does not by itself prove durable Reader Fantasy.

---

## Execution-risk separation

The task did not reject high-demand fantasies merely because they are hard to write.

Demand and production difficulty are recorded separately.

Execution-heavy but clearly demanded examples include:
- deep mystery;
- serious historical agency;
- multi-identity / multi-avatar;
- multi-generation family / legacy;
- institution-heavy social systems;
- low-stakes life fiction requiring high observational specificity.
