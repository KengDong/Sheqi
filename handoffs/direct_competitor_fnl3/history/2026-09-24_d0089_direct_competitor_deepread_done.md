## Core Result

The direct-competitor answer to the special question is:

> Long apocalypse/base serials do not survive 500k—1M+ characters by indefinitely scaling the same loot event.

The durable transition is:

> acquisition -> activation -> lived experience -> human dependency -> recurring bottleneck -> rules/relationships -> external strategic position/order.

For FNL3:
> "steal the whole building" should remain the visible Premium, but "how a mobile society lives because those buildings now exist" must become the second engine.

## Strongest External Calibration

《重生末世：从打造最强基地开始》 is the clearest execution reference for the second engine:
- early base/food logic is already measured against people and duration;
- later growth brings contribution/rules, survivor inflow, talent, branch bases and energy limits;
- around the official million-character marker, chapter topics already include conservation, school, reorganization and harvest.

This supports FNL3's frozen Reservoir direction:
> school / repair / professional people / route / energy / population are not side complexity; they are the mechanism that prevents building-of-week exhaustion.
