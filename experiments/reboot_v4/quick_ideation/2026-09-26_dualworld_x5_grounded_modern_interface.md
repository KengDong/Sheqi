# Correction

Reject reader-facing term:
> 修炼实训库

Reason:
> sounds like author-invented system nomenclature, not a real modern institution.

Modern society should have different names for:
- technology;
- school course / facility;
- archive content;
- student slang.

# Recommended modern naming stack

## Underlying technology
> **道痕复现技术 / 道痕复现系统**

Function:
> use preserved Dao traces, relic data, historical records and institutional archives to reconstruct a past cultivator's experience / technique / combat context.

This is the technical term specialists / documents use.

## School course
> **实境课 / 实境训练课**

Students say:
> “下午实境课。”
> “你选哪套回放？”

No need to repeat “修炼”.

## Facility
> **实境中心 / 实训中心 / 训练舱**

Same logic as modern:
- clinical skills center;
- simulator center;
- practical training lab.

## Content unit
Formal:
> **案例 / 道痕档案**

Everyday:
> **回放**

Examples:
- 张真人筑基回放
- 青城斗法案例
- 第七码头事故回放
- 无名剑修残档

## Archive platform
Formal:
> **道痕档案库**
or simply:
> **案例库**

Student language:
> “我去案例库找个便宜回放。”

# No game rarity

Do not use S/A/B/C.

Cases have administrative metadata:
- 复原率 92%;
- 是否计入实境学时;
- 适用方向;
- 认证机构;
- 单次费用 / 课时额度;
- 稳定性;
- 风险提示;
- 是否开放公众访问.

Examples:

> 张真人筑基回放
> 复原率 94%
> 可计筑基预备课程 2 学时
> 单次 680 元 / 校内名额有限

vs

> 青石镇无名修士记录
> 复原率 21%
> 不计学时
> 仅供研究
> 免费

Normal society's pricing is rational.

# Protagonist inversion

Normal students pay for:
> high restoration + stable training + recognized hours.

Protagonist discovers:
> low-restoration gaps are exactly where the reconstruction may fail and connect to the real upstream world.

Working line:
> **别人抢名家回放，因为学得稳。**
> **我专翻没人看的残档，因为缺掉的那一段，是真的。**

# Grounded opening example

Teacher:
> “下午实境课，课程平台已经放开案例选择。剑修方向至少完成两学时认证回放。”

Classmate:
> “张真人那套还有名额吗？”

Another:
> “没了。二手课时都炒到八百了。”

Protagonist checks remaining quota / money.

Famous certified cases are too expensive / unavailable.

He selects:
> “青石镇无名修士记录｜复原率 18%｜不计认证学时｜免费”

This is believable because:
> it is worthless for the normal purpose.

Inside the missing section:
> a real person responds.

# Social feedback

When the record later changes:
do NOT say:
> “E级升D级.”

Instead:
- new evidence appears;
- restoration rate rises;
- previously unavailable segment becomes replayable;
- the case gains research value;
- teacher / archive curator notices;
- later it may receive formal certification for a training purpose.

Modern rewards:
- course credit;
- research contribution;
- archive access;
- teacher recommendation;
- scholarship / competition opportunity;
- licensing / contribution payment;
- practical advantage.
