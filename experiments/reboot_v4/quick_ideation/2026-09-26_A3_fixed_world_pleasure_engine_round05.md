# SINGLE-WORLD MODERN CULTIVATION

## A1｜《别人走火入魔会废，我走火入魔会转职》

### Core rule
Modern cultivation is highly standardized.
"Deviation /走火入魔" means:
> failed path, injury, career-ending event.

For protagonist:
> a genuine cultivation deviation does not merely reverse into a buff.
> It opens the **failed branch itself as a coherent new cultivation path**.

Example:
- standard sword-breathing fails → opens a reverse-meridian sword path;
- body-refining medicine conflict → opens a mutation/body route;
- meditation hallucination → opens a mental / dream route.

The new path must have:
- its own costs;
- its own techniques;
- its own social use;
not random skill drops.

### Ch1–3
Ch1:
> standard cultivation lesson / certification, protagonist truly goes off-route.

Everyone thinks:
> done, future ruined.

Ch2:
> he discovers the "failed" route continues and gives one immediately useful ability.

Ch3:
> deliberately tests whether another small deviation also has a path.

Next desire:
> **what other things society calls "修错了" are actually roads nobody continued walking?**

### Ch20 growth
He is not collecting random classes.
He begins mapping:
> standardized cultivation's discarded branches.

Society:
> teachers / doctors / institutions first treat him as pathological, then cannot explain his results.

### Why this is stronger than repair
The object of curiosity is:
> **my own body / route changes every time.**

## A2｜《修错功法可以退款，我把仙路当试玩》

### Core rule
In this society, choosing the wrong:
- cultivation major;
- root modification;
- technique;
- career Dao
can waste years.

Protagonist alone can:
> **退道 / reset a chosen cultivation route and recover the invested cultivation base / resources**, while retaining memory and understanding.

Not time reversal.
The WORLD remains changed.
Only his invested path can be refunded.

### Ch1–3
Ch1:
> protagonist is trapped in a low-upside route.

Ch2:
> accidentally refunds several months / years of accumulated cultivation.

Ch3:
> reinvests the recovered base into another method and immediately confirms:
> he can test roads with almost no sunk cost.

Next desire:
> **试下一条。**

### Ch20 growth
Normal people ask:
> which major / sect / job path should I commit my life to?

He can:
> actually live several versions before choosing.

Long pleasure:
> his advantage is not omnipotence; it is **zero-cost trial and error in a civilization built on irreversible choices**.

### Social fit
This collides naturally with:
- schools;
- career certifications;
- expensive root procedures;
- sect recruitment;
- employment.

## A3｜《全网都在练同一门功法，我这本会自己抄作业》

### Core rule
Modern cultivation society uses mass-standardized techniques.

Millions practice:
> the same basic breathing / body / sword programs.

Everyone's tiny successful adjustment is usually lost as personal experience.

Protagonist's version can:
> **automatically absorb proven successful variations made by other practitioners of the same technique.**

Not copy whole skills.
It crowdsources one technique's evolution.

### Ch1–3
Ch1:
> protagonist gets the cheapest / most common public basic technique.

Ch2:
> his version silently updates after observing other practitioners.

Ch3:
> he gets a small improvement that no official edition has yet.

Next desire:
> get more practitioners / environments / styles feeding the same technique.

### Ch20 growth
He starts posting / spreading a harmless variant because:
> more users = more evolutionary data.

Now:
> cultivation + internet fame + crowdsourced Dao begins compounding.

### Long fantasy
From one public breathing method:
> eventually builds the first cultivation path evolved by millions of ordinary people.

### Risk
Must not become programmer / version-number jargon.

## A4｜《我每学一门功法，都会多一个未来的我》

### Core rule
When protagonist truly commits to a cultivation path,
he sees / contacts:
> **a future self who stayed on that path for years.**

Sword path:
> future sword cultivator.

Alchemy:
> future alchemist.

Body path:
> future body cultivator.

They are not permanent clones.
They are:
> conditional futures.

Change present choices enough,
and that future may disappear.

### Ch1–3
Ch1:
> learns an ordinary beginner method.

Ch2:
> a future version of himself appears / communicates and says:
> "别这么练，我练废了三年。"

Ch3:
> protagonist follows one correction and gets immediate advantage.

Next desire:
> if I try another route, what kind of future me appears?

### Ch20 growth
The futures disagree.

Reader pleasure becomes:
- which future is right?
- which life does protagonist actually want?
- can he steal the strengths of a future without becoming that person?

### Modern-world fit
Perfect for a society where young cultivators must choose:
- major;
- sect;
- job;
- body modification;
- life route.

## A5｜《别人修仙选专业，我的专业会自己进化》

### Core rule
Modern cultivation divides Dao into standardized professions / majors.

Protagonist receives a low-rated generic path.

But:
> every time he solves a real problem with it,
> the path itself sprouts a new specialization based on what he actually did.

Not random skill tree.
His life choices WRITE his profession.

Example:
basic "御物" major:
- used in rescue → develops precision/control branch;
- used in combat → develops high-load branch;
- used with medicine → develops surgical branch.

### Ch1–3
One generic low-value major.
One real use.
First unexpected branch.

Next desire:
> **what can I make this useless major become?**

### Ch20
Others graduate into fixed job labels.
He is growing a profession the system has no code for.

### Why it fits
Identity and growth are the same mechanism.

# DUAL-WORLD: MODERN CULTIVATION + TRADITIONAL CULTIVATION WORLD

## D1｜《我在古界改一招，现代教材连夜改版》

### Core rule
The traditional cultivation world is not merely a resource map.

It is:
> **a causal source layer for many techniques / lineages used by the modern world.**

When protagonist crosses and changes a technique / choice at its source,
he returns to find:
> the modern version has updated as if history always worked that way.

Only protagonist remembers the old version.

### Ch1–3
Modern:
> he struggles with one standard technique.

Cross:
> meets its young creator / source school while the technique is still crude.

He makes / causes one tiny practical adjustment.

Return:
> his textbook has changed.
Teacher and classmates all remember the new edition.

Immediate advantage:
> only he knows both versions and why the change happened.

Next desire:
> **what happens if I touch the source again?**

### Ch20
A few tiny historical edits have created:
- changed techniques;
- changed people;
- changed modern relationships.

The modern world starts becoming partly "his" history.

### Major strength
Each crossing gives an immediate, visible modern payoff WITHOUT commodity trade.

## D2｜《全班绑定道种，我绑定的是异界活人》

### Core rule
Modern people cultivate by binding Dao seeds / cultivation templates.

Everyone assumes:
> Dao seed = abstract inheritance.

Protagonist's seed is bidirectional:
> it is connected to a REAL living cultivator in the traditional world.

If the ancient counterpart improves:
> protagonist's seed evolves.

If protagonist develops the seed in modern conditions:
> the counterpart receives inspiration / a mirrored gain.

### Ch1–3
Binds a low-rated seed.
Hears / sees the other person.
Both initially think the other is some kind of inner demon.

They solve one immediate cultivation problem together.
Both benefit.

Next desire:
> **help the other guy break the next problem because I also get stronger.**

### Ch20
It becomes:
> two people in different worlds co-developing one Dao.

Relationship itself is the long asset.

### Difference from 《原来我是仙道天骄》
No:
- here-trash / there-heavenly-genius;
- commodity arbitrage;
- ancient side worship.

The pleasure is:
> **cross-world co-op cultivation.**

Single-world strongest zones:
- failure becomes new road;
- irreversible career choice becomes refundable;
- mass-standardized cultivation becomes crowdsourced / self-evolving;
- future selves turn career decisions into characters.

Dual-world strongest zones:
- actions at the source rewrite modern cultivation;
- cross-world co-op cultivation with a living counterpart;

Death / return may still exist later,
but only if it naturally emerges from the selected pleasure engine.

No final pick.
