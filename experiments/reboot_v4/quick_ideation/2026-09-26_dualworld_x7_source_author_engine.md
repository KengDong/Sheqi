# 0. Author correction

Do not claim "surpass 《原来我是仙道天骄》" merely because a concept uses a different lane.

To qualify as a stronger candidate, the new design must compete on the SAME pleasure axes:
1. immediate identity contrast;
2. Ch1–3 concrete payoff;
3. high-frequency repeatable advantage;
4. current-world social revaluation;
5. low cognitive load;
6. plus one additional durable engine.

# 1. Benchmark bar

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
Current public evidence:
- 2026-09-01 listed with first subscription 6691;
- 2026-09-10 listed as reaching 万订.
Core pleasure:
> modern low-value student becomes high-value "天骄" under another world's standards;
> cross-world methods/resources repeatedly raise identity and access.

## 《没钱修什么仙？》
Current official honor:
- 十万均订 on 2026-04-01.
Core modern-cultivation function:
> cultivation is commercialized / financialized / platformized and directly tied to money, school, work, contracts, products and social mobility.

The new concept should use THIS social grammar rather than inventing a "history system".

# 2. New modern anchor｜功法 is a paid digital / licensed product

In a cyber-cultivation civilization:
- techniques have versions;
- schools license teaching editions;
- companies / sects own commercial rights;
- premium modules / annotations cost money;
- safer optimized versions cost more;
- old public editions are cheap / free;
- updates matter;
- compatibility / risk data is part of the product;
- students use phones / implants / training terminals to access them.

Daily language:
> 买课 / 买功法 / 解锁完整版 / 更新版本 / 学校授权 / 公开版 / 老版本.

No special invented institution is needed.

# 3. Highest hook

> **别人没钱，只能练免费版功法。**
>
> **我没钱，直接去三百年前找作者。**

More precise:
> modern protagonist cannot afford the complete licensed version of a mainstream cultivation technique.
> He somehow reaches / contacts the upstream era when its future founder is still a nobody and the technique is unfinished.

Modern protagonist knows:
> the downstream mature version / public fragments / centuries of known results.

Ancient creator knows:
> the actual body, environment, original problem and why each move exists.

Neither has the full answer alone.

# 4. Ch1–3

## Ch1｜买不起完整版
Modern side:
- protagonist has a concrete school / cultivation need;
- his school only licenses a basic edition OR his personal account cannot afford the full module;
- he uses an old/public/obsolete edition and hits a real bottleneck.

Do not make every classmate rich.
The inequality is ordinary:
> some students have family plans / school premium access / paid tutors;
> protagonist uses free/public material.

At the end:
> while tracing an old source / origin note / damaged technique lineage, he falls into the upstream world / establishes contact.

He meets:
> the young person who will later be recorded as the founder / key developer of the technique.

But at this moment:
> he is not a founder.
> he is stuck on the same problem.

## Ch2｜你怎么会我的招？
Modern protagonist demonstrates / describes a fragment of the FUTURE mature form.

Ancient creator's reaction is not worship:
> shock + suspicion.

Because:
- the technique is currently unfinished;
- some details are ideas he has never told anyone;
- modern protagonist knows a later solution but NOT the original embodied reason.

This is stronger proof than "I know the future":
> he knows the other man's unfinished work AFTER completion.

The ancient creator tests him:
- asks why a movement exists;
- protagonist cannot fully answer;
- creator sees he has a strange downstream version, not omniscience.

They become rationally interested in each other.

## Ch3｜共同改出一个“历史上不存在的新版”
They combine:
- modern mature result;
- ancient original context.

They make a small correction neither original history nor modern textbook had.

Return modern.

The technique / documentation has changed.

Not "system prompt history changed."

Concrete:
- the app / textbook version number differs;
- the move he just practiced is now the officially recognized form;
- a new annotation / derivative branch exists;
- the technique's current performance / price / institutional history changes in a bounded way.

And protagonist remembers the old version.

Immediate reader question:
> what happens if he goes back and changes another source?

# 5. Extreme identity contrast

《仙道天骄》 contrast:
> low-value here / heavenly genius there.

X7 should use:
> **poor end-user here / impossible insider at the source there.**

Modern:
> he cannot afford the premium technique.

Ancient:
> he is discussing the technique as an equal / strange future peer with the person modern society calls “祖师”.

Later:
> modern students pay to learn a standardized technique whose source notes mention an unidentified collaborator.

Reader knows:
> that collaborator is the broke student who currently cannot afford the premium license.

Extreme scene:
> protagonist's phone says:
> “完整版需付费解锁.”
>
> provenance page below says:
> “奠基者：X祖师；早期共同论道者：佚名.”
>
> 佚名就是他.

This is not yet public status, but it is a strong reader-only identity contrast.

# 6. How present-day social payoff becomes public

The protagonist cannot live only on secret irony.

He obtains current-world advantages that institutions recognize:

1. **double-version understanding**
   - remembers old and new versions;
   - understands what changed and why.

2. **source-context mastery**
   - others learn the finished move;
   - he knows the original problem the move solved.

3. **unpublished branches**
   - the ancient creator may test variants that never become mainstream but remain useful.

4. **reverse inheritance**
   - creator leaves manuscripts / tokens / permissions / sealed objects for future him.

5. **source-lineage credentials**
   - later current institutions find impossible evidence that he has access to a lost source tradition.

These improve:
- practical performance;
- tests;
- competitions;
- teacher treatment;
- scholarship / internship / team access;
- eventually institutional status.

# 7. Repeat engine｜future version feeds source, source rewrites future

Cycle:
1. modern protagonist needs something but has limited access / current bottleneck;
2. traces technique / artifact / institution to upstream source person;
3. modern downstream knowledge gives source person a strange future answer;
4. source person's lived context reveals why that answer is incomplete;
5. together they change the source;
6. modern downstream version updates;
7. protagonist's old knowledge is partly obsolete;
8. new version creates new opportunity / problem.

Thus:
> information gap self-refreshes.

Using future knowledge destroys the exact future that produced it.

# 8. Multiple people without anthology collapse

Do NOT jump random founders every arc.

First 80–100 chapters:
> one historical "innovation generation" / one region / one network.

The first future founder knows:
- future alchemist;
- formation reformer;
- sect organizer;
- merchant / patron;
- rival.

Modern protagonist gradually realizes:
> many techniques / institutions his cyber world now commercializes were born from this small network.

Thus:
> one author → collaborator network → historical institution.

Recurring cast persists.

# 9. Resource return

No cheap item teleport.

Ancient actors can:
- leave original manuscripts;
- preserve a branch version;
- hide an artifact;
- create a lineage instruction;
- reserve a future right;
- leave an account / trust / token through descendants;
- establish a free/public version of a technique.

Time delivers it forward.

This uses historical causality rather than warehouse trading.

# 10. Cyber-cultivation escalation

Early:
> one paid technique.

Mid:
- version rights;
- school licenses;
- sect / company ownership;
- technique compatibility data;
- proprietary training modules;
- body / implant upgrades tied to licensed methods.

Later:
> protagonist is entangled with the historical origins of systems that modern corporations / sects monetize.

Potential conflicts:
- current rights-holder benefits from one historical version;
- protagonist's change creates an alternate lineage;
- improving safety may reduce raw power;
- ancient creator may reject a future commercialization path;
- a technique public in the past becomes proprietary in the present.

Do NOT make this a legal-thriller first.
These are later consequences of cultivation choices.

# 11. Three variants

## A｜找祖师本人
Primary fantasy:
> I cannot afford the technique, so I go to the person who hasn't invented it yet.

Strongest immediate click / identity contrast.

## B｜改源码
Primary fantasy:
> modern cultivation methods are downstream versions; protagonist can change their source era.

Strongest cyber / history-update engine.

Risk:
> too programmer-metaphor-heavy if reader-facing.

Use "原本 / 初本 / 祖本 / 源流" rather than "source code" in prose.

## C｜我是佚名共同作者
Primary fantasy:
> present-day poor user gradually discovers his hidden historical identity is embedded in the origin stories of modern mainstream cultivation.

Strongest long-term identity contrast.

Recommended combination:
> A as opening
+ B as repeat engine
+ C as identity payoff.

# 12. Direct comparison against 《原来我是仙道天骄》

> extremely strong and immediate: ordinary / low-value here, genius there.

X7:
> poor end-user here, impossible future insider / peer of founder there.

## Immediate concrete gain
仙道天骄:
> resources / methods / talent status quickly change options.

X7:
> source correction must change a modern technique / performance by Ch3.

Assessment:
> X7 must pay by Ch3 or it loses.

## Repeatability
仙道天骄:
> two-world resource / knowledge / identity revaluation.

X7:
> every altered source rewrites downstream versions and refreshes the information gap.

Assessment:
> X7 has a potentially stronger self-refreshing engine, but only if the changes stay easy to follow.

## Social revaluation
仙道天骄:
> direct school + sect identity ladders.

X7:
> modern performance / source-lineage access / hidden co-author identity must convert into formal school / institutional treatment.

Assessment:
> currently weaker until concrete Ch10–20 public payoff is designed.

## Cognitive load
仙道天骄:
> very low.

X7:
> higher due causality / version changes.

Assessment:
> this is X7's biggest disadvantage.
Rules must be brutally simple.
