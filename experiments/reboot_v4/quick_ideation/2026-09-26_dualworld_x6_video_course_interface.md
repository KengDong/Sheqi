# Author correction

Previous interface still felt constructed:
- 实境课
- 实训中心
- 复原率
- 认证学时

Even if logically defensible, they sound like author-designed system terms.

New rule:
> Start from an action a modern student would already understand without explanation.

That action:
> **看课 / 看录像 / 刷复盘。**

# 1. Modern cultivation society should normalize cultivation through familiar media

Students use ordinary phones / tablets / school accounts.

Common content:
- 名师课程;
- 突破复盘;
- 斗法录像;
- 功法拆解;
- 公开课;
- 训练直播 / 回放;
- 历史留影数字化资料.

Nobody in daily speech says:
> “进入修炼实训库.”

They say:
> “你买哪套课？”
> “把那场斗法录像发我。”
> “这招去看XX老师的拆解。”
> “我没钱买正版，先翻公开区老录像。”

# 2. Cultivation-native technical basis

Earlier generations could leave:
> 留影 / 神念留痕 / 道痕记录.

Modern society digitized these records into:
> video-like teaching content.

Technical details stay background.

Reader-facing reality:
> it looks and behaves like video / course playback.

# 3. Famous content vs low-value content

No RPG grades.

## Famous masters
Their content is valuable because:
- complete;
- authentic;
- officially annotated;
- lineage-owned;
- stable;
- high teaching value;
- sometimes paid / limited through school licensing.

Students rationally prefer:
> famous-master classes and clean battle recordings.

## Obscure old recordings
Cheap / free because:
- unknown actor;
- incomplete;
- corrupted;
- no commentary;
- missing beginning / ending;
- unclear technique;
- bad image / sound;
- no proven teaching value.

Normal market pricing is correct.

# 4. Protagonist inversion

For ordinary viewers:
> missing footage = worthless.

For protagonist:
> missing footage can become a LIVE window into the source event / source-world.

Working line:
> **别人花钱买名师课，我专翻没人看的老录像。**
>
> **因为他们看到的是记录，我看到的那个人还活着。**

Alternative hook:
> **我点开一段三百年前的残缺录像，画面里的人突然抬头看了我一眼。**

This should be the true premise ignition.

# 5. Opening shape

## Ch1
Modern cultivation student has a concrete current problem:
- cannot afford premium course;
- school account lacks access;
- needs a technique explanation before practical test;
- assigned course does not solve his specific problem.

He searches a public / free video archive.

Clicks:
> “青石镇斗法残片｜上传者：市立档案馆｜播放 47”

The clip is broken.

At the missing section,
one young cultivator suddenly looks outside the frame:
> “谁？”

No system explanation.

## Ch2
Protagonist replays.
The person remembers.

He checks accompanying notes / modern encyclopedia:
> obscure cultivator dies soon / disappears.

He gives one near-term falsifiable warning.

## Ch3
Ancient cultivator changes one choice.

The video changes:
- new seconds appear;
- later footage exists;
- accompanying notes update;
- maybe a different clip elsewhere now includes him.

Protagonist uses the newly appeared technique / context to solve his current modern problem.

Immediate loop:
> old footage → live person → altered history → new footage / knowledge → current-life payoff.

# 6. Why this is more natural than archive grading

Modern society already understands:
- paid courses;
- premium creator content;
- public archives;
- copyright / licensing;
- school subscriptions;
- view counts;
- comments;
- recommended lists;
- old digitized footage;
- pirated / secondhand circulation;
- creator / lineage-owned content.

Cultivation changes the subject matter,
not the institutional grammar.

# 7. Low-value high-return is visible without an explicit score

A low-value clip can simply have:
- 47 views;
- no comments;
- no teacher recommendation;
- incomplete audio;
- unknown person;
- public-domain / free access.

A premium master course can have:
- expensive subscription;
- school-limited seats / licensed access;
- thousands of students;
- formal teaching notes;
- teacher endorsements.

Reader understands the hierarchy instantly.

# 8. Historical network access

Do NOT randomize every episode.

Use:
> video graph / recommendation graph / citation graph.

If first obscure cultivator survives:
- a second old clip appears where he meets someone;
- a manuscript cites another person;
- an old battle recording now contains a new figure;
- the platform's related-material section gains a new item.

Thus:
> one changed person naturally unlocks more source people.

This keeps continuity while preserving discovery.

# 9. Ancient trust

Still evidence-based:
1. protagonist predicts a near-term event;
2. ancient person verifies it;
3. protagonist admits what records do NOT know;
4. ancient person deliberately deviates;
5. protagonist later returns with changed downstream record.

No instant belief.

# 10. Resource return

No item teleport.

Ancient side can:
- leave an object at a location;
- record a technique;
- hide a note;
- establish a password;
- leave a lineage instruction;
- preserve a right.

Modern side retrieves through ordinary archaeology / institution / descendants.

Reader-facing phrase should be cultivation-native:
> 留给后世.

Not:
> historical investment.

# 11. Modern social feedback

Protagonist's gain should enter ordinary life:
- practical test score;
- teacher attention;
- club / team invitation;
- paid tutoring / contract;
- access to better courses;
- scholarship;
- internship;
- competition qualification.

Do not invent “archive points.”

# 12. Extreme identity payoff

Later, an obscure altered event becomes famous enough that:
- teachers use its digitized footage as standard teaching material;
- a mysterious unnamed participant repeatedly appears in surviving records;
- modern editors / AI reconstruction build a best-effort visual model of him.

The protagonist watches classmates study:
> a reconstructed version of his own hidden historical identity.

But this should emerge from normal course media,
not a special “S-grade immortal-history” system.

# 13. Three grounded variants

## Variant A｜课程平台
Most grounded school / money / access logic.

Daily language:
> 买课 / 听课 / 看回放.

Best for 《没钱修什么仙？》 influence.

## Variant B｜视频平台 + 公共档案
Most natural / vivid.

Daily language:
> 找录像 / 看旧视频 / 搜残片.

Best opening hook:
> person in old video looks back at protagonist.

## Variant C｜校内教学平台
Most controlled / institutional.

Daily language:
> 课程资源 / 课堂录像 / 复盘资料.

Less flashy but very believable.

Current preference:
> **B as discovery surface + A as school/economic layer.**

Students discover / browse through video-like interface,
while premium courses / school licensing determine access and social inequality.
