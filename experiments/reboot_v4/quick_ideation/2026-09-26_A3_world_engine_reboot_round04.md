> A/D innovation too weak; maintenance / appraisal / recycling engine returned.

- 《没钱修什么仙？》
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》
- 《我正经学生，每天只吃九种魔药》

Borrow:
> strange cultivation rule is already embedded in school / work / ordinary society.

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》

> one small cross-world difference immediately changes protagonist's available options; status payoff can be delayed.

## 《我正经学生，每天只吃九种魔药》
Borrow:
> one bizarre repeated action gives immediate useful benefit and naturally creates the next desire.

> one weird ecological rule keeps making the same premise larger.

# New rule

Death must not mean:
> broken equipment.

Death / legacy / the dead must be:
> something this civilization actively USES every day.

# A1｜现代全民修真 + “祖师副本教育”

## Shelf premise

> **这个时代学修仙，不看录像，也不只读教材——学生直接进入死去修士留下的“遗境”，体验他们的一段人生。**

Every major cultivator dies with a posthumous "遗境":
- one battle;
- one breakthrough;
- one craft session;
- one period of life.

Modern education has standardized them:
- beginner sword course;
- pill-making practicum;
- combat simulations;
- professional certification.

This is normal society.

## Protagonist anomaly

Everyone enters:
> the safe, edited, repeatable version.

The protagonist enters:
> **the unedited final day.**

The dead master inside can SEE him.

Not an NPC.
Not a recording.

First time:
> a famous sword cultivator who has been dead 400 years stops the scripted lesson, looks directly at protagonist and asks:
> “你是谁？这里怎么还没结束？”

## Ch1–3 small loop

Ch1:
> ordinary class / certification session → protagonist is routed into the wrong version.

Ch2:
> instead of doing the textbook move, he survives / helps complete one tiny unfinished action from the master's real final day.

Ch3:
> returns to class.
He now knows one detail / one move the official legacy does not contain.

Immediate benefit:
- passes / scores unexpectedly high;
- earns a small practical cultivation gain;
- teacher notices anomaly.

Next desire:
> find out whether another cheap "祖师副本" also has a hidden real layer.

## Repeat engine

> enter a legacy everyone knows → find the true terminal layer nobody knows → solve one real unfinished ending → return with a practical difference.

Not repair.
Not trash picking.

## Why it can later become A3

The civilization has spent centuries repeatedly replaying / extracting dead cultivators' residual lives.

The early question:
> why can the dead see me?

Only much later:
> are these people actually being allowed to finish dying?

## Title-level directions

- 《全班都在刷祖师副本，只有我刷到本人临终》
- 《别人上祖师课，我进的是祖师临死那天》
- 《修仙实训而已，祖师怎么突然跟我说话了？》

# A2｜现代全民修真 + “全民继承死人道种”

## Shelf premise

> **这个时代的人不是从零修仙。十八岁以后，绝大多数人会绑定一枚死去修士留下的“道种”，沿着前人的路继续修。**

It has become a complete modern system:
- public basic道种;
- family inherited道种;
- expensive elite道种;
- career-specific道种;
- compatibility exams.

This is as ordinary as choosing a major / profession.

## Protagonist anomaly

He is assigned / can only afford:
> a low-grade abandoned道种.

Everyone else's道种 behaves like:
> skill package / cultivation template.

His:
> **the dead owner wakes up.**

The first dead cultivator is not a legendary immortal.

Maybe:
> an obscure, funny, difficult low-level cultivator whose道种 is cheap because the recorded technique is considered mediocre.

But the person behind it was not mediocre.

They can:
- argue;
- teach;
- hide things;
- disagree with textbook interpretations;
- have one genuine unfinished relationship.

## Ch1–3 small loop

Ch1:
> binding ceremony / assignment.
Everyone compares道种 value.
Protagonist gets a discarded one.

Ch2:
> dead owner speaks directly.
Shows protagonist the "official inheritance" is a simplified / wrong version of what they really practiced.

Ch3:
> protagonist uses one real correction and gets a visible small advantage.

Next desire:
> keep this strange dead teacher around / find out why they are awake.

## Repeat engine

Do NOT immediately collect 20 ghost masters.

First 30–50 chapters can be:
> one dead master + protagonist + modern school / work.

The attraction is relationship + secret original path.

Later new道种 enter slowly.

## A3 latent conflict

Modern cultivation prosperity depends on:
> the dead continuing to work after death.

The eventual send-off conflict becomes personal:
> if he truly lets his first teacher go, he may lose the thing that made him special.

That is much stronger than "retire a broken machine."

## Title-level directions

- 《全班都在继承祖师，我的祖师怎么醒了？》
- 《别人绑定道种，我绑定了祖师本人》
- 《我的修仙外挂，是个死了三百年的老师》

# A3｜现代全民修真 + “死者人生是修炼课程”

## Shelf premise

More radical than A1:

> **现代修士提升某项能力，不一定靠自己练；可以购买 / 申请一段死者生前的“人生片段”，短暂加载。**

Examples:
- swordman's final 10,000 cuts;
- alchemist's ten years of fire-control;
- scout's wilderness experience;
- healer's operating memory.

Society has built:
- schools;
- certification;
- job markets;
- licensing
around "合法加载死者人生".

## Protagonist anomaly

Everyone loads:
> a skill segment.

He loads:
> **the person.**

For a short period he experiences:
- their emotions;
- their unfinished decision;
- their true memory.

The practical advantage:
> he gets context, not just skill.

He can do things the skill-only package cannot.

## Ch1–20

One low-cost life fragment → unexpected real-person contact → immediate practical use.

Then protagonist deliberately seeks:
> cheap / unpopular life fragments whose official ratings are poor but may contain hidden context.

This feels more like:
> "find the next medicine"
than "fix the next object."

## Risk

Can become generic memory-instance / possession fiction.

Hard:
> relationship with dead people and the moral cost of civilization consuming their lives must remain central.

## Title directions

- 《别人加载技能，我把死去的本人加载出来了》
- 《修仙考试让我加载剑术，我加载了剑仙本人》
- 《这个时代拿死人上课，我却听见他们在说话》

# D1｜双界｜“同学继承祖师，我去异界找祖师本人”

## Shelf premise

Modern world uses:
> dead masters'道种 / legacy packages.

Protagonist binds a cheap, low-rated legacy.

Instead of merely activating it,
he gets pulled through the legacy's source connection and appears in:
> a traditional cultivation world.

There he meets:
> **the living original owner.**

Crucially:
> the "great ancestor" revered in modern textbooks is currently a young nobody / small cultivator / low-status disciple.

This creates instant dramatic irony.

## Why this is not 《原来我是仙道天骄》

Not:
- modern low root → ancient heavenly root;
- generic破界珠;
- free travel;
- commodity arbitrage;
- ancient world worships protagonist.

Instead:
> each cross-world connection is anchored to a PERSON whose posthumous legacy exists in modern civilization.

The protagonist's special value is:
> knowing their future reputation while meeting their living, unfinished selves.

## Ch1–3

Ch1 modern:
> protagonist chooses / gets one low-rated legacy for a practical cultivation need.

Ch2:
> activation misfires → ancient world.
He meets its living source, who is nothing like the official heroic biography.

Ch3:
> protagonist accidentally helps / works with them and learns one rough original technique.
Return to modern.

Immediate payoff:
> the "rough living original" behaves differently from the standardized posthumous version.

Next desire:
> go back to the SAME person.

No cosmic mystery yet.

## Ch4–20

The fun is relationship:
- modern textbook says the ancestor was solemn; actual person is not;
- modern legacy says technique version 7; protagonist witnesses version 1 being invented;
- protagonist can tell the living master which ideas survive centuries, but not spoil everything without consequences.

Modern side:
> one teacher / classmate notices protagonist's technique does not match any official edition.

Ancient side:
> the future ancestor slowly treats protagonist as a strange recurring friend.

## Expansion

Only much later:
> why can a dead legacy in one world connect to its living source in another?

A3 can emerge when protagonist realizes:
> modern civilization's "inheritances" may be pulling something away from those people's eventual deaths.

## Title directions

- 《同学都在继承祖师，我直接去找祖师本人》
- 《现代修仙靠祖师传承，我在异界跟祖师做朋友》
- 《你们拜的祖师，在异界还欠我一顿饭》

The third is deliberately small / relationship-first.

# D2｜双界｜“全班刷祖师副本，我匹配到真人”

## Shelf premise

Modern cultivation school runs official:
> 祖师遗境 / historical cultivation simulations.

Everyone treats them like:
- classes;
- exams;
- dungeon instances.

One day protagonist queues for a normal beginner legacy.

System does NOT send him to the simulation.

It connects to:
> **the actual ancient cultivation world at that historical person's current life stage.**

The other students are fighting scripted enemies.

He is:
> talking to the living person those scripts were built from.

## Ch1–3

Modern:
> school test / training need.

Cross:
> meet living young version of a famous figure.

Return:
> his score contains actions impossible in the official simulation.

Teacher reaction:
> "the instance doesn't have that NPC."

Next desire:
> can he match in again?

## Repeat engine

At first:
> same person, same area.

Not many worlds.

Each session:
- relationship advances;
- living source reveals one small technique / fact;
- modern simulation changes or reveals discrepancy.

This creates:
> school life + dual-world relationship + mystery.

## Differentiation

Compared with 《原来我是仙道天骄》:
- portal is public cultivation infrastructure misrouting one person;
- ancient value comes from relationship with source-person, not talent revaluation;
- no merchant engine;
- modern official history vs living reality creates repeated contrast.

## Title directions

- 《全班都在刷祖师副本，我匹配到了真人》
- 《修仙实训课上，我连进了三百年前的真人》
- 《别人刷历史副本，我在里面改祖师作业》

# D3｜双界｜“现代世界卖的是死者残本，古界还在写原版”

## Shelf premise

Modern cultivation civilization has standardized every technique into licensed editions.

The most valuable editions are:
> derived from dead masters.

Protagonist can cross to a traditional cultivation world where:
> those techniques are still being INVENTED.

His advantage is not:
> merchandise.

It is:
> he sees live evolution.

A famous modern technique may have:
- safe student edition;
- professional edition;
- forbidden original;
- the living creator's still-changing draft.

## Small loop

Modern:
> needs a technique for one practical goal.

Ancient:
> watches / helps its creator solve the actual problem that gave birth to that technique.

Return:
> understands WHY the move exists,
so he can use / adapt it beyond standardized limits.

Next desire:
> see the next revision happen.

## Risk

Too technique-centric; can become "better textbook" fiction.

Use only if characters / relationships carry the ancient side.

## Title directions

- 《现代功法全是定稿，我在古界追作者连载》
- 《你们练祖师定版，我在异界看祖师现编》
- 《这门神功还没写完，我先在三百年后学会了》

# Stronger structural observation

A and D do NOT need different cosmologies yet.

The first decision can be:

## A flavor
> the DEAD are already embedded inside modern cultivation society.

Reader pleasure:
> "the thing everyone treats as a normal educational / cultivation product is actually still a person / unfinished death."

## D flavor
> the same dead legacy has a LIVING source in another cultivation world.

Reader pleasure:
> "everyone else gets the dead standardized version; protagonist meets the living original."

Both preserve gradual expansion.

Neither relies on:
- repairing old gear;
- retirement diagnostics;
- Heaven in Ch1;
- ascension in Ch1.
