# Author correction

AUTHOR:
> “仙史”太不接地气。
> 既然采用《没钱修什么仙？》式现代全民修真底盘，就要找一个现代社会真正会日常使用的东西锚定历史交互。
> S/A/B/C等级也太游戏化、不修仙。

Accepted.

# 1. Replace “仙史实境” with an ordinary modern cultivation infrastructure

Official working name:
> **修炼实训库**

Content units:
> **修行案例 / 道痕回放 / 前人修行档案**

Student slang:
> “刷案例”
> “刷回放”

This is analogous in function to:
- exam question banks;
- clinical case libraries;
- sports replay / simulator data;
- flight / driving simulators;
- online course practical modules.

But cultivation-native:
> archived Dao traces can reproduce fragments of a cultivator's breakthrough / combat / technique-formation experience.

# 2. Why society would build it

Modern mass-cultivation civilization has:
- large student populations;
- safety requirements;
- standardized education;
- expensive real combat / breakthrough conditions;
- huge historical accumulation of cultivation traces.

Therefore schools do not let every student:
> trial-and-error dangerous cultivation from scratch.

They use archived experiences as:
- practical training;
- technique understanding;
- combat decision practice;
- breakthrough-risk education;
- certification / exam preparation.

The infrastructure would exist even without protagonist.

# 3. No letter grades

Do NOT use S/A/B/C/D/E.

Every case instead has practical metadata:

## 复原度
> how much original Dao-trace / historical evidence survives.
Example:
- 96%
- 73%
- 18%

## 认证用途
Examples:
- 可计实训学时
- 可用于剑修二级考核
- 可用于筑基风险课
- 仅供研究
- 不计学分

## 训练项目
Examples:
- 实战反应
- 灵力控制
- 剑意辨识
- 突破风险识别
- 炼丹火候

## 使用成本
- school quota;
- training credits;
- subscription / paid hours;
- special relic license.

## 风险 / 稳定性
- simulation stability;
- mental load;
- reconstruction uncertainty.

This feels like modern institutional metadata rather than RPG rarity.

# 4. Famous vs obscure cases

## 名师 / 经典案例
Famous masters have:
- many surviving records;
- complete Dao traces;
- institutional archives;
- descendants;
- relics.

Therefore their cases naturally have:
- high reconstruction percentage;
- stable training outcome;
- official certification;
- high usage fee / limited quota.

Students rationally want them.

## 残缺案例 / 失真档案
Unknown cultivators have:
- one broken artifact;
- a few contradictory records;
- incomplete Dao traces.

Their cases have:
- low reconstruction percentage;
- no reliable training outcome;
- no official credit;
- often free / public archive access.

Normal students rationally ignore them.

# 5. Protagonist's inversion

Normal simulator:
> fills missing data with reconstruction / model inference.

For unknown reason, protagonist's sessions sometimes do NOT receive model-filled content.

At a sufficiently large “missing section”:
> he falls through the reconstruction and connects to the LIVE source in the other cultivation world.

Therefore:

> complete cases = excellent training, but closed / fixed.
> broken cases = poor ordinary training, but their missing regions may be live windows.

Working line:
> **别人花钱刷名师回放。**
> **我专挑没人要的残档，因为只有缺的那一段还活着。**

This replaces:
> “别人抢S级，我抢E级。”

# 6. Modern economic pressure｜《没钱修什么仙？》 function

Do NOT force identical poverty plot.

But make training access economically real:

- school gives limited monthly practical hours;
- famous cases consume more quota / money;
- wealthy students buy extra premium hours;
- strong schools own better licensed archives;
- public library cases are cheaper but lower quality;
- some certification exams require recognized training logs.

Protagonist has a practical reason to use cheap public / broken cases:
- budget;
- quota;
- school level;
- needing extra hours;
- a specific skill not covered by assigned case.

This is not “destiny sends him to trash”.
It is ordinary modern incentive.

# 7. Low-value / high-return without game rarity

Normal value of a case:
> reliable learning output per hour / per yuan / per credit.

A broken case is genuinely bad by that metric.

Protagonist's hidden value:
> **causal access.**

He can obtain returns normal students cannot:
1. live communication;
2. changed historical / cross-world outcome;
3. new traces that did not exist before;
4. reverse inheritance;
5. double-version understanding;
6. access to the ancient person's evolving relationship network.

Thus the market is NOT stupid.
It prices the normal use correctly.

Protagonist has a different use case.

# 8. Replace “史缺升级” with a believable institutional progression

Do not:
> E → D → C → B → A.

Use:

### Stage 0｜残档
- 复原度 12%
- 不计学分
- no reliable training output.

### Stage 1｜可用案例
After protagonist changes source and new traces / records appear:
- reconstruction rises;
- some training content becomes stable.

### Stage 2｜校内收录
School can use it as an internal practical case.

### Stage 3｜认证案例
External institution validates it for a defined training purpose.

### Stage 4｜经典案例 / 核心案例
Only after historical / practical significance becomes large.

This is slow and socially meaningful.

# 9. Modern feedback loop

A changed broken case can create formal modern rewards:

- training-hour reimbursement;
- archive contribution credit;
- school practical score;
- researcher / teacher attention;
- access to better library layers;
- scholarship / competition recommendation;
- technique licensing / contribution payment;
- internship / organization opportunity.

Important:
> reward must connect to protagonist's current life pressure.

Do not award generic “history points.”

# 10. Historical person trust logic stays evidence-based

Ancient actor should not believe “future person” immediately.

Use:
1. near-term falsifiable prediction;
2. protagonist admits archive gaps;
3. ancient actor deliberately changes a choice;
4. next session protagonist reports a changed downstream record / trace.

Trust emerges from repeated verification.

# 11. Access model

## First contact
Semi-random among cheap / public broken cases protagonist can access.

## After causal interaction
The living person becomes a persistent anchor.

## New people
Unlock through real local relationships:
- friend;
- rival;
- sect;
- event;
- correspondence;
- organization.

Thus:
> cheap case discovery → fixed recurring cast → social graph expansion.

No random anthology.

# 12. Resource return

Keep:
> **逆向遗产 / natural historical delivery**

No direct item teleport early.

Ancient actors can leave:
- sealed objects;
- manuscripts;
- passwords;
- locations;
- rights;
- lineage instructions.

Modern protagonist retrieves them later.

This is more cultivation-flavored if expressed as:
> 留契 / 藏剑 / 传书 / 封存 / 祖训 / 道契
rather than “historical investment.”

Avoid finance metaphors in-reader-facing prose.

# 13. Identity contrast reworked into modern life

Old:
> modern people study “史外客.”

Ground it through current institutions:

- protagonist's class later studies a famous practical case reconstructed from the very event he changed;
- the case contains an “unknown participant” whose decisions are important;
- the simulator reconstructs that participant from surviving evidence.

Extreme scene:
> classmates pay / compete for time in a highly regarded classic training case;
> one of the important reconstructed actors is an approximate version of protagonist's hidden past identity;
> the real protagonist sits beside them as an ordinary student.

This creates:
> current low social access vs hidden canonical training identity.

Not mystical history worship.

# 14. Strongest early engine under X4

## Ch1
Modern practical training day.

Students discuss:
- who got quota for expensive famous-master replay;
- which cases count toward certification;
- how many practical hours remain.

Protagonist needs extra training but cannot / does not spend quota on premium replay.

He selects a free broken public case:
- 复原度 17%;
- 不计正式学时;
- usually considered useless.

Inside:
> one missing section becomes real.

## Ch2
He encounters a real young cultivator and leaves.

Searches the archive:
> this obscure actor is documented to die soon.

Re-enters.
Gives a near-term verifiable warning.

## Ch3
The actor changes one choice and survives.

Back modern:
- archive data changes;
- reconstruction percentage changes slightly;
- one new technique fragment / downstream trace appears;
- protagonist can use that new content in his current training problem.

No “case upgraded to D”.
Just:
> yesterday useless content now has something real.

# 15. Ch4–20

Ch4–7:
> modern protagonist uses the newly available trace to improve a real practical metric.
Teacher / system / certification responds.

Ch8–12:
> new linked actor appears inside same ancient social graph.
Not random.

Ch13–16:
> helping second actor changes first actor's downstream record negatively / unexpectedly.
First causal tradeoff.

Ch17–20:
> two ancient actors form a relationship / conflict.
Modern archive gains a new case cluster / technique lineage trace.
School notices protagonist repeatedly finds value in broken cases.
First reverse inheritance or access right appears.

Reader wait:
> not “what grade next?”
> but:
> **“下一个没人要的残档里，还缺着哪一段真的人生？”**

# 17. Benchmark functions explicitly retained

《没钱修什么仙？》
- training access tied to school, money, quotas, certification and current-life pressure;
- formal results change resource allocation.

《我有一个修仙世界》
- modern infrastructure remains useful long-term;
- archives / institutions / access can compound.

《原来我是仙道天骄》
- other-world contact quickly changes present action and status opportunity;
- do not copy talent inversion.

《九种魔药》
- each broken case is an open slot with uncertain qualitative payoff;
- protagonist should actively seek the next useful broken case.

《苟在初圣魔门当人材》
- changed history means next interaction occurs on a changed state.

- 把E级刷成S级

> **别人花钱刷名师回放，我专挑没人要的残档。**
>
> **因为他们刷到的是前人留下的经验，我刷到的那个人还没死。**

Alternative:
> **学校里最便宜的是残缺修行档案，因为谁都学不到东西。**
>
> **直到我发现，档案缺掉的那部分，正在另一个世界真实发生。**

# 20. Current unresolved questions

1. Should the other world be:
   - actual causal past of modern world;
   - causally linked source world;
   - parallel cultivation world whose traces feed modern archive?

2. How much can modern records change without destabilizing continuity?

3. Is protagonist's first practical pressure:
   - limited training quota;
   - money;
   - certification;
   - weak school resources;
   - a specific practical deficiency?

4. Should first ancient actor become a long-term recurring core, or one of several equal historical anchors?

Do NOT build full framework until these are discussed.
