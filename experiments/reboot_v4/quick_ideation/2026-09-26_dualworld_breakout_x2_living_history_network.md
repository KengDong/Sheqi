# Author correction

AUTHOR proposes:
> X1 need not bind one fixed person.
> protagonist can browse different historical simulations, influence the other world, obtain resources / gains from there, and build status in modern cultivation society.
> But current version lacks the extreme contrast pleasure of 《原来我是仙道天骄》.

Accepted.

# Highest correction

Do NOT make this:
> random historical NPC anthology.

Make it:
> **one coherent ancient era + multiple originally minor people whose lives intersect because of the protagonist.**

The protagonist does not merely "save NPCs".

He gradually creates:
> a historical relationship network.

Later modern society knows these people as:
- founders;
- ancestors;
- famous cultivators;
- originators of techniques;
- historical organizations.

But when protagonist first meets them:
> they are nobodies / low-status / unfinished people.

# Core fantasy

> **别人刷仙史，是向祖师学习。**
>
> **我刷仙史，认识的是祖师还没成为祖师的时候。**

Then stronger:

> **我救过、教过、坑过、一起做过事的那些历史小人物，三百年后全进了教材。**
>
> **而他们留下的史料里，开始反复出现一个查无此人的“史外之人”。**
>
> **那个人就是我。**

# Extreme contrast axis

《原来我是仙道天骄》:
> one protagonist is low-value in modern civilization / high-value genius in ancient civilization.

X2 should NOT copy that.

X2 contrast:
> **present social identity = ordinary student / low public status**
vs
> **historical hidden identity = person who personally altered the youth of multiple figures modern civilization reveres.**

This is:
> present nobody
vs
> hidden causal ancestor / friend / benefactor / rival of history.

Not "you are talented there".
Rather:
> **your actions there are one reason this world's history became what it is.**

# Modern institution｜仙史实境

Modern cultivation schools use:
> 仙史实境 / 历史道痕训练

Normal students:
- choose famous historical figures / events;
- enter fixed reconstructions;
- learn stable techniques / decisions;
- scenarios reset / stay within known historical rails.

Protagonist's abnormal access:
> can enter **史缺节点 / 未定史**.

These are low-confidence historical blanks:
- records incomplete;
- minor figures unrecorded;
- event causality uncertain.

What modern civilization thinks is:
> low-quality / incomplete simulation.

What protagonist eventually discovers:
> these nodes are LIVE access to the ancient world.

# Why multiple people does not fragment the novel

First ~100 chapters should stay in:
> ONE ancient era / ONE major historical region / ONE looming historical catastrophe.

The protagonist can access different "史缺人物" in that same causal web.

Example functions:
- minor sword apprentice;
- low-status alchemist;
- courier / scout;
- clan child;
- failed formation student.

They can:
- know each other;
- hear rumors of the same mysterious visitor;
- affect each other's lives;
- eventually form a small network / organization.

Therefore:
> different historical nodes converge rather than reset.

# Ch1–3

## Ch1｜the first nobody
Modern class chooses famous simulation targets.

Protagonist gets:
> a low-confidence historical node nobody wants / regards as useless.

Inside:
- the person behaves off-script;
- is in real danger;
- remembers what protagonist says.

Protagonist gives one small useful intervention.

Exit:
> system logs an impossible deviation.

## Ch2｜modern history says he dies
Protagonist searches public historical records.

The person exists only as:
> a footnote / unknown corpse / failed disciple.

He dies very soon.

Protagonist enters again and warns him.

The person does not blindly obey.
He uses local knowledge to change the plan.

## Ch3｜history acquires a new line
The person survives.

Back in modern:
> the record has changed.

Not world-shaking.

A small new line appears:
> he lived several more years / wrote one minor note / left one object / met someone he previously never met.

Only protagonist remembers the previous version.

And crucially:
> that changed record unlocks a real present-day gain.

Examples:
- a forgotten technique note now exists in the school archive;
- a buried item can be located;
- a historical coordinate / password appears;
- a minor lineage exists where none existed before.

Reader's next desire:
> if another forgotten person is connected, what will survive into the present?

# Resource return｜逆向遗产

Do NOT allow free item teleport.

Modern → ancient:
> information / warnings / concepts / limited training input.

Ancient → modern:
> **time itself carries the return.**

If an ancient person wants to repay protagonist:
- bury / seal an item;
- leave a technique;
- found a lineage;
- write a password / inscription;
- reserve a right / promise;
- tell descendants to wait for a phrase.

Three hundred years later:
> it already exists in modern world after history updates.

This creates a clean resource engine:
> help someone in history
→ they choose how to repay
→ legacy survives
→ protagonist retrieves it in modern society.

Because the OTHER person chooses the repayment:
> not a vending machine.

# The first "extreme contrast" scene

Later, a modern teacher / class visits a famous small lineage / memorial.

Teacher:
> this founder changed cultivation history after receiving guidance from an unknown expert in youth.

Classmates see:
> a carved sentence / token / phrase attributed to that mysterious expert.

Protagonist recognizes:
> it is literally something he casually said in the last historical session.

This should create:
> the first true "holy shit" identity contrast.

By Ch20–50:
- 2–3 historical nobodies have become connected;
- modern records begin to show the SAME unidentified external figure around them;
- historians give this figure a placeholder title:
  > 史外客 / 无名先生 / 某 unknown name.

The protagonist slowly realizes:
> modern history already contains a legend built from his own future interventions.

This produces a title-level long fantasy:

> **别人进仙史拜祖师。**
>
> **祖师年轻的时候，喊我先生 / 道友 / 前辈 / 怪人。**

Use relationship-specific forms rather than everyone worshipping him.

# Important variation

Do NOT make every historical person a disciple.

Different relationships:
- one treats protagonist as benefactor;
- one as rival;
- one distrusts him;
- one takes only half his advice;
- one becomes famous partly by rejecting his solution;
- one later leaves a message criticizing him.

This preserves independent actors and avoids "I raised all ancestors" cheapness.

# Modern-world fame engine

Protagonist should not become famous merely because:
> "I know history."

His historical intervention produces modern advantages that society can recognize:

1. **Double-version knowledge**
   - only protagonist remembers old and new versions of a technique / event.
   - he understands WHY the change occurred.

2. **Legacy access**
   - caches / tokens / permissions left specifically for him.

3. **Lost-context mastery**
   - modern people know the finished technique;
   - protagonist knows the messy original problem that created it.

4. **Historical anomaly solving**
   - he repeatedly predicts / identifies things official historians cannot explain.

5. **Practical cultivation performance**
   - historical insight must improve real combat / cultivation / competition, not only grades.

Thus:
> historical advantage → current measurable result → school / institutions revalue him.

Borrow function from 《没钱修什么仙？》:
> ability must become social access.

# Why information gap remains fresh

Unlike simple future prophecy:

Every intervention:
> changes later records.

Therefore:
- old records become invalid;
- a new future is generated;
- protagonist must investigate again.

With multiple connected figures:
> changing A may alter B's record even before protagonist visits B again.

This creates second-order surprises:
> "I saved him, why did her history disappear?"

Now the system becomes:
> historical causal network,
not save-one-person repetition.

# Long horizon

Later arcs can unlock:

- eventually a second historical epoch.

- old lineages;

No anthology reset.

# Logic constraints

1. Only **史缺 / 未定史** nodes are initially accessible.
   - prevents rewriting famous macro history on day one.

2. Major fixed historical anchors resist casual change.
   - protagonist can alter routes around them before he can alter the anchors themselves.

3. No physical items sent backward.
   - resources return through natural historical preservation.

4. Only protagonist retains old-version memory.
   - connection acts as memory anchor.

5. Ancient people own their actions.
   - warning is information, not command.

6. Changes propagate through actual causal links.
   - no arbitrary butterfly effect just because author wants surprise.

7. First 100 chapters stay in one coherent ancient causal web.
