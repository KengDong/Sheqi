# 0. Why V1 needs upgrading

Old shorthand:
> touch source-bearing object → follow trace to past.

Problem:
- can feel like "touch relic, open dungeon";
- over-centers techniques / objects;
- later relationship and multi-era layout require extra exceptions;
- source scene may feel arbitrarily selected.

New model:
> protagonist does not travel to "a date".
> protagonist connects two moments on the SAME surviving causal chain when they are asking / enacting the SAME underlying question.

Reader-facing:
> **他穿的不是某一年，而是某件事还没有成为历史的那个时刻。**

# 1. Underlying metaphysics

Modern cyber-cultivation civilization already recognizes:
> techniques / artifacts / medicines / institutions / places can retain source signatures and provenance.

Normal people can read:
- static Dao trace;
- version lineage;
- provenance;
- compatibility / residual information.

The protagonist's Earth-origin world-scar is abnormal because:
> his deepest soul anchor sits partially outside this world's rewritten causal history.

Therefore when a surviving causal trace becomes sufficiently active,
his world-scar can "over-read" the trace:
> not only see residue,
> but establish a live connection to a source-era causal node.

Do NOT explain this fully early.

# 2. Three conditions for first-stage resonance

A source connection needs ALL THREE:

## Condition 1｜surviving causal carrier
Something in the present genuinely descends from / was caused by the past:
- technique;
- medicine;
- artifact;
- lineage / institution;
- location;
- old contract / ritual / practice;
- established relationship trace.

## Condition 2｜personal participation
The protagonist must actually:
- practice it;
- use it;
- test it;
- suffer from it;
- repair it;
- enter / operate inside it;
- hold real access / responsibility.

Reading Wikipedia / seeing a statue is not enough.

## Condition 3｜problem resonance
The protagonist must hit a present situation that strongly resembles the source-era causal problem.

Examples:
- high-pressure movement transition ↔ creator testing that same transition;
- recovery failure ↔ healer first confronting that injury pattern;
- artifact overload ↔ original craftsman solving the same breakage;
- signing / challenging an institutional rule ↔ founding-era debate that created it;
- entering a ruin during a similar environmental condition ↔ original disaster.

This is the key optimization.

The "door" comes from:
> **same surviving cause + protagonist's real use + same unresolved question.**

# 3. Entry progression｜叠影 → 应答 → 投身

## Stage A｜叠影
Early / accidental.

Experience:
- modern scene and source scene overlap;
- sound / movement / sensation leaks through;
- protagonist can imitate one action / notice one detail;
- source-side actor may see an indistinct figure.

Duration:
> moments.

Commercial function:
> Ch1 immediate payoff.

Opening:
combat-data gig
→ protagonist's known movement transition fails under pressure
→ source-era creator is testing an early branch under a similar pressure
→ two scenes overlap
→ protagonist copies one transition
→ modern exchange reverses
→ source actor looks back.

This is not "watching a historical recording":
> source actor can react.

## Stage B｜应答
After protagonist intentionally recreates the resonance.

Experience:
- both sides can hear / see each other intermittently;
- short conversation;
- no full free-roaming ancient map yet.

Key requirement:
> reciprocal recognition.

Once both sides recognize:
> "there is a person on the other side,"
the connection becomes much stronger.

Commercial function:
> Ch2 proves this is relationship-capable, not a one-off vision.

## Stage C｜投身
After repeated mutual recognition / sufficient resonance.

Protagonist's consciousness follows the causal link upstream.

At source side:
> forms an interactable "映身" / temporary source-body.

Reader experience:
> effectively physical travel.

Can:
- speak;
- touch;
- train;
- fight;
- be injured;
- eat / manipulate local objects.

Cannot:
> carry nonliving matter directly back.

Modern body:
> enters deep low-response / meditation state.

This makes a safe modern environment necessary.

Do not use "projection-body" exposition early.
Reader can learn from experience.

# 4. Why this is smoother than instant hard-cut

Ch1:
> modern action remains continuous.
Ancient layer leaks into it.

Ch2:
> protagonist voluntarily tries to reconnect.
Ancient actor answers.

Only after Reader has formed the desire:
> "I want them to actually meet,"
does full source-body entry occur.

Thus crossing escalates the already-open question
instead of replacing the scene.

# 5. The source node is NOT necessarily "creation day"

A surviving chain has multiple high-density causal nodes.

Example technique:
- first invention;
- first real-combat failure;
- branch split;
- standardization decision;
- inheritance crisis.

Early protagonist cannot pick a date.

The active modern problem selects:
> the closest / strongest matching causal node.

Therefore:
> high-pressure combat in modern day
naturally reaches
> a high-pressure source test,
not random childhood of the founder.

This keeps every trip plot-relevant.

# 6. Revisit time model

Do NOT run ancient world continuously 1:1.

Each connection opens:
> a causal window around a dense node.

Between visits:
- source-side days / months / years may pass;
- actors continue living independently.

The next visit happens when:
- same relationship produces another dense node;
- protagonist recreates a stronger resonance;
- source actor deliberately leaves / creates a call.

This allows:
> long relationships without ancient tourism.

Modern time during a deep trip:
> heavily compressed but not frozen.

Reader-facing:
> he may spend hours source-side while only minutes pass modern-side.

Do not set a precise global conversion ratio early.

# 7. Anchor evolution｜物 → 人 → 约 → 网

This is the core long-form upgrade.

## Phase 1｜物锚 / 事锚
Door depends on:
- technique;
- medicine;
- artifact;
- institution;
- location / event.

The modern current plot discovers the door.

## Phase 2｜人锚
After repeated reciprocal contact,
the PERSON becomes part of the causal chain.

Now protagonist can reconnect through:
- the person's signature;
- a phrase;
- a technique mark;
- a private note;
- an intentionally preserved object.

Meaning:
> relationship itself begins to carry continuity.

## Phase 3｜约锚
Ancient actor deliberately makes a promise / marker for protagonist.

Examples:
- "If you return, look for this mark."
- leaves a personal Dao-signature in a place / object / record;
- preserves a resource specifically for him;
- builds a ritual / phrase only the two know.

This is the first deliberate cross-era layout.

## Phase 4｜网络锚
One trusted source actor connects protagonist to:
- collaborator;
- student;
- enemy;
- organization.

A referral is not enough alone.
There must still be a surviving trace / relationship link.

This creates:
> a living historical network,
not celebrity collection.

## Phase 5｜召引
Ancient-side actor learns enough to intentionally create a high-density trace:
> effectively "call" the protagonist.

Modern protagonist unexpectedly feels a familiar resonance.

This is the first time:
> ancient side initiates the plot.

Critical for world agency.

## Phase 6｜链式布局
Protagonist and source actors deliberately create traces intended to survive through multiple eras.

Example:
- Actor A plants / preserves resource;
- successor B inherits a private instruction;
- B modifies / carries it into another era;
- modern protagonist later touches the surviving chain;
- connection can open at B's era as another major causal node.

Thus one layout can produce:
> multiple historical contact points,
not merely one ancient friend + one modern reward.

This is the real transition from:
> "crossing"
to
> "cross-era strategy."

## Institution

## Relationship

> one side intentionally calls / fulfills / breaks the promise.

# 9. Matter transfer

What comes back:
- memory;
- understanding;
- body / soul impression;
- relationship;
- injury feedback.

Ancient material reward must survive normally:
- plant;
- preserve;
- seal;
- bury;
- inherit;
- institutionalize.

This converts time itself into a resource.

Later true-body / matter transfer can remain optional far-horizon escalation,
but should NOT be needed for the core book.

# 10. Why layout becomes satisfying

A "layout" is not:
> protagonist edits a past menu.

It is:
> protagonist builds a causal chain strong enough to survive into the present.

Examples:

### Resource layout
ancient healer plants a spirit strain
→ lineage maintains it
→ modern botanical institution unknowingly preserves descendant strain
→ protagonist earns institutional access and retrieves / studies it.

### Relationship layout
young founder leaves a private instruction
→ generations preserve it
→ modern lineage must grant audience when a matching mark appears.

### Institutional layout
protagonist influences one founding clause
→ it quietly shapes an institution for centuries
→ modern protagonist later invokes / collides with that rule.

### Disaster layout
protagonist cannot stop one major event directly
→ seeds people / methods / shelters / alternate supply lines across multiple eras
→ centuries later those redundancies are what make the final crisis survivable.

# 14. Why this beats "touch relic = teleport"

It binds travel to:
- current protagonist desire;
- modern social access;
- a concrete problem;
- recurring relationships;
- later historical layout.

Every modern climb changes:
> what causal chains he can genuinely participate in.

Therefore deeper historical access is earned through story,
not handed out by level-up UI.

# 15. Benchmark-function comparison

## Stable portal / physical crossing works
Benefit:
> instantly understandable and easy to repeat.

Risk:
> can become fixed second map / logistics.

Our borrow:
> repeated access must be legible.

Our change:
> no universal fixed gate; each living causal chain can become a gate.

## Bounded future/dream access works
Benefit:
> limited window preserves anticipation and mystery.

Our borrow:
> short causal windows / compressed modern time.

Our change:
> interaction can mature into real reciprocal relationships.

## Reset / carryover works
Benefit:
> next use inherits previous state.

Our borrow:
> relationships, marks, preserved resources and institutions persist.

Our change:
> state accumulates across eras rather than repeated same-life resets.
