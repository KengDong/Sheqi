# REBOOT-V4｜X8 溯源式穿越规则 V1

date: 2026-09-26
branch: reboot-v4-dualworld-breakout-framework
status: AUTHOR REVIEW / OPERATIONAL RULESET

# 0. Design goal

The travel rule must serve X8's core:
> ancient side builds cross-era relationship / patron network;
> modern side uses matured source knowledge, favors and resources to climb from low social access.

Avoid:
- arbitrary portal;
- all-day ancient-world residency;
- unrestricted item transport;
- choosing any famous historical person from a menu;
- history as a static strategy guide;
- timer UI / RPG cooldown language.

# 1. Modern-world substrate｜道痕 / 源流 already exists publicly

In modern mass-cultivation civilization, techniques, artifacts and lineages naturally preserve:
- Dao traces;
- creator signatures;
- version lineage;
- cultivation imprints.

Modern uses:
- authenticity;
- compatibility analysis;
- technique revision;
- relic identification;
- teaching / research.

This is ordinary infrastructure, not protagonist-only lore.

# 2. Protagonist anomaly｜溯源

When protagonist deeply synchronizes with a present object / technique / inheritance that carries a strong causal trace,
he can sometimes:
> follow that trace upstream past all surviving records
> and reach the source-era causal node.

He does NOT choose:
> "three hundred years ago, person X."

He chooses / encounters:
> a present thing he genuinely has access to.

The trace decides:
> what source person / moment can be reached.

This keeps modern-line needs tied to ancient access.

# 3. What counts as an anchor

Early viable anchors:
- a cultivation technique protagonist is personally practicing;
- a relic / tool he personally handles;
- a medicine / formula tied to his current body problem;
- a current institution's old token / founder record;
- a lineage method he has been officially exposed to.

Not enough:
- reading a name in an encyclopedia;
- knowing someone famous existed;
- seeing a public statue.

Rule:
> no personal contact with the surviving cause → no travel.

# 4. First entry

Protagonist is working on a current modern bottleneck.

He synchronizes deeply with the technique / relic.

Instead of receiving only:
> known trace / stored record,

he falls through to:
> the source moment when that trace is being created.

At first he does NOT understand this as literal past.

The ancient person does NOT understand him as future traveler.

# 5. Form of travel｜source-body / projection

Recommended early implementation:
> protagonist's consciousness is projected upstream and forms a temporary local body.

Reader experience:
> it feels like physically entering the ancient world.

Properties:
- can speak;
- can touch;
- can eat / test / practice locally;
- can be injured;
- uses protagonist's own cultivation understanding / general body level;
- body collapses on forced return.

Why use this instead of physical body:
1. no easy commodity smuggling;
2. current body remains in modern world;
3. ancient physical resources must survive through history to be claimed;
4. damage can create soul / cognitive consequences without instant permanent death;
5. later "true body crossing" can remain a far-horizon upgrade if ever needed.

Do not over-explain "projection body" in Ch1.
Ancient people may interpret it differently.

# 6. Modern body during travel

Modern body remains:
> in deep meditation / low-response state.

Therefore:
- protagonist needs a safe place;
- being externally disturbed can force return;
- extended absence is risky;
- school / family / roommates make all-day travel impractical.

This grounds anti-addiction in ordinary life.

# 7. Primary anti-addiction rule｜one deep source-trip per recovery cycle

Early:
> one deep source-trip significantly exhausts spirit / cognition.

Symptoms:
- headache;
- sensory lag;
- poor focus;
- cultivation precision drops.

A normal sleep / recovery period is required before another stable trip.

No visible "23:59 cooldown".

Reader-facing:
> he learns quickly that forcing a second trip the same day makes both worlds blur and risks real damage.

Later progression:
- stronger soul / cultivation shortens recovery;
- can maintain more complex anchors;
but never removes all cost.

# 8. Secondary anti-addiction rule｜the more you change, the faster the anchor breaks

A source-trip exists because:
> the present anchor still points cleanly to its source.

Observation / learning:
> connection can remain stable longer.

Strong intervention:
- changes the technique;
- saves someone;
- redirects a relationship;
- prevents a death;

causes:
> the present causal trace that brought protagonist there to stop matching.

Therefore:
> the connection destabilizes and ejects him.

This gives a natural session ending.

Important:
> changing history is literally what burns the current doorway.

# 9. Reconnection

After return:
- modern downstream state rewrites;
- old anchor may change or disappear;
- protagonist must find the NEW surviving trace to reconnect.

Sometimes:
- same person leaves a stronger new trace;
- a new artifact / text / lineage appears;
- another linked person becomes the next reachable source.

This creates:
> intervention → modern consequence → new anchor → next interaction.

No arbitrary mission list.

# 10. Time model｜causal nodes, not continuous clock tourism

Protagonist is not free-roaming the entire past timeline.

He reaches:
> source moments that remain strongly connected to the modern anchor.

Therefore next contact may be:
- hours later in ancient time;
- days later;
- months later;
- occasionally years later.

He cannot select the exact date.

From ancient actors' perspective:
> the mysterious visitor may disappear for unpredictable intervals.

This supports:
- people growing independently;
- investments maturing;
- relationship history;
- future founders becoming stronger over book arcs.

# 11. Why current history can already show their later outcomes

Operational model:
> each upstream change creates / selects a new downstream branch.
> the modern world settles into that branch immediately.

Protagonist is the only stable memory anchor across branch changes.

Do not explain multiverse / block-universe theory early.

Reader only needs:
1. he changed a source event;
2. present records / techniques / institutions differ;
3. everyone else remembers the new version;
4. he remembers both.

# 12. Causal inertia｜why one word does not rewrite the whole planet

No mystical "history correction force" needed.

Use normal causal structure:
- one person's choice first changes that person;
- then close relationships;
- then technique / small group;
- only repeated accumulated changes alter large institutions.

Large modern outcomes have many independent causes.

Therefore:
> small intervention → local downstream difference;
> repeated network-level intervention → institution-scale difference.

This prevents butterfly-effect nonsense without a game rule.

# 13. Matter transfer

Early hard rule:
> no nonliving matter jumps directly between eras.

What crosses:
- consciousness;
- learned understanding;
- memory;
- injuries / mental strain as feedback.

What does NOT:
- pills;
- spirit stones;
- guns;
- books;
- artifacts.

Ancient material returns only by surviving normally:
> seal it, bury it, plant it, preserve it, pass it down, leave a token / instruction.

This is the foundation of:
> reverse inheritance / time-compound returns.

# 14. Technique / cultivation transfer

Do NOT auto-copy cultivation.

Protagonist can bring back:
- understanding;
- body-memory impression;
- source logic;
- new method.

But modern body must:
> practice it himself.

Likewise ancient actors receiving a future mature method must:
> understand / test / adapt it.

No free realm sharing.

# 15. Risk

## Mild
- exhaustion;
- temporary sensory overlap;
- bad modern training performance.

## Medium
- source-body injury causes soul pain / memory fragmentation;
- one anchor becomes inaccessible for a period.

## Severe
If source-body is destroyed during a violently destabilized connection:
- forced return;
- substantial spiritual injury;
- potentially lose the ability to reconnect to that causal chain until healed.

No early permanent death requirement.

# 16. Anchor capacity

Early:
> protagonist can maintain only one or two active source relationships reliably.

Reason:
- each changed source creates a different downstream memory map;
- too many unstable chains create cognitive interference.

Reader-facing symptom:
> names / old-and-new versions start crossing in his memory.

Progression:
- stronger mind / better notes / modern training lets him handle a wider network.

This prevents:
> collecting 30 future founders in 20 chapters.

# 17. Modern-world secrecy

Why doesn't protagonist prove time travel immediately?

Because after branch rewrite:
- public records;
- databases;
- manuals;
- institutional memories

all match the new branch.

Only protagonist retains old-version memory.

Therefore:
> he can prove unusual source knowledge,
not "yesterday the whole world was different."

This naturally creates:
> experts think he has a hidden inheritance / source lineage,
not that time travel is obviously real.

# 18. Ancient trust

Trust ladder:

1. anomaly:
   > ghost / hidden senior / strange projection / dangerous thing.

2. impossible source knowledge:
   protagonist shows a mature form of something not yet created.

3. falsifiable downstream fact:
   near-term event / later known consequence.

4. bounded honesty:
   protagonist openly admits missing records / uncertainty.

5. repeated changed outcome:
   ancient actor changes a choice; next contact protagonist can describe how downstream changed.

6. referral:
   first trusted actor introduces protagonist to another.

Trust spreads through:
> reputation network,
not everyone independently believing "future traveler".

# 19. Anti-abuse examples

## Why not find every future founder?
Need a present anchor protagonist can genuinely touch / practice / access.

## Why not stay in ancient world forever?
Modern body remains vulnerable; spirit exhausts; one trip per recovery cycle early.

## Why not bring a truckload of modern goods?
Matter does not cross.

## Why not grab ancient treasures and return?
Matter does not cross; must preserve them through time.

## Why not warn everyone of every disaster?
Strong changes burn the anchor and rewrite the records he relied on.

## Why not use future history as perfect answer sheet?
Using the answer changes the downstream branch, so the old answer expires.

## Why not change one small event and become emperor in the present?
Causal inertia; institution-scale effects require accumulated network changes.

## Why not collect endless ancient contacts?
Limited active-anchor / memory capacity.

# 20. Progression ladder of the travel ability

## Phase 1｜单一溯源
- one technique / one source person;
- short visits;
- severe recovery cost.

## Phase 2｜关系锚
- can reconnect through a person / promise / lineage trace,
not only original technique.

## Phase 3｜网络溯源
- can follow introductions / connected source nodes;
- ancient investment network begins.

## Phase 4｜主动召引
- a highly trusted ancient actor can create a source trace specifically to call protagonist.
This makes ancient side capable of initiating plot.

## Phase 5｜institutional source
- protagonist can reach source moments behind larger organizations / widespread techniques.

Optional far horizon:
> short true-body crossing / two-way physical meeting.
Do not need it early.

# 21. Benchmark-function integration

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
Official Qidian listing records 2026-09-01 launch, first subscription 6691; premise uses direct physical cross-world travel and immediate identity revaluation.

Function retained:
> contact with other world must rapidly change current options / identity.

X8 distinction:
> no unrestricted physical goods loop;
> source access is tied to modern causal anchors and burns when heavily changed.

## 《我有一个修仙世界》
Official Qidian material shows the modern line begins with school records, exam consequences and paid spiritual-energy access; 2024 Twelve Heavenly Kings list classifies it as a two-world cultivation work.

Function retained:
> current modern pressure must remain active even while second world exists.

## 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》
Project benchmark + current Qidian honor confirm it reached 十万均订 in 2026.

Function retained:
> every repeat acts on a changed state; earlier outcomes alter later options.

## 《天才俱乐部》
Qidian public lists describe a repeated bounded access to another temporal layer and confirm ten-wan average subscription status.

Function retained:
> bounded access windows can create anticipation, and actions must change the later information state.

Do not copy:
- dream;
- exact daily reset;
- specific temporal mystery.

# 22. Recommended concise reader-facing rules

By Ch10 reader should only need these five:

1. **他只能顺着现世还存在的“源流”回去。**
2. **一次只能待一阵，回来以后得缓。**
3. **改得越狠，这次入口断得越快。**
4. **东西不能直接带回来，只能让它活过三百年。**
5. **他一改过去，现世就换成新的版本，只有他记得旧的。**

Everything else can be revealed later.

# 23. Current verdict

Recommended mechanism:
> **因果锚 / 溯源式投身**

Why it fits X8:
- every ancient trip originates from a modern need;
- no merchant collapse;
- natural anti-addiction;
- recurring people can grow independently;
- history-changing itself ends sessions;
- resources must compound through time;
- modern and ancient lines remain causally interlocked.

Main open choice:
> whether reader-facing term should ever be “溯源”, or protagonist simply describes the phenomenon informally until a later specialist gives it a name.
