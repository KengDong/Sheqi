# 1. Benchmark Function Map

## 1.1 《捞尸人》

> 死亡 / 禁忌职业只是入口，真正长期吸引是：别人不敢进入的死亡世界，主角能进入、识别、处理，并逐步获得更高的接近权、处理权和身份秘密。

Borrow:
- death-handling competence;
- forbidden access;
- professional authority that grows;
- death world becomes larger than first job.

Do NOT copy:
- specific民俗事件;
-水鬼 / 捞尸事件;
-人物关系 / 世界设定.

A3 extension:
> 从处理死者，扩大到处理“已经死亡却仍被修真文明强行使用”的器物、土地、灵脉、道统、区域、最终天道。

---

## 1.2 《1984：从川菜馆开始》

Observed market function:
> 小事件当场有感官 / 生活奖励，并让第二天真实变化；熟客、收入、店铺、关系都会回返。

- result changes tomorrow;

Forbidden:
> 送终 → 系统弹奖励 → 下一个地点.

## 1.3 《没钱修什么仙？》

Observed market function:

Borrow:
- system-level contradiction;
- daily life visibly demonstrates the larger system;
- no single villain can solve the problem.

A3 extension:
> “全民修仙”是真正的繁荣，同时也是天地衰亡的原因。

The tragedy:
> everyone is rational at local scale and collectively killing the world.

They:
- recover naturally;
- remember protagonist;
- produce people / resources / knowledge that return later;
- eventually connect into a visible world-scale cycle repair network.

This is NOT territory ownership.
It is:
> proof that the world can be repaired without continuing extraction.

   > 旧送终节点以后继续工作。
6. scale:
   > 主角处理的“死物”越来越大。
7. long mystery:
   > 天道为什么会死？全民修仙为何走到今天？

# 4. World Model｜全民修仙盛世

## 4.1 Surface

This is not a dying wasteland at opening.

It looks prosperous.

- children learn basic qi refinement;
- towns use formations;
- sects cultivate industrial-scale spirit fields;
- medicine / tools / transport increasingly depend on spiritual energy;
- cultivators live longer than historical generations;
- breakthroughs become more accessible.

This is important:
> the crisis hides inside success.

If the world already looks apocalyptic,
the systemic reveal loses force.

## 4.2 Underlying life-cycle rule

Heaven and Earth have a simple natural cycle:

> 生 → 借天地灵机成长 → 死 → 遗余归还天地 → 再生.

Cultivation civilization perfected:
> **取。**

But gradually lost / suppressed:
> **还。**

Therefore two things happen simultaneously:

1. living cultivators extract faster;
2. dead objects / dead systems do not properly return their remaining vitality.

The world is being attacked from both ends.

## 4.3 Why universal cultivation makes it worse

One elite cultivator is not enough to kill Heaven.

The current civilization is dangerous because:
> cultivation has become infrastructure.

Everyone extracting a little,
every tool / city / field consuming a little,
creates civilization-scale debt.

This makes the crisis analogous in structural function to 《没钱修什么仙？》:
> each individual's local choice can be reasonable while the total system becomes destructive.

## 4.4 Observable environmental degradation

Early:
- spirit crops mature faster but die earlier;
- old tools break earlier than records predict;
- spirit beasts lose stable bloodlines;
- wells / springs show sudden exhaustion;
- weather around high-consumption regions becomes unstable.

Middle:
- local laws fail;
- cultivation backlash increases;
- “死地” appear where regeneration no longer works;
- regions fight over remaining high-quality veins.

Late:
- heavenly tribulations become more violent;
- some Dao laws intermittently stop functioning;
- day/night / seasonal / spatial anomalies appear;
- Heaven itself begins reclaiming power.

# 5. System｜万物送终

The system must be:
> easy to understand like a commercial system novel,
but not a mission menu.

## 5.1 What the system shows

When protagonist truly contacts a dying / dead-but-unreturned thing:

### 寿
> whether it is alive / dying / dead / forcibly sustained.

### 残因
> the unresolved causal knot preventing proper ending.

### 归处
> the direction in which its remaining value should return.

Important:
> the system does NOT tell the full cause, culprit, or exact solution.

It gives:
> diagnosis,
not walkthrough.

This preserves competence and mystery.

## 5.2 What the system does NOT do

No:
- daily mission;
- map marker;
- “go bury X”;
- shop;
- lottery;
- arbitrary skill drops.

The protagonist chooses:
- what to investigate;
- whether to intervene;
- who deserves help;
- what can be saved;
- what must be allowed to die.

## 5.3 Settlement

A correct send-off produces three kinds of result.

### A. 归还
Most remaining vitality returns to:
- land;
- descendants;
- ecology;
- lineage;
- local cycle.

This is the main world reward.

### B. 遗泽
A concrete residue stays in reality.

Examples:
- usable seed;
- repaired spring;
- inheritance memory;
- stabilized node;
- descendant bloodline;
- safe location.

It must matter later.

### C. 余荫
The protagonist receives a small “cycle resonance”.

It does NOT steal lifespan from the dead.

It is created because:
> a broken cycle was correctly closed.

Use:
- cultivates protagonist's normal realm;
- tempers the three system authorities below.

No generic points.

# 6. Protagonist Growth｜Two-layer system

To remain commercially readable:
> normal cultivation + unique death authority.

## 6.1 Normal cultivation

The protagonist still participates in familiar cultivation society.

He needs:
- qi;
- body;
- techniques;
- combat competence.

Reason:
> he cannot handle a dead mountain if any random cultivator can kill him.

This keeps the book inside mainstream cultivation pleasure.

## 6.2 Unique authority A｜观终

Early:
> sees terminal status.

Then:
> sees what is forcibly keeping dead things running.

Later:
> sees life-flow between large systems.

End:
> can read Heaven's remaining lifespan / terminal structure.

## 6.3 Unique authority B｜归还

Early:
> return remaining vitality of a small object.

Middle:
> redirect legacy to descendants / land / lineage.

Later:
> restore regional circulation.

End:
> decide how the remainder of a dead Heaven returns to the next world cycle.

## 6.4 Unique authority C｜送终权

This is not combat level.

It is:
> what scale of “death” the world recognizes him as qualified to close.

Escalation:
- 器;
- 生灵;
- 地;
- 脉;
- 宗;
- 域;
- 界;
- 天.

Names can change later.
Function is fixed:
> access and authority rise together.

This borrows from 《捞尸人》 / 《以神通之名》:
> skill becomes social / institutional / metaphysical handling authority.

# 7. Daily Story Engine

Every good A3 mini-arc must include six beats.

## Beat 1｜Everyone sees a usable asset or useless trash

Not always a monster.

Examples:
- a still-functioning cracked tool;
- a declining spirit field;
- a town formation;
- a lineage;
- a spirit beast;
- a spring.

## Beat 2｜Protagonist sees “it is already dead”

Instant distinction.

## Beat 3｜Someone has a rational reason to keep using it

This is essential.

Not idiot villains.

Examples:
- town cannot survive without the formation;
- sect cannot afford new spirit field;
- family identity depends on old inheritance;
- poor cultivators need cheap recycled tools.

## Beat 4｜Protagonist finds the unresolved causal knot

Mystery / forbidden access.

## Beat 5｜Send-off causes immediate visible consequence

Not just system reward.

The environment / person / institution changes now.

## Beat 6｜Something remains

At least one:
- place;
- relationship;
- resource;
- right;
- reputation;
- clue;
- returning person.

No reset.

# 8. First 20 Chapters｜Commercial Proof

Not full chapter outline.
Four compact story units.

## Ch1–3｜“已死三年，仍在服役”

Opening role:
> lowest worker in sect mortuary / corpse hall.

The corpse hall uses an old spiritual tool to suppress corpse qi.

Everyone trusts it because:
> it still functions.

System appears:
> **已死三年。仍在服役。**

Ch1:
- forced use causes abnormal corpse movement;
- protagonist realizes “functioning” ≠ alive.

Ch2:
- administrator attempts emergency repair because replacing it is unaffordable;
- protagonist sees the unresolved causal knot;
- he argues for closing / sending it off.

Ch3:
- correct send-off stops a larger corpse backlash;
- the tool's remaining force does not become protagonist loot;
- it returns to stabilize the corpse hall's ground / seals;
- protagonist receives first 余荫;
- one skeptical but competent senior changes how they treat him.

First screen delivers:
- system;
- competence;
- social verification;
- world rule;
- small personal growth.

End sting:
> the system identifies a second “dead thing” that is far bigger than an object.

## Ch4–8｜死田

A spirit field is still producing crops but at worsening quality.

Owners keep pouring qi into it because:
> dozens of outer disciples depend on it.

Protagonist discovers:
> soil-cycle has already collapsed.

The correct ending requires:
- sacrificing the last crop;
- returning accumulated residue to the soil.

Immediate payoff:
- the current crop is lost;
- nearby ordinary fields recover;
- a few seeds inherit concentrated stable vitality.

Persistent returns:
- one disciple family that hated protagonist later survives because of restored land;
- the recovered plot becomes first visible “归还节点”.

This is the 《1984》 lesson:
> today's action changes later life.

## Ch9–14｜死阵

A small town's defense formation is technically working.

It is also:
> draining residents' lifespan to compensate for a dead core.

The town leaders know something is wrong,
but shutting it down exposes everyone to external danger.

This is first hard moral conflict.

Protagonist must not simply say:
> stop using it.

He must find:
> a temporary alternative + correct closure.

This begins to prove:
> society cannot quit cultivation overnight.

Persistent return:
- town becomes recurring ally / evidence site;
- a technical cultivator who opposed protagonist becomes a recurring relationship.

## Ch15–20｜第一条死灵脉

The local sect's minor vein is kept alive through intensive extraction / infusion.

System:
> **已进入真死。**

If simply cut:
> sect economy collapses.

If force-fed:
> it will corpse-transform and poison surrounding land.

This is first large send-off.

During the process protagonist discovers:
> this vein should have lived decades longer.

End Ch20:
> he finds the first cross-case pattern proving multiple things are dying prematurely.

Macro signal:
> **天地寿数：异常缩短。**

Do NOT show full Heaven obituary yet.

Ch20 reader now has:
- repeated daily pleasure;
- first regional scale;
- recurring relationships;
- system growth;
- world mystery.

# 9. Ch20–100 Escalation

## 20–40｜“为什么东西都死早了？”

Travel grows organically.

People seek him because:
> he can judge whether expensive cultivation assets are worth saving.

Cases diversify:
- beast lineage;
- inheritance;
- old ferry;
- sect tool;
- village spirit tree.

Important:
> not all should be sent off.
Some are still alive and can be saved.

This prevents formula:
> protagonist always says “bury it”.

## 40–60｜第一次碰到“死掉的道统”

A lineage can die even if disciples remain.

Symptoms:
- techniques produce diminishing returns;
- disciples increasingly injure themselves;
- everyone keeps teaching the same system because identity depends on it.

Protagonist must decide:
> preserve the name, or bury the old path and let successors build something new.

This expands death from object → social system.

## 60–80｜大城灵网

A city-scale spiritual network is failing.

Millions depend on it.

This arc proves:
> environmental reform has massive human cost.

Opposition becomes rational and large.

The protagonist cannot solve by:
> “turn it off”.

He must use old归还 nodes / allies / restored resources to construct a transition.

This is where previous small cases pay back.

## 80–100｜第一次看到“天寿账”

Multiple recovered archives / system observations show:

> world consumption > regeneration.

The protagonist sees a new system layer:
> **天地剩余寿数 is not fixed; civilization's choices can accelerate / slow it.**

At Ch100:
- he is known beyond one sect;
- his role is controversial;
- he has a visible network of repaired / closed nodes;
- mainstream cultivation institutions begin to notice him;
- Heaven crisis is undeniable but not yet understood.

# 11. Core Mystery｜Why the cycle broke

Do not reveal early.

Working answer:

Cultivation itself is ancient and natural.

The disaster began when an ancient civilization discovered how to:
> **retain what should return after death.**

This was initially a miracle:
- inherit more power;
- preserve dead veins;
- extend tools;
- maintain sects;
- defeat mortality.

Over generations:
> “not returning” became civilization.

Everyone forgot death was part of the resource cycle.

The world's “death function” was gradually suppressed / fragmented.

The protagonist's system is:
> **the lost return / ending function of the world.**

Why does it look like a system?

Because protagonist is an outsider soul.

The lost world-law translates itself into:
> a framework he can understand.

This makes transmigration functional,
not decorative.

# 12. Ascension Reveal

This should be a major mid/late-stage reversal.

Public belief:
> cultivation culminates in ascension to a higher world.

Hidden function:
> dying Heaven has begun reclaiming concentrated cultivation through ascenders.

Not necessarily malicious.

Heaven is dying and doing the equivalent of:
> emergency organ recycling.

This is important:
> Heaven is not a villain.

It is also trying to survive.

Therefore late conflict is not:
> kill evil Heaven.

It becomes:
- continue feeding Heaven and preserve current world a little longer;
- end mass cultivation and collapse civilization;
- allow Heaven to die and risk cosmic chaos;
- build a new return cycle and perform a controlled funeral.

# 13. Opposition｜No idiot villains

Belief:

Reason:
- cities depend on old formations;
- poor regions cannot replace veins;
- ordinary people need cheap spiritual infrastructure.

They are often right in the short term.

## 13.2 大宗 / high-consumption core regions

They know peripheral regions are degrading,
but believe:
> concentrating resources in strong centers preserves civilization.

Not cartoon evil.

## 13.3 停修派

When truth emerges:
> stop cultivation immediately.

Problem:
> mass starvation / infrastructure failure / security collapse may follow.

They offer morally clean but socially brutal answers.

## 13.4 葬天派

Want to accelerate Heaven's death and loot the corpse / seize new order.

They exploit protagonist's discoveries.

## 13.5 Heaven itself

Late pressure:
> the system of tribulations / ascension / reclamation becomes more aggressive as Heaven weakens.

Not human-minded villain.
