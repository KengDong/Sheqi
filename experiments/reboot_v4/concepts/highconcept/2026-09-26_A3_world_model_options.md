# REBOOT-V4｜A3 World Model Options

date: 2026-09-26
branch: reboot-v4-fanqie-market-entry
status: AUTHOR DISCUSSION / NO FINAL CHOICE

concept:
> A3《全民修仙把天道吸干了，我来给它办丧事》

# Governing rule

World model is not chosen for novelty alone.

It must maximize:
- shelf clarity;
- daily story generation;
- systemic contradiction;
- cultivation pleasure;
- escalation to Heaven funeral;
- newcomer readability;
while preserving current hot-market mother genres.

# Option 1｜现代全民修仙 / 资本修真社会

Reference functions:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》
- current modern cultivation / cultivation-civilization works

Surface:
- schools / universities / companies / hospitals / public infrastructure all use cultivation;
- artificial roots / standardized techniques / spiritual-energy utilities;
- cultivation is mass education and mass industry.

A3 advantage:
- “everyone is consuming Heaven” becomes literal and easy to understand;
- can write corporate, civic, household, school, hospital, city-grid death cases;
- systemic contradiction is strongest.

Risk:
- too close in flavor to 《没钱修什么仙？》 if finance / platform / loans dominate;
- modern terms can reduce mythic / xianxia atmosphere;
- world-ending scale must not feel like climate-change allegory with cultivation skin.

Best A3 version:
> prosperous cultivation civilization that resembles modern society structurally, but uses original institutions and aesthetics rather than copying contemporary China one-to-one.

# Option 2｜传统宗门修仙世界

Reference functions:
- 《天渊》
- 《从金丹仙族开始的老祖之路！》
- traditional xianxia / xuanhuan long-form market

Surface:
- sects / clans / immortal cities / spirit veins / secret realms;
- cultivation resources visibly come from land and Heaven;
- hierarchy is familiar.

A3 advantage:
- “灵脉 / 山川 / 道统 / 天道 death” feels native and mythic;
- funeral aesthetics naturally fit;
- very easy to preserve cultivation atmosphere.

Risk:
- “全民修仙把天道吸干” is less literal if only a small elite cultivates;
- systemic crisis can look like ordinary spirit-qi decline;
- daily cases may drift into classic sect missions.

Best A3 version:
> do not use a sparse classical world; use a highly mature late-stage traditional cultivation civilization where cultivation has spread deeply into ordinary production and governance.

# Option 3｜双界：成熟现代修真文明 + 古老传统修仙界

Reference function:
- current new-book example 《原来我是仙道天骄》 uses a modern/capitalized cultivation world plus an ancient cultivation world.

Surface:
- protagonist crosses between two worlds;
- one world has industrialized cultivation;
- one preserves older life-cycle / burial knowledge.

A3 advantage:
- natural comparison experiment:
  > which world is dying faster, and why?
- resources / knowledge from one world can solve problems in the other;
- reader gets both modern and classical cultivation pleasures.

Major A3 high-concept:
> modern world has perfected extraction;
> ancient world is poorer but still preserves fragments of “return after death”.
> protagonist slowly learns neither world is complete.

Risk:
- two complete world systems double exposition and continuity burden;
- can dilute the simple promise “I send off dead things”;
- easy to become two-world trading / arbitrage novel.

Best A3 version:
> only use two worlds if the second world is indispensable to the Heaven-death mystery, not merely a resource warehouse.

# Option 4｜仙朝 / 国家化修真文明

Reference functions:
- 《以神通之名》: cultivation ability enters institutions / duties / public action;
- current cultivation-civilization new-book examples use府学、仙朝、multi-world governance.

Surface:
- immortal court / prefectures / academies / official spirit-vein bureaus;
- mountains, rivers and formations are public infrastructure;
- cultivators hold registered offices and legal rights.

A3 advantage:
- protagonist's “送终权” can grow from low-level undertaker to officially recognized authority;
- city / river / regional cases become natural;
- systemic contradiction can be political without becoming pure modern parody.

Strong identity arc:
> 义庄杂役 → 送终吏 → 掌脉官 / 天葬使 → someone with legal authority to close a sect / region / Heaven-system.

Risk:
- bureaucracy / process can become dull;
- requires consistent state structure;
- may shift attention from cultivation fantasy to official procedures.

Best A3 version:
> institutions should certify consequences, not become meeting-room content.

# Option 5｜灵气复苏后的“第二代成熟社会”

Reference functions:
- modern spiritual-revival stories;
- 《没钱修什么仙？》 proves mass cultivation can carry institutional pressure.

Surface:
- not the first day of revival;
- revival happened 100–300 years ago;
- modern Earth has already rebuilt around cultivation;
- old technology and cultivation infrastructure coexist.

A3 advantage:
- readers intuitively understand how an entire planet industrializes spiritual energy;
- archaeology of “pre-revival Earth” can reveal what nature looked like before extraction;
- strong contrast between historical environmental records and current accelerated death.

Risk:
- if too Earth-like, book becomes urban supernatural rather than xianxia;
- geopolitics / real-country analogues create unnecessary baggage;
- “天道” may feel less mythic.

Best A3 version:
> fictional Earth-like civilization rather than direct contemporary Earth.

# Option 6｜修仙工业革命 / 灵能都市群

Surface:
- cultivation technology has just crossed from elite craft to mass production;
- factories refine qi, artificial veins power cities, standardized pills expand population.

A3 advantage:
- crisis unfolds while industrialization is visibly accelerating;
- protagonist can witness “the decision that kills Heaven” rather than only inherit it;
- extremely strong causal clarity.

Risk:
- less directly validated as a broad market chassis;
- can become engineering / factory fiction;
- world may feel designed around thesis rather than fantasy.

Best use:
> as a historical stage inside Option 1 or 4 rather than standalone novelty chassis.

# Option 7｜仙朝统御多界 / 位面殖民修仙

Surface:
- home Heaven is overconsumed;
- civilization begins mining other worlds' spirit veins;
- “saving our Heaven” becomes justification for draining others.

A3 advantage:
- massive escalation after Ch100;
- exposes the moral consequence of refusing to fix the cycle;
- world funeral can scale into “civilization funeral”.

Risk:
- too large too early;
- destroys intimate daily send-off pleasure if introduced before readers care about one village / one spring;
- easy to become generic plane conquest.

Best use:
> late-stage expansion, not opening world.

# Option 8｜盛世末期的古典修真文明 (“看上去传统，运行逻辑已现代化”)

Surface:
- robes / sects / immortal cities / sword flight / spirit fields;
- but cultivation has become mass infrastructure:
  - public spirit wells;
  - standardized low-level techniques;
  - sect-operated transport;
  - city formations;
  - mass-produced pills / tools;
  - ordinary households depend on spiritual networks.

A3 advantage:
- retains traditional xianxia imagery;
- still makes “全民修仙” literal enough to create civilization-scale extraction;
- death objects (spirit vein / old formation / sect lineage) feel native;
- avoids directly shadowing 《没钱修什么仙？》's smartphones / loans / corporations.

Risk:
- requires careful world design so mass cultivation feels organic rather than modern society with robes;
- must explain why ordinary people can cultivate enough to matter.

A3 special potential:
> the reader first thinks this is familiar traditional xianxia, then gradually realizes the whole civilization is functioning like an industrial consumption machine.

# Key decision questions for AUTHOR

1. Do we want A3's daily pleasure to feel more like:
   - familiar modern life made strange by cultivation?
   - or ancient xianxia life revealing a hidden systemic rot?

2. When the protagonist walks into a new place, which image is more exciting?
   - cultivation university / hospital / city spiritual grid;
   - immortal town / spirit field / ancestral formation / old sect;
   - official immortal court / river-and-mountain bureau?

3. Should the protagonist's rise feel mainly like:
   - outsider → expert / public figure;
   - low servant → respected cultivator / funeral master;
   - low clerk → holder of world-recognized handling authority?

4. Do we want “天道 is dying” to feel:
   - like discovering civilization's energy debt;
   - like uncovering an ancient cosmic taboo;
   - or both?
