# Benchmark prerequisite

This version is generated only after explicitly benchmarking:

- 《没钱修什么仙？》
  - modern cultivation society continuously prices / pressures protagonist;
  - protagonist's growth changes bargaining power and social position.
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》
  - two worlds work because the same protagonist has radically different value / options in each;
  - every crossing creates immediate status / resource asymmetry.
- 《天渊》
  - traditional cultivation still opens with concrete personal conflict rather than world exposition.
- 《从水猴子开始成神》
  - a unique underpriced domain gives protagonist a repeatable advantage lane and visible endpoint.
- 《以神通之名》
  - competence becomes real handling authority and public consequence.
- 《剑烛大荒》
  - spectacle, capability and life/world scale rise together.

# A｜Single-world Modern Mass Cultivation

## Core benchmark combination

《没钱修什么仙？》
+
《捞尸人》
+
《以神通之名》

Borrowed function:
> society undervalues protagonist → protagonist owns a professional ability nobody else can replace → every successful handling increases both personal power and real social action rights.

## Working title

> **《全民修仙把天道吸干了，我来给它办丧事》**

## Shelf hook

> **这个世界人人靠吸天地灵气修仙，而我偏偏是个“吸不进灵气”的废灵根。直到系统告诉我：别人靠夺天地修行，我可以靠把死物该还的东西还回去修行。**

This gives protagonist a personal contradiction immediately.

## World

Cultivation is modern infrastructure:
- public qi grid;
- cultivation schools;
- spirit hospitals;
- artificial roots;
- spirit-powered transport;
- city-scale formations.

But do NOT copy 《没钱修什么仙？》's finance / loan vocabulary as the main texture.

The world's defining technological belief is:
> **只要还能运行，就值得继续续。**

Maintenance / life-extension technology has become civilization's pride.

## Protagonist opening position

He has an extremely low-efficiency root.

Everyone else:
> draws spiritual energy from the public grid.

He:
> can barely absorb it.

That means:
- cultivation school ranking low;
- normal cultivation careers closed;
- only qualifies for hazardous low-level spiritual-infrastructure retirement / disposal work.

He is not poor because "capitalism" says so.
He is low because:
> the entire civilization measures value by how efficiently you can take qi from Heaven and Earth.

This makes A3's world contradiction personal.

## First case

An old district qi tower is still serving tens of thousands of people.

System shows:

> 【已死三年】  
> 【仍在服役】  
> 【当前运行来源：辖区生灵寿元微量代偿】

Everyone has a rational reason to keep it alive:
- hospital;
- homes;
- students;
- businesses.

Protagonist handles its true ending.

## First personal payoff

This is the new key.

When the dead tower's remaining vitality correctly returns to the underground cycle, protagonist receives:
> **余荫.**

He discovers:
> he cannot cultivate by taking qi,
> but he can cultivate from the resonance created when a broken cycle is restored.

So the book's protagonist-level fantasy becomes:

> **全世界修仙靠“拿”，只有我越“还”，修得越快。**

This is much stronger than:
> "I get small system points."

It is also a concrete alternative cultivation path.

## Repeated pleasure

Every arc can produce four simultaneous gains:

1. professional:
   > I diagnosed what nobody else could.
2. personal cultivation:
   > correct return produces余荫 / improves his unique route.
3. social position:
   > larger institutions must give him access / authority.
4. world proof:
   > another piece of evidence that civilization is draining Heaven.

## Long status line

Low hazardous worker
→ certified terminal assessor
→ person cities must consult before forcing old spiritual systems to continue
→ holder of regional closure authority
→ only person qualified to declare a Dao lineage / Heaven truly terminal.

Borrowed function from 《以神通之名》:
> ability → responsibility → real-world consequence.

## Why A now earns its modern world

Modernity is not decoration.

It creates:
- mass extraction;
- shared infrastructure dependency;
- many people rationally refusing shutdown;
- visible public controversy;
- protagonist's status climb.

If removed, the story fundamentally changes.

# D｜Two Worlds｜Modern Extraction World + Ancient Return World

## Core benchmark combination

《原来我是仙道天骄》
+
《没钱修什么仙？》
+
traditional cultivation references

Borrowed function from 《原来我是仙道天骄》:
> same protagonist must have immediately different value in two worlds.

Hard rule:
> ancient world cannot exist only to explain lore.

## Working title

Primary feel-test title:
> **《全民修仙都嫌我是废灵根，古修界却等我来给天道送终》**

Alternative retaining strongest original shelf promise:
> **《全民修仙把天道吸干了，我在古修界学会给它送终》**

## Shelf hook

> **在现代修仙界，我因为吸不进灵气只能拆报废灵器；到了古修界，他们却说：送终之人最忌夺天地灵机——你这种“废灵根”，才是失传万年的送终道体。**

This creates the immediate cross-world status inversion that previous D lacked.

## World A｜modern extraction civilization

Same principle as A:
- mass cultivation;
- everything optimized around taking qi;
- protagonist's low-absorption constitution = worthless.

What World A offers protagonist:
> enormous quantity of dead-but-forced-running systems.

It is a huge "death resource field".

He can accumulate:
- actual cases;
-余荫;
- modern repair / diagnosis knowledge;
- access to civilization-scale infrastructure.

## World B｜ancient return civilization

Not just "traditional xianxia".

Its cultivation civilization preserves remnants of an older law:
> things must return after true death.

But it has its own defect:
> it has become overly conservative and poor at renewal.

Many sects:
- bury traditions too early;
- refuse certain innovations;
- have strong funeral law but weak regeneration / production.

The protagonist's "modern" knowledge matters here because:
> he can distinguish "truly dead" from merely damaged.

So:
- World A keeps corpses alive too long.
- World B sometimes buries things that can still live.

Protagonist is the only person who can see both errors.

## First crossing

Modern first case:
> dead qi tower.

Correct send-off produces a black "return door".

He crosses.

Ancient cultivators test him.

They discover:
> he barely absorbs free spiritual qi.

Modern verdict:
> trash root.

Ancient funeral-lineage verdict:
> ideal.

Reason:
> a send-off cultivator who greedily absorbs ambient qi can contaminate the final return and steal from the dead.

Therefore protagonist's defect becomes:
> professional purity / lost qualification.

This is the D-version equivalent of:
> low-value artificial root in one world → heaven-root genius in another.

But its function is directly tied to A3's death theme.

## Immediate ancient-world payoff

Ancient world does NOT simply hand him a complete lost manual.

It gives him:
- access to real funeral rites;
- a place / lineage that recognizes his unusual qualification;
- first technique for separating "can still be saved" from "true terminal state";
- first clue that his modern system is part of a lost cosmic function.

At the same time,
his modern repair logic lets him save an ancient object / inheritance that the locals planned to bury.

Therefore within the first ancient arc:
> both worlds prove useful.

## Repeatable two-world engine

Every major problem must create at least one of these:

### Value inversion
What is worthless in A is precious in B or vice versa.

### Method inversion
A knows how to prolong / repair;
B knows how to end / return.

### Status inversion
A treats protagonist as low-level;
B increasingly treats him as a rare professional / lineage bearer.

### Truth inversion
The same phenomenon has two different historical interpretations.

This is what makes crossing itself pleasurable.

## Cross-world compounding

Example:
- learn an ancient return rite in B;
- use it to close a modern dead spirit-grid in A;
- the restored modern node produces data /余荫 that lets him prove an ancient "dead" spring in B is actually saveable;
- saving it changes his status in B and unlocks a higher-level funeral archive;
- that archive explains a larger modern infrastructure failure.

Thus:
> the worlds feed one another.

Not trade.
Not sightseeing.
Not encyclopedia.

## Long mystery

The two worlds are ultimately not independent.

Working deep truth:
> they are two incomplete successor cycles after a failed Heaven funeral.

Modern world inherited:
> growth / extraction / expansion.

Ancient world preserved:
> ending / return / memory.

Neither can produce a complete cycle alone.

The protagonist's unique route:
> grows through closing the loop between them.

## Ascension reveal

Modern ascenders disappear.

Ancient world occasionally sees:
> mysterious heavenly corpses / stripped cultivation remnants falling from spatial cracks.

The protagonist gradually proves:
> modern "ascension" is feeding the broken boundary between both cycles.

This makes the reveal physical rather than explanatory.

## Why D now earns its second world

Remove World B and the story loses:
- protagonist's status inversion;
- return-method access;
- cross-world repeated pleasure;
- half of the central Heaven mystery.

Therefore B is no longer optional lore storage.

# Direct Feel Difference

## A's promise

> **全世界都靠夺天地修仙，我这个废灵根却靠“归还”一路修上去，最后成为唯一有资格给天道判死的人。**

It is:
- cleaner;
- one-world;
- stronger social-system conflict;
- easier to serialize fast.

## D's promise

> **在一个世界我是吸不进灵气的废物，在另一个世界这个缺陷却让我成为最纯粹的送终人；我一边给现代修真文明收尸，一边从古修界找回天地遗失的另一半循环。**

It is:
- stronger identity contrast;
- stronger crossing pleasure;
- bigger mystery;
- higher complexity.

# Author feel-test

Ignore execution difficulty first.

Ask:
- Does A's "全世界靠拿，我靠还修仙" excite more?
- Or does D's "一个世界判我废，一个世界认我为送终奇才" excite more?
- If D, is the two-world crossing itself something you would look forward to every time?
