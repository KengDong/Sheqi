# REBOOT-V4｜A3 Protagonist Identity Options

date: 2026-09-26
branch: reboot-v4-fanqie-market-entry
status: AUTHOR DISCUSSION / NO FINAL PICK

concept:
> A3

# First principle

Do NOT invent a strange profession merely because it gives access to dying infrastructure.

Primary identity should borrow a proven reader-entry function from current strong works:
- public evaluation / youth transition;
- forbidden professional access;
- craft competence;
- public handling authority;
- underpriced resource domain;
- unstable social position.

The identity must answer:
1. why readers understand protagonist's pressure immediately;
2. why protagonist naturally encounters "dead-but-still-used" things;
3. where recurring relationships come from;
4. how social position can visibly rise;
5. how the identity can naturally expire / scale into larger world action.

---

# I01｜修仙高考生 / 高三学生

Benchmark function:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》
- 《刚准备高考，离婚逆袭系统来了》
- current high-martial school ladder

Immediate pressure:
> grades / qi absorption / ranking / entrance exam.

A3 access:
> school聚灵阵、药田、实训法器、宿舍灵网、考场阵法.

Recurring cast:
> classmates, teachers, parents, rivals.

Status ladder:
> poor student → special candidate → university / institution → wider society.

Strength:
> broadest lived-experience entry and easiest public verification.

Risk:
> school flavor may dominate A3; modern cultivation student is currently common.

Best fit:
> A strongest; D possible only if crossing geometry avoids 《原来我是仙道天骄》.

---

# I02｜修真大学新生 / 专业学院实习生

Benchmark function:
> school familiarity + adult specialization + immediate access to infrastructure.

Possible majors:
- 灵植;
- 阵法;
- 山河工程;
- 医修;
- 灵能维护.

A3 access:
> internship projects can touch real city / sect systems.

Recurring cast:
> roommates, classmates, mentors, internship unit.

Strength:
> larger world than high school; protagonist can travel earlier.

Risk:
> if “职业技术学院 + modern cultivation” then too close to 《原来我是仙道天骄》.

Use only if:
> college field is broad and not the key gimmick.

---

# I03｜义庄 / 宗门收殓堂的年轻学徒

Benchmark function:
> 《捞尸人》 forbidden death access + traditional xianxia low-status entry.

Immediate pressure:
> low-status dirty work nobody wants.

A3 access:
> corpses, broken spirit tools, failed formations, unclaimed relics, dead beasts naturally arrive.

Recurring cast:
> old undertaker / corpse-hall elder / families of dead / sect administrators.

Status ladder:
> unwanted apprentice → trusted death-handler → sects / cities request him → Heaven funeral.

Strength:
> title premise and death aesthetics fit naturally.

Risk:
> narrower reader self-insertion; easy to become weekly weird-case fiction.

Best fit:
> traditional / immortal-court version; D's return-world side.

---

# I04｜祖传小铺 / 小道观 / 送终世家的年轻继承人

Benchmark function:
- 《1984》 small place + competence + returning customers;
- 《捞尸人》 family / profession access;
- ownership / long-memory fantasy.

Opening:
> family business is dying; nobody believes old funeral practices matter in mass-cultivation society.

A3 access:
> people bring failed tools / strange deaths / old rites to the shop.

Persistent asset:
> same shop /道观 becomes a long-term home base.

Recurring cast:
> family, neighbors, repeat clients, rival modern institutions.

Strength:
> gives A3 a “home” and compounding relationship asset.

Risk:
> can feel like shop/case-of-week; must expand beyond customers quickly.

Best fit:
> modern-classical hybrid world; A could use it instead of student.

---

# I05｜灵农 / 灵田家庭的年轻人

Benchmark function:
- 《从水猴子开始成神》 underpriced resource domain;
- 《1984》 life improvement / tangible production.

Opening pressure:
> family depends on one spirit field / well that is failing.

A3 first discovery:
> field is “dead but still producing”.

Natural access:
> soil, water, spirit beasts, local veins, seasonal formations.

Status ladder:
> family survival → village / local agriculture → regional resource cycle → Heaven-Earth ecology.

Strength:
> the world crisis becomes immediately physical and understandable;
> strong “things getting better” payoff.

Risk:
> harder to justify early access to tools / cities / institutions;
> can look like farming novel unless expansion is fast.

Best fit:
> traditional-looking mass-cultivation world.

---

# I06｜修真医院 / 疗养院实习医修

Benchmark function:
> medical profession = competence + being needed + life/death decisions.

Opening pressure:
> junior trainee / low certification.

A3 access:
> life-extension formations, damaged roots, spiritual prosthetics, long-term treatment systems.

Central contradiction:
> medicine is good at keeping people alive but may also create “should have ended / cannot return” states.

Strength:
> extremely human stakes;
> every reader understands “救还是停”.

Risk:
> too human-body focused; A3 wants 万物 / 山河, not only patients;
> medical logic burden high.

Best use:
> recurring major relationship / institution rather than protagonist primary identity.

---

# I07｜山河巡检 / 灵脉司见习员

Benchmark function:
> 《以神通之名》 competence → official handling authority.

Opening pressure:
> low-rank trainee sent to inspect boring peripheral resources.

A3 access:
> wells, rivers, formations, mountains, veins, towns.

Status ladder:
> trainee → certified inspector → regional authority → world-level handling right.

Strength:
> almost perfect access to A3's scale ladder;
> old cases naturally return in public records.

Risk:
> sounds like a designed job;
> can become bureaucracy / inspection-case treadmill;
> weaker broad emotional entry.

Best fit:
> immortal-court / state cultivation.

Use only if:
> job title is simple and culturally native, e.g. 巡山吏 / 山河吏, not “灵能资产生命周期评估师”.

---

# I08｜宗门外门弟子 / 杂役弟子

Benchmark function:
> classic validated cultivation low-status chassis.

Opening pressure:
> resources scarce; bottom of hierarchy; must earn cultivation access.

A3 access:
> assigned to dead/dirty corners:
- graveyard;
- spirit fields;
- old formations;
-废器库;
- mountain maintenance.

Strength:
> extremely familiar; low exposition;
> can smoothly become normal cultivation novel.

Risk:
> generic; A3's unique premise must carry almost all click power;
> “杂役逆袭” has huge competition.

Best fit:
> traditional cultivation world.

---

# I09｜宗门 / 城市“守库人” / 旧物库学徒

Benchmark function:
> collection / hidden-value access.

Opening:
> protagonist manages obsolete spirit tools / sealed relics / old records.

A3 discovery:
> many “discarded” things are not merely broken; some are dead, some are improperly sustained, some still can be saved.

Strength:
> strong curiosity and collection potential;
> old objects carry world history.

Risk:
> easy to become鉴宝 /捡漏 / inventory novel;
> primary identity narrower than student / family / farmer.

Best use:
> secondary early location, not necessarily lifelong identity.

---

# I10｜普通散修 / 城市底层年轻人

Benchmark function:
> low-status freedom + everyday survival.

Opening pressure:
> rent / cultivation resources / work / certification.

A3 access:
> through gig jobs / cheap housing / public qi infrastructure.

Strength:
> adult self-insertion, flexible movement, no forced school or institution.

Risk:
> lacks recurring cast and automatic story engine;
> can turn into “random encounter” fiction.

Requires:
> strong home base / family / neighborhood to create continuity.

---

# I11｜家族旁支 / 被边缘化的年轻族人

Benchmark function:
> family identity / inheritance / status repositioning.

Opening pressure:
> low family value; assigned poor resources / old property.

A3 access:
> family ancestral land, spirit fields, old formations, ancestral tools.

Strength:
> death of things can tie directly to family legacy;
> recurring relationships naturally strong.

Risk:
> could narrow world to family politics;
> less “全民” at opening.

Best fit:
> traditional / hybrid world.

---

# I12｜庙祝 / 山神庙年轻守庙人

Benchmark function:
> local sacred-site ownership + forbidden access + mythic aesthetics.

Opening:
> old temple considered obsolete in modern cultivation era.

A3 access:
> dead gods, dried rivers, abandoned rites, local mountains.

Strength:
> extremely strong “送终 / 天地归还” cultural flavor;
> natural path from one shrine → mountains/rivers → Heaven.

Risk:
> niche / folk-religion flavor may overshadow mainstream cultivation;
> can resemble民俗玄学 lane.

Best use:
> recurring world institution or D return-world identity.

---

# I13｜法器维修 / 阵法维修学徒

Benchmark function:
> craft competence.

Opening:
> low-level maintenance trainee; everyone else fixes function, protagonist sees whether the thing is truly alive.

A3 access:
> equipment / infrastructure is perfect.

Strength:
> easy daily cases; protagonist competence visibly improves.

Risk:
> author already identified the audience-entry weakness:
> specialized maintenance identity has less natural attention than student / family / status-pressure roles.

Recommendation:
> secondary skill, internship or parent profession rather than primary identity.

---

# I14｜“退役修士”家庭的孩子 / 普通修真家庭青年

Benchmark function:
> family pressure + status + intergenerational contrast.

Opening:
> parents spent their lives inside mass cultivation system; protagonist faces same expected path.

A3 access:
> first dead object can be something family depends on:
- home qi unit;
- family spirit field;
- parent's failing technique;
- neighborhood formation.

Strength:
> broad domestic stakes and adult/young-adult relatability.

Risk:
> identity itself is not enough to generate plot; needs school / work / institution layer.

---

# I15｜仙朝县城里的“学徒吏 / 书院生”

Hybrid identity.

Benchmark function:
> student evaluation + public authority ladder.

Opening:
> studies in local academy for a civil / cultivation exam and assists a local public office.

A3 access:
> both school and town infrastructure.

Strength:
> avoids modern-school sameness while keeping exam / ranking / recurring cast;
> can naturally evolve into public handling authority.

Risk:
> needs clean, intuitive world terminology;
> can feel like historical bureaucracy if overbuilt.

Best fit:
> immortal-court / classical-looking mass-cultivation world.

# Important insight

The best primary identity may be a **two-layer identity**, because current hits often combine:
> universally understandable life position
with
> unique access lane.

Examples for later testing (not frozen):
- 高三学生 + 家里经营旧道观 / 送终铺;
- 仙朝书院生 + 在山河司做学徒;
- 灵农家庭青年 + 学宫备考;
- 外门弟子 + 义庄轮值;
- 普通修真家庭青年 + 校园 /城市公共体系.

This avoids:
- overly generic student;
- overly niche weird profession.

# No final pick

Author should first decide which reader-life flavor feels right:
- youth / exam pressure;
- family / home ownership;
- adult craft competence;
- public responsibility;
- traditional sect low-status;
- resource / land survival.

Then build A/D around that identity instead of the other way around.
