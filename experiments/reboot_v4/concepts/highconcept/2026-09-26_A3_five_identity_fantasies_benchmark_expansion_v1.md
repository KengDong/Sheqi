# Highest rule

Do not design from job-access convenience.

Each identity fantasy must preserve:
> OLD PRICE → EXPLOSIVE REVALUATION → RARE VALUE → VISIBLE ENDPOINT → REPEATABLE PAYOFF.

For every candidate:
1. nearest strong references;
2. proven function borrowed;
3. title / hook;
4. first-screen reversal;
5. Ch1–3 proof;
6. Ch20+ repeated fantasy;
7. biggest collapse risk.

No final ranking.

# H1｜修仙高考落榜生 → 天道指定送终人

## 1. Nearest strong references

### 《刚准备高考，离婚逆袭系统来了》
Borrowed function:
> universal life-stage + impossible mismatch.
Reader understands “高考” instantly, then the absurd system creates a much larger second life-track.

### 《没钱修什么仙？》
Borrowed function:
> public cultivation evaluation materially determines a young person's future.

### 《什么叫我成了灭世级怪谈？》
Borrowed function:
> title / premise gives a visible extreme identity endpoint from the start.

## 2. Core fantasy

OLD PRICE:
> 修仙高考落榜 / 仙途评价系统已经给主角判定“无资格进入主流修炼路径”。

REVALUATION:
> on the same day society tells him “you are not qualified to cultivate”, the lost death-law of Heaven gives him the only qualification that ultimately matters:
> **送终天道。**

RARE VALUE:
> not “天赋SSS”, but only he can receive true obituaries / close broken return cycles.

VISIBLE ENDPOINT:
> from someone society says has no仙途 → the person who must decide how Heaven's death is handled.

## 3. Title directions

- 《修仙高考落榜，我却收到了天道的讣告》
- 《落榜那天，天道请我给它送终》
- 《所有仙门都不要我，天道却把后事交给了我》

The first direction preserves broad life-stage familiarity.
The second is more extreme / direct.
The third shifts from exam to social rejection.

## 4. Shelf hook

> **修仙高考落榜当天，所有仙门都把我从名单上划掉；晚上，我却收到了一封只有我能看见的讣告——死者：天道。送终人：我。**

Important:
> obituary does NOT explain why Heaven will die or give full countdown mechanics immediately.

## 5. First-screen reversal

Ch1:
- protagonist receives final exam / qualification failure;
- classmates' futures visibly split away from his;
- no melodramatic ten-chapter bullying: the pain is concrete future closure.

At end of Ch1:
> a school / city cultivation facility shows the first abnormal “death status”.

Ch2:
- protagonist follows the impossible status;
- correct handling of one small dead-but-still-running object creates first 余荫;
- his body, which could not progress through normal path, changes.

Ch3:
- the system opens its first true obituary.
Not a mission:
> **天道：将死 / death record incomplete.**
> **送终资格：唯一可见者 / provisional bearer.**

Public proof:
> a respected adult / institution verifies that the small incident was real.
The protagonist is still socially low, but reader now knows:
> society's evaluation system measured the wrong future.

## 6. Repeatable fantasy after Ch20

The danger:
> “天道讣告” can become a one-time gimmick.

Therefore the repeatable fantasy must be:
> every time protagonist closes a larger death, society is forced to revise the meaning of his original “落榜”.

Ch20:
> one institution that rejected him now needs his diagnosis.

Ch50:
> people with higher cultivation but no death-authority must ask him to make a call they cannot make.

Ch100:
> he can enter places / decisions unavailable even to elite exam winners.

The emotional line:
> **他们当初说我没有仙途；后来他们才发现，我走的是天道死后仍然存在的那条路。**

## 7. Biggest collapse risk

- heaven-scale promise shown too early can dwarf daily life;
- if exam failure becomes generic face-slapping, it becomes ordinary school power fantasy;
- if Heaven obituary gives too much information, mystery dies.

Hard fix:
> Ch1–3 shows the contradiction, not the answer.
The small death must still be satisfying on its own.

# H2｜绝灵废体 → 世界唯一“靠归还修仙”的人

## 1. Nearest strong references

### 《从水猴子开始成神》
Borrowed function:
> protagonist is structurally disadvantaged in the crowded mainstream, then discovers an underpriced lane uniquely suited to him; that lane has a visible divine endpoint.

### 《没钱修什么仙？》
Borrowed function:
> the world's accepted cultivation economy defines people's value; protagonist finds a route that breaks the pricing logic.

### 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》
Borrowed function:
> one rule inversion repeatedly turns what should be disadvantage / death into usable agency.

## 2. Core fantasy

OLD PRICE:
> this cultivation civilization defines talent by extraction efficiency:
> how much qi can you draw, store and convert.

Protagonist:
> **绝灵 / 不受灵.**
Normal reading:
> cannot take qi = cannot cultivate.

REVALUATION:
> a proper return cycle creates **余荫**, and his body accepts it with near-zero loss precisely because he does not compete for ambient qi.

RARE VALUE:
> everyone can take.
> almost nobody can cultivate from **return**.

VISIBLE ENDPOINT:
> when ambient Heaven-Earth qi becomes unstable / exhausted, mainstream cultivation weakens while his path becomes the only complete cycle-compatible cultivation route.

## 3. Title directions

- 《别人吸灵气修仙，我靠归还天地成仙》
- 《全世界都在夺灵气，只有我往回还》
- 《绝灵体不能修仙？我靠给万物送终成仙》

First is clearest cultivation promise.
Second has stronger contradiction.
Third preserves low-status flip.

## 4. Shelf hook

> **他们说绝灵体一辈子都吸不进一口天地灵气。可我第一次给一件死物送终后，才发现——别人修仙靠从天地拿，我修仙靠天地还。**

## 5. First-screen reversal

Ch1:
- protagonist's lack of qi absorption causes a concrete loss:
  exam / sect admission / job / family expectation.
- no need to make every person cruel; the system genuinely has no use for him.

Ch2:
- he encounters a small dead-but-still-running spiritual object;
- system reveals correct return;
- he closes it.

Immediate visible personal reward:
> no generic “+10”.
A warm current produced by the completed cycle enters him.
For the first time in his life:
> **his cultivation actually moves.**

Ch3:
- he repeats with a second, different terminal object to prove it was not coincidence;
- crucially, his gain is larger when more value is correctly returned rather than stolen by him.

This establishes the signature rule:
> greed weakens his route;
> correct return strengthens it.

## 6. Repeatable fantasy after Ch20

Every new scale of return gives two pleasures:
1. protagonist's cultivation grows;
2. his path increasingly proves viable under conditions where mainstream “extraction cultivation” begins to fail.

Ch20:
> he has a usable low-level cultivation system that no textbook recognizes.

Ch50:
> conventional cultivators need high-density qi; he can recover / fight in places they consider dead.

Ch100:
> first major region suffers extraction collapse.
Other cultivators weaken.
His restored-cycle nodes make him stronger / more mobile.

Long fantasy:
> **the world's worst cultivation body becomes the body best adapted to the world's next era.**

## 7. Biggest collapse risk

- can become a simple “reverse-energy system” with environmental skin;
- if every funeral gives cultivation, reader starts wishing things would die;
- if protagonist gains too fast, “return” becomes disguised plunder.

Hard fix:
> personal gain is a by-product of cycle completion, not corpse loot;
> major growth often requires restoring future regeneration, not harvesting the dead.

# H3｜无人收徒 → 死去的仙人却都认他

## 1. Nearest strong references

### 《剑烛大荒》
Borrowed function:
> dangerous ancient / mythic inheritance creates immediate curiosity and visible evolution toward a higher identity.

### 《玄鉴仙族》
Borrowed function:
> inheritance matters because memory / lineage / old choices remain active, not because another skill card was added.

### 《我的化身正在成为最终BOSS》
Borrowed function:
> multiple important identities / legends accumulate social consequence rather than behaving as interchangeable tools.

## 2. Core fantasy

OLD PRICE:
> protagonist has no living lineage willing to accept him:
- aptitude ordinary / abnormal;
- no sponsor;
- no orthodox inheritance.

REVALUATION:
> he discovers that when a dead lineage is properly sent off, its true “遗泽” does not automatically go to current sect leaders.
It chooses:
> the one who actually completed its final cause.

The first dead master recognizes him.

Then another.

RARE VALUE:
> he can receive the final truths / names / unresolved authorities of lineages that living cultivators can no longer truly inherit.

VISIBLE ENDPOINT:
> living sects once said “you have no master”.
Late:
> he becomes the only living person carrying the authenticated final wills of an entire dead era.

## 3. Title directions

- 《活着的仙门都不要我，死去的仙人却都认我》
- 《无人收我为徒，历代祖师却争着把后事交给我》
- 《我给仙门送终，历代祖师都成了我的引路人》

First is strongest pure identity reversal.
Second is more exaggerated / Fanqie-like.
Third risks making “masters collection” too explicit.

## 4. Shelf hook

> **测灵台上，没有一家仙门肯收我。可当我替一座废弃祖祠完成最后一场送终，三千年前已经死去的祖师却在牌位里睁开眼：你不是没人要——是活人没资格教你。**

Important:
> dead master does not simply dump full cultivation onto protagonist.

## 5. First-screen reversal

Ch1:
- public disciple selection / inheritance test;
- protagonist is genuinely unwanted.

Ch2:
- he encounters an abandoned ancestral site / dying lineage remnant nobody values;
- system shows the lineage itself has reached true death, but one final authority / name / unresolved consequence remains.

Ch3:
- protagonist completes the final cause;
- ancestral relic that never recognized current heirs reacts to him;
- a dead founder leaves one **final instruction / authority / true principle**, not 100 years of power.

Public proof:
> the same sect that refused him sees its own founder acknowledge him.

That is the first revaluation hit.

## 6. Repeatable fantasy after Ch20

Do NOT:
> new dead immortal every arc → new technique → inventory.

Instead, each dead lineage must add one of:
- a true name / legitimacy;
- an unresolved enemy;
- a location;
- a relationship with living descendants;
- one missing piece of why the old era failed;
- an authority that makes previous inheritance more useful.

Ch20:
> 2–3 dead lineages have recognized him, but they contradict each other.

Ch50:
> living sects begin fighting over whether he is heir, undertaker, or thief of ancestral legitimacy.

Ch100:
> protagonist can enter sealed places because dead founders' final authorities stack into a real social / metaphysical identity:
> **the acknowledged executor of dead Dao lineages.**

Long emotional reversal:
> **我不是没有师承；我的师承只是都死了，而且他们留下的事没人敢接。**

## 7. Biggest collapse risk

This one can become extremely generic:
> collect dead masters / inherit supreme skills.

Hard fix:
- dead masters give burdens / authorities / truths more often than raw power;
- old inheritances conflict;
- every recognition changes living social relations;
- protagonist must sometimes bury a lineage permanently rather than preserve it.

# H4｜普通人 → 最终获得天地“判死权”

## 1. Nearest strong references

### 《以神通之名》
Borrowed function:
> personal competence becomes real public / institutional authority; higher position means the protagonist can actually change larger outcomes.

### 《捞尸人》
Borrowed function:
> other people fear / misunderstand a domain; protagonist gains credible handling authority inside it.

### 《青山》
Borrowed function:
> people increasingly react to who protagonist is and trust his judgment, not just his raw ability.

## 2. Core fantasy

OLD PRICE:
> protagonist is not necessarily humiliated.
He is simply:
> **a person whose judgment carries no weight.**

REVALUATION:
> first major public failure occurs because recognized experts treat a dead system as saveable;
> protagonist's contrary judgment is proven correct.

RARE VALUE:
> he can distinguish:
- damaged;
- dying;
- truly dead;
- dead-but-forced-running.

VISIBLE ENDPOINT:
> from “nobody listens when I say this thing is dead”
to:
> **no sect, city, Dao lineage or Heaven can be formally / metaphysically closed without his judgment.**

This is a pure authority fantasy.

## 3. Title directions

- 《他们都说还能救，只有我知道它已经死了》
- 《天道还没死，天下已经在等我判它的后事》
- 《万物将死，我说了才算终》

The first has immediate repeated scene promise.
The second is bigger but more abstract.
The third is aggressive but may feel overpowered.

## 4. Shelf hook

> **第一次我说聚灵阵已经死了，全校都当我疯子；后来一条灵脉、一座仙城、一个道统要不要继续，全天下都要等我先开口。**

## 5. First-screen reversal

Ch1:
- protagonist witnesses an authority / expert team diagnosing a dangerous system;
- he sees “true death” status and says stop.
- nobody listens because he has no credentials / status.

Ch2:
- system begins corpse-like cascade exactly along the causal line he identified;
- protagonist is forced to act because he is inside the consequences.

Ch3:
- his handling prevents a larger loss;
- one credible authority does NOT kneel / praise him.
Instead:
> they ask, seriously: “你刚才到底怎么看出来的？”

This is the first social price change.

## 6. Repeatable fantasy after Ch20

The growth is not title collection.
It is:
> **the cost of ignoring his judgment keeps getting larger, while the scope of things he can correctly judge expands.**

Ch20:
> local institutions begin requesting his opinion.

Ch50:
> he can overrule / challenge expensive expert consensus only when willing to carry responsibility for the outcome.

Ch100:
> he has legal / social / metaphysical authority to issue a terminal declaration on a regional cultivation system.

Important:
> his judgment can be wrong at the boundary between dying and saveable.
He must earn authority through results.

Long fantasy:
> **the person nobody listened to becomes the person the whole world cannot make a final decision without.**

## 7. Biggest collapse risk

- too procedural / bureaucratic;
- protagonist becomes omniscient judge;
- repeated “experts wrong, protagonist right” becomes low-IQ face-slapping.

Hard fix:
- experts are competent;
- terminal judgment often involves incomplete evidence;
- protagonist sometimes argues to SAVE something everyone wants to bury;
- authority brings liability / consequences.

# H5｜现世普通人 → 上个纪元所有临终强者等待的未来送终人

## 1. Nearest strong references

### 《捞尸人》
Borrowed function:
> protagonist has access to a forbidden death-world unavailable to ordinary people.

### 《剑烛大荒》
Borrowed function:
> ancient mythic past is not lore wallpaper; every contact changes protagonist's identity / capability and raises world scale.

### 《我的化身正在成为最终BOSS》
Borrowed function:
> the protagonist accumulates multiple legendary social identities / perceptions that eventually point back to one person.

### Time / mystery function from current strong mystery works
Borrowed function:
> each revealed fragment must recontextualize previous facts rather than merely add another historical anecdote.

## 2. Core fantasy

OLD PRICE:
> in the present, protagonist is ordinary / low-value / unrecognized.

REVALUATION:
> after correctly sending off one ancient object, he is pulled not into a second living world, but into its **临终回响**:
> the exact period / causal layer where that object or lineage truly died.

A dying ancient powerhouse can see him.

Their reaction is not:
> “天才!”

It is:
> **“你终于来了。”**

RARE VALUE:
> only the future undertaker can enter terminal echoes and carry an unresolved ending back into the present.

VISIBLE ENDPOINT:
> every major death in the current era opens another fragment of the previous world's final days;
> eventually protagonist reaches the last day of the previous Heaven itself.

## 3. Title directions

- 《每送走一样东西，我都能回到它死前》
- 《我给万物送终，却总有人在三千年前等我》
- 《上个修仙纪元死前，所有人都见过我》
- 《我给万物送终，最后回到了天道死去那天》

Second / third contain the strongest identity mystery.
Fourth gives endpoint too directly but very clickable.

## 4. Shelf hook

> **我给一件三千年前的废器送终，却突然回到了它真正死去的那一天。那位本该死在史书里的仙人看见我，没有问我是谁，只说了一句：你比我算的，晚来了三百年。**

This is not free time travel.

## 5. Crossing / echo hard rules

To keep it distinct from 《原来我是仙道天骄》:

- no破界珠;
- no free physical travel to a second living cultivation world;
- no goods arbitrage;
- no “here trash / there top genius” as core;
- each entry is caused by completing a real send-off;
- destination is bound to that death's terminal causal layer;
- early echoes are limited in time;
- protagonist cannot simply rewrite all history;
- only things whose ending is causally unresolved can interact with him across the echo.

Why protagonist?
> as a transmigrated soul, he was never registered in the old Heaven's life/death ledger;
> therefore terminal echoes can treat him as an external witness / executor.

## 6. First-screen reversal

Ch1:
- present protagonist's normal / low-value position is established briefly;
- he encounters and correctly sends off an ancient dead object.

End:
> world changes.

Ch2:
- he appears in the ancient terminal echo;
- sees a fully alive version of something history calls dead / ruined;
- meets a dying important person who recognizes the **role**, not the individual.

The line:
> “送终人终于来了.”

Ch3:
- protagonist cannot save the whole past;
- but he can complete one unresolved final act / carry one true “归处” back.

Returning to present:
> the supposedly worthless relic / location changes,
and an impossible ancient inscription now contains:
> a reference to a future undertaker.

Public revaluation begins:
> archaeologists / sects / authorities realize the protagonist knows something no living inheritance knows.

## 7. Repeatable fantasy after Ch20

Every echo must do three things:
1. solve / complicate a present terminal problem;
2. reveal one piece of why the prior era died;
3. increase the strange historical fact that **people in the past were already waiting for him**.

Ch20:
> multiple unrelated ancient records contain descriptions of the same future送终人.

Ch50:
> protagonist realizes the past did not merely predict him; some dying people changed their final choices because they knew he would arrive.

Ch100:
> he reaches a terminal echo involving the previous era's world-scale disaster and learns:
> the current Heaven may be built on an unfinished funeral.

Long fantasy:
> **I am nobody in this era, yet the greatest people of the dead era arranged their last moments around my future arrival.**

## 8. Biggest collapse risk

- turns into historical vignette anthology;
- reader loses present-world stakes;
- time rules collapse;
- every dead master gives convenient answer.

Hard fix:
- present problem always triggers echo;
- ancient people know only their own piece;
- echoes can conflict / lie / misunderstand;
- protagonist often returns with burden rather than treasure;
- each echo materially changes present relationships / status / world evidence.

# Cross-candidate structural observation

These five are not all mutually exclusive.

They occupy different layers:

- H1 = **opening social wound / life-stage reversal**
- H2 = **unique cultivation engine**
- H3 = **inheritance / legacy identity**
- H4 = **long-term social authority reward**
- H5 = **world-mystery / crossing engine**

Therefore future synthesis is possible.

Example functional combinations only — NOT final proposals:

### Combination X
H1 + H2 + H4:
> 落榜 / low-price → 靠归还修仙 → judgement authority grows.

### Combination Y
H2 + H5:
> non-extractive body → terminal echoes → old era mystery.

### Combination Z
H3 + H5:
> dead immortals recognize him because he literally reaches their terminal echoes.

Hard warning:
> do not stack every cool feature.
A final concept still needs ONE first-screen fantasy and ONE dominant long engine.
