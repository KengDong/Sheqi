# A2｜《仙门把我当炉鼎，我把整座宗门炼进了丹田》

## Benchmark base

### 《从水猴子开始成神》
Proven function:
> one underpriced arena becomes protagonist's persistent home field; resources, companions, body and territorial control rise together.

### 《玄鉴仙族》
Proven function:
> accumulated people / land / lineage / memory make one identity increasingly weighty; reader consumes the growing whole, not isolated upgrades.

### 《全民大航海，我开局一条幽灵船》
Proven function:
> same named asset remains for hundreds / thousands of chapters; old facilities keep returning, combining and widening future choices.

### 《末日逃亡：开局苍穹列车车长》
Proven function:
> asset growth becomes stronger when people, roles, rights, costs and responsibility enter the same asset.

## A2 design direction

The inner sect is not a skill list.

It is:
> **one persistent asset whose completeness, inhabitants, authority and available actions keep expanding.**

### Fixed object
The protagonist's dantian contains the same incomplete sect from the beginning.

At first:
> only a broken gate foundation and one dark ancestral lamp.

The reader should always be able to remember:
> what this place used to look like.

### Collection unit
Do not collect arbitrary buffs.

Collect:
> **正统碎片 / lineage pieces that belong to a real part of the outer sect.**

Examples:
- 丹峰炉火;
- 剑峰剑碑;
- 药园地脉;
- 藏经楼真页;
- 山门阵眼;
- 祖祠香火 / name authority.

Each piece must have:
1. visible place in the inner sect;
2. a small current use;
3. future synergy with at least one old piece.

### Slow-growth pleasure
A fragment should usually improve an existing place before creating a new one.

Example:
- first丹峰 fragment: dead furnace can keep heat;
- later药园 fragment: furnace finally gains a stable ingredient source;
- later藏经 fragment: old prescriptions can be preserved / repaired;
- later fire-vein fragment: furnace no longer depends entirely on outside fire.

Reader pleasure:
> the same old furnace keeps becoming more useful.

### Milestone pleasure
A full building does not mean instant combat power.

It means:
> **new option category unlocked.**

Examples:
- complete丹房 → protagonist can begin producing / repairing certain cultivation resources;
- complete藏经楼 → can preserve damaged lineages and compare different inheritances;
- complete山门 → can grant limited access to another person;
- complete祖祠 → the inner sect can begin recognizing real disciples / lineage.

### Critical escalation
Eventually:
> other people can be registered into the inner sect without physically entering his dantian.

This is the point where:
> "my internal collection"
becomes
> "a real second sect".

Borrowed from H1/H2 asset evidence:
> people must change the asset, and the asset must change their lives.

### Long-term conflict
The outer sect begins to discover:
> some official inheritances respond more strongly to the inner sect than to their current holders.

Therefore the ultimate fight is not:
> protagonist collects every technique.

It is:
> **which side has the right to call itself the true continuation of the sect?**

## A2 key rule

Every new collection must answer:
> What old part becomes newly useful because this arrived?

If answer is only:
> +attribute / +damage / new skill
reject it.

# A3｜PRIMARY PIVOT

## Proposed main title

> **《全民修仙把天道吸干了，我来给它办丧事》**

Alternative title retaining original flavor:
> 《全世界都在修仙，只有我在给天道办丧事》

Current preference:
> first title is more aggressive and immediately explains the world crisis.

## Benchmark base

### 《捞尸人》
Proven function:
> a death-related profession is only the entrance; long-term pleasure is forbidden access + handling authority + deeper identity / world secret.

A3 must not become:
> new corpse / new dead object / new order every week.

### 《1984：从川菜馆开始》
Proven function:
> each small daily cycle produces concrete satisfaction, makes tomorrow materially different, and causes old people / places to return.

A3 must make:
> each send-off leave a persistent local change, not a disposable reward.

### 《没钱修什么仙？》
Proven function:
> contemporary pressure becomes a cultivation-world system; the protagonist learns to see and resist the structural logic.

A3 can use:
> universal cultivation as prosperity that is also draining the world.

### 《剑烛大荒》 / 《夜无疆》
Proven function:
> mythic / world-scale wonder must lift protagonist and world scale together, not remain decorative lore.

A3 escalation:
> dead tool → dead place → dead lineage → dead region → dying Heaven.

### 《幽灵船》 / 《苍穹列车》
Proven function:
> old gains must return and change future options.

A3 send-offs must create:
> places, relations, rights, knowledge or environmental changes that remain in the book.

# A3｜World premise

The protagonist transmigrates into a cultivation world at the height of a true:
> **全民修真盛世.**

Cultivation is no longer reserved for sect elites.

Children refine qi.
Cities run on spiritual veins.
Pills, formations, spirit fields, flying tools and mass cultivation all consume the same underlying thing:
> **the lifespan / vitality of Heaven and Earth.**

For centuries everyone assumed:
> spiritual qi regenerates.

It does not.

What people call:
> 灵气枯竭
is actually:
> **天地寿命被抽短。**

The world is prosperous precisely because:
> it is burning tomorrow faster and faster.

This borrows the structural function of 《没钱修什么仙？》:
> the system everyone depends on is also the thing trapping / harming them.

But the original angle is:
> not economic debt;
> ecological / cosmic lifespan debt.

---

# A3｜Transmigration + system

The protagonist transmigrates into a low-status corpse / funeral worker or sect mortuary apprentice.

System name can remain simple:
> **万物送终**

System does only three things.

## 1. See whether something is truly dying / already dead
It does NOT mark everything as a quest.

Only things whose natural / causal life is genuinely ending can be read.

## 2. Show the last unresolved causal knot
Not anthropomorphic "wish list".

Examples:
- a spiritual spring's final current has nowhere to return;
- a broken sword's final intent has no successor;
- a dead formation is still being forcibly powered and poisoning the land;
- a dying lineage still has one unpassed core principle.

## 3. Settle a successful send-off
Settlement has two outputs:
- **遗泽**: a small, concrete, nature-linked benefit;
- **归还**: most of the remaining vitality returns to the place / world instead of becoming protagonist loot.

Important:
> the system is a diagnostic / settlement tool, not a mission dispatcher.

It never says:
> go bury X.

The protagonist chooses where to go and what to touch.

This preserves agency.

# A3｜The daily pleasure loop

## Step 1｜Everyone else sees trash / failed asset / dying resource
Examples:
- exhausted spirit field;
- cracked magic tool;
- old spirit beast;
- collapsed minor vein;
- abandoned road / ferry / temple / inheritance.

## Step 2｜Protagonist sees that it is already beyond saving
This creates competence pleasure:
> everyone is still trying to squeeze one more use out of it;
> protagonist knows continuing will turn death into disaster.

## Step 3｜He discovers the real final causal knot
This is the mystery / forbidden-access part from 《捞尸人》.

## Step 4｜He performs the correct send-off
Not always burial.

Depending on nature:
- release;
- return;
- dismantle;
- pass on;
- burn;
- sink;
- close;
- complete one last cycle.

## Step 5｜Immediate concrete payoff
Like 《1984》:
> the result must be physically / socially legible now.

Examples:
- toxic field becomes usable ordinary farmland;
- dead spring's final current opens a new clean well;
- broken sword leaves a usable sword intent;
- dying beast leaves a bloodline seed for its offspring;
- dead formation stops poisoning a village and leaves one stable defensive node.

## Step 6｜Persistent return
The place / person / ability returns later.

This is mandatory.

Otherwise it becomes case-of-week.

# A3｜Why the protagonist travels

Not:
> system sends him to next mission.

Instead:
> after the first few send-offs, people begin seeking the one person who can tell whether a thing is worth saving, should be allowed to die, or is already turning into a corpse-disaster.

He gains:
- access to dying resources;
- invitations to sects / towns / ruins;
- reputation among people who handle failed spiritual assets;
- enemies among those profiting from forced life-extension.

This borrows 《捞尸人》:
> profession gives access and authority.

Travel therefore grows organically from:
> people + consequences + world deterioration.

# A3｜The strongest daily contrast

The cultivation world believes:
> **more cultivation = more progress.**

The protagonist keeps seeing:
> progress built on dead things nobody is willing to bury.

Therefore his most satisfying recurring scene is not simply:
> "I got a reward."

It is:
> **everyone still wants to squeeze the last drop out of a dying thing; I prove letting it die correctly creates more value than continuing to consume it.**

This can create:
- face reversal;
- resource gain;
- environmental recovery;
- professional authority;
- moral / strategic choice.

# A3｜Power-growth system

Avoid:
> one funeral = one new spell.

Borrow the hit lesson:
> old gains must thicken the same core capability.

The protagonist grows in three persistent dimensions.

## A. 送终眼
At first:
> sees death date / terminal state.

Later:
> sees deeper causal knots and which parts are artificially prolonging death.

Eventually:
> sees lifespan flow of regions / sects / Heaven.

## B. 归还权
At first:
> can correctly return one object's remaining vitality.

Later:
> can choose how a larger legacy is distributed:
- descendants;
- land;
- lineage;
- local people;
- himself only in a small share.

This creates real choices.

## C. 葬权 / handling authority
At first:
> can handle dead tools / small places.

Later:
> can legally / metaphysically close:
- spirit vein;
- sect inheritance;
- city formation;
- regional heaven-earth structure.

Final:
> **qualified to perform Heaven's funeral.**

So growth is:
> access + judgment + authority,
not a spell inventory.

# A3｜The global crisis

## Phase 1｜Things are dying too early
Small send-offs reveal:
- wells;
- fields;
- tools;
- spirit beasts;
- local veins
are all reaching true death earlier than historical records suggest.

## Phase 2｜The common cause appears
Every premature death traces back to:
> larger cultivation extraction.

Not one evil sect.

The whole civilization is doing it.

## Phase 3｜Heaven begins "collecting debt"
As Heaven's lifespan falls below a threshold:
- tribulations become harsher;
- spiritual disasters increase;
- areas lose stable laws;
- breakthroughs fail;
- dead zones spread.

The world interprets this as:
> a golden age requiring even more cultivation resources.

Therefore the cure initially worsens the disease.

## Phase 4｜Ascension truth
Possible strong reveal:
> ascension is Heaven's emergency recovery mechanism.

High-level cultivators are not simply leaving the world.

Their accumulated cultivation is being:
> recycled / reclaimed to patch the dying Heaven.

This creates the ultimate political split:
- keep feeding ascenders to Heaven;
- stop cultivation and collapse civilization;
- kill Heaven early and seize the corpse;
- find a new cycle.

## Phase 5｜Heaven obituary
The protagonist eventually sees:
> a fixed Heaven death date.

At that point the title becomes literal.

Final question:
> **Should Heaven be saved, replaced, or allowed to die properly so a new world can be born?**

---

# A3｜System identity mystery

Optional but high-value long magnet:

The system is not a modern game system.

It is:
> **the world's lost death / recycling function.**

Cultivation civilization learned how to take vitality,
but over time destroyed / suppressed the mechanism that returns vitality after death.

The "system" appears game-like to the transmigrator because:
> it translates that ancient function into a form he can understand.

Late reveal:
> protagonist is not chosen to become strongest.
> he is carrying the missing half of the world's life cycle.

This makes:
> transmigration + system
serve the theme instead of feeling pasted on.

# A3｜Ch1–3 candidate delivery

## Ch1
- protagonist transmigrates into low-status mortuary / corpse hall role;
- system activates only when he touches an old spirit tool everyone is still forcibly using;
- it shows:
  > **已死。仍在服役。**
- forced use causes a real local accident / backlash.

## Ch2
- others try to repair / reactivate it because resources are expensive;
- protagonist sees the final causal knot and argues it must be retired / sent off;
- he takes social risk to act.

## Ch3
- correct send-off prevents larger damage;
- immediate persistent result:
  > some local resource / relationship / usable legacy remains;
- system shows a shocking macro entry for the first time:
  > **天地寿数：正在异常缩短**
or
  > a fragment of Heaven's future obituary.

Do not reveal full crisis yet.

The first three chapters must simultaneously deliver:
- system;
- professional competence;
- tangible reward;
- permanent local change;
- larger mystery.

# A3｜Why this is stronger than the previous version

Previous version risk:
> cosmic funeral concept + small episodic curiosities.

New version:
> **a cultivation boom is killing the world; the protagonist is the only person who can correctly close dead systems and return their remaining life to the cycle.**

Every daily episode now serves both:
1. immediate practical pleasure;
2. evidence in the same world-scale disaster.

No separate "case layer" and "mainline layer".
