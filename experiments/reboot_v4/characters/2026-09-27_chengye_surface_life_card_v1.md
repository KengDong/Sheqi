# 程野｜SURFACE LIFE CARD V1
status: WORKING / AUTHOR REVIEW / NOT CANON
scope:
> visible everyday personality layered on top of existing CHENG_YE_CHARACTER_BIBLE.
> Does NOT replace the Core Engine.

## 0. Design goal
If Ch1 temporarily removed:
- anomaly;
- ancient world;
- special mechanism;

Reader should still be able to tell:
> this is Cheng Ye,
not a generic cheerful smart protagonist.

---

## 1. Social default｜陌生人面前

### Working direction
程野不是社牛，也不是冷淡型。

First-contact mode:
> 先正常办事，再决定要不要多说。

He will:
- answer directly;
- notice tone quickly;
- if the other person is easygoing, loosen naturally;
- if the other person is purely transactional, keep it transactional.

He will NOT:
- force a joke within first three lines;
- turn every stranger into banter partner;
- deliberately act cool.

### Ch1 implication
With Zhou Hang:
> first few exchanges can be very ordinary.
Personality should come from what Cheng Ye DOES in the test,
not from making Zhou Hang laugh.

---

## 2. Money texture｜有资源感，但不“穷人表演”

Hard-derived from core:
- basic life/tuition manageable;
- upward-mobility resources expensive;
- he actively earns optional cultivation resources.

Visible behavior:
- remembers the price of things he genuinely wants;
- converts windfalls into concrete access:
  > “this pays for one diagnostic / one course / one recovery item.”
- notices expensive training through EFFECT, not brand:
  > an opponent moves like someone has corrected them repeatedly.

He does NOT:
- calculate every cup of water;
- constantly announce he is poor;
- resent richer peers by default;
- refuse useful spending just to prove thrift.

### Emotional texture
He is not ashamed of “currently can’t afford”.
He dislikes pity more than he dislikes admitting budget limits.

---

## 3. Pride / embarrassment｜他哪里会尴尬

Working direction:
程野的尴尬点不是“被发现没钱”。

More likely:
- confidently read a rule one way and discover he read it wrong;
- tried a clever shortcut and created extra work;
- said something lightly, then situation suddenly became serious.

Response:
> brief self-reframe -> move on -> fix the practical problem.

He does NOT:
- defend ego for five paragraphs;
- become self-deprecating clown.

This creates human imperfection without making him stupid.

---

## 4. Body relationship｜他怎么对待自己的身体

Derived from repeated cultivation / crowdwork:
- knows what “normal soreness” vs “bad pain” feels like;
- after being hit, first checks whether movement/breath/qi circulation is affected;
- does not dramatize small pain;
- if injury becomes real, immediately stops treating it as joke material.

Visible actions may include:
- flexing ankle/wrist once;
- taking one breath to test qi flow;
- touching hit area only if it matters.

Guard:
> do not turn this into a repeated tic every scene.

---

## 5. Competence map｜必须有不会的东西

Hard principle from mature protagonists:
> clever != universally competent.

Cheng Ye is good at:
- current-world ordinary platform/rule logic;
- practical decisions;
- noticing immediate constraints;
- testing hypotheses.

He is NOT automatically good at:
- ancient history;
- elite cultivation theory;
- reading high-realm politics;
- identifying every old technique;
- understanding people perfectly.

He can:
- misread another person's motive;
- overlook one social cue;
- try something and be wrong.

His intelligence should be:
> fast updating,
not:
> instant omniscience.

---

## 6. Non-optimization｜他必须有“不想算”的时候

Working direction:
程野 does not spend all day optimizing.

When stakes are low:
- willing to take the convenient option;
- can procrastinate boring maintenance;
- may choose “good enough” over perfect;
- does not research every minor purchase / conversation.

Why:
> otherwise rule-reading becomes robotic.

Important contrast:
> ordinary life: loose.
> meaningful opportunity / danger: attention sharpens.

This supports:
> 平时轻，遇事稳.

Specific hobbies/preferences:
> NOT YET LOCKED.
Do not invent a quirky hobby just to humanize him.

---

## 7. Attention pattern｜程野先看什么

Default attention:
1. what affects him now;
2. what can be acted on;
3. what is odd / useful;
4. only then broader meaning.

Examples:
- order: pay + condition;
- training opponent: actual movement quality, not expensive clothes;
- anomaly: first survives current exchange, then studies impossible scene;
- archive: matching face/detail before philosophical implications.

Guard:
> not every observation must become a calculation.

---

## 8. Humor texture｜不是“会说笑话”

His lightness should come from:
- practical reframing;
- ordinary treatment of absurdity;
- slight self-mockery;
- one unexpected but valid interpretation.

Frequency:
> often zero punchlines in a conversation.

A good Cheng Ye scene can be funny because:
> he genuinely follows an interpretation farther than others expected.

He does NOT:
- need NPC approval/laughter;
- attach witty commentary to every outcome.

---

## 9. Relationship modes

### Stranger peer
Tone:
> normal / concise / equal-footed.

He neither:
- overshares;
nor:
- performs coolness.

### Familiar peer
More lateral humor;
more willing to admit embarrassment;
can tease if relationship has earned it.

### Competent authority
Listens first.
Asks concrete questions.
Will challenge wording/rules if it affects action,
but not for rebellion.

### Incompetent authority
More likely to test edge cases / find workaround,
not start moral speeches.

### Person in real danger
Humor drops.
Focus becomes:
> what can be done now.

---

## 10. Success / failure surface

### Small success
He enjoys it briefly,
then checks:
- repeatable?
- what does it unlock?

But not every success needs verbal summary.

### Failure
First beat can be irritation / embarrassment / pain.
Then:
> inspect why.

Important:
> allow first emotional beat BEFORE rational analysis.
Otherwise he feels machine-like.

## 13. Scene test
A scene contains Cheng Ye if Reader can answer:
1. What did he notice?
2. What did he feel BEFORE analysis?
3. What did he choose?
4. What did he ignore because it was not important to him?
5. Did his social mode fit the person in front of him?

If only #1 and #3 exist:
> he is still an Agent.
