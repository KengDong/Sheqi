## 1. Reference function: 《谁让他修仙的！》陆阳型

Do NOT copy jokes / wording.

Borrow character functions:
- optimistic baseline;
- quick associative thinking;
- likes to use rules rather than complain about them;
- can say something slightly crooked but logically self-consistent;
- humor grows from decisions, not stand-up monologues;
- willing to test a strange path himself;
- when real danger appears, joke stops and attention sharpens;
- not shameless for its own sake;
- not cold / gloomy / over-calculating.

For our protagonist:
> light, practical, audacious, observant.

He is not:
> "穷所以自卑";
he is:
> "没资源就找别的办法搞资源."

## 2. Behavior engine

Preferred repeatable behavior:
1. sees a rule;
2. finds the actual incentive / loophole;
3. tests it;
4. accepts small cost;
5. gets a result;
6. makes a dry / slightly absurd conclusion.

Opening example:
> insurance already active
> → training wants extreme data
> → he deliberately pushes into his weak transition
> → source resonance happens.

Thus the cheat is triggered by CHARACTER CHOICE.

## 3. Humor rule

Use:
- one sentence;
- dry logic;
- scene-dependent.

Avoid:
- three jokes in a row;
- internet meme strings;
- protagonist commenting on everything;
- keeping comedy during horror / real danger.

Good tonal switch:
> before anomaly: light.
> historical person looks back: smile disappears.
