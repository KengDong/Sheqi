# Benchmark facts

## 《没钱修什么仙？》
- official Qidian honor: 十万均订 (2026-04-01).
- Ch1 uses fresh Earth-person transmigration into a modern cultivation high-school student.

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
- current Qidian list: 2026-09-01 launch, first sub 6691; 万订 2026-09-10.
- gives protagonist a concrete portable artifact: 破界珠.
Function:
> crossing cause is one-line understandable; no ontology debt before payoff.

## 《我有一个修仙世界》
- Qidian honors include 2024 十二天王; 万人追捧.
Function:
> protagonist is already native/socialized in modern cultivation society; second-world discovery changes aspiration without requiring first-world acclimation.

## 《苟在妖武乱世修仙》
- official platform synopsis explicitly sells "穿了，而且是二穿".
Function:
> second crossing itself can be a clear commercial promise.

## 《天命皆烬》
- Qidian synopsis explicitly labels 武道乱世 / 赛博修仙 / 两界门 and tag 穿越.
Function:
> a crossing protagonist can still sustain two distinct high-energy social orders.

# Highest design requirement

Need simultaneously solve:
1. why protagonist alone can cross source eras;
2. why he remembers overwritten history;
3. why crossing begins NOW;
4. why accessible historical dates are not fixed to three hundred years;
5. how modern social climb unlocks deeper / more valuable source eras.

# OPTION A｜早已穿越的地球人 + 第一次穿越留下“界痕” + 普通大学逆袭
status: CURRENT RECOMMENDED

## Identity

Earth person crossed into this cyber-cultivation world years ago:
- either childhood crossing at ~7–10;
- or reincarnation /胎穿 with Earth memory awakening early.

By Ch1:
> he has already lived here long enough that this world is genuinely his home.

He has:
- classmates / friends;
- family relationships;
- school record;
- real social stakes.

Earth is backstory, not an active third world.

## Why he can cross again

The first crossing did not give him an item.

It left:
> a **causal scar / world-boundary imprint** on his soul.

Internal logic:
> his body belongs to this world,
> but his soul was never originally generated by this world's causal history.

Modern cultivators can all perceive / study:
- technique origins;
- Dao traces;
- relic creator imprints.

Ordinary people stop at:
> "this trace points to founder X / era Y."

When protagonist enters sufficiently deep resonance with a source trace:
> his foreign soul is not fully locked to the present causal layer,
> so the trace pulls him upstream into the actual source node.

## Why only he remembers old history

When upstream is changed,
local people / records settle into the new downstream branch.

Protagonist's deepest soul anchor:
> originated outside this world's history.

Therefore:
> he keeps old-version memory.

This makes Earth origin FUNCTIONAL rather than decorative.

## Why now

Choose one:
A1. at 18 / first year university he reaches the cultivation level where the soul can perceive source traces;
A2. first time he performs a normal source-analysis / founder-trace exercise;
A3. a current cultivation bottleneck forces him into unusually deep synchronization with a technique.

Recommended:
> A3 on top of A1.
He has only recently gained enough spiritual perception, and current pressure makes him push too deep.

## Modern identity

Recommended:
> first-year student at an ordinary local cultivation university / ordinary public university cultivation track.

Why:
- avoids copying 《没钱》 high-school fresh-transmigration opening;
- avoids 《仙道天骄》 vocational-college geometry;
- gives freedom for work / competition / internship / adult money pressures;
- still has clear ranking / scholarship / team / academic ladder.

## Modern climb

Layer 1｜freshman survival
- scholarship / training resources;
- practical ranking;
- part-time paid sparring / tutoring.

Layer 2｜school core track
- school team;
- better mentor;
- internal resource allocation;
- technique access.

Layer 3｜city / interschool
- city training camp;
- exchange;
- external qualification;
- stronger-school peers.

Layer 4｜industry / sect partnership
- internship;
- company / lineage project;
- professional certification;
- higher income.

Layer 5｜elite national institutions
- major lineage;
- top university / research-training center;
- national competition / professional route.

Ancient assets open gates.
Modern performance keeps him inside.

## Pros
- Earth origin explains crossing + memory retention elegantly.
- current world has real emotional weight.
- avoids Ch1 triple-world exposition.
- strong long-term modern line.
- “二穿” still exists as commercial fact.

## Risk
- Earth origin may feel irrelevant if never used beyond causality.
Guard:
> occasionally his Earth social intuition / language can matter, but never make Earth tech the cheat.

# OPTION B｜Ch1 fresh Earth soul → cyber-cultivation student → soon discovers second crossing
status: HIGH HOOK / HIGH IMITATION RISK

## Identity

Ch1:
> Earth adult / student wakes in a current cultivation student body.

Within first few chapters:
> discovers source-era crossing.

Commercial line:
> "我刚穿越成修仙学生，结果还能再穿一次。"

## Why it works
- immediate reader lens;
- "二穿" is extremely easy to package;
- 《苟在妖武乱世修仙》 shows explicit "二穿" itself is a market-readable promise.

## Main problem
Very close on opening function to 《没钱修什么仙？》:
> Earth outsider → modern cultivation student → current social pressure.

Even if details differ,
reader comparison will be immediate.

## If used, must differ strongly
- protagonist should NOT have sick-parent / huge debt opening;
- not high-school interview;
- perhaps university freshman / recent graduate;
- crossing #2 must happen extremely early so story identity becomes "二穿源流", not "没钱-like modern cultivation".

## Verdict
Strong hook,
but current project already relies heavily on 《没钱》 social grammar.
Using its protagonist-entry shape too would make derivative smell unnecessarily high.

# OPTION C｜native cyber-cultivation student + concrete source relic
status: SIMPLE / ICONIC

## Identity
No Earth crossing.
Native modern cultivation student.

## Cause
He obtains a specific item that can follow source causality:
working types:
- blank Dao seal;
- broken founder token;
- unowned "source register";
- ancient relic whose original owner is unknown.

It behaves like:
> a compass to source moments,
not a free portal.

## Advantage
Like the 破界珠 function:
> one concrete object, one-sentence crossing cause.

Low cognitive load.

## Problems
Need separately explain:
- why only he can use it;
- why he remembers overwritten histories;
- why item can reach many unrelated eras.

If we solve all three by adding more lore,
it becomes more complicated than Option A.

## Better twist
The relic may later be revealed as:
> something an ancient ally deliberately left for him after their relationship was established.

But it cannot fully explain first contact without a bootstrap / previous owner.

## Verdict
Good for package simplicity,
weaker deep logic than A.

# TIME DEPTH｜Do NOT fix at 300 years

Highest recommendation:
> **accessible date is determined by the source anchor.**

Examples:

## Near-source｜30–100 years
- modern teacher lineage;
- recent technique reform;
- still-living descendants.

High relational danger,
less historical information advantage.

## Middle-source｜100–500 years
Best early-book range:
- current mainstream techniques were being created;
- current institutions' founders were young;
- enough modern records exist to give protagonist useful future information;
- enough time exists for reverse inheritances / lineages to mature.

## Deep-source｜500–2000 years
Unlocked after modern protagonist reaches stronger institutions:
- great lineage roots;
- lost techniques;
- old wars;
- ancient medical / alchemy traditions.

Information becomes less complete.
Protagonist shifts from:
> "I know the answer"
to
> "I know fragments / long-run outcomes."

## Ancient-source｜2000+ years
Late-book:
- foundational practices;
- civilization-scale source events;
- modern records are unreliable / mythologized.

At this depth,
protagonist's Earth-outsider advantage is no longer information.
It is:
> accumulated relationships, judgment and ability to compare civilizations.

# Natural time progression

Modern social climb unlocks better anchors:

ordinary university
→ public/common techniques
→ middle-source people

school core track / city institutions
→ better relics / private annotations
→ deeper source nodes

major modern lineage
→ founder relics / sealed archives
→ ancient source eras

Thus:
> **modern climb and time-depth progression are the SAME progression.**
