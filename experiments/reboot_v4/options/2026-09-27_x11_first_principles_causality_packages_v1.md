# 0. First principle reset

PRIMARY READER FANTASY:
> bottom-up mastery / visible growth / social revaluation / access / autonomy.

SECONDARY:
> historical agency / fate resistance / hidden relationship capital / mystery.

DELIVERY VEHICLE:
> world-scar / source-era interaction / ancient network.

Therefore:
> modern protagonist growth is the spine.
> ancient interaction must amplify / complicate / compound that growth.
> causality rules exist to preserve pleasure and crisis weight, not for their own elegance.

Benchmark functions:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》: ability → recognized result → money / teacher / job / access.
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》: immediate cross-system revaluation.
- 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》: stateful repetition / old result changes new round.
- 《九种魔药》: immediate small payoff + open next slot.
- 《天才俱乐部》: mechanism first, deeper mystery later; old anomalies gain new meaning.
- 《我有一个修仙世界》: both lines remain independently productive.

# 1. PACKAGE A｜闭环隐史型

Core:
> protagonist's early source-era actions were ALWAYS already part of history.

He does not "rewrite" public present early.
He discovers:
> historical gaps / unexplained figures / lost branches / hidden inheritances were caused by his own future interventions.

Example:
Modern record:
> founder once mentioned an unknown traveler.

Protagonist later becomes:
> that traveler.

Modern record:
> obscure healer died / disappeared at 25.

Reality:
> protagonist saves her; she survives under another identity.
> public history still records "death", but a hidden lineage now exists.

Reader fantasy:
> "I am secretly inside the history everyone studies."

Pleasure:
- hidden identity;
- destiny;
- private source authority;
- modern low status vs deep historical importance.

Modern climb:
> primary.
Ancient interaction unlocks hidden knowledge / assets / relationships,
but protagonist still wins current-world opportunities himself.

Crisis weight:
> very high.
If a catastrophe is already recorded even WITH protagonist's interventions,
he cannot remove it by "going back once."

Endgame:
> protagonist eventually learns that many attempts to prevent the catastrophe were already part of the history leading toward it.
To escape:
> he must break the closed loop itself.

Benchmark fit:
- 《天才俱乐部》 layered recontextualization;
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》 hidden identity contrast;
- 《没钱修什么仙？》 modern growth remains primary.

Risk:
> too much "it always happened" can make agency feel fake.

Guard:
> close only early / medium small events.
Do NOT reveal every important choice was predetermined.

# 2. PACKAGE B｜支线改写 + 主干惯性型

Core:
> people, private relationships, technique branches, resources, hidden lineages can change.
> civilization-scale events are causally heavy and resist single-node intervention.

Early:
- protagonist can save people;
- create hidden branches;
- plant resources;
- alter local fates.

Modern public history:
> usually changes little;
> private / obscure evidence changes or new hidden assets appear.

Major event:
> protagonist blocks Cause A.
> Event still occurs through Cause B.

Reader fantasy:
> "I can change lives, but to change fate I need to become powerful enough to move the whole board."

Modern climb:
> strongest.
Every modern rise unlocks better anchors and deeper historical leverage.

Crisis weight:
> strong.
Current crises cannot be deleted by a single ancient action.

Endgame:
> multi-era layout changes enough underlying conditions that one major causal attractor finally breaks.

Benchmark fit:
- 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》 stateful accumulation;
- 《我有一个修仙世界》 multi-capital compounding;
- market fantasy: rule resistance / fate control.

Risk:
> if "history inertia" feels arbitrary, reader calls author cheating.

Guard:
> every inevitable event must have visible MULTIPLE causes.
No mystical correction force.

# 3. PACKAGE C｜可改史，但改史有“价格”型

Core:
> public history CAN change visibly.
But large rewrite has direct cost.

Possible costs:
- current knowledge invalidates;
- one useful relationship disappears;
- anchor burns;
- protagonist loses access to an era;
- modern institution changes in an unfavorable way;
- one saved life causes another problem.

Reader fantasy:
> "I really can rewrite history, but every edit is a wager."

Modern climb:
> strong if protagonist cannot use edits casually.

Crisis weight:
> medium-high.
He COULD attempt to rewrite the crisis,
but cost / uncertainty / causal complexity make that dangerous.

Endgame:
> final rewrite is a massive wager using all accumulated people / resources / current strength.

Benchmark fit:
- 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》 choices leave state;
- 《九种魔药》 power always has a different consequence / open slot;
- 《天才俱乐部》 information changes after intervention.

Risk:
> most system-like;
> can become "history editing game".

Use if:
> author wants higher immediate “I changed the world” dopamine.

# 4. PACKAGE D｜双世界源头映射型

Core:
> ancient realm is NOT literally the past of the current world.

It is:
> another world / source civilization whose people, methods and artifacts periodically crossed into the modern world in historical contact windows.

Modern cultivation history:
> built partly from things transmitted from that world.

Protagonist source-travel reaches:
> that world's earlier source moments.

Changing ancient realm:
> changes what later gets transmitted into modern world,
NOT all of modern history.

Example:
- help ancient healer → a medical inheritance later crosses worlds;
- help founder → his revised method becomes one of the methods imported into modern civilization;
- save person → descendants / artifact may appear later through a contact window.

Reader fantasy:
> true dual-world reciprocity + long-horizon investment.

Modern climb:
> very strong because modern world remains structurally autonomous.

Crisis weight:
> strongest mechanically.
You cannot erase a modern crisis by changing one "past" because ancient world is not your full past.

Fate tone:
> less "this was always destined";
> more "two worlds have been entangled for ages."

Benchmark fit:
- 《我有一个修仙世界》 autonomous two-world productivity;
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》 value revaluation;
- avoids time-edit trivialization.

Risk:
> loses some powerful “I am in history” identity pleasure.
More cosmology to explain later.

# 5. PACKAGE E｜闭环前期 + 支线可改中期 + 主干可破后期

Hybrid:
> strongest candidate for current book.

Early:
> mostly Package A.
Many first interactions turn out to be hidden history that was always there.
This gives:
- destiny;
- mystery;
- identity irony.

Middle:
> Package B.
Protagonist discovers he CAN change local fates and hidden branches.
But large events converge through alternate causes.

Late:
> after enough growth / cross-era layout,
he earns the ability to alter a trunk event.

Meaning:
> change-history power itself scales with protagonist's REAL growth.

Reader journey:
1. "I was already inside history."
2. "I can change people."
3. "I can change branches."
4. "Why can't I change this disaster?"
5. "Because one person is not enough."
6. "Then I'll change the conditions that make it inevitable."

This aligns primary fantasy:
> growth → more agency.

Not:
> cheat is maxed at Ch1.

# 6. Modern spine under ANY package

Modern growth loop must stay:

current pressure
→ training / money / work / competition
→ measurable improvement
→ teacher / institution / employer reallocates access
→ protagonist gets better anchors / source objects
→ ancient interaction opens
→ source interaction creates leverage / layout
→ protagonist DIGESTS it
→ solves next current-world challenge
→ climbs again.

Ancient side must never replace:
- training;
- money;
- current allies;
- current credentials;
- current combat / professional competence.

# 7. What to borrow from each benchmark, specifically

## 《没钱修什么仙？》
Borrow:
> social machine.
Strength must cash out as:
- money;
- teacher attention;
- jobs;
- contracts;
- rankings;
- access.

Do not borrow:
> exact poor high-school / debt / family structure.

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
Borrow:
> identity / value revaluation must be immediate when worlds touch.

For us:
> modern ordinary student
↔ source-era person who may treat him as an impossible downstream witness / future-informed collaborator.

## 《苟在初圣魔门当人材》
Borrow:
> every next interaction must operate on changed state.

For us:
> previous ancient relationships / modern rise change which source objects he can touch and what information remains valid.

## 《九种魔药》
Borrow:
> each repeat should reveal a new reward class.

For us:
- source insight;
- modern credential;
- money;
- hidden lineage;
- preserved resource;
- relationship gate;
- failed investment;
not "new move +10" each time.

## 《天才俱乐部》
Borrow:
> delayed ontology, not delayed payoff.
Early anomalies should later gain deeper meaning.

For us:
> early "unknown collaborator" / hidden branch / weird scar can later become evidence of intentional cross-era architecture.

# 8. Author-facing decision lens

If desired feeling is:
> "宿命感 / 我早就在历史里"
choose A.

If desired feeling is:
> "成长越强，我越能撬动命运"
choose B.

If desired feeling is:
> "每次改史都像一次高风险豪赌"
choose C.

If desired feeling is:
> "两个真实世界长期互相塑造"
choose D.

If desired feeling is:
> "前期宿命，中期抗命，后期真正逆天改命"
choose E.

Do NOT lock yet.

> modern growth is primary;
