# 《全班绑定道种，我绑定的是异界活人》｜Framework Choice Board V1

date: 2026-09-26
branch: reboot-v4-living-dao-seed-framework
status: AUTHOR CHOICE / NO WINNER SELECTED

# 0. Evidence base

This board integrates:
- Phase 1A modern cultivation benchmark;
- Phase 1B dual-world benchmark;
- Phase 1C relationship benchmark;
- Phase 1D early-loop benchmark;
- Benchmark Synthesis V1.

All options keep:
- modern mass-cultivation civilization;
- second traditional / different cultivation world;
- two persistent male co-leads;
- title premise that modern protagonist binds a living person in another world.

Hard anti-copy:
- no artificial-root → heavenly-root inversion;
- no ordinary commodity arbitrage;
- no free shared cultivation;
- no ancient side as warehouse / teacher NPC;
- no copied school / sect ladder;
- no generic "modern advanced, ancient backward".

# 1. Mandatory shared functions

Every viable option must satisfy:
1. first connection quickly changes a present decision;
2. both protagonists actively want another connection;
3. each interaction leaves persistent residue;
4. each protagonist owns independent local story;
5. information gap evolves from simple information into identity / institution / relationship differences;
6. both worlds socially revalue their local protagonist over time;
7. physical crossing is not required early;
8. delayed ontology, not delayed payoff.

# OPTION 1｜共修第三条路
label:
> SAFEST / closest to current V1

## One-line engine
> 两个人各自拥有一半有效答案，只有在两个世界都练通的东西，才会成为他们共同的新道。

## Mechanic
- modern protagonist: broad standardized knowledge / tests / public data;
- ancient protagonist: embodied original cultivation / natural environment / live danger;
- they do NOT share qi / realm;
- a principle becomes part of the live seed only after both independently make it work.

## Early pleasure
Interaction 1:
> one side identifies the other's blind spot.

Interaction 2:
> they combine two incomplete methods into one small technique.

Interaction 3:
> both local worlds independently verify the result and begin revaluing them.

## Long engine
> one shared principle → technique family → original path → two local branches.

## Relationship engine
Borrow from:
- 《泼刀行》: same road, different paths;
- 《诡秘之主》: remote exchange + independent local problems;
- 《从姑获鸟开始》: separation strengthens later reunion.

## Hot-work functions borrowed
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》: every other-world contact changes current choice;
- 《我有一个修仙世界》: different comparative advantages;
- 《初圣》: repeat on changed state;
- 《九种魔药》: immediate proof / next-slot desire.

## Strength
- logical;
- supports equal protagonists;
- long-run capacity is clear.

## Main risk
> "共同研究功法" can feel academic / consultancy-like.
If scenes become:
> diagnose → discuss → optimize,
the premise loses heat.

# OPTION 2｜活种会“实时长大”
label:
> HIGH-CONCEPT / stronger title payoff

## One-line engine
> 别人的道种是一棵已经定型的树；我的道种是另一个世界里一个活人的人生，他每走出一步，我的道种都会长出新的枝。

## Core logic
Normal modern Dao seeds:
> finished / stabilized cultivation paths.

Modern protagonist's abnormal seed:
> bound to an unfinished living path being walked by ancient protagonist right now.

When ancient protagonist:
- makes a major cultivation choice;
- survives a dangerous breakthrough;
- learns a new way of using his Dao;
the modern live seed grows a corresponding branch.

But:
> modern protagonist does NOT automatically receive mastery.

He can:
- see the new branch;
- attempt it under modern conditions;
- generate a different local expression.

His successful modern expression then feeds BACK:
> ancient protagonist receives a new possible interpretation of his own Dao.

Thus:
> life → seed branch → alternate implementation → feedback → life changes.

## Early pleasure
Ch1:
> everyone else receives known seed category;
> protagonist's seed shows no fixed category because "source still developing."

First contact:
> hears the living source.

First payoff:
> the other person's immediate choice causes a visible new branch in his seed.

Second payoff:
> modern protagonist uses that branch differently and ancient protagonist discovers:
> "我的路居然还能这么走？"

## Reader's repeat desire
> "他下一次经历什么，我这颗道种又会长成什么？"

This is closer to 《九种魔药》's Open Slot:
> every new lived event can produce a different branch category.

## Relationship value
The ancient protagonist is not a teacher.
His LIFE is the evolving input.

The modern protagonist is not a student.
His local use becomes experimental feedback that can alter the ancient protagonist's future choices.

## Hot-work functions borrowed
- 《九种魔药》: each new input can yield a qualitatively different output;
- 《初圣》: each iteration changes later option set;
- 《原来我是仙道天骄》: contact immediately changes practical options;
- 《我的化身》: persistent identity causes social consequences;
- 《没钱修什么仙？》: modern society can measure a seed that keeps changing and react.

## Long expansion
Early:
> one branch / one choice.

Mid:
> modern institutions discover an unregistered seed is continuously updating.

Ancient:
> local elders discover his Dao repeatedly develops "impossible second answers."

Later:
> both become co-authors of a living path.

## Strength
> "活人就是会长大的道种" is extremely literal and title-native.

## Risk
- must define what counts as a "major event" or seed could update every page;
- branch UI can become game-system flavored if over-labeled.

# OPTION 3｜我绑定的是“道种源头本人”
label:
> MYSTERY + INSTITUTION / strongest later reveal potential

## One-line engine
> 现代人以为道种是祖先留下的修炼模板；我的道种却告诉我——它的“源头”现在还活在另一个世界。

## Core logic
Modern civilization believes:
> Dao seeds are stable patterns distilled from historical cultivation data.

Actual deeper possibility, NOT revealed early:
> some seed patterns are resonances with cultivation paths across worlds.

Most are static because:
- source is complete / inaccessible / no longer changing from modern perspective.

Protagonist accidentally connects to:
> a live, bidirectional source.

## Early story
Do NOT reveal the whole ontology.

Ch1:
> his seed behaves unlike any registered type.

Ch2:
> the "seed" talks.

Ch3:
> they verify each other and a small change on one side affects the seed's function.

Reader only knows:
> this Dao seed is a real person somewhere.

## Information-gap engine
Modern protagonist knows:
> how his society interprets / classifies this seed.

Ancient protagonist knows:
> what he is actually experiencing when creating the underlying path.

They constantly compare:
> "what your world thinks this thing is"
vs
> "what it really feels like from the source."

## Long surprise
Later institutional question:
> if his seed has a living source, where do ordinary Dao seeds come from?

This can grow naturally into:
- ownership;
- copying;
- legitimacy;
- forced resonance;
- whether a civilization can patent / mass-produce a living Dao.

## Hot-work functions borrowed
- 《我有一个修仙世界》: institutional differences can sustain long-form;
- 《让仙门再次伟大》: rules surface when identity / rights / control become valuable;
- 《诡秘之主》: remote actors + hidden structure can grow from useful early interaction;
- 《仙道天骄》: two evaluation systems reinterpret the same object.

## Strength
- title becomes mystery as well as gimmick;
- strongest C-type long horizon;
- modern Dao-seed society and ancient protagonist are causally tied.

## Risk
- can become lore-heavy;
- if "where seeds come from" appears too early, it burns the book's later ceiling;
- first 20 chapters still need strong small payoffs independent of mystery.

# OPTION 4｜活种不是一条路，而是一棵“可能性树”
label:
> BOLDEST / combines live-person + choice fantasy

## One-line engine
> 别人的道种只有一条成熟路线；我的道种连着一个还没定型的活人，所以他每遇到一次真正的人生选择，我这里都会长出不同的可能分支。

## Core mechanic
Ancient protagonist is living an unfinished cultivation life.

At genuine forks:
- fight / retreat;
- accept lineage / reject it;
- choose sword / body / formation solution;
- save someone / preserve an opportunity;

the live seed does NOT show the future.

It shows:
> multiple cultivation expressions implied by that fork.

Modern protagonist can test one branch under his own world's safer / different conditions.

The result becomes feedback:
> not prophecy,
but evidence about what that kind of choice might produce.

Ancient protagonist still decides for himself.

## Why this is two-way
Ancient protagonist's life generates branches.

Modern protagonist's experiments reveal:
> branch properties / costs / unexpected possibilities.

Then ancient protagonist may:
- choose differently;
- combine branches;
- reject modern result because his local responsibility differs.

Thus:
> one lives the fork;
> one explores its possibilities.

## Reader repeat desire
> "这次他遇到新的选择，会长出什么分支？"

## Social engine
Modern society:
> protagonist appears to possess a seed that violates fixed specialization.

Ancient society:
> ancient lead's Dao repeatedly avoids becoming one known lineage.

## Hot-work functions borrowed
- 《九种魔药》: next input = unknown category payoff;
- 《初圣》: alternate attempts change available routes;
- future-self concept's appeal WITHOUT using actual future selves;
- 《没钱修什么仙？》: life choices must affect education / career / status;
- relationship scout: different responsibilities create real disagreement.

## Strength
- extremely compatible with a dual protagonist;
- every major character choice can also be a cultivation event;
- relationship and growth mechanism become the same thing.

## Risk
- hardest to explain simply;
- can feel like branching-game mechanics;
- must avoid making modern protagonist "optimize" the ancient protagonist's life like a player.

# OPTION 5｜两人共享的不是力量，而是“盲区”
label:
> RELATIONSHIP-HEAVY / anti-answer-machine

## One-line engine
> 道种不给我他的能力，只会让我看见他自己看不见的修炼问题；他也能看见我世界替我遮住的盲区。

## Core mechanic
The live connection does NOT transfer answers.

It exposes:
> blind spots.

Modern protagonist may see:
- ancient habit whose risk has been normalized locally;
- missing comparison case;
- hidden pattern.

Ancient protagonist may see:
- modern standardized technique's dead angle;
- what simulations cannot reproduce;
- how certification incentives distort actual cultivation.

Neither receives the solution.

They need to:
> persuade / test / solve it themselves.

## Early pleasure
First contact:
> one tells the other an impossible-to-know flaw.

They distrust each other.

The flaw is verified.

Next interaction:
> the direction reverses.

Now each has reason to keep the other alive / listening.

## Repeat engine
Every higher realm creates new blind spots because:
- local experts normalize local assumptions;
- outsider perspective becomes more valuable as specialization deepens.

## Hot-work functions borrowed
- 《学习群》: reciprocal value, not one-way rewards;
- 《诡秘之主》: remote information exchange + independent local stakes;
- 《我有一个修仙世界》: different knowledge-production systems create structural gaps;
- relationship scout: objective conflict and permission ladder.

## Strength
- naturally equal;
- ancient protagonist cannot become resource NPC;
- modern protagonist cannot become Wikipedia.

## Risk
- less flashy title payoff;
- if every arc is "spot flaw → fix flaw", it becomes diagnostic fiction;
- needs other reward outputs.

# OPTION 6｜双界对同一条道“判分完全不同”
label:
> SOCIAL-STATUS / identity-compounding

## One-line engine
> 我们走的是同一条道，但在两个世界，它一次次被判成完全不同的东西。

## Core premise
The live seed connects two people cultivating related / shared principles.

Same result:
- modern world may classify it as unstable / low-grade / unregistered;
- ancient world may treat it as a rare orthodox phenomenon;
OR sometimes the reverse.

Important:
> the direction cannot always favor the protagonists.

A technique can be:
- high-scoring modern but taboo ancient;
- respected ancient but illegal modern;
- useless under one environment but crucial in the other.

## Pleasure engine
Every meaningful co-created result gets TWO social evaluations.

That creates:
- double status movement;
- double opportunity;
- double conflict;
- double pressure.

## Hot-work functions borrowed
- 《仙道天骄》: same person / ability revalued by two systems;
- 《没钱修什么仙？》: recognized metrics alter resources;
- 《我有一个修仙世界》: institutional access compounds;
- 《让仙门再次伟大》: identity changes permissions.

## Strength
- excellent for social revaluation;
- modern society is not wasted;
- ancient society remains equally active.

## Risk
- closest to 《仙道天骄》's identity-difference function;
- must avoid "here rubbish, there genius" repetition;
- by itself relationship may be weak.

# 2. Combination families

Options are not all mutually exclusive, but only one should be the PRIMARY mechanic.

## COMBO P｜2 + 5 + 6
Primary:
> live seed grows with the ancient protagonist's real life.

Support:
- blind-spot reciprocity;
- dual social evaluation.

Reader waits for:
> "他下一次经历什么，这颗活种会长出什么；两个世界又会怎么判它。"

Commercially immediate and relationship-safe.

## COMBO Q｜3 + 1 + 6
Primary:
> protagonist binds the living source behind a Dao seed.

Support:
- co-create a third path;
- two institutions evaluate it differently.

Reader waits for:
> "这个活人到底和现代道种体系是什么关系？两边共同创造的新道会走到哪？"

Strongest mystery / long horizon.

## COMBO R｜4 + 5
Primary:
> living person's real choices grow possibility branches.

Support:
- each exposes the other's blind spots.

Reader waits for:
> "下一次人生岔路会长出什么可能；两个人会怎样利用或拒绝它。"

Most character-native / most original, but hardest to execute.

## COMBO S｜1 + 6
Primary:
> co-create third path.

Support:
- every result receives two social evaluations.

Reader waits for:
> "下一门两界共创出来的东西是什么；它在两边会掀起什么反应。"

Safest and easiest to understand, but least distinctive of the strong pool.

# 3. Rejected / weak lanes

## Reject A｜modern database + ancient resources
Reason:
> too close to existing two-world cultivation geometry; insufficient title-level uniqueness.

## Reject B｜shared realm / shared qi
Reason:
> second protagonist becomes power battery; effort and local competence collapse.

## Reject C｜one side teaches, other side learns
Reason:
> teacher-student asymmetry kills dual protagonist promise.

## Reject D｜commodity / physical transfer as main early engine
Reason:
> merchant collapse already strongly evidenced.

## Reject E｜one smart / one reckless
Reason:
> fake contrast; same objective function.
