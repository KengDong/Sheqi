# Design correction

Do not ask AUTHOR to choose abstract terms such as:
- 共道;
- 可能性树;
- 双重判分;
- 盲区.

First prove:
- logic;
- immediate pleasure;
- repeat desire;
- relationship necessity;
- Ch1–20 causal arrangement.

Highest title-native law:
> **the protagonist's cheat is a person he cannot own.**

A living person must be mechanically irreplaceable:
- chooses;
- refuses;
- changes independently;
- can hide;
- can be hurt;
- has local obligations;
- may grow in a direction inconvenient to the protagonist.

# Shared early-world assumptions

Only operational logic is needed early.

Do NOT force early explanation of:
> why exactly the cosmos matched these two people.

Early reader only needs to verify:
1. the other side is a real living person;
2. connection has stable observable rules;
3. it creates useful consequences.

Origin ontology can remain delayed, following:
> Delayed Ontology, Not Delayed Payoff.

# CANDIDATE 1｜人生写种
working hook:
> **你活成什么，我的道种就长成什么。**

## Core rule

Normal Dao seed:
> a stabilized path written by people who already finished proving it.

Living Dao seed:
> the source is still alive, so the seed is continuously unwritten.

Only when the other protagonist passes a REAL irreversible life / cultivation proof point does the seed gain a new trainable property / branch.

Not:
> he learns sword, I automatically learn sword.

Instead:
> he proves something through real stakes;
> my seed gains the POSSIBILITY corresponding to that proof;
> I must cultivate my own local expression.

If my local expression proves something new,
that alternate expression becomes feedback the source can understand / use.

Cycle:
> his life proves raw possibility
→ my seed grows
→ I cultivate a local version
→ my result feeds back
→ his next choices change.

## Why a living human is necessary

A recording / dead inheritance cannot:
- make a new choice tomorrow;
- refuse the route I want;
- unexpectedly change direction;
- create a new proof that never existed when the seed was bound.

The seed is valuable precisely because:
> its source is still becoming someone.

## Ch1 pressure

Modern protagonist reaches a real seed-selection / qualification threshold.

He needs:
> a usable seed because seed classification changes a concrete near-term route / access / resource.

Do NOT require humiliation.

His abnormal seed is:
> unclassified / unfinished.

That creates a real practical problem immediately.

End:
> the seed speaks.

## Ch2 verification

The voice belongs to a real young cultivator facing a local urgent problem.

They test:
- unknown terminology;
- local facts;
- cultivation sensation.

Both conclude:
> the other is not an inner demon / recording.

## Ch3 first concrete payoff

Ancient protagonist survives / resolves one small but real cultivation event through his own choice.

The modern seed changes immediately.

Not "+1".

It gains:
> a visible trainable property / new direction that did not exist before.

Modern protagonist uses that new possibility to solve his CURRENT practical problem:
- retest;
- training requirement;
- access threshold;
etc.

Reader proof:
> the living person's life really writes the seed.

## Why BOTH want contact #2

Modern protagonist:
> if the source keeps growing, my seed can keep producing new possibilities.

Ancient protagonist:
> the modern protagonist's first local adaptation reveals a version of his own new insight that he had never considered.

Thus:
> neither side is merely feeding the other.

## Second interaction MUST differ

The ancient protagonist makes a choice that has NOTHING to do with what modern protagonist wants.

The seed grows an unexpected branch.

Modern protagonist initially thinks:
> useless to me.

Then local pressure forces him to use it creatively.

This proves:
> the source is a person, not a vending machine.

## First true conflict

Modern protagonist later strongly wants the source to pursue a route likely to generate a useful branch.

Ancient protagonist refuses:
> "这是我的命，不是你的技能树。"

His local responsibility / relationship makes another choice more important.

The disagreement changes what the seed can become.

Mechanic and relationship are now the same conflict.

## Ch20 persistent state

By Ch20:
- seed has 2–3 qualitatively different proven branches;
- modern society can no longer classify it as a normal fixed seed;
- modern protagonist has converted at least one branch into a socially recognized result;
- ancient protagonist has used at least one modern-born alternate expression;
- both now negotiate rather than command / distrust;
- one earlier branch has already altered a later interaction.

## Ch100 same pleasure, larger scale

The live seed becomes:
> a cultivation path shaped by the ancient protagonist's real biography AND the modern protagonist's reinterpretations.

Reader still waits for:
> the next major life proof and what it will make possible.

Scale expands:
- technique;
- body;
- role;
- identity;
- institutional recognition.

But core pleasure remains:
> **a living person keeps writing a path nobody can predict.**

## Benchmark functions
- 九种魔药: Open Slot / qualitatively different next output.
- 初圣: each prior result changes later option set.
- 没钱修什么仙: local social ratification.
- 仙道天骄: other-world contact changes present choice.
- relationship benchmarks: independent choice / objective-based conflict.

## Main risk
> ancient protagonist becomes a human resource farm.

Hard mitigation:
- he can refuse;
- many useful branches come from choices unrelated to modern protagonist;
- modern protagonist's reinterpretation must feed value back;
- the seed cannot be optimized without respecting the source's independent life.

# CANDIDATE 2｜互为道关
working hook:
> **我的下一步仙路，得等另一个世界的人先把他的这一关活过去。**

## Core rule

Normal Dao seeds come from completed routes.

Their next milestones are known.

A living source has NOT completed the next milestone.

Therefore the modern protagonist's seed literally has:
> an unfinished next node.

He cannot receive a prewritten answer.

He can only:
- see the shape of the unresolved gate;
- try analogous approaches in his own world;
- send useful evidence / alternate methods back.

Only when the living source genuinely resolves that gate does the seed's next route become real.

To keep equality:
> when the modern protagonist solves the same underlying problem under modern conditions, he creates an alternate proof that the ancient protagonist may use.

Both must independently pass their local version before the shared route stabilizes.

## Why a living human is necessary

A dead / finished inheritance already has all milestones fixed.

Only a living source can produce:
> a next step that does not yet exist.

## Ch1 pressure

Modern protagonist binds an unfinished seed at a moment when he NEEDS progress / classification.

The seed's path stops after the first small node.

He hears the source while trying to understand why.

## Ch2 verification

They discover:
> the exact bottleneck shown in the seed is the ancient protagonist's current real cultivation problem.

That is the first impossible proof.

## Ch3 first payoff

Modern protagonist uses modern conditions to test one safer / alternate way through part of the gate.

Ancient protagonist uses that information to survive / partially resolve his real bottleneck.

The seed immediately unlocks:
> one concrete next capability / cultivation direction.

Modern protagonist can now use it to change his current situation.

## Why BOTH want contact #2

Their progress is not shared for free.

But:
> each has access to experiments / conditions / viewpoints the other lacks.

The next gate is now a mutually valuable problem.

## Second interaction differs

The next gate is NOT technical.

It may involve:
- a real combat environment;
- social permission;
- a choice between two incompatible paths.

The modern side cannot solve it with data alone.

This prevents:
> every arc = look up answer.

## First true conflict

Modern protagonist may NEED the source to attempt a route because his own path is stuck.

Ancient protagonist may rationally refuse because:
- local family;
- sect;
- life;
- timing.

Thus:
> one person's progression pressure directly conflicts with the other's life priorities.

## Ch20 persistent state

- first major gate completed by both in different ways;
- their two local solutions produce distinct branches;
- both worlds recognize local results;
- they have an explicit rule:
  > no one gets to force the other through a gate for personal gain.

This becomes a permanent relationship boundary.

## Ch100 same pleasure

Higher "gates" stop being simple techniques.

They can become:
- role;
- vow;
- responsibility;
- organizational access;
- philosophy.

Core remains:
> the next step cannot be prewritten because the source is alive.

## Benchmark functions
- 初圣: route changes from accumulated attempts.
- 仙道天骄: each contact changes present action.
- relationship scout: objectives / responsibilities create real conflict.
- 我有一个修仙世界: different environments preserve exclusive bottlenecks.

## Main risk
> can feel like game quests / solve-the-bottleneck-of-the-week.

Hard mitigation:
- gates must vary in category;
- not every gate has a clean solution;
- some gates resolve through character choice, not optimization.

# CANDIDATE 3｜彼此的人生就是对方的“活道场”
working hook:
> **别人的道种里是前人留下的训练记忆；我的道种里，是另一个世界正在发生的人生。**

## Core rule

Modern Dao seeds normally contain:
> stabilized experiential traces / training patterns from completed cultivation routes.

They help a user:
- understand pressure;
- rehearse a technique;
- experience a safe abstraction of a proven path.

Protagonist's seed is live.

Instead of archive traces,
it receives limited **living echoes** from the other protagonist's real high-stakes experiences.

Not full memory.
Not replaying his whole life.
Only moments where his Dao was genuinely tested.

Modern protagonist can later enter a bounded echo and attempt the SAME underlying challenge with:
> his own body / knowledge / modern methods.

If he finds an alternate solution,
that solution can be fed back as insight to the living source.

At higher trust / connection,
selected echoes can be shared deliberately.

## Why a living human is necessary

A dead seed only gives:
> fixed historical training.

A living source generates:
> new real challenges, new pressure, new context.

And crucially:
> he owns his experiences and can refuse to share deeper ones.

Thus permission / trust is mechanically meaningful.

## Ch1 pressure

Modern protagonist needs a seed because seed training is required for a concrete progression / qualification.

His seed is classified:
> empty / defective — no archived training trace.

Then a completely nonstandard live scene appears.

## Ch2 verification

What modern protagonist sees / senses is not a simulation:
> the other protagonist is currently dealing with a real situation.

They establish contact and discover:
> neither controls the other's body.

## Ch3 first concrete payoff

Ancient protagonist survives one real challenge.

That experience leaves the first living echo.

Modern protagonist enters the echo afterward and attempts the pressure with his own method.

He gains:
> a concrete combat / cultivation understanding unavailable in normal training seeds.

He uses it immediately in his modern local test / problem.

## Why BOTH want contact #2

Modern protagonist:
> the other world produces real conditions modern standardized training cannot reproduce.

Ancient protagonist:
> modern protagonist can replay / analyze the challenge under different assumptions and send back an alternate solution for future encounters.

He becomes:
> not a spectator, but a second solver.

## Second interaction differs

Modern protagonist's OWN standardized training / social challenge creates something valuable to the ancient protagonist:
- precision;
- repeatability;
- a technique constraint;
- a perspective on the same principle.

The ancient protagonist gains from an experience he could never access locally.

Now exchange is genuinely two-way.

## First true conflict

The live seed surfaces an echo the ancient protagonist considers private / traumatic / not the modern protagonist's business.

Even if access was accidental,
the issue becomes:
> who owns a lived experience once it becomes Dao-seed material?

They establish the first permission boundary.

This makes relationship progression mechanically visible.

## Ch20 persistent state

- several living echoes exist, each from genuinely different local situations;
- modern protagonist has one capability / status gain directly traced to an echo;
- ancient protagonist has adopted one alternate solution from modern re-experimentation;
- access permissions have changed after conflict;
- one local institution notices protagonist's seed contains nonstandard real-time training content.

## Ch100 same pleasure

Each world becomes:
> an exclusive training reality the other can never fully reproduce.

Connection can upgrade from:
- after-the-fact echo
→ partial live perception
→ consensual shared training
→ much later physical meeting.

Core pleasure remains:
> **I can learn from a real person's changing life, and he can learn from what I do with it.**

## Benchmark functions
- 九种魔药: every new input can yield a different output type.
- 学习群: reciprocal remote value.
- 诡秘之主: remote actors + permission / delegation.
- 泼刀行 / 姑获鸟: independent local experiences brought back into relationship.
- 没钱修什么仙: gains must affect real modern status / access.

## Main risk
> can degenerate into "new instance every arc."

Hard mitigation:
- echoes are not random content;
- only Dao-defining high-stakes experiences generate them;
- old echoes remain useful / reinterpretable;
- local plot must matter independent of whether an echo is produced.
