# Naming rule

Reader-facing title should sell:
1. modern ordinary / low-background protagonist;
2. source-era access;
3. relationship / hidden-background payoff;
4. easy one-glance fantasy.

Avoid titles centered on:
- 界痕
- 源流
- 因果锚
- 溯源
- 投资框架
- 返乡计划

These are internal mechanics, not click fantasy.

> [familiar genre anchor] + [one concrete impossible advantage]

> [别人... ] + [我...]

> crossing + source + history + background + Earth + mastermind

in one title.
