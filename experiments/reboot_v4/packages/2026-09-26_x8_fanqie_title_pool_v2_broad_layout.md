# Correction

V1 over-centered:
> 祖师 / 找祖师 / 祖师欠人情.

This narrows the book incorrectly.

Actual long-form engine includes:
- future founders;
- obscure people who become important only after intervention;
- failed bets;
- medicine / technique / institution origins;
- preserved resources;
- lineage favors;
- historical relationship capital;
- deliberate long-horizon layout across multiple eras.

Therefore title should sell:
> **过去布局 → 现代兑现**
rather than:
> **专门找祖师.**

# Title architecture 1｜过去布局 / 现代兑现

A1｜《全民修仙：我在过去给自己铺路》
A2｜《现代修仙：我在过去留下了后手》
A3｜《全民修仙：我在过去给未来留后手》
A4｜《我在过去布局，回来一路翻盘》
A5｜《修仙大学：我的后手全在过去》

Core fantasy:
> broadest representation of X8.

# Title architecture 2｜隐藏背景 / 过去积累

B1｜《别人修仙拼背景，我的底牌都在过去》
B2｜《别人拼家世，我的后手都在过去》
B3｜《全民修仙：我的人脉都在过去》
B4｜《现代修仙：我的背景来自过去》
B5｜《别人靠祖辈，我靠自己在过去留下的人》

Core fantasy:
> modern low-visible-status × hidden accumulated capital.

Risk:
> "人脉" is narrower than resources / techniques / institutions.
> "背景" is strong but can imply only relationships.

# Title architecture 3｜跨时代能力 direct

C1｜《全民修仙：我能回到过去》
C2｜《现代修仙：我能进入过去》

C5｜《我能去到一切还没开始的时候》

# Current strongest broad-fit shortlist

## T1
> 《别人修仙拼背景，我的底牌都在过去》

Why:
- broader than “背景都在过去”;
- covers:
  - techniques;
  - resources;
  - people;
  - founder favors;
  - hidden source knowledge.

## T2
> 《全民修仙：我在过去给自己铺路》

Why:
- directly expresses long-form engine;
- broad enough for many ancient contact types;
- modern climb remains implied.

## T3
> 《现代修仙：我在过去留下了后手》

Why:
- very broad;
- leaves room for failed / delayed / varied returns;
- “后手” has male-frequency flavor.

## T4
> 《全民修仙：我的底牌都在过去》

Why:
- simple;
- strong hidden-capital fantasy;
- no overcommitment to who/what exists in the past.

## T5
> 《我在过去布局，回来一路翻盘》

Why:
- most direct on action + payoff;
- slightly more colloquial / Fanqie.

# Strongest thematic line for packaging

> 现代我只是普通修仙生。
> 过去我帮过的人、改过的法、留下的资源和关系，
> 正一笔一笔变成我往上爬的底牌。

# Naming principle going forward

Title should NOT encode a specific ancient-contact type.

It should encode:
> **past-side agency + present-side payoff.**

Ancient contacts remain free to vary:
- future founder;
- forgotten healer;
- failed genius;
- merchant / organizer;
- future enemy;
- anonymous craftsman;
- institution origin point.

This preserves long-form diversity.
