# 1. Cheng Ye decision line｜whole chapter

## Scene A｜抢单
External:
> a high-value crowdwork order appears.

Cheng Ye notices:
> bonus first, then conditions.

His interpretation:
> this is a legal, insured way to get paid while also stress-testing himself.

His choice:
> grab it quickly.

Personality proof:
- practical;
- fast;
- money-aware without misery;
- reads rules for opportunity.

Do NOT prove humor with a punchline.
One light Earth callback is enough.

Transition:
> order requires immediate arrival -> he goes.

## Scene B｜training hall arrival
External:
> Zhou Hang is already training because HE paid for the session.

Cheng Ye notices:
- Zhou is serious;
- buyer has a specific weak point;
- coach controls test conditions.

His interpretation:
> this is not social sparring; he is here to provide pressure and get paid.

His choice:
> cooperate professionally, not ingratiate himself.

Personality proof:
- can deal with strangers normally;
- no need to make everyone laugh;
- respects a clear transaction.

Zhou Hang goal:
> get his own high-pressure flaw exposed.

Coach Shao goal:
> produce a useful report safely.

Transition:
> test begins because all three goals align.

## Scene C｜first exchange
External:
> Cheng Ye successfully exposes Zhou Hang's problem.

At same time:
> Cheng Ye's own old flaw is incidentally exposed.

Important asymmetry:
- Zhou cares about his own report;
- Coach notices both;
- Cheng Ye already knows his flaw and is mildly annoyed it showed up again.

Cheng Ye interpretation:
> same old problem; no new information yet.

His choice:
> do not derail paid session to talk about himself.

Personality proof:
- not protagonist-centered;
- can keep business/business;
- slight self-mockery stays internal/light.

Transition:
> Zhou requests another hard round because first sample was useful.
> Coach agrees.
> Cheng Ye sees more pressure as both good for the job and potentially useful to himself.

## Scene D｜voluntary risk
External:
> second round pressure rises.

Cheng Ye notices:
- platform rewards rare/extreme valid samples;
- his own flaw only appears under pressure;
- Zhou explicitly wants stronger pressure;
- safety conditions are active.

His interpretation:
> if he keeps pre-avoiding his own weak point, he is wasting both the buyer's money and his own chance to learn something.

His choice:
> intentionally stop giving himself the comfortable escape route.

THIS is the key Cheng Ye action.
Humor/personality lives here:
> rule-reading -> slightly unconventional conclusion -> he really does it.

Transition:
> because he personally enters the bottleneck, resonance can occur.

## Scene E｜source overlap
External:
> rain sound + source-era scene overlaps.

Cheng Ye first response:
> NOT immediately awe / joke.
> He stays on the current fight because Zhou Hang is still attacking.

He notices:
- same technique;
- same structural bottleneck;
- source actor has no retreat because of HIS own crisis;
- risky transition.

His interpretation:
> not "ancient secret technique";
> simply: that person solved the exact problem in front of him by taking a path modern teaching does not use.

His choice:
> copy once under pressure.

Personality proof:
- quick learner;
- dares to test;
- accepts risk on himself;
- does not need certainty before action when evidence is immediate.

Transition:
> copied branch works -> modern payoff.
> But source scene remains -> mystery escalates.

## Scene F｜reciprocal gaze
External:
> Ji Changgeng turns and sees him.

Cheng Ye state change:
> humor drops.

He notices:
- Ji's reaction is not recording-like;
- gaze timing follows Cheng Ye's own action;
- source actor has independent emergency: injury / pursued / protecting something.

His interpretation:
> whatever this is, it is interactive.

His choice:
> stop joking, remember details.

Personality proof:
> light normally, steady when impossible becomes real.

Transition:
> overlap ends -> he needs evidence.

## Scene G｜modern verification
External:
> Zhou's paid report is completed.
> Cheng Ye's abnormal sample is separately flagged for verification.

Zhou Hang behavior:
> stays mostly on his own result.
> May ask once whether the strange move can be repeated only because it affected his report.
> Once coach stops the test, Zhou has no reason to become protagonist assistant.

Coach behavior:
> checks safety + lineage because that is his job.

Cheng Ye interpretation:
> first question is whether the move is reproducible / dangerous;
> second is whether what he saw has an external trace.

His choice:
> ask to search historical lineage because platform must verify the bonus anyway.

Personality proof:
> practical even under mystery.
> He uses an existing institutional process instead of giving a speech about time travel.

Transition:
> archive returns a face.

## Scene H｜archive identity
External:
> Ji Changgeng identified as ancient cultivator from ~4,000 years ago; later high realm/long life.

Important presentation:
> archive does NOT read like a full CV.
Only enough facts:
- name;
- age/distance;
- connection to technique lineage;
- one detail that matches visual memory.

Cheng Ye notices:
> face / scar / one source-era detail.

His interpretation:
> the person he saw was not a generic founder image.

Final state:
> he now has one impossible fact:
> a man dead for millennia reacted to him in real time.

End line should be concrete, not explanatory.

# 2. Cheng Ye personality continuity test

Across the chapter he should make these choices:
1. grab a useful paid opportunity;
2. treat buyer/coach normally;
3. keep his own issue from hijacking paid session;
4. voluntarily expose his weak point because rules/incentives make it rational;
5. copy a risky move because he understands its immediate value;
6. become serious when Ji sees him;
7. use platform verification to test reality.

This is one continuous person.

If a prose version removes any two of these:
> Cheng Ye will flatten.

He does NOT:

# 5. Ji Changgeng independent line

Before Cheng Ye sees him:
> already in a crisis.

He wants:
> preserve / deliver / protect something important (working: medicine box or equivalent).

He is:
> being pursued / blocked / forced into a dangerous movement solution.

His risky branch exists because:

He does not demonstrate it for Cheng Ye.

When he sees Cheng Ye:
> his first concern is intrusion / unknown observer during crisis,
not curiosity about future technique theory.

# 6. Scene transition chain

Order -> travel -> arrival -> test -> valid buyer result -> buyer asks harder repeat -> Cheng Ye accepts more pressure -> his own bottleneck -> resonance -> risky copy -> payoff -> reciprocal gaze -> safety stop -> institutional verification -> archive identity.

Every next beat comes from:
> previous consequence or actor choice.

No paragraph may appear solely because:
> author needs to explain something.

# 7. Prose drafting guard

New draft should use close-third around Cheng Ye.

Narrator may describe:
- what he sees;
- what he recognizes;
- what he chooses;
- one light inference.

Narrator should rarely announce:
- "this means";
- "this is reasonable";
- "the problem is";
- "this is exactly";
- "this was like a paying customer";
- "he finally understood the key."

Let consequences carry meaning.
