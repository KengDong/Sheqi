# X7｜Ch1–20 Pleasure Stress Test V1

date: 2026-09-26
branch: reboot-v4-dualworld-breakout-framework
status: AUTHOR REVIEW / NOT CANON

# Goal

Stress-test X7 against the SAME early-pleasure bar as 《原来我是仙道天骄》.

Must prove:
- Ch2 identity contrast;
- Ch3 concrete payoff;
- Ch10 public modern revaluation;
- Ch20 major hidden-history / social payoff;
- repeat engine remains simple;
- no DLC / research-paper / legal-thriller smell.

# 0. Modern-world correction

Do NOT use:
> "poor students only get half a cultivation technique."

Use a modern education inequality model:

Everyone has:
- nationally / school-provided standard foundational techniques;
- basic safety annotations;
- standard exercises.

What costs money / access:
- famous-teacher correction courses;
- individual compatibility diagnosis;
- high-quality sparring data;
- advanced training plans;
- competition modules;
- private lineage annotations;
- premium recovery / medicine / equipment;
- extra training hours.

Therefore:
> textbook is public;
> coaching / adaptation / upward mobility is expensive.

This is structurally closer to real modern education and to the social logic learned from 《没钱修什么仙？》.

# 1. Core early premise

Modern protagonist is an ordinary student using a standard public technique.

He has a concrete deficiency:
> his circulation / movement is technically "qualified" but inefficient under his own body condition.

A paid personalized correction course could likely solve it,
but he cannot afford / access it before a near-term practical test.

He searches:
- old editions;
- founder notes;
- expired public lectures;
- free source materials.

This is ordinary modern behavior:
> no money for tutoring → self-study old / free material.

While tracing the technique to its earliest surviving version,
he reaches the upstream era / source person.

Early ontology can remain unexplained.

# 2. Historical target

Do NOT pick a random nobody with no reader-recognizable significance.

Use:
> a famous MODERN founder whose historical early life is obscure.

Modern side already establishes in one or two lines:
- the technique is standard / important;
- the founder's portrait / name is familiar;
- his early drafts are historically incomplete.

Upstream:
> protagonist meets him BEFORE fame,
> before the technique is complete,
> while he is low-status / struggling / not yet a "祖师".

This gives immediate reader knowledge:
> this ordinary-looking young cultivator becomes a major figure later.

# 3. Ch1｜I cannot afford correction

Modern life:
- near-term school practical test / ranking / qualification;
- protagonist's standard technique is stuck at a measurable weak point;
- teacher says the issue is individual adaptation, not missing basics;
- optional paid solution exists:
  - famous-teacher correction clinic / course;
  - personalized body-data analysis;
but protagonist cannot pay / get a slot.

He searches old free material.

He notices a strange inconsistency:
> current standard edition contains a movement whose old explanation is missing / contradictory.

He follows the source note.

End:
> source material / cultivation state suddenly becomes "live";
> he sees a young cultivator practicing the primitive version.

The young cultivator turns:
> "你偷学我的法？"

Pleasure:
- present-day pain established;
- famous modern method has a living source;
- source founder appears BEFORE fame.

# 4. Ch2｜You know the move I haven't invented

The modern protagonist recognizes:
> this is the future founder.

He proves it not by saying "I am from the future",
but by performing / describing a mature downstream movement.

The young creator freezes.

Because:
> the movement solves the exact problem he has been unable to solve.

Then he asks:
> "为什么这里要沉肩半寸？"

Modern protagonist cannot answer.

He only knows:
> modern standard instruction.

The creator immediately understands:
> this stranger has seen a mature descendant version,
> but does not understand its original logic.

Identity contrast payoff:
> modern ordinary student / budget self-learner
becomes
> impossible future insider in front of a person modern society venerates as a founder.

To strengthen visceral reaction:
the creator does NOT call protagonist a genius.
He reacts as a creator:
> "这不是你偷来的。偷来的人不会只知道结果，不知道为什么。"
> "你见过它以后真正长成的样子。"

Now both need each other.

# 5. Ch3｜First source rewrite, first real modern gain

They test together:
- modern mature movement;
- ancient original bodily condition.

They discover:
> modern standardization removed a tiny branch that is actually better for protagonist's body type.

The creator writes / memorizes:
> a revised original branch.

Protagonist returns modern.

No giant reality rewrite.

Concrete changes:
1. current technique manual contains a new optional branch / historical note;
2. public explanation says it has always existed as an early branch;
3. protagonist alone remembers yesterday's manual lacked it.

He practices it.

His stuck metric improves enough to solve the immediate problem.

This is the Ch3 paid payoff:
> source contact → usable modern improvement.

End:
> provenance page now contains one new line:
> "early discussion record references an unidentified collaborator."

No reveal of identity yet.

# 6. Ch4–5｜Use it for money / current-life relief

Do NOT immediately return to history.

Let the modern gain cash out.

The protagonist:
- passes / improves a school practical metric;
- uses his understanding to help another student correct the same issue;
- realizes he can make small tutoring / sparring money OR earn school support / resource.

This ties cultivation advantage back to:
> money / school / social value.

Important:
> the historical engine changes modern life immediately.

# 7. Ch6｜Second contact is主动

Now protagonist actively returns to the source era.

Reason:
> he wants to know whether the new branch caused further consequences,
> and the creator explicitly has another unresolved point.

Agency Flip complete.

The creator has been living off-screen.

His local situation changed because of the first correction:
- a senior notices his technique;
- a local trial / rivalry opens;
- he has to decide whether to reveal the new branch.

This proves:
> upstream world is not frozen.

# 8. Ch7–8｜First non-technical gain

The creator's problem is now social, not just technical.

If he publishes the branch:
- he may gain status;
- but lose control / attract a local faction.

Modern protagonist checks downstream history:
> current records say the founder eventually joined organization A / suffered event B.

He warns him about one near-term documented consequence.

The creator does not obey automatically.

He changes only PART of the decision.

Modern return:
> downstream record changes slightly,
> producing a new historical note / lost sub-branch.

This shows:
> X7 is not only "fix technique versions."

# 9. Ch9–10｜First public modern revaluation

Modern school practical / competition / selection.

Other students know the same standard technique.

Protagonist's advantage:
> he understands why the standard movement exists AND where the lost branch applies.

In a real variable situation:
- standard execution fails / underperforms;
- protagonist switches to the source branch;
- achieves a result visibly above his previous level.

Teacher / coach reaction is institutional:
- asks where he learned it;
- checks official records;
- discovers it is a newly recognized old branch;
- offers protagonist:
  - training-team spot;
  - extra hours;
  - assistant / paid sparring;
  - recommendation;
depending final modern-life setup.

Ch10 hard payoff:
> cross-world secret has changed how modern society allocates resources to him.

This is the first true public revaluation.

# 10. Ch11–12｜Modern irony becomes sharper

Protagonist now gets access to material he previously could not afford / reach.

Inside a paid / restricted source-commentary course,
he sees the modern institution's authoritative explanation of the founder's early development.

He notices:
> it is wrong / incomplete in a way only someone who met the founder could know.

This is a new pleasure type:
> low-status user privately knows the origin better than the premium experts.

But do NOT immediately expose everyone as fools.
Modern scholarship is mostly correct; the gap exists because source records are incomplete.

# 11. Ch13｜Second source person enters naturally

The founder introduces:
> a peer / rival / healer / alchemist / formation student
whose later modern identity is also recognizable.

This person is NOT random.

He exists in the same innovation network.

Modern protagonist recognizes the name only after return.

New open slot:
> the "famous founders" were once one small social circle.

Reader now wants:
> who else is here before they become famous?

# 12. Ch14–15｜First conflict: future optimal ≠ present acceptable

Modern records show:
> if founder makes choice X, his future status becomes better / safer.

But source-era founder refuses because:
- a friend;
- teacher;
- local responsibility;
would be harmed.

Modern protagonist cannot simply optimize history.

They disagree.

Key:
> both are rational.

This prevents:
> protagonist uses history as spreadsheet.

# 13. Ch16｜Historical change hurts protagonist

Founder makes his own choice.

Modern protagonist returns and discovers:
- one source branch he was relying on no longer exists / changed;
- a modern advantage he expected is gone;
OR
- the modern course / lineage changed in a way that makes his immediate life harder.

This is crucial:
> upstream actor has agency and can hurt protagonist's interests.

Now the second world is not an obedient cheat.

# 14. Ch17–18｜Reverse inheritance, but small and concrete

Because protagonist still helped him despite disagreement,
the founder wants to repay him.

No item teleport.

He says:
> "你若真在后世，就去某地找。"

He leaves:
- an original handwritten sheet;
- a small cultivation tool;
- a sealed test piece;
something valuable but not world-breaking.

Modern protagonist searches.

The location still exists, but access is now part of modern reality:
- redevelopment;
- museum;
- sect property;
- abandoned industrial zone;
etc.

He must solve a CURRENT-world problem to retrieve it.

This makes the two worlds interlock rather than ancient gift → instant loot.

# 15. Ch19–20｜Major hidden-history identity payoff

The retrieval / source change triggers a modern historical-source re-evaluation.

A newly authenticated founder manuscript becomes public / institutional news.

It contains:
- a short exchange;
- a phrase;
- a correction;
from an unidentified collaborator.

Modern historians / teachers are interested because:
> this unknown collaborator appears at a key moment in the founder's technique formation.

The protagonist recognizes:
> those words are his.

Stronger variant:
the manuscript preserves an exact criticism / joke / phrasing he used.

Modern institution calls the figure:
> "佚名同参" / "无名道友" (working only).

At the same time:
- protagonist receives first real institutional access because of his modern performance / recovered source item;
- reader sees the enormous identity contrast:
  > in the present he is only beginning to gain school access;
  > in the historical origin of a mainstream technique, he is already becoming an unnamed co-participant.

Ch20 end desire:
> if one mainstream technique has already written him into its source history, what happens when he enters the next source node in the same generation?

PASS.

not merely a changed document.

PASS / CONDITIONAL.

- team spot;
- paid role;
- recommendation;
not generic praise.

PASS.

PASS / PROMISING.

# 18. Biggest remaining weakness

X7 still lacks one thing that 《原来我是仙道天骄》 has in spades:

> **a brutally obvious ancient-side status ladder.**

In current X7,
the ancient side gives:
- founder shock;
- peer collaboration;
- historical influence.

But not yet:
> immediate public "you are high value here" treatment.

Possible solutions for later design:
A. source-era people misclassify protagonist as a hidden lineage senior because he knows mature methods;
B. founder actively vouches for him, giving local social access;
C. protagonist is valuable as a "见过后世之法的人", creating a unique consultant / witness identity;
D. do NOT force ancient public status; instead make modern hidden-history status the larger contrast.

Need AUTHOR decision.
Do not fake a second 天骄 ladder just to imitate benchmark.

# 19. Verdict

X7 survives the Ch1–20 stress test.

But it does NOT yet prove commercial superiority to 《原来我是仙道天骄》.

Current strengths:
- natural cyber-cultivation anchor;
- Ch3 real payoff;
- self-refreshing information gap;
- modern social feedback;
- long-run hidden source identity;
- source-era actors retain agency.

Current weak point:
> ancient-side immediate public-status pleasure is still weaker.

Next best task:
> generate 3–4 concrete ways to make source-era interaction itself socially explosive WITHOUT copying "modern low-tier → ancient heavenly genius".
