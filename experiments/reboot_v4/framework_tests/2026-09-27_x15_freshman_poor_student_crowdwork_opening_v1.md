# REBOOT-V4｜X15 大一穷学生 × 众包呼应 × 早穿身份轻揭露 V1

date: 2026-09-27
branch: reboot-v4-dualworld-breakout-framework
status: AUTHOR REVIEW / OPENING IDENTITY TEST

# 0. Core identity

Protagonist:
- Earth-origin soul;
- crossed years ago, NOT fresh transmigration at Ch1;
- spent years actually adapting to cyber-cultivation society;
- has just passed university entrance / admission and is in the summer before freshman enrollment;
- ordinary / poor family in current world;
- needs to earn part of his own living / cultivation expenses.

Reader should NOT receive a biography dump.

Earth origin is exposed casually through:
> present action + one self-deprecating comparison.

# 1. Best callback

Earth:
> after Gaokao, protagonist ran Meituan crowdsourcing to earn university money.

Current world:
> after finally getting into a cultivation university, he is again doing crowdsourced gig work over summer.

Difference:
> last life delivered food / parcels;
> this life delivers real combat data / sparring samples.

Functional line:
> "穿了个越，就业方向倒没怎么变。"

Do not overexplain:
- exact Earth family;
- exact crossing scene;
- adaptation chronology.

One line is enough to establish:
> he remembers Earth, but this current world is already his lived life.

# 2. Why this works against benchmarks

## 《没钱修什么仙？》
Borrow:
> start from a familiar money / upward-mobility pressure.
Cultivation becomes part of:
- food;
- training;
- work;
- school;
- resource allocation.

Our difference:
> no fresh-transmigration exposition;
> university summer gig instead of school-interview / debt chassis.

## 《我有一个修仙世界》
Borrow:
> modern cultivation society is shown through normal infrastructure,
not lore lectures.

Our implementation:
> app order / insurance / rating / data settlement / school admission / training credit.

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
Borrow:
> cross-world contact must rapidly change a current-world choice / value.

Our implementation:
> first source anomaly changes a paid combat-data order in Ch1,
then changes his modern market / school valuation.

# 3. First-screen structure

Do NOT open with:
> "我叫X，我是穿越者."

Open with an order.

Possible functional shape:

[实战数据众包]
- 高压身法样本
- 预计时长 15 min
- 基础报酬 268 / 320
- 有效稀有动作 + bonus
- injury insurance included
- client: training company / incoming university student / reserve-team candidate

MC checks:
> if completed, it covers:
- several days of recovery;
- a personalized diagnostic session;
- dorm / registration / travel money.

Then one short internal joke:

> 上辈子高考结束，他跑美团众包。
> 这辈子考上修真大学，暑假还是众包。
> 穿了个越，就业方向倒挺稳定。
> 只是以前送外卖，现在把自己送上去挨打。

This exposes:
- Earth;
- prior life;
- early crossing;
- current poverty;
- university admission;
without pausing action.

# 4. Important tone

Do NOT make him bitter / miserable.

Preferred:
> slightly self-mocking, practical, experienced.

Reason:
he has lived in this world for years.

He should not think:
> "这个世界真神奇."

He should think like a native:
> which order pays better;
> which recovery patch is cheaper;
> which insurance clause matters;
> whether training credit can be exchanged after enrollment.

This proves:
> Earth is backstory;
> cyber-cultivation world is home.

# 5. Modern economic logic

He is "poor" in a relative upward-mobility sense.

Not starving.

He can:
- eat;
- live;
- attend university.

But cannot casually buy:
- private coach;
- personalized meridian / movement analysis;
- premium recovery;
- extra simulation / AI coaching compute.

Thus gig work is:
> a rational strategy to buy upward mobility.

This is closer to the strongest function from 《没钱修什么仙？》:
> money buys recovery / time / training quality / access.

# 6. Ch1 proposed flow

## Beat 1｜take order
MC accepts a high-value combat data job before university starts.

Reason:
> wants to earn enough for:
- first month training / recovery;
- optional freshman assessment advantage;
- better diagnostics.

## Beat 2｜arrive / verify
Modern cyber-cultivation infrastructure shown naturally:
- biometric / cultivation identity check;
- risk tier;
- injury insurance;
- data authorization;
- official rating.

No exposition block.

## Beat 3｜fight
Opponent is better-trained,
perhaps another incoming freshman / reserve trainee.

MC is competent but clearly lower-resource.

His recurring transition flaw appears.

## Beat 4｜source bleed
At the exact failure point,
another scene overlaps:
> ancient / source-era person performs a different version.

MC copies one step.

Modern:
> he reverses an exchange / forces rare edge-case data.

Platform:
> unmatched movement.

Ancient:
> person looks back.

## Beat 5｜money + hook
Order bonus increases.

MC's first grounded thought:
> this one move paid more than half a day of normal gigs.

Then the deeper anomaly:
> source-lineage matching returns an impossible historical similarity.

Ch1 end:
> same face.

# 7. Ch2 proposed flow

Platform freezes part of payout / flags data for verification.

Why:
- unregistered technique;
- potential tampering;
- abnormal lineage mismatch.

MC now has practical motivation:
> reproduce it to get paid / preserve rating.

While reproducing:
> overlap returns;
> two-way recognition begins.

He discovers:
> the ancient person is not a recording.

Earth-origin /界痕 still not explained.

# 8. Ch3 modern cash-out

After first reciprocal contact,
MC understands enough to reproduce the source action.

Formal retest:
- payout released;
- data contributor rating rises;
- better gig tier;
- school / training staff notice his result;
- he gets a useful freshman training / diagnostic opportunity.

This is the first:
> ability → formal proof → money / access.

# 9. Freshman timing advantage

Summer-before-enrollment is better than opening after school fully starts because:
- protagonist has a short-term need for money;
- he has just completed one social ladder (Gaokao / admission);
- new ladder is about to begin;
- Reader can share anticipation:
  > he enters university already carrying a secret edge.

Ch1–10 can bridge:
> summer gig → admission / registration → freshman assessment / training.

This avoids a cold school exposition opening.

# 10. Earth callback recurrence

Use Earth references sparingly.

Early:
> Meituan crowdsourcing callback.

Later:
- one familiar platform behavior;
- one old cultural habit;
- perhaps one moment of homesickness.

Do NOT use Earth memory as:
> endless joke machine / tech superiority.

Earth origin's deeper function remains:
- explains outsider soul;
- later explains history-memory immunity;
- supports homecoming mystery.

# 11. Strongest identity contrast

At Ch1:
> just-admitted poor freshman selling combat data.

Ancient/source side:
> historical actor / source event treats him as someone with impossible downstream knowledge.

Modern progression:
> sells body/time
→ sells skill
→ sells judgment
→ gains professional / institutional authority.

This mirrors:
> low visible modern value
↔ high hidden source value.

# 12. Opening principle

The reader should learn the protagonist is from Earth:
> because he jokes about his current gig.

The reader should learn the world is cyber-cultivation:
> because the gig itself could only exist in such a society.

The reader should learn he is poor:
> because he is calculating what this order buys.

The reader should learn he is going to university:
> because the money is being earned for the upcoming freshman stage.

No separate exposition scene needed.
