# Ch1｜最后一个补录名额

## Scene 1｜名单出来
- ordinary public cultivation university.
- core training / reserve roster is posted.
- protagonist is just below automatic cutoff.
- there is one supplementary practical challenge slot in several days.
- the roster matters because it carries:
  - free coach time;
  - recovery subsidy;
  - better training venue;
  - eligibility for later school selection.

Reader understands:
> one result changes money + training + future path.

## Scene 2｜coach diagnosis
Coach is not insulting.

After replaying protagonist's test:
> "your realm is enough; the problem is the transition."

Standard movement is fine for most people,
but protagonist loses force when shifting under pressure.

Coach suggests:
- school general correction session is full;
- individualized analysis / private correction is available but expensive.

He also gives a realistic alternative:
> self-study and come back for the supplementary assessment.

This keeps society fair.

## Scene 3｜ordinary money reality
Roommate / friend mentions:
- a better-off student already booked private correction;
- protagonist checks cost and decides not to spend that much.

Not poverty porn.

He can afford living expenses,
not every upward-mobility service.

## Scene 4｜self-study
Protagonist searches the standard movement's source evolution:
- current edition;
- older public explanation;
- founder source notes.

He has practiced it for years but never cared who created it.

He notices:
> one transition explanation is suspiciously vague.

Modern text says:
> "later standardized for broad compatibility."

He starts physically reproducing older transitions.

## Scene 5｜world-scar response
During deep practice:
- the scar / old crossing residue reacts;
- sound and light distort;
- he thinks he overtrained.

No long ontology.

The technique's rhythm becomes:
> not information but a direction.

He follows it instinctively.

## Scene 6｜source era
He opens his eyes outdoors / in a rough practice yard.

A young cultivator is repeating a primitive version.

Protagonist recognizes the motion before the face.

Then recognizes the face:
> the modern founder portrait,
except younger, harsher, alive.

The young man notices protagonist completing one of his unfinished transitions.

Weapon out.

End:
> "Who taught you that?"

# Ch2｜你见过它以后

## Scene 1｜misunderstanding
Young founder accuses protagonist of spying / stealing.

Protagonist:
> cannot prove identity,
> cannot even explain where he is.

He sees the founder retry the failed transition.

## Scene 2｜future move
Protagonist says:
> "your third step isn't supposed to end there."

He performs the modern mature version.

Founder freezes.

He copies it.

It works enough to expose the missing logic.

## Scene 3｜creator's test
Founder asks:
> "why shift force here?"
> "why shorten this angle?"
> "why does the fifth turn disappear?"

Protagonist answers only some from school knowledge.

Then gets stuck.

Founder realizes:
> this person knows the result,
> not the creation process.

Key line:
> "You haven't stolen my method."
> "You've seen what it becomes later."

## Scene 4｜reader identity payoff
Protagonist internally connects:
> this abrasive young man is the founder whose portrait hangs in modern teaching material.

Modern:
> protagonist cannot afford customized correction.

Source era:
> the founder of the method is personally asking him to show the future version again.

Do not overstate it.
Let the contrast carry itself.

## Scene 5｜first cooperative test
Founder refuses blind copying.

He makes protagonist run the mature movement against:
- uneven terrain;
- a sudden attack;
- source-era spiritual conditions.

Modern mature version exposes one limitation for protagonist's body.

Founder says:
> "This later form was made for everyone."
> "That means it stopped being made for you."

This is the source logic protagonist was missing.

End:
> founder proposes testing the abandoned branch.

# Ch3｜昨天没有这一式

## Scene 1｜co-create branch
They compare:
- future standardized form;
- founder's current primitive branch;
- protagonist's body rhythm.

They create a narrow variant.

Not stronger universally.

Better for:
> protagonist's specific transition / scenario.

Founder records it because:
> even if it never becomes the main line, it proves the path.

## Scene 2｜connection breaks
As the founder commits to the new branch:
- protagonist's perception tears;
- words cut out;
- source body destabilizes.

Founder reaches for him:
> "Wait—"

Protagonist is forced back.

Reader learns:
> intervention breaks the route,
without a system explanation.

## Scene 3｜modern disbelief
He wakes in his room / training space.

Checks time:
> little modern time passed.

He immediately opens the modern source material.

Yesterday's page:
> he remembers it clearly.

Today:
> a short early-branch note exists.

Everyone else treats it as ordinary history.

## Scene 4｜hard proof
He practices it.

The problem that cost him the roster place:
> materially improves.

Not complete mastery.

Enough that:
> the supplementary assessment is now within reach.

## Scene 5｜tiny historical residue
In the founder's biography / source note:
> a new sentence appears.

Working:
> "During early experimentation, he once discussed the method with an unidentified traveler; the record is fragmentary."

Protagonist knows:
> that's me.

## Final beat
He searches the young founder's early timeline.

The history is already different from what he remembers.

One later event / decision has changed.

Reader question:
> what did the founder do after I disappeared?

This drives Ch4–6.
