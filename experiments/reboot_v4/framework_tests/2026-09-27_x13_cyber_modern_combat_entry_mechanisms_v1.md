# 1. Option M-A｜实战数据众包单
status: CURRENT STRONGEST

## Reader-facing concept
Modern cultivation algorithms / coaches need huge quantities of real combat data.

Universities, training companies and technique developers post:
> 实战采样单 / 陪练数据单 / 功法适配单.

Students can accept them through a campus / city platform.

Payment depends on:
- risk tier;
- valid exchange time;
- data quality;
- whether rare movement / edge cases are triggered.

Platform provides:
- sensors;
- injury insurance;
- automatic settlement;
- official data record.

Familiar reference:
> Didi / Meituan order-taking + game matchmaking + wearable sports data + AI data labeling.

Freshness:
> cultivators literally sell real combat data to fund their own cultivation.

## Protagonist position
He is not a trash student.

He lacks enough money for:
- private correction;
- premium recovery;
- high-level individualized training.

So he takes:
> real combat data gigs.

This naturally integrates M1 + M4.

## Ch1 opening
Phone / wrist terminal:
> [High-pressure movement adaptation sample]
> base reward: 320
> valid rare-action trigger: +800
> injury insurance: platform covered
> client: school reserve-team candidate / technique lab

MC accepts.

He is literally being paid to:
> force a better-trained student to expose edge-case data.

This creates a low-status but competent role.

During fight:
> ancient/source overlap appears.

He follows one strange source step.

Result:
- opponent is broken out of rhythm;
- platform flags:
  > UNMATCHED MOVEMENT / source lineage mismatch;
- rare-action bonus triggers;
- human coach looks over;
- ancient young cultivator looks back.

Ch1 has:
> money reward + combat reversal + data anomaly + source hook.

## Ch1 final hook
Platform lineage matching:
> "No current registered branch."
> "Highest historical similarity: early source sample / unpublished fragment — 71%."

Then MC sees the historical portrait.

No full ancient cut.

## Ch2 stake
Platform freezes rare-data bonus / flags possible:
- illegal technique injection;
- unregistered inheritance;
- falsified motion data.

MC must retest / provide origin or risk:
- account downgrade;
- losing lucrative gigs;
- school compliance review.

This is much more modern than:
> opponent asks for rematch because pride.

While trying to reproduce it:
> two-way ancient contact establishes.

## Ch3 payoff
Formal retest confirms:
- technique is real;
- MC can reproduce;
- source logic improves his performance.

Outcome:
1. payout unlocks;
2. platform raises his sparring / data-contributor tier;
3. school coach offers reserve-team / advanced training trial;
4. personalized training credits become available.

Social revaluation is institutional, not crowd yelling.

# 2. Option M-B｜功法适配公测

## Concept
Before a new standardized technique / update is certified,
developers need diverse body-type users to test:
- compatibility;
- injury rate;
- energy efficiency;
- emergency response.

Universities recruit students as:
> 功法公测员.

Familiar:
> software beta test + clinical / sports trial + product testing.

Opening:
MC joins because:
- pays well;
- gives free diagnostic report.

Ancient overlap reveals:
> modern update removed / misunderstood an original branch.

MC produces abnormal compatibility result.

Pros:
- extremely cyber-cultivation;
- naturally connects technique origin to ancient source.
- source creator can become relevant immediately.

Cons:
- less combat drama unless public test includes sparring.
- can skew too "lab-like".

# 3. Option M-C｜灵网天梯悬赏

## Concept
Students / young cultivators have official city combat ranking.

The platform posts:
> challenge contracts / bounty matches.

Higher-ranked users can set:
- prize;
- training credits;
- sponsorship;
- opponent constraints.

Familiar:
> ranked matchmaking + esports + UFC fight card + gig bounty.

Opening:

Ancient anomaly flips a fight.

Pros:

Cons:

# 4. Option M-D｜企业功法压力测试

## Concept
Technique / equipment companies hire students to:
> pressure-test products / combat protocols.

Example:
- defensive clothing;
- movement assist module;
- recovery patch;
- cultivation algorithm.

MC is low-paid contract test combatant.

Ancient source move breaks the expected model and exposes a flaw.

Familiar:
> QA / crash test / beta test + gig labor.

Pros:
- work / company / money all immediate.
- can open higher social spaces naturally like 《没钱修什么仙？》 work line.

Cons:
- corporate plot can dominate too early.
- user may read it as "weird job" if overdesigned.

# 5. Option M-E｜校内训练算力兑换

## Concept
Students receive limited personalized AI coaching / simulation compute.

To earn extra compute:
> contribute valid sparring / training data.

Thus:
> no money → fewer personalized corrections.
> strong / useful data → more compute → better correction.

Familiar:
> cloud compute quota + academic credits + fitness apps.

Opening:
MC participates to earn enough compute for a key correction.

Ancient anomaly produces rare data,
so the system grants high-value compute / flags the move.

Pros:
- tight school loop.
- elegant reason for MC to fight.

Cons:
- reward feels abstract unless compute immediately produces something concrete.
- less adult economic texture than M-A.
