# 2. Opening correction

Old Alpha:
> roster announced
> → coach diagnosis
> → search old materials
> → direct source-world switch.

Problems:
1. first screen administrative;
2. protagonist is passive;
3. ancient cut feels like changing channels;
4. first modern visible win arrives too late.

New requirement:
> **Ch1 must contain action + money/status pressure + ancient anomaly + immediate modern reversal.**

Ancient world enters as:
> bleed / overlap / impossible second perspective,
not full map switch.

Full source-world embodiment only after reader already WANTS to meet the person.

# 3. PACKAGE F1｜陪练反客为主
status: CURRENT STRONGEST FANQIE FIT

## First-screen situation

Protagonist is NOT on the core team.

He is earning money as:
> paid practice opponent / sparring assistant for students competing for the university reserve-team places.

This instantly shows:
- school hierarchy;
- money;
- protagonist low-but-not-useless position;
- cultivation in modern economy;
- action.

Working first-screen line:
> "陪练两百一小时，打伤另算。唯一的规矩——别把正式队员打得太难看。"

Do not use exact sentence if prose changes later; function only.

## Ch1 first half｜modern pressure without exposition

Protagonist needs:
- training / recovery money;
- wants a future chance to enter better training,
but currently is outside the roster.

The student he is sparring:
- has personalized coaching;
- uses the same standardized movement system;
- is better prepared, not cartoon villain.

During the fight protagonist is repeatedly caught by the same transition flaw that has limited him for months.

No teacher lecture needed:
> Reader SEES the flaw hurting him.

## Ch1 anomaly｜ancient shadow appears inside the fight

At one critical movement,
protagonist sees:
> another training yard overlapping the modern arena for a fraction of a second.

A young stranger in old clothing is making the SAME transition.

But his step is different.

Not a system prompt.
Not a cut to another chapter/map.

Protagonist instinctively follows the shadow's step.

Result:
> he breaks the elite student's rhythm and lands a clean counter that he should not have been able to land.

Modern room goes quiet.

Immediate payoff:
> low-status paid sparring assistant forces the formal candidate to take him seriously.

## Ch1 second anomaly

The young shadow also stops.

For one instant:
> **he looks directly at protagonist.**

Meaning:
> this was not a recording / hallucination.

Then overlap ends.

## Ch1 public hook

Coach / instructor asks:
> "Where did you learn that transition?"

Protagonist does not know.

The training system cannot match it to the current standard branch,
but an instructor recognizes:
> it resembles an early source lineage / discarded method.

Important:
> no lore dump.

## Ch1 end

Protagonist looks at the wall / training interface containing the history of the movement.

He sees an old portrait / name.

The face:
> is the person who just looked back at him.

End question:
> **Why could a dead historical person see me?**

This is both:
- immediate proof;
- open slot;
- delayed ontology.

# 4. F1 Ch2｜他也看见了我

Ch2 should NOT spend entire chapter researching.

## Scene 1｜modern consequence

Because protagonist broke the agreed sparring pattern / showed unusual technique:
- coach offers a second demonstration;
OR
- the elite candidate demands a rematch;
OR
- a short video / instrument data is under review.

Protagonist now has something to lose:
> job / chance / credibility.

## Scene 2｜trying to reproduce the step

Alone, protagonist repeats the transition.

The ancient overlay returns faster.

This time:
- source person sees protagonist clearly;
- both can hear fragments.

Source person:
> "Who are you?"
> "Why are you walking the road I haven't finished?"

This is the first direct two-way contact.

No full crossing yet.

## Scene 3｜reciprocal proof

Protagonist recognizes the source person's future name.

Instead of announcing future identity,
he performs:
> the mature modern form of the next movement.

Source person freezes because:
> he has not created it.

Then source person shows:
> the original body-force logic that modern standardization later hid.

Both gain something.

## Ch2 end

Source person says a line with strong identity reversal:
> "You have seen what my road becomes."

He asks:
> "Then tell me—does this road survive?"

Now ancient interaction has a desire.

# 5. F1 Ch3｜陪练变候补

Ch3 must cash out in modern society.

## Modern retest / formal demonstration

Coach asks protagonist to reproduce the strange transition in:
- live spar;
- movement instrument;
- supplementary team evaluation.

Protagonist now understands enough source logic to use it intentionally,
not by luck.

He performs above his previous record.

Institutional result:
- he is no longer only paid sparring labor;
- he gets a reserve / trial-training invitation;
- free coach hours / training access;
- better pay / stipend opportunity.

This is the first social revaluation.

## E-causality closed-loop seed

Later in Ch3:
protagonist checks a deep / obscure source note.

It already contains:
> a fragment about an unnamed peer / "traveler" who once demonstrated a strange descendant movement.

Crucially:
> protagonist cannot prove the record changed.

It may have ALWAYS been there.

The phrase is something he said in Ch2.

Reader gets:
> **I may already be inside this history.**

Ch3 end:
> ancient source person appears again,
but time on his side has advanced.

Now the reader wants:
> what happened after I disappeared?
