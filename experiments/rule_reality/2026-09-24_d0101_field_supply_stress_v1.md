# D-0101F｜Rule Field Supply Stress V1
## 验证“副本本身能不能长期好看”，而不是只靠现实端救书

# Rule

These are NOT canon arcs.
They are supply stress.

Pass condition:
- 12 distinct premises;
- 6+ dominant solving modes;
- <=3 primarily written-rule-sheet fields;
- each field has:
  - visual hook;
  - human want/collision;
  - physical pressure;
  - present-world mirror;
  - potential return-rule family;
  - at least one way old R1 could matter or fail to matter.

# 1｜末班地铁没有终点
Mode:
> spatial pursuit + passenger social logic

Hook:
> the last train never reaches the terminal; each stop adds a passenger who insists they boarded earlier.

Human collision:
- one survivor desperately wants to “go home”;
- another wants everyone to stay together;
- an NPC conductor treats “home” as a role, not an address.

Physical:
> train cars progressively disappear / routes shorten.

Present mirror:
> current city's future transit line.

Return-rule family:
> travel / destination / passenger identity.

Why distinct:
> movement and route inference, not written rule sheet.

# 2｜急诊室只剩十二张床
Mode:
> role + resource triage

Hook:
> more injured people arrive every hour, but the number of legal “patients” cannot exceed twelve.

Human collision:
- save the worst injured;
- keep your teammate legally recognized as a patient;
- staff/NPCs have conflicting definitions of “treatable”.

Physical:
> untreated people become dangerous / environment deteriorates.

Present mirror:
> current hospital system.

Return-rule family:
> permission / patient status / rescue priority.

Why distinct:
> moral/resource pressure, not word puzzle.

# Supply Findings

## Strong
The concept can generate varied fields WITHOUT relying on:
> “read 7 written rules, find contradiction.”

The richest recurring social systems:
- family/identity;
- work/role;
- property/doors;
- commerce;
- transit;
- medicine;
- memory/public information.

These naturally produce:
> reality consequences after return.

## Watch
Several premises can still collapse into:
> define who counts as X.

Writer must rotate physical action / resource / intelligent adversary.

## Critical Insight

The best fields are not:
> “weird rule first.”

They are:
> **ordinary human system taken to an extreme survival law.**

This should become the field-generation principle.
