## Invitation evidence

> “医生可以进。”

The door releases without the resident physically operating it.

This plants:
> an inside-recognized resident can allow an outsider through.

## Wrong interpretation
Cheng Ye explicitly says BEFORE checking 804:

## Minimum-cost test / fallback
Because the interpretation is unproven:

## Fallback payoff

Reader sees:

# 5. Cheng Ye non-replaceability

A generic smart protagonist could solve a door puzzle.

This version specifically requires Cheng Ye because he repeatedly does TWO things:

## A. Uncertainty management
He does not claim certainty.
He openly says:
> “倾向”
> “没证据”

Then limits the blast radius of being wrong.

His visible competence fantasy is:
> not perfect inference, but plans that survive bad inference.

Recognition/resistance:
- Tang Ji recognizes the value of his fallback after 804;
- she still resists his delay instinct;
- other survivors follow his structure once it becomes executable.

## B. Refusal of anonymous cost
When a survivor says:
> “牺牲两户，总比整栋楼死强。”

Cheng Ye asks:
> “哪两户？”
> “说门牌。”
> “里面几个人？”
> “谁帮他们撤？”

He does not answer with:
> “一个都不能放弃。”

He forces the cost back onto specific households.

The decision then changes:
- 2808 refuses;
- 2811 voluntarily accepts;
- 2802 elderly resident opens only after Tang Ji personally assumes medical responsibility.

This cannot be replaced by a generic moral speech without changing the scene.

# 6. Cheng Ye flaw cost

Cheng Ye admits:

# 9. Midpoint reinterpretation

Initial survival instinct:
> sealed apartments are safest.

Planted evidence before reinterpretation:
- 803 balcony airflow visibly pulls corridor smoke;
- 1203 is open and its floor has thinner smoke;
- completely sealed floors become increasingly stagnant.

Reinterpretation:
> sealed rooms are individually safer, but a tower with too many sealed apartments loses the only lateral ventilation paths available to shared corridors/stairs.

This turns the conflict from:
> “How do we open locked doors?”

into:
> “How many safe homes must voluntarily stop being safe so the whole building can breathe?”

No hidden last-minute clause is needed.
