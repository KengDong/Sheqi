# D-0113｜Field #1 Rebuild Review Pack
## 第一副本不能再只是“18天背景”

# 0. Why Rebuild

Old architecture reduced Field #1 to:
> already happened off-page / name-identity family / Returned R1 / do not retell.

That is too thin.

Field #1 now needs to independently prove:

1. **Field is terrifying enough that returning to reality creates a real dimensional contrast.**
2. **R1 has a mature ecology, so Cheng Ye's Rule Experience Gap is earned.**
3. **Cheng Ye's current enemy-mode / ally-mode personality has an origin in lived events, not author description.**
4. **R1 is chosen from worse candidate laws for a defensible reason.**
5. **The Field contains early evidence that Fields are future fragments of the same real city.**
6. **At least one soft clue pays much later into Second Reality / terminal spine.**

This pack proposes THREE routes.

---

# ROUTE A｜南门综合安置区
## Recommended
### Internal shorthand: “全城点名”

# One-Line Human Situation

> **一座断网的未来灾后安置城区，白天靠实名系统找人，天黑后任何被正确说出的真名都会把目标位置告诉所有听见的人；程野必须在十八天后转运列车发车前，把一群身份已经泄露的人送出这座城市。**

# Why It Can Be Big

This is not:
> ten people in a room avoiding names.

It is:
> **thousands of people have already lived under R1 long enough to build an entire civilization around it.**

At night:
- shops stop speaking legal names;
- hospitals cover patient name cards;
- families use call signs / kinship terms;
- identity photos and verified rosters are controlled assets;
- ear protection / noise zones are normal equipment;
- lost-child rescue and predatory tracking use the SAME rule;
- one valid trigger in a crowded hall can teach hundreds of listeners the same target identity.

The Field looks like a future city that has made R1 ordinary.

# Visual First Impact

At dusk:
> a city-sized emergency settlement goes quiet.

Not total silence.
Instead:
- hand signs;
- numbered tickets;
- nicknames;
- chalk boards;
- visual queue lights;
- shopkeepers point instead of calling customers;
- public-address speakers are physically covered or disconnected after sunset.

Then one newcomer panics and shouts a missing child's true name.

For one second:
> everyone in the concourse knows exactly where the child is.

Miracle.

They find the child.

Five minutes later:
> people who heard the name also know who that name refers to.

Someone calls it again.

Now a predatory patrol knows too.

The “rescue miracle” becomes a hunting network.

# Signature Horror Image

Late Field climax:

> Cheng Ye's own confirmed true name is broadcast through the settlement's emergency PA.

Across the dark district:
- balconies open;
- corridor heads turn;
- people in multiple buildings stop what they are doing;
- thousands of listeners know where Cheng Ye is.

Not because they are monsters.

Because the city has been trained to exploit names.

Reader memory target:
> **“全城同时知道男主在哪。”**

This should be the first Field's iconic image.

# Why Network Is Down

The future fragment's normal telecom network is mostly dead / locally fragmented.

This matters structurally.

Cheng Ye becomes expert in:
- local acoustic propagation;
- sound isolation;
- route traps;
- verified identity containment;
- daylight-only identity work.

But he does NOT experience:
> millions of normal-real-world livestream viewers receiving a trigger at once.

Therefore his R1 choice can remain rational:
> in the Field, its danger was severe but locally manageable.

Reality's intact phone/livestream infrastructure later scales the same law far beyond his Field expectation.

This fixes the old weak logic:
> “他活过18天还以为闭嘴就够了？”

New truth:
> **he knew the rule was dangerous; he underestimated what a fully connected reality would do with it.**

# 18-Day Story Spine

## Days 1–2｜Miracle
Cheng Ye enters near future South Gate transit hub.

He sees R1 first used to:
> find a missing child.

The rule appears almost benevolent.

Human seed:
> names can save people.

## Days 3–5｜Contagion
A valid trigger teaches identity binding to listeners.

A target's identity spreads from:
> 1 person -> 8 -> 70.

First serious deaths / captures happen because people keep re-triggering the target.

Cheng Ye learns:
> the dangerous asset is not the string of characters.
> it is verified name-person binding.

## Days 6–9｜Mature Ecology
He enters the settlement's real night economy.

Learns:
- call signs;
- ear protection;
- white/noise corridors;
- visual signals;
- ID-photo compartmentalization;
- “direction is not route” traps;
- only a few people in each group know certain true names.

This is where later Freight-Yard tactics come from.

## Days 10–13｜Institution Turns Predatory
The settlement's real-name registration system was originally built to:
- reunite families;
- distribute medicine;
- issue evacuation slots.

But the same verified registry becomes perfect hunting infrastructure.

A security / resource faction controls:
> who has verified bindings to whom.

They can find:
- debtors;
- deserters;
- people with scarce medicine;
- political/organizational enemies;
- anyone whose identity enters the registry.

The antagonist is not “evil because evil.”
The system genuinely also rescues people.

Human conflict:
> destroy the registry and you save hunted people;
> destroy it and missing children / patients / separated families may never be found.

## Days 14–17｜The Group Gets Named
Someone leaks / captures the group's verified identity set.

Now ordinary hiding stops working.

Cheng Ye's solution cannot be:
> stay unseen.

It must become:
> control who can HEAR;
> manipulate routes despite accurate direction;
> split identity knowledge;
> create multiple physical chokepoints;
> exploit daytime reset of location while remembering bindings persist.

This becomes his mature R1 strategic education.

## Day 18｜南门转运夜
The final evacuation train boards around dawn.

Problem:
> the old system still uses legal-name boarding / emergency announcement procedures.

The hostile side plans to use the PA to expose selected passengers.

Climax:
- sound zones are deliberately broken;
- some plans fail;
- one trusted person's identity is exposed;
- Cheng Ye uses route topology + sound isolation + prebuilt fallbacks;
- his own name is finally broadcast citywide;
- he becomes the most visible person in the settlement;
- he uses that visibility as the final decoy while the rest of the group reaches the train.

The key is NOT heroic suicide.

He survives because:
> **knowing exactly where a person is does NOT tell you how to reach them.**

The settlement itself becomes his maze.

This directly seeds the later Freight-Yard “direction is not route” move.

# Cheng Ye Character Origin

## Enemy Mode
During Days 10–17 he learns:
> a person who knowingly weaponizes a verified identity cannot be treated like an ordinary argument opponent.

He becomes:
- quiet;
- careful;
- ruthless about removing rule access;
- more interested in cutting information / communication / routes than hurting bodies.

This is the origin of:
> enemy Cheng Ye.

## Ally Mode
The group survives 18 days together.

If Cheng Ye is permanently grim:
> FAIL.

He should:
- joke at dawn after hard nights;
- keep food / water aside for teammates;
- let others tease his obsessive prep;
- become the person people feel relief around.

This is the origin of:
> ally/family Cheng Ye.

# R1 Exit Choice

At normal exit, three black plaques should be from the SAME identity/recognition ecology.

Do NOT finalize exact wording yet.

Required design:

## Candidate 1｜R1
> after dark, a correctly bound spoken true name reveals the target location to listeners.

Utility:
- rescue;
- search;
- coordination.

Cost:
- privacy;
- stalking;
- identity propagation.

Known counters:
- silence;
- hearing control;
- compartmentalization;
- route design.

## Candidate 2｜Compulsion-type identity law
Example family:
> a person who hears their true name must respond / reveal presence.

Why Cheng Ye rejects:
> removes target agency too directly.

## Candidate 3｜Registration-type identity law
Example family:
> a verified identity becomes bound to an address / role until dawn.

Why Cheng Ye rejects:
> turns public infrastructure into a prison.

Therefore R1 is not “harmless.”
It is:
> **the least uncontrollable of three terrible but useful laws.**

# Future Foreshadowing

## HARD CLUE｜Future Geography
The Field is built around:
> **future South Gate transit hub / current South Gate area.**

One transit map contains:
> a line / station / district tied to **青禾里**.

Cheng Ye does not yet understand why that matters.

After returning:
> current South Gate has no such completed route.

When the next countdown says:
> 青禾里一期·二号楼,

the reader gets the first possible connection:
> these Fields may occupy pieces of the SAME future city.

Do NOT explain.

## SOFT CLUE｜Terminal Date
A paper transfer timetable / evacuation notice has schedules for many dates.

Then:
> after one future date, all later rows are blank / crossed out.

No “归一日” term.
No explanation.

Later Fields repeat the same date-window.

## SOFT CLUE｜Previous Human Selection
At the black-plaque selection area:
> signs of prior human use exist.

Possible:
- one old blood-dark fingerprint;
- a scratched tally;
- an empty mounting slot;
- handwriting too faded to read.

Do NOT write:
> “there are other Returners.”

It should only become meaningful later.

# How Much To Show In Opening

Do NOT retell 18 days immediately.

Opening should reveal only one brutal afterimage:
> Cheng Ye's true name is broadcast.
> A whole dark future settlement turns toward him.
> He runs through a route he prepared.
> black plaque / rule choice.
> hard cut to roast-sweet-potato smell in current South Gate.

Later:
- Freight Yard tactics reveal pieces of his Field experience;
- conversations / nightmares / old equipment reveal more;
- another Returner can recognize one of his techniques;
- a later visit to current South Gate makes the geography clue pay.

The underwater Field must be complete even if early reader sees only 300–800 Chinese characters.

# Biggest Strengths

- massive scale without adding monsters;
- R1 ecology is genuinely mature;
- directly earns Rule Experience Gap;
- turns reality livestream into a NEW escalation rather than something Cheng Ye should have already anticipated fully;
- gives Field #1 an iconic image;
- strongly seeds Second Reality geography;
- naturally creates Cheng Ye's cold-enemy / warm-ally duality.

# Biggest Risks

- if written as “identity policy simulation,” it becomes dry;
- if too much time is spent explaining registry systems, it becomes institutional fiction;
- needs named people and one emotionally specific group goal;
- 18-day spine must be dramatized through 4–6 major set pieces, not daily diary.

# ROUTE B｜南门最后一班车
## More action / less city ecology

# One-Line Human Situation

> **未来南门地铁网进入夜间失踪模式：任何真名被说出，所有听见的人都会知道目标在哪；程野和一群陌生人必须穿过不断停运、缩短、封闭的线路，在最后一班转运列车离城前活下来。**

# Core Pleasure

- moving train;
- shrinking stations;
- tunnel pursuit;
- name calls over platform PA;
- direction-vs-route tricks;
- passengers weaponize identities.

# 18-Day Logic

The group lives across:
- depot;
- abandoned stations;
- service tunnels;
- temporary transit shelters.

Each night they move closer to the one operating transfer line.

# Foreshadow

Future transit map can directly contain:
- Qingheli;
- one impossible northern district;
- terminal blank date.

# Strength

Very bingeable.
Clear physical movement.
Easy first-screen spectacle.

# Weakness

R1 civilization / social ecology is thinner.

Risk:
> feels like one excellent subway dungeon rather than proof of a failed future that has lived under the law for years.

Verdict:
> strong reserve; weaker macro foundation than Route A.

# ROUTE C｜十二万人实名避难中心
## More moral / institutional horror

# One-Line Human Situation

> **未来灾后的巨型避难中心靠实名系统维持医疗、食物、亲属查找与床位；可天黑后，任何真名都能变成位置广播，整座避难中心因此在“必须实名才能活”和“实名就可能被找到”之间崩坏。**

# Core Pleasure

- resource allocation;
- family separation;
- hospital/food/bed registration;
- black-market identity;
- name brokers;
- fake/borrowed identities;
- public announcement catastrophe.

# Midpoint

Destroying identity records would protect people from hunters,
but also:
> patients lose medicine,
> children lose parents,
> evacuation slots collapse.

# Climax

The center must perform one mandatory final roll call before evacuation.

Cheng Ye must decide:
> save the registry / destroy it / corrupt it / selectively preserve it.

# Foreshadow

- future urban institutional documents;
- same terminal date;
- same city district names.

# Strength

Very strong moral engine.
Excellent thematic source for:
> identity binding.

# Weakness

Most likely to become:
> smart people discussing systems / policy.

Physical spectacle is weaker than A/B unless aggressively designed.

Verdict:
> useful elements should be absorbed into A rather than chosen alone.
