- Ch1: dead vein follows Lu Zhaochuan; abandoned canal shelter becomes livable; Qinghemen arrives.
- Ch2: Qinghemen's claim is made concrete but not thuggish; Fang Congjian identifies the vein as following Lu rather than the cart. Tao-shen refuses to be used as Lu's excuse and reveals prior lived connection to the old South Canal.

## Required validations

### 1. From spectacle to home / people / relationship / belonging / responsibility
PASS.

The vein's value becomes water, shelter, cultivable ground and a public canal. The cost of staying is responsibility for downstream consequences. The first place becomes meaningful because it can demand something from them and because others must now recognize their role.

### 2. Qinghemen not cardboard villains
PASS.

Fang Congjian has a legitimate sealed-slope claim, proposes compensation/temporary return rather than immediate violence, warns about the canal risk, contributes temporary vein-fixing nails, and cooperates during the emergency. His institutional interest remains unresolved.

### 3. Tao-shen / child not decorative humanization
PASS.

Tao-shen:
- rejects being used as Lu's reason to refuse Qinghemen;
- identifies the shelter's old public history;
- remembers the spill gate;
- has a documented prior family connection to canal maintenance;
- is named on the temporary-warden permit.

Tao Man:
- notices/engages with practical objects;
- retrieves the re-straightened old nail that becomes part of reopening the gate;
- is also named on the permit.

### 4. First mini-climax answers "does Lu have the right to call somewhere ours?"
PASS, partially and meaningfully rather than absolutely.

He does not gain legal ownership. He gains a recognized 30-day right to stay/use the canal shelter and adjacent plots, with duties and public accountability. The place is no longer merely "somewhere nobody currently wants."

### 5. Larger concrete desire remains
PASS.

Lu explicitly wants to replace a permit marked "30 days" with a place where their names can stay without an expiry. This naturally expands the desire from surviving tonight to securing durable belonging that may move with / be enabled by the vein.
