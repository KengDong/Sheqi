## 1. Core Promise

Frozen Premium kept intact:

> 主角低价收报废/闲置商用设备，修复后不卖而出租，逐台沉淀长期现金流资产。

Arc does not mutate into:
- 系统文；
- 拍卖捡漏文；
- 单纯维修Case集；
- 每章换一台怪机器；
- 商业教程。

The whole arc stays on **one old 50L walk-behind floor scrubber, A-001**.

## 2. Opening Arc Required Experience

### Core promise on-page
PASS.

Ch1 directly stages:
- scrap/idle commercial equipment acquired cheaply;
- repaired with skill;
- reseller offers immediate cash;
- protagonist chooses **not to sell**;
- same machine is rented out instead.

The concept is dramatized through a real choice, not explained first.

### Protagonist concrete desire
PASS.

Immediate desire:
> 在下周三前解决 4,800 元店租，不让刚开的维修铺因为现金流断掉。

This makes the 7,200 resale offer materially tempting later, not a fake choice.

### First meaningful fruit
PASS.

First two-night rent = 1,600 RMB.

Life-layer fruit is visible:
- overdue electricity is paid;
- part of battery debt is repaid;
- protagonist finally eats a proper meal;
- most importantly, the shop can keep operating.

The deposit is explicitly kept separate and not spent, so the fruit does not rely on dishonest accounting.

### Fruit creates cost
PASS.

Keeping the machine produces:
- midnight support calls;
- battery capacity failure;
- emergency battery rental;
- stronger replacement battery purchase;
- on-site responsibility;
- future substitute-machine obligation.

The asset is not “free passive income.”

### Relationship changes
PASS.

孙莉 begins as a stranger asking for a two-night rental.

By the mini-climax she becomes:
- a three-month anchor renter;
- someone who trusts the protagonist’s response speed more than a random used-machine purchase;
- a business counterpart whose six workers and contract are materially tied to A-001 reliability.

She is not a payment NPC.

### World/social interface expands
PASS.

The arc moves from:
> tiny repair stall

into:
> warehouse trial clean → small cleaning subcontractor team → logistics park property requirements → recurring contracted night work.

Expansion comes through the same machine/customer relationship.

### Pressure learns/intensifies
PASS.

Pressure escalates in three layers:
1. immediate resale cash vs uncertain rent;
2. first rental exposes battery endurance and support burden;
3. long-term contract doubles work area and turns a small defect into contract-level risk.

No cartoon villain is needed.

### Real protagonist choice
PASS.

Two explicit choices:
- Ch1: reject 6,800 immediate resale for 1,600 first rent;
- Ch3: reject 7,200 immediate resale again after real service pain has already been experienced.

The second refusal is harder because he now knows the costs.

### Mini-climax/payoff
PASS.

The mini-climax is not “rental system unlocked.”

It is:
- same old machine survives the first long-term contract night;
- protagonist fixes a real on-site failure;
- Sun Li’s three-month job is preserved;
- six workers retain ongoing shifts;
- three-month rental is signed;
- 4,400 prepayment arrives;
- protagonist pays the 4,800 shop rent **without selling A-001**.

The proof is causal:
> old asset → repeated use → repeated responsibility → stronger trust → recurring cash flow.

### Larger desire remains
PASS.

Ending desire:
> not a pile of repaired machines waiting to be sold, but multiple owned machines out working, paying rent back monthly, and returning for service.

The arc stops before acquiring A-002.

## 3. B8 Special Must-Prove

### First machine faces “sell now vs keep to rent”
STRONG PASS.

6,800 RMB first offer and 7,200 RMB second offer are both concrete, immediate and enough to solve the protagonist’s urgent rent problem.

### First rent creates tangible life improvement
PASS.

Not only ledger gain:
- electricity stays on;
- debt pressure drops;
- food/living state changes;
- shop survival becomes possible.

### Same machine / same renter returns before mini-climax
STRONG PASS.

Both return.

A-001 returns physically to the shop after the first two-night job.

Sun Li also returns with a materially larger need:
> three-month contracted warehouse cleaning.

### Second return is bigger in value / responsibility / relationship / cash-flow meaning
STRONG PASS.

First use:
> 2 nights / 1,600 RMB / trial work.

Second use:
> 3 months / 6,600 RMB contracted rent / regular night work / 24h recovery expectation / substitute-machine responsibility.

The second return changes the protagonist’s business model and the renter relationship.

## 4. Hard Watch

### “New machine → repair → new customer → next machine”
AVOIDED.

No second machine arc is started.

### “奇怪机器之周”
AVOIDED.

Only one ordinary commercial floor scrubber carries the whole arc.

### “商业教程”
MOSTLY AVOIDED.

There are concrete numbers and repair details, but they appear inside:
- rent deadline pressure;
- negotiation;
- night work;
- emergency failure.

No chapter becomes an ROI lecture or process list.

### “只有账面数字，没有生活变化”
AVOIDED.

Money changes whether:
- lights stay on;
- rent is paid;
- protagonist eats properly;
- six workers retain shifts;
- the shop survives without liquidating its first asset.

## 5. Prose Risk / Honest Notes

1. The final 1/4 deliberately becomes more reflective after the warehouse crisis. This helps make “repair skill becomes asset” legible, but several paragraphs are close to thesis-level clarity. It still remains attached to A-001, Sun Li, the empty shop bay and paid rent rather than becoming abstract business exposition.

2. The property manager is intentionally thin because the arc’s human relationship focus is protagonist ↔ Sun Li and her six-person team. He functions as external standard/pressure, not a third major character.

3. Technical repair texture is plausible but intentionally compressed. The story treats repair competence as character capability, not as a manual.

Mini-climax has fully settled:
- first long-term rental survived its decisive first night;
- shop rent paid;
- A-001 remains owned and in service;
- recurring relationship established;
- next desire visible.

No A-002 acquisition is written.

**STOP here.**
