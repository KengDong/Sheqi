# W2 Opening Arc Selfcheck｜《把整座城市当成超能力》

status: PASS
decision: D-0092
scope: Ch1 authoritative + Ch2—Ch3 continuation through first natural mini-climax

## Natural stopping point

The first mini-climax lands at the end of Ch3:
- the second manifestation reaches a populated residential area;
- residents are evacuated before the confrontation;
- Xu Jin voluntarily enters the controlled risk zone;
- the same streetlight phenomenon is reused under stricter public constraints rather than replaced by a new facility power;
- the anomaly is temporarily contained;
- the cost remains: blackout, damaged lighting hardware, displaced residents, ongoing investigation, uncertain residue;
- Xu Jin is placed into future same-class incident response without being granted administrator authority.

Continuing further would cross the task boundary into post-mini-climax material.

## Arc question

> Can the city anomaly become a durable relationship between Xu Jin, real people, public responsibility and the city itself?

Assessment: **PASS for this opening arc.**

The city is no longer only a spectacular ability surface. The second incident forces the phenomenon through:
- resident evacuation;
- local commerce disruption;
- emergency command;
- infrastructure damage;
- worker liability and records;
- uncertainty over who has authority to act.

## Protagonist test

Xu Jin is not merely an interface.

He makes an active, costly choice:
- argues to accompany the response team because he may be causally involved;
- proposes the controlled relighting test only after evacuation;
- recognizes that the lights follow him rather than obey him;
- uses himself as the moving target so the anomaly is forced into the light;
- accepts that the event creates future responsibility rather than a clean reward.

His competence remains frontline/physical rather than remote-administrator fantasy.

## Relationship movement

- **Lao Liang -> Xu Jin:** moves from concealment/protection ("say less") to explicit shared responsibility ("this is not your job alone") and formal corroboration.
- **Qiu Lan -> Xu Jin:** moves from questioning him as a risk/source to conditionally incorporating him into controlled response.
- **Residents/community -> incident:** public consequences become concrete through blackout, evacuation, elderly residents, shop losses, and temporary shelter.

## World/social expansion

The arc expands from one flooded road to:
- district emergency coordination;
- a residential community;
- community grid management;
- firefighters / municipal lighting / drainage interface;
- temporary shelter at a school gym;
- public video/social-media attention.

This is enough expansion for the first arc without becoming an investigation manual.

## Hard Watch

### facility-of-week
**CLEARED in this arc.**
No new power category is introduced. The same type of streetlight phenomenon is forced to do a harder job under new constraints.

### investigation-procedure drift
**CONTROLLED.**
Questioning is brief and immediately converted into a live public-risk decision.

### municipal-manual drift
**CONTROLLED.**
Infrastructure details are present only when they change stakes, options, evacuation, or the physical tactic.

### remote administrator fantasy
**AVOIDED.**
Xu Jin cannot command the city. The lights track him unpredictably, and he must physically position himself inside the risk.

## Fruit / cost

Fruit:
- repeatability is partially demonstrated;
- Xu Jin discovers a usable causal rule: the lights appear to seek him, allowing him to manipulate geometry rather than issue commands;
- emergency responders now take the phenomenon seriously enough to build a response protocol around his presence.

Cost:
- damaged lamp hardware;
- blackout and resident displacement;
- public visibility;
- institutional scrutiny;
- no guarantee the method will work again;
- Xu Jin becomes harder, not easier, to live a normal life.

## Specific reader desire after payoff

The next concrete question is:

> What happens when the next city abnormality calls Xu Jin to the scene—and what role can a frontline maintenance worker occupy if the city keeps responding to him but never fully obeys him?

STOP after Ch3. Do not continue into the next incident in this task.
