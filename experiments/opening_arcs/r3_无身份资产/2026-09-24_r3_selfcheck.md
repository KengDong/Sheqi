## Mission Check

### 1. First identity-loss day creates concrete humiliation/danger
PASS.

Immediate sequence:
- wife treats protagonist as a home intruder and holds a knife;
- residential identity lookup cannot verify him;
- ID / bank / resident records fail;
- at the warehouse he is treated as an unknown outsider carrying the owner's keys;
- later he cannot legally drive his own company's truck.

The failure is social, physical and operational rather than abstract.

### 2. Protagonist cannot solve it through explanation
PASS.

Private memories make the wife more frightened, not convinced.
At work, detailed knowledge makes him look more suspicious.
He eventually stops trying to win recognition through verbal proof.

### 3. A physical / relational asset genuinely works
STRONG PASS.

Delivered assets:
- mechanical key;
- red emergency binder;
- two-person emergency cash protocol;
- diesel backup;
- old trucks with physical keys;
- prepaid mutual-backup cold-storage agreement and physical 017 tag.

These solve different stages of the same continuous crisis rather than functioning as disconnected case rewards.

### 4. Close person treats him as a stranger with emotional weight
PASS.

Xu Tang never magically recognizes him.
The emotional progression is:
knife / fear -> suspicion -> operational tolerance -> controlled trust -> offers him a folding bed.
She still records him as "unknown name" and continues searching for "Zhou Qi."
The pain remains active after the business payoff.

### 5. First mini-climax proves old construction can carry him while identity remains broken
STRONG PASS.

The central payoff is not identity restoration.
The cold-chain outage is survived because assets and protocols created before identity failure continue to execute without requiring the protagonist's current recognition.
The 017 prepaid empty slots provide the clearest commercial fruit: while others bid desperately for space, his previously purchased reserve space remains usable.

### 6. After payoff Reader wants the next build
PASS.

The protagonist begins a concrete "before next time" list:
- identity-independent lodging;
- distributed cash;
- mechanically operable vehicle;
- non-single-point business permissions;
- expanded reserve cold-storage capacity;
- offline supplier contacts / settlement routes.

Arc ends on desire to build more doors before the next missing day.
