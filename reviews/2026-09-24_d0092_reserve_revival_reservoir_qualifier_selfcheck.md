## 2. Reward Sameness Audit

### 可缺席帝国
**PASS.**
The reward does not stay at “company survives another absence.” It naturally expands into:
- authority transfer;
- independent team victories;
- cashflow;
- brand and public identity;
- failed delegation;
- family / intimacy that cannot be delegated;
- old contract holders becoming protectors, counterparties, or political pressure.

### 劫债杠杆
**PASS WITH WARNING.**
The first visible loop can repeat as:
> advance debt -> get stronger -> pay later.

The reservoir escapes that loop through:
- recovery periods;
- permanent injuries;
- team scheduling;
- resource windows;
- deliberate deleveraging;
- old asset protection;
- refusal to transfer risk to poorer people;
- public responsibility.
Execution must not resolve three consecutive climaxes by “advance another debt.”

### 天道赊境
**PASS WITH WARNING.**
The obvious repeat risk is:
> spot cheap debt -> borrow realm -> win.

The engine diversifies through:
- real cultivation settlement;
- collateral;
- guarantees;
- bad debt;
- restructuring;
- bankrupt sect assets;
- resource markets;
- city-level systemic debt;
- public credit and governance.
Every borrow must leave permanent real assets / status / cultivation or the reward will feel hollow.

### 限制越多，异能越强
**PASS WITH WARNING.**
The obvious collectable loop is:
> add another restriction -> get another multiplier.

The reservoir remains alive even when new restrictions are frozen:
- enemies study old rules;
- team composition changes;
- old restrictions combine;
- public trust and law appear;
- career paths narrow;
- personal relationships absorb the cost.
Opening execution must prove this early.

### 岗位就是异能

- jurisdiction;
- team growth;
- old-department conflict;
- institutional ownership;
- public service;
- replication;
- decentralization.

### 幸存者必须给下一轮立法

- daily-life institutions;

- new-generation legitimacy;

### 个人十级封顶，组织才能继续升级

## 3. Premium Survival Audit

All eight pass.

- **可缺席帝国:** the more replaceable the founder becomes in business, the more expensive the irreplaceable real person becomes in family/public identity.
- **劫债杠杆:** higher cultivation makes old risk timing, permanent injury, recovery windows, and the temptation to re-outsource risk more important.
- **天道赊境:** scale moves the same credit logic from individuals to sects, cities, regions, and higher realms.
- **限制越多，异能越强:** restrictions become public identity, career structure, team architecture, and governance.
- **岗位就是异能:** the premium scales from one improvised job to who can define society's official permission structure.
- **幸存者必须给下一轮立法:** later dangers can be generated mainly by accumulated old rules written by the survivors themselves.
- **个人十级封顶，组织才能继续升级:** higher personal power still depends on whether the organization can truly feed, defend, govern, and retain people.
- **修旧设备，不卖，拿去收租:** holding repaired equipment scales from one rental asset into a service/infrastructure network.

No candidate shows a clear “mother market continues while premium becomes background” failure.
