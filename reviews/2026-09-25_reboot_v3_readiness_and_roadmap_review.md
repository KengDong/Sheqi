# REBOOT-V3 Readiness Review
## 从哪里重新开始，怎样一路走到真正的热门小说候选

# 0. Verdict

Current process:
> **substantially improved and ready for evidence-gathering, NOT ready for fiction drafting.**

The project should restart from:
> **Market Dossier -> dual-lane concept forge**

NOT from:
- Rule-Reality rewrite;
- Cheng Ye rewrite;
- Field redesign;
- old Opening;
- old candidate patching.

# 1. Confirmed First Principle

Highest objective:

> **For an unknown first-time commercial web-fiction author, maximize the probability of: click -> voluntary continuation -> character attachment -> repeatable rewards -> long serialization -> sustained income.**

Everything else is subordinate.

This means:
- no concept has sunk-cost privilege;
- no character has preservation privilege;
- no architecture has preservation privilege;
- no genre has preservation privilege;
- comedy/horror/system/rules are tools, not goals.

# 2. What We Learned From Every Major Wrong Turn

## Wrong Turn 1｜Checklist became the generator
Symptom:
> more standards -> more “correct” fiction -> less natural desire.

Lesson:
> checklists diagnose after behavior; they do not generate story.

Fixed in V3:
> architecture/diagnostics moved later.

## Wrong Turn 2｜Forced Reader completion inflated PASS
Symptom:
> AI Readers strongly passed books the author voluntarily abandoned around Ch7.

Lesson:
> evaluation-mode != consumption-mode.

Fixed:
> voluntary stop is Level-1 evidence.

Still required:
> when possible, add real human target-reader evidence before launch.

## Wrong Turn 3｜Architecture before desire
Symptom:
> Ch100/300/700 planning before short prose proved attraction.

Lesson:
> expensive design resources were allocated too early.

Fixed:
> long-run architecture now after P20/P50.

## Wrong Turn 4｜Protecting a promising concept
Symptom:
> Rule-Reality became “the book we must rescue.”

Lesson:
> sunk-cost attachment distorted design.

Fixed:
> no active candidate; all legacy assets are controls.

## Wrong Turn 5｜Rule-first Field design
Symptom:
> “name dungeon”, “door dungeon”, mechanism demonstrations.

Lesson:
> concept mechanic is not a complete story.

Fixed:
> candidate-specific lessons retained, but Field work is frozen.

## Wrong Turn 6｜Scale words instead of dramatic intensity
Symptom:
> city overlap / void / disappearing floors.

Lesson:
> bigger map != stronger scene.

Fixed:
> current universal pipeline begins from market desire and scene proof, not scale.

## Wrong Turn 7｜Character adjectives instead of lived behavior
Symptom:
> Cheng Ye = smart/calm/responsible project manager.

Lesson:
> character magnetism comes from relationship modes, scars, behavior, contradiction.

Fixed as reusable method:
> character non-replaceability and collision before heavy bibles.

## Wrong Turn 8｜Generic competence sold as intelligence fantasy
Symptom:
> recording, reporting, backup plans.

Lesson:
> commercial competence must be book-specific and visible.

Valuable historical insight:
> Rule Experience Gap was better because the advantage came from the book's engine.

Generalized rule:
> competence fantasy must be impossible to transplant unchanged into another book.

## Wrong Turn 9｜Benchmark after ideation
Symptom:
> AI made the design first, then used hits as justification.

Lesson:
> research became decoration.

Fixed:
> benchmark-first is highest-process authority.

## Wrong Turn 10｜Benchmark feature shopping
Symptom:
> popular A threat + popular B character + popular C reversal.

Lesson:
> average of hit features produces derivative AI soup.

Fixed:
> BENCHMARK_CAUSALITY_STANDARD:
> borrow reader-level causal function, not feature list.

## Wrong Turn 11｜Designer foreshadowing
Symptom:
> road signs / dates / symbols treated as major payoff.

Lesson:
> strongest recontextualization changes the meaning of people, decisions, deaths, relationships.

Retained as later diagnostic principle.

## Wrong Turn 12｜Author became the training loop
Symptom:
> author had to point out one missing dimension after another.

Lesson:
> process failed upstream.

Fixed:
> author only sees gate survivors; internal system kills weak material first.

## Wrong Turn 13｜Authority drift
Symptom:
> old NEXT / READY tasks survived after rejection.

Lesson:
> Git-only memory is dangerous if CURRENT is polluted.

Fixed:
- CURRENT_AUTHORITY shortened;
- STATE reset;
- ACTIVE_WORKSTREAMS reset;
- stale Rule-Reality Field calibrator retired;
- candidate-specific pipeline/taste files demoted.

# 4. What Still Needs Improvement

## Gap 1｜Market Dossier can become research addiction
Risk:
> never stop studying.

Fix:
- one bounded dossier;
- after editor validation, freeze market conclusions for the first concept forge;
- only reopen if a named evidence gap appears.

## Gap 2｜Need a real-human evidence plan
AI clean-room readers are useful but have known inflation risk.

Before Launch Candidate:
> strongly prefer several real target readers with voluntary-stop behavior.

Do not require this at earliest concept stage,
but make it a pre-launch requirement if practical.

## Gap 3｜Packaging needs its own behavioral test
Title/blurb can kill a good book before prose.

Before P0 finalists:
> test title + one-line + short blurb separately from prose.

Record:
- which one would be clicked;
- what reader expects;
- whether P0 delivers that expectation.

## Gap 4｜Commercial Mirror originality audit needs teeth
Lane B may become derivative without noticing.

Before P0:
- identify primary benchmark;
- list mirrored functions;
- list all non-cosmetic differences;
- reject if the first 3 scenes map one-to-one onto benchmark scenes.

## Gap 5｜Concept Forge must not generate “30 AI variations”
20–30 nuclei is useful only if they come from distinct commercial engines.

Require diversity across:
- reader fantasy;
- social setting;
- protagonist role;
- repeatable reward;
- emotional tone.

Not:
> same system premise with 20 nouns changed.

## Gap 6｜Need production simulation earlier than P50 for first-time author
V3 puts full simulation at P50.

Improvement:
> add a lightweight Production Check at Reservoir / P3:
- can 10 ordinary chapters be generated without bespoke genius?
- weekly research burden?
- scene-template diversity?
- expected writer fatigue?

Full simulation still at P50.

## Gap 7｜Need explicit “fun without novelty” test
A concept may pass Reservoir but rely on novelty.

At P3/P7 ask:
> if reader already fully understands the premise, is the next scene still attractive because of people/reward/status?

This should be explicit.

# 5. Recommended Reboot Roadmap

## Stage 0｜NOW
Complete:
> 2024–2026 Newcomer Commercial Breakout Dossier.

No fiction.

## Stage 1｜Editor Synthesis
Convert dossier into:
- 8–12 current commercial constraints;
- 3–5 market motherboards worth entering;
- Qidian/Fanqie differences;
- newbie execution warnings.

Do NOT choose a novel yet.

## Stage 2｜Dual Concept Forge
Lane A:
> 10–15 Native Breakout nuclei.

Lane B:
> 10–15 Commercial Mirror nuclei.

Each only:
- title;
- one-line promise;
- platform;
- fantasy;
- repeatable reward;
- protagonist scene engine;
- distinctive premium;
- production reason.

## Stage 3｜Internal Desire Cut
No author exposure yet.

Kill:
> obvious generic / derivative / explanation-heavy / high-execution-risk concepts.

Retain:
> 4–6.

## Stage 4｜Packaging Test
For retained concepts:
- 2–3 title variants;
- one-line;
- short blurb.

Anonymous click-intent test.

## Stage 5｜Situation Reservoir + Light Production Check
30–50 scenes per contender.

Also simulate:
> 10 ordinary chapters.

Kill:
- repetition;
- fresh-gimmick dependence;
- impossible production burden.

Retain:
> 2–4.

## Stage 6｜Character Collision
Only now define:
- protagonist;
- 3–5 recurring people;
- independent desires;
- recurring chemistry/conflict.

No heavy bible.

## Stage 7｜P0
800–1500 characters.

Author sees only:
> top 1–2 internally surviving P0 packages.

Ask only:
> would you keep reading?

## Stage 8｜P1
Full Ch1 for survivors.

Test:
- promise delivery;
- character;
- reward/danger;
- relationship seed;
- next-click desire.

## Stage 9｜P3
Must prove:
- same Premium creates different scenes;
- reader follows people too;
- first permanent state;
- no novelty dependence.

## Stage 10｜Full Opening Arc
First natural climax.
Must:
- settle something;
- leave permanent fruit/cost;
- create more desire than before payoff.

## Stage 11｜P7 / P20
Critical evidence gates.

P7:
> novelty faded — still following?

P20:
> engine actually compounds?

## Stage 12｜Realistic Serialization / Production Proof
P20–P50:
- ordinary chapter supply;
- update rhythm;
- writer fatigue;
- reader desire;
- paid/free platform economics.

## Stage 13｜Long-Run Architecture
ONLY now:
- Ch100;
- Ch300;
- 1–3M character supply;
- mode changes;
- IP optionality.

## Stage 14｜Launch Candidate
Requirements:
- behavior proof;
- market fit;
- packaging proof;
- production buffer;
- flexible first 30–50 chapter horizon;
- originality audit;
- no obvious unresolved root weakness.
