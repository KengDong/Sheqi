# D-0091A Author Process Override
## 不在 P1 过早淘汰；第一次强比较后移到 Opening Arc / First Mini-Climax

Author correction:
> “只对比到第一章不太行，起码得是一个开头到小高潮的对比。不用急于筛选掉那么多，大不了多做点，多给点机会。”

# New Rule

P1 = diagnostic evidence.

It answers:
> 第一屏之后，完整第一章有没有小说。

It does NOT by default answer:
> 哪一本应该被淘汰。

Unless a candidate produces an unmistakable catastrophic failure:
- Fresh Reader voluntarily stops very early;
- premium promise is not delivered;
- prose becomes unreadable / mechanism manual;
- Concept must be mutated to continue;

all current finalists should continue to:
> **Opening Arc / First Mini-Climax Comparison.**

# First Meaningful Comparative Gate

Target:
> each candidate reaches its first natural mini-climax / first meaningful settlement.

Expected range:
> approximately Ch3—Ch5, not mechanically identical chapter count.

The comparison must include:
1. opening click;
2. protagonist desire;
3. first reward;
4. consequence;
5. at least one relationship change;
6. first expanded scene/social interface;
7. first meaningful pressure/opponent response;
8. first mini-climax / payoff;
9. concrete desire for what comes after that payoff.

# P1 Fresh Read

Still useful.

But its role changes to:
> diagnostic / continuation-friction evidence.

It should NOT force a 3->2 cut.

All three may continue if they remain organically readable.

# Later Cut Philosophy

Do not force fixed survivor counts.

Prefer:
> keep all candidates that still produce real voluntary continuation.

First serious cut:
> after Opening Arc / mini-climax blind read.

Even then:
> if all three remain strong, all three may continue to P7.

The goal is not to reduce candidate count efficiently.

The goal is:
> maximize the chance of not killing the eventual hit before enough story exists to judge it.
