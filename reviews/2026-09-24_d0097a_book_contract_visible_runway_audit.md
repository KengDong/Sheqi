# D-0097A｜Book Contract / Macro Promise / Visible Runway Audit

## Trigger

Author reports two linked failures across current Sheqi prototypes:

1. prose often enters the incident immediately without exposing the larger "meaning system" / book-scale promise;
2. after the first mini-climax, many arcs leave no concrete, visible next target, causing a "small sitcom episode finished" feeling.

This is confirmed as an architecture-level problem, not merely blurb/marketing.

# 1. First-Principles Definition

A strong commercial opening needs three simultaneous horizons:

## Near Horizon
> What must happen next?

Concrete:
- survive tonight;
- pass the exam;
- earn the fee;
- reach the next safe place;
- prepare for the next dungeon.

## Mid Horizon
> What visible ladder can I watch the protagonist climb over the next 20–100 chapters?

Examples of functions:
- realm / rank;
- score;
- debt;
- qualification;
- job;
- money;
- team;
- territory;
- resource balance;
- countdown;
- named institution / destination.

## Far Horizon
> If this book succeeds, what scale can it plausibly become?

Not detailed exposition.
A glimpse is enough:
- one family becoming a cultivation clan;
- one poor student trying to cross an industrialized cultivation hierarchy;
- survivors discovering that death games are only one layer of a world-scale mystery;
- one minute of saved time compounding toward godlike power;
- returned rules gradually rewriting reality.

A book feels small when it has only Near Horizon.

# 2. External Pattern｜Top Works Expose a Book Contract Early

## 《没钱修什么仙？》

The book does not stop to explain the whole world.

Instead the early chapters move through:
> interview -> school -> sports -> supernatural contract -> personal goal -> inequality -> teacher/loan -> work -> competition.

Each ordinary container reveals another social layer.

The book contract becomes legible very early:
> **cultivation is expensive; money, education, jobs, rank and bodily cultivation are one system; Zhang Yu wants to keep cultivating and eventually gain freedom.**

Visible runway after local payoffs:
- debt still exists;
- school ranking exists;
- jobs exist;
- cultivation level exists;
- exams/competition exist;
- richer institutions exist.

Therefore a small chapter never feels like the whole book.

## 《玄鉴仙族》

Its premise itself reveals scale:
> a small family receives cultivation inheritance and begins a long family rise.

The protagonist structure is generational.
Therefore:
> village incident -> family state -> territory -> descendants -> history.

A local payoff is automatically nested in a larger family ledger.

## 《十日终焉》

Official introduction explicitly reveals multiple recontextualization layers:
> ordinary day -> death games -> supernatural awakening -> "造神之地" -> annihilation-scale mystery.

This tells the reader before 1496 chapters unfold:
> the current game is not the whole book.

The first game is evidence of a larger machine.

## 《时停起手，邪神也得给我跪下！》

Title + synopsis expose both:
- current mechanic: daily one minute of time stop;
- compounding rule: unused time accumulates;
- implied ceiling: from seconds/minutes to a power that can confront extreme threats.

Therefore even a small early fight has a visible future asset:
> time balance + stronger use + stronger enemy.

## 《全球死考》

Synopsis exposes a literal reward ladder:
> points -> money -> genetic power -> apocalyptic-level reward.

The first exam is not merely one survival case.
It is step 1 on a visible scale ladder.

# 3. What Sheqi Currently Lacks

Many current openings do this well:
> scene -> pressure -> local payoff -> human consequence.

But often fail to expose:
- what exactly grows;
- what the next named step is;
- what the medium ladder is;
- what the far scale is.

The result:
> a satisfying mini-arc can paradoxically reduce desire because the reader feels "this episode is done."

This is the **Sitcom Closure Problem**.

# 4. Anti-Sitcom Rule｜Climax Must Handoff to a Tangible Runway

Every first mini-climax should settle one problem AND materialize at least two of:

1. **Next Target**
   - named exam / place / person / mission / date / entry window.

2. **Persistent Asset**
   - money / qualification / territory / scar structure / rule / team / relationship / job.

3. **Visible Ladder**
   - rank / score / realm / debt / balance / credential / countdown / organizational level.

4. **Larger Layer**
   - a new institution / region / class / faction / second returner / higher-order mystery.

5. **Irreversible Cost**
   - debt / body damage / obligation / public identity / rule consequence.

6. **New Choice**
   - two mutually exclusive paths now available.

At least one must be literally visible / nameable.

Avoid:
> "there is still a bigger mystery."

Prefer:
> "七天后参加承劫师资格试";
> "十二天后第二次规则场开启";
> "净痕院的录取名单";
> "一张更大的劫路图上亮起三个区";
> "新闻里出现一条程野从未带回的规则".

# 5. Book Contract Gate｜First 10k–20k Words

By the end of the Opening Arc, a target reader should be able to answer without reading a design doc:

1. What repeated pleasure does this book promise?
2. What concrete thing can the protagonist accumulate?
3. What is the next visible target?
4. What 20–100 chapter ladder exists?
5. What larger world layer has been glimpsed?
6. Which person/relationship will recur?
7. What old state from this arc will still matter later?

If 3–5 are unclear:
> the book will feel locally good but globally small.

# 6. 《劫痕道途》 Required Correction

Current post-mini-climax pull:
> investigate the old scar / old route.

Good mystery, but too intangible as the only runway.

Required first-arc handoff should expose at least four concrete continuing ledgers:

## A. Job / Status
Example function:
> probationary 劫务 role / qualification exam / temporary permit.

Reader can see:
> from laborer -> licensed professional -> specialist -> authority.

## B. Money / Resource
The first arc should leave:
> actual pay / treatment debt / cultivation-resource cost.

Reader can see the economic ladder.

## C. Power
One scar should become:
> the first stable piece of a cultivation structure, not only a detector.

Reader can name:
> "this is what he owns now."

## D. Relationship
阮青禾 should receive:
> a concrete professional next step independent of Zhou Ye.

Their futures become coupled but not identical.

## E. Macro Layer
Before or at the end of the Opening Arc, glimpse:
> the city's visible tribulation-cost system.

Not by exposition.
Possible functions:
- a public map;
- a work queue;
- hospital classification;
- exam categories;
- a higher-tier contract;
- proof that "clean" cultivation has downstream cost.

The reader should end with:
> "I know what Zhou Ye is doing next week AND I can glimpse what this could become in 100 chapters."

# 7. 《把一条副本规则带回现实》 Required Correction

Current post-mini-climax pull:
> what is the next dungeon / rule?

Strong but vulnerable to Case Supply.

Required first-arc handoff:

## A. Countdown
By the end of Opening Arc:
> next Rule Field entry has a visible time/window.

This creates a literal next target.

## B. Preparation Asset
Cheng Ye owns:
- family protocol;
- official contact;
- prepared space/resources;
- knowledge from Rule #1.

The next arc starts before the dungeon starts.

## C. Evidence of Wider World
Before Rule #2 arrives, show:
> one impossible event caused by a rule Cheng Ye did NOT bring back.

This single beat proves:
> other returners / other rule sources exist.

Do NOT dump an agency or global organization yet.

## D. Rule Ledger
Rule #1 remains visibly alive:
> it affects family / police / work / criminals / future dungeons.

The reader sees that rules accumulate into reality rather than disappear between arcs.

## E. Far Promise
The book should expose, without explaining:
> "survivors are not merely escaping dungeons; they are collectively rewriting reality."

That is the macro contract.

# 8. Information Exposure Rule

"Overall introduction" should NOT become a prologue or lore dump.

Use four surfaces:

### 1. Title / Synopsis
Expose the largest clean promise.

### 2. Opening Incident
Demonstrate the smallest working version.

### 3. Early Institution / Public Artifact
Show there are larger systems:
- score board;
- job board;
- map;
- ranking;
- permit;
- news;
- market price;
- contract.

### 4. Post-Climax Handoff
Name the next concrete rung and glimpse the next scale layer.

This creates:
> small scene + large shadow.

# 9. New Architecture Requirement

D-0097 benchmark and architecture lanes must explicitly deliver:

- Book Contract statement;
- Near / Mid / Far Horizon;
- first-arc Visible Runway Handoff;
- tangible post-climax asset/target;
- first 20-ch "bigger than the opening" proof;
- anti-sitcom audit.

No architecture can PASS if its mini-climax closes more desire than it opens.

# 10. Final Principle

> **A climax should not merely answer a question. It should change the protagonist's state so visibly that a larger question becomes unavoidable.**

The reader should not leave thinking:
> "这一集挺完整。"

The desired reaction is:
> **"这一段结了，但现在他已经站到一个更大的东西面前了。"**
