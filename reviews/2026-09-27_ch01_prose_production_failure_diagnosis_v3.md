## Root failure
The failure is NOT lack of benchmark knowledge.

The failure is:
> benchmark insights were converted into independent local constraints, then optimized one by one.

Recent sequence:
1. fix fragmentation;
2. fix cultivation ontology;
3. fix Zhou Hang buyer role;
4. fix dialogue naturalness;
5. fix technique readability;
6. fix narrator explanation.

Each fix locally improved one dimension,
but the chapter lost:
- Cheng Ye's continuous personality engine;
- scene-level emotional/causal momentum;
- natural transitions;
- a unified narrator voice;
- whole-chapter rhythm.

This is constraint-fragmentation in the writing process.

## New hard rule: WHOLE-SCENE BEFORE LOCAL-PROSE

Do not draft by checklist item.

Before prose, define for each scene:
1. Cheng Ye's current desire;
2. his current interpretation of the situation;
3. what he personally chooses;
4. what Zhou Hang wants independently;
5. what Coach Shao wants professionally;
6. what changes physically/socially by scene end;
7. what new pressure carries into the next scene.

Then write the scene in one continuous pass.

Only AFTER a whole scene exists may local QA check:
- dialogue;
- action clarity;
- fragmentation;
- cultivation texture.

Local QA must not rewrite character motive or scene rhythm.

## Cheng Ye continuity guard
Every Ch1 scene must visibly preserve:
> light / practical / quick-thinking / optimistic / rule-reading / willing to test.

Not through jokes.
Through:
- what he notices;
- what he chooses;
- what risk he accepts;
- how he reframes setbacks.

If a prose fix removes this engine,
the fix is invalid.

## Scene transition guard
Every paragraph transition must answer:
> Why is the next sentence/paragraph happening NOW?

Avoid transitions created only because:
- author needs to explain a rule;
- author needs to introduce a concept;
- author needs to demonstrate personality.

Narrative transition should come from:
> action -> reaction -> consequence -> next choice.

## Prose voice guard
The narrator should not alternate between:
- technical manual;
- product-manager explanation;
- joke delivery;
- cultivation glossary.

One stable close-third voice:
> mostly follows Cheng Ye's attention and practical judgment.

## Benchmark application correction
Benchmark learning must be applied as:
> global scene behavior,
not:
> sentence-level imitation or checklist proof.

Examples:
- “角色有独立欲望” means Zhou Hang's decisions follow his goal across the whole scene.
- “修真生活化” means objects/institutions appear because people use them, not because prose needs a cultivation noun.
- “人物幽默来自行为” means Cheng Ye's decisions reveal him; do not insert extra quips.
- “快不等于碎” means scene state changes efficiently; not every paragraph must be dense.
