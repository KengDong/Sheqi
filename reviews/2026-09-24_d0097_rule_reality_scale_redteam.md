# 《把一条副本规则带回现实》Scale Red Team

# Verdict

> **REVISION REQUIRED, NOT ROOT SCALE FAILURE.**

The architecture materially improves the original CR6 warning:
- Reality is now an equal engine;
- 40-ch no-new-rule span is plausible;
- other returners prevent protagonist monopoly;
- recurring cast can compound.

But four blockers remain.

# 1. PASS｜40 Chapters Without New Rule

PASS.

R1 + R2 can generate:
- family;
- rescue;
- crime;
- housing;
- police;
- preparation;
- other-returner conflict.

This is the most important structural improvement.

# 2. BLOCKER｜Protagonist Personal Growth Is Still Too Soft

At Ch100 Cheng Ye gains:
- team;
- reputation;
- knowledge;
- base;
- relationships.

All good.

But male-commercial readers may still ask:
> “他本人现在比Ch1多了什么主动能力/选择权？”

The answer cannot be:
> “he knows rules better.”

Required:
> create a sparse, non-RPG **Rule-Field Access Authority** progression tied to surviving/returning.

Suggested function, NOT a numeric level system:
- initially: only forced entry;
- later: can enter early;
- later: can identify/choose between nearby rule-field openings;
- later: can synchronize entry with one consenting bound teammate;
- much later: can refuse/close one candidate return path at serious cost.

This gives personal agency without a second combat system.

# 3. BLOCKER｜R2/R3 Are Too Canon-Like Before Prose Proof

R2/R3 are good stress-test examples, but locking them now may over-engineer the novel.

Risk:
> architecture designs social consequences first, then picks rules to serve them.

Required:
> mark R2/R3 as **Prototype Rules**, not canon.

Before prose:
- generate 3–5 candidate simple rules for each slot;
- choose based on:
  1. dungeon fun;
  2. reality consequence;
  3. old-rule interaction;
  4. character choice;
  5. low cognitive cost.

Do not let the outline become a rule-design checklist.

# 4. BLOCKER｜Reality Engine Can Still Become “Policy Montage”

Architecture lists:
- housing;
- medicine;
- crime;
- commerce;
- schools;
- emergency response.

This can easily recreate the user's earlier complaint:
> world effects are being demonstrated rather than lived.

Required:
> every major reality arc must be anchored in 2–4 recurring humans.

No broad “society adapted” montage until:
> one family / one cop / one survivor / one adversary has paid or benefited.

# 5. Rule Scope / Zones

PASS WITH WATCH.

Local reality-zone anchoring is a strong scale-control device.

But it is a meaningful mechanics commitment not yet proven in prose.

Required:
> expose it as an observed consequence, not a system explanation.

Do not show a game map or “radius = 30km” unless story demands it.

# 6. Other Returners

PASS.

This is the main macro-scale unlock.

Important:
> different returners need different human reasons for choosing rules.

Not factions first.
People first.

# 7. Mystery

PASS.

“Rules are candidates for reality” is a valid far horizon.

Danger:
> reveal too early and collapse wonder.

Keep:
- first other rule;
- zones;
- field families;
- only later candidate-reality theory.

# 8. Comedy / Tone

Strong opportunity.

The premise supports:
> normal society behaving extremely rationally under absurd physics.

Best version:
> black comedy from consequences.

Avoid:
> characters joking while people die.

# 9. Head-Tier Gap

Main remaining issue:
> Cheng Ye can become the organizer/solver while the world gets bigger around him.

Need visible personal control progression.

# Required Revision

1. Add Rule-Field Access Authority growth ladder.
2. Downgrade R2/R3 to prototype, not canon.
3. Rewrite every reality arc around recurring people before systems.
4. Preserve zone scope but reveal via lived evidence.

After these:
> likely PASS, with CR6 reward-sameness risk still requiring P20/P50 behavioral proof.
