# Hot-work correction

Strong current works tend to use ONE core rule to do multiple jobs at once.

## 《我正经学生，每天只吃九种魔药》
One rule:
> dangerous drug side-effects reverse into benefits.

Same rule creates:
- opening low status / victim position;
- repeated daily behavior;
- growth;
- body transformation;
- world access;
- eventual high-level identity.

# New A3 hard gate

Reject any proposal where:
- opening identity is one mechanic;
- cultivation growth is another;
- send-off cases are a third;
- Heaven mystery is a fourth.

A qualified A3 premise must use ONE central rule to explain at least five:
1. old low value;
2. immediate revaluation;
3. repeated scene pleasure;
4. personal cultivation growth;
5. social/status rise;
6. world deterioration;
7. Heaven-scale endpoint.
