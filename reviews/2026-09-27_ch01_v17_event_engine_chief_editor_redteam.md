# 0. Verdict

The proposed move away from the paid-sparring shell is directionally correct.

The strongest new engine is NOT:
> everyone wants a clean result / Cheng Ye wants an abnormal result.

That contrast is too designed and becomes logically unstable if the event is genuinely a freshman risk/adaptation screening.

The stronger engine is:

> the public test has already completed ITS purpose by detecting instability and protecting the student;
> Cheng Ye's personal purpose is different: he needs the exact five-year 0.11s transition failure to appear under standardized pressure;
> therefore the safety system is correctly trying to stop, while Cheng Ye is correctly trying to continue within an allowed monitored option.

Both sides are reasonable.
Cheng Ye's personality creates the escalation.
This is a real event engine.

Decision:
> PASS the new shell, but freeze the corrected engine below rather than the proposal verbatim.

# 1. Why this is stronger than paid sparring

## A. Cheng Ye causes the event
Paid sparring required a buyer, payment logic, coach logic, and repeated explanation before Cheng Ye's own choice became central.

The new shell can begin with a result already in progress:
- system detects instability;
- safety logic reduces/stops load;
- Cheng Ye does not get the specific failure he came to expose;
- he chooses the legal complete-load/retest path.

The event can therefore become:
> situation -> Cheng Ye judgment -> Cheng Ye action -> consequence

instead of:
> order explanation -> buyer need -> coach rules -> first round -> Cheng Ye finally acts.

## B. The oddity exists without witty prose
Even if every joke is removed, the situation remains interesting:

> a safety system is doing its job correctly;
> one freshman is mildly frustrated because it protected him before the exact problem he wanted to catch appeared.

That is much closer to event comedy than V16's quip layer.

## C. It naturally makes Cheng Ye recognizable
The choice expresses:
- resource awareness;
- practical rule-reading;
- willingness to test on himself;
- bounded risk;
- evidence-seeking.

No narrator needs to call him practical.

## D. It can move Ji Changgeng earlier
Routine registration / buyer motivation / transaction closure disappear.
The anomaly can plausibly arrive during the first substantial test scene rather than after half the chapter.

# 2. P0 structural correction: remove “everyone wants a clean result”

This line should NOT survive into the writer brief.

Problem:
If the event is a freshman adaptation / injury-risk screen, a student hiding abnormalities is irrational and conflicts with the institution's purpose.

Do not manufacture:
> normal students want clean reports
just so Cheng Ye can look unusual.

Correct contrast:
> the system/operator regards “instability detected + load reduced” as a successful test outcome;
> Cheng Ye regards it as incomplete evidence for HIS narrower five-year problem.

No crowd-wide thesis is required.

Zhou Hang may personally prefer a clean / high-quality result for his own reasons, but he must not stand in for “everyone.”

# 3. P0 structural correction: define one institutional purpose

Freeze the event as:

> pre-enrollment freshman movement/adaptation screening with standardized load and safety monitoring.

Its job:
- produce baseline movement data;
- catch obvious instability/injury risk;
- stop or reduce load when thresholds are crossed.

It is NOT:
- entrance examination;
- ranking test;
- talent ceremony;
- public face-slapping stage;
- free full medical diagnostic.

This distinction matters because Cheng Ye is exploiting a useful overlap:
> standardized load can expose his problem,
> but the service was never designed to diagnose that problem fully.

That is why the conflict is natural.

# 4. P0 structural correction: one intervention, one choice

Do NOT build a long system battle.

Writer may use only:
1. one ordinary test attempt;
2. one safety reduction / early termination;
3. one Cheng Ye request/selection for an allowed monitored complete-load retest;
4. real pressure -> anomaly.

No:
- multiple menu screens;
- repeated warnings;
- three failed settings;
- loophole debate;
- operator arguing for paragraphs.

Otherwise the new engine recreates the old workflow problem under a school skin.

# 5. Zhou Hang

Strong recommendation:
> keep Zhou Hang as same-cohort peer.

This is better long-term than buyer.

But Zhou does NOT need to embody the normal world by speeches.

Minimum useful function:
- he is in the same test batch / paired or adjacent;
- his own movement quality shows better training access;
- he sees Cheng Ye choose to continue after the system already considers the result sufficient;
- he makes one independent choice during/after the test.

If a paired pressure component is needed for the movement test, Zhou can naturally participate.
If not needed, do not force him into the mechanism merely to preserve a character.

Do not recreate:
> Zhou pays / Zhou requests more pressure / Zhou asks for another sample
under a different label.

# 6. Safety-system comedy

PASS, with a tone warning.

The event is naturally funny only if nobody treats it as a joke.

The operator/system:
> instability detected; reduce load / end segment.

Cheng Ye's genuine response:
> this did not reproduce the thing he came to reproduce.

He then checks what monitored continuation is actually allowed.

Do not add:
- witty system notices;
- sarcastic narrator comments;
- NPCs laughing at him;
- repeated “你怎么还想把自己测出问题” dialogue.

The funny fact is:
> protection succeeds at exactly the wrong moment for Cheng Ye's private goal.

# 7. Anomaly / Ji Changgeng

PASS.

This shell improves the causal collision because Cheng Ye personally chooses to enter the exact pressure band the safety system tried to keep him out of.

Hard sequence:
> monitored retest -> 0.11 bottleneck -> rain overlap -> Ji in independent crisis -> same transition problem -> dangerous ancient solution -> Cheng Ye copies under current pressure.

Do NOT:
- let Ji appear before Cheng Ye's choice;
- let anomaly randomly unlock the high-load mode;
- let Cheng Ye instantly identify historical importance.

# 8. 800-yuan reward

Recommendation:
> REMOVE it from the required Chapter-1 engine.

Reason:
The paid-sparring shell naturally supported a sample bounty.
The freshman screening shell does not naturally produce an 800-yuan cash reward without inventing a new data-market/subsidy rule specifically to preserve an old payoff.

That would be author intent showing.

The new chapter already has a resource payoff:
> Cheng Ye gets a standardized high-load sample / evidence that would otherwise cost meaningful money to obtain.

If later canon already supports payment for validated rare motion data, 800 may appear AFTER validation as a secondary surprise.
But the Writer must not depend on it.

Therefore:
- do not title the chapter around 800;
- do not introduce an anomaly bounty in the first half;
- do not make money the reason for the high-load choice.

# 9. Resource/money texture

Keep:
- full dynamic diagnostic around 1200 as meaningful resource friction;
- Cheng Ye has collected fragmented evidence before;
- free standardized screening is attractive because it provides something useful without pretending to replace a full diagnostic.

Do not over-explain exact savings.
Reader only needs to understand:
> the free environment gives him a rare chance to get better evidence.

# 10. Opening shape

The new engine permits a materially stronger opening.

Recommended function:
> open inside the test or immediately after the safety system produces a result Cheng Ye finds insufficient.

The first-screen curiosity should be:
> Why is this freshman dissatisfied that the safety system successfully protected him?

Do NOT begin with:
- registration fields;
- explanation of free testing policy;
- 1200 price;
- biography;
- school brochure;
- “程野和别人不一样，因为……”

Let the reason emerge after the odd action.

# 11. Camera allocation

Stay longer on:
1. Cheng Ye realizing the test stopped before his real problem appeared;
2. his decision to request/choose monitored continuation;
3. the exact moment the 0.11 bottleneck appears;
4. Ji's forced solution;
5. reciprocal sight.

Compress:
- registration;
- school policy;
- full test procedure;
- all settings menus;
- repeated safety legitimacy;
- historical database process.

# 12. Risk of author intent in the proposal

Current proposal still smells designed in four places:

1. “everyone wants a clean result” — manufactured contrast.
2. hidden/available full-load option — can look written specifically for Cheng Ye unless presented as an ordinary standard option.
3. keeping 800 cash reward after removing the paid platform — likely transplanted baggage.
4. keeping Zhou in every beat — may be attachment to old cast function rather than scene need.

Correcting these removes most of the design scaffolding.

# 13. True comedy chain after correction

The final event chain should be:

1. standardized freshman movement screening;
2. system detects instability and safely truncates/reduces the segment;
3. institution considers that result sufficient;
4. Cheng Ye checks his own data and sees his actual 0.11 failure still did not fully appear;
5. he requests/selects the ordinary monitored continuation that the rules already permit;
6. reasonable observers realize he is intentionally returning to the condition the system just protected him from;
7. Cheng Ye does it anyway because that is what he came to learn;
8. his exact bottleneck appears;
9. dual-world overlap occurs at the bottleneck;
10. he copies Ji's dangerous answer and pays a physical cost;
11. modern test records something unprecedented;
12. Ji turns and sees him.

Remove all witty lines:
> the chain remains interesting.

PASS.

# 14. Final approval

The new shell is approved ONLY in this corrected form.

It is stronger than paid sparring because:
- Cheng Ye becomes the causal center earlier;
- the event is funny/odd through action rather than dialogue;
- Zhou can become a peer instead of a temporary client;
- modern cultivation institutions affect ordinary student life;
- Ji can enter substantially earlier;
- the first chapter no longer needs to spend half its length proving a transaction is rational.

The Writer should not read the original proposal.
The Writer should read only the frozen brief created from this redteam.
