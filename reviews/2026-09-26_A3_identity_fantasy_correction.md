# Benchmark distinction

## 《原来我是仙道天骄》
The strong first-glance function is:
> low-value artificial-root student in one world → high-value heavenly-root genius in another.

Reader reward:
- instant revaluation;
- immediate new options;
- status gap;
- future endpoint.

It is not strong merely because protagonist is a student or can cross worlds.

## 《从水猴子开始成神》
Function:
> low-status poor youth discovers an underpriced arena that uniquely favors him → visible path to river-god-like status.

## 《我的化身正在成为最终BOSS》
Function:
> ordinary / restricted identity → multiple legendary identities → world-scale endpoint.

## 《什么叫我成了灭世级怪谈？》
Function:
> prey / victim → the thing even monsters must fear.

# New hard gate for A3 identity

Any A3 opening identity must contain:

1. **OLD PRICE**
   - why society currently values protagonist low / normally.

2. **REVALUATION EVENT**
   - one event that instantly proves society's metric is incomplete.

3. **RARE VALUE**
   - what protagonist suddenly has that others cannot substitute.

4. **VISIBLE ENDPOINT**
   - what this can become at world scale.

5. **REPEATABLE PAYOFF**
   - how the revaluation keeps happening beyond Ch3.

A job title alone is insufficient.
