# 1. Frozen Commercial Constraints

For REBOOT-V3 first concept forge:

## C1｜Consumption promise must be legible
Unknown reader should understand the recurring pleasure without learning a glossary.

## C2｜Premise must be active immediately
Title/blurb promise is already happening in Ch1.

## C3｜Early tangible fruit
Prefer first material payoff/cost within roughly first 2–5 chapters.

## C4｜One dominant reusable engine
Early book should not require 4–5 equal systems.

## C5｜Second use must differ
The same Premium must naturally solve/create a different kind of scene.

## C6｜Recurring social arena
There must be a place/network of recurring humans that can generate scenes without fresh gimmicks.

## C7｜State must compound
Major arcs leave assets/status/relationships/access/debt/enemy knowledge/cost.

## C8｜No-fresh-gimmick survival
Book must run for meaningful stretches without a new skill/rule/dungeon/world/customer.

## C9｜Newbie production cost is a first-order metric
Per-2k-word author cost matters.

## C10｜150w-character capacity stress
Not an outline requirement.
Concept should plausibly support long serial life without becoming a different book.

## C11｜Comedy is optional
Use if it improves:
- comprehension;
- relationship attachment;
- low-friction reading;
- sharing;
- ordinary-chapter supply.

Do not force it.

## C12｜High-ceiling complexity gets a default newbie penalty
Puzzle-per-arc, many timelines, huge ensembles, hard-science, constant new worlds, etc. require extraordinary proof.
