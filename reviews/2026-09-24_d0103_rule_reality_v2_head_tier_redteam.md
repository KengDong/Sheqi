# D-0103｜Rule-Reality V2 Head-Tier Red Team

# Verdict

> **V2 is materially broader and closer to head-tier completeness, but do NOT call it “matched/exceeded” yet.**
>
> It is now strong enough to justify a dedicated prototype campaign.

The architecture has three plausible category-leading differentiators:

1. **a cleared instance permanently changes public reality;**
2. **the reward is an irreversible social choice rather than only personal loot;**
3. **dungeons are failed future fragments, so the instance engine and terminal mystery can be the SAME machine.**

But seven execution risks remain.

# 1. BLOCKER｜On-Page Dungeon Arrives Too Late

Current Opening starts AFTER an unseen first dungeon.

Therefore reader curiosity includes:
> “the next actual dungeon better be worth it.”

D-0102 placed second Field at Ch21.

For an unknown author this is too slow.

Required:
> Opening Ch1–4 -> reality/preparation Ch5–8 -> second Field starts ~Ch9.

The longer no-new-rule reality proof should happen AFTER the second on-page Field.

# 2. BLOCKER｜Rule Field Quality Is Still Theoretical

This is the candidate's biggest unproven dimension.

Competitive books:
- 《十日终焉》
- 《诡舍》
- 《天官赐邪》
- 《灵境行者》

can survive even if the macro mystery is temporarily absent because the local instance is itself strong.

Our candidate has only one completed reality-side Opening.

Required before P20:
> design and prose-test at least ONE full on-page Rule Field that readers would enjoy even if it produced no returned rule.

# 3. BLOCKER｜Cheng Ye Signature Exists in Design, Not Behavior

“asks who bears the worst-case cost” is good.

But it can still read like:
> competent responsible man.

Need one visible social/personality edge:
- dry humor;
- annoying insistence on clarity;
- one petty/ordinary preference;
- one behavior that causes friction even when no rule is active.

Voice Lab must prove:
> readers describe Cheng Ye without mentioning responsibility/power in generic terms.

# 4. WATCH｜Personal Progression Is Elegant But Less Visceral Than Competitors

Access Authority progression is Root-aligned.

But compared with:
- 《灵境行者》 skills/levels;
- 《天官赐邪》 divine gifts;
- 《时停起手》 accumulating minutes;
- 《全球死考》 score/reward ladder;

it is less immediately appetizing.

D-0102 added overlap-memory/anchoring.

Required:
> make each Access upgrade change an action scene immediately.

Examples:
- early entry saves prep time;
- opening choice avoids a known death field but sacrifices a clue;
- synchronized entry enables a relationship payoff;
- rejecting a rule is a major “win by refusing reward” spectacle.

Do NOT add stats merely to compensate.

# 5. BLOCKER｜Return-Rule Selection Procedure Is Unresolved

This cannot stay vague past Field Prototype.

It determines:
- climax;
- survivor conflict;
- protagonist agency;
- number of public rules.

Need 3 prototype mechanisms and prose-test ONE.

Hard:
> no meeting/vote climax;
> no automatic protagonist privilege with no cost;
> no multiple-returner rule explosion.

# 6. WATCH｜Terminal Secret Is Strong, But Must Avoid “Parallel Future” Genericness

Failed-future fragments are not unique by themselves.

The unique function is:
> **surviving a Field imports one of that future's laws and increases present/future similarity.**

Every macro reveal must preserve this causal link.

Do not drift into:
> generic multiverse / timeline war.

# 7. BLOCKER｜Recurring Ensemble Still Needs Commercial Roles

Current cast functions are sound but not yet hooky enough.

Need a minimum early durable set:
- family ordinary-life anchor;
- Fang Cen public-safety anchor;
- one survivor the reader likes;
- one survivor/rival the reader disagrees with but enjoys.

By Ch30:
> at least 3 non-MC people should create independent next-chapter desire.

# 8. WATCH｜Reality World Can Become Too “Correct”

Rules affecting:
- hospitals;
- police;
- commerce;
- housing

is logically rich.

But a head-tier novel cannot feel like a policy simulation.

Hard prose rule:
> **one person suffers/benefits first; system consequence second.**

No chapter exists merely to demonstrate “what society would do.”

# 9. PASS｜Terminal Rival Direction

“Future-Wanter” is strong.

Why:
- personal;
- tied to terminal mechanism;
- reader can sympathize;
- can sometimes save protagonist;
- not a policy-debate villain.

Strong mirror requirement:
> eventually Cheng Ye must see a future branch that tempts HIM in the same way.

# 10. PASS｜Endgame Circle

Opening:
> must bring one offered rule back.

Endgame:
> try to create a rule that no failed future offered.

This is a strong structural circle and should remain.

# 11. REQUIRED Pacing Revision

Recommended early skeleton:

## Ch1–4
Current Opening, tightened.
End:
- countdown;
- outside anomaly;
- future clue seed.

## Ch5–8
- family adaptation;
- Fang Cen case;
- prep;
- one proof other source exists.
No broad montage.

## Ch9–28
SECOND FIELD ON PAGE.
This is the first real proof the novel can compete with infinite-flow heads.

## Ch29–55
Reality consequence.
No new Returned Rule.
This is first old-state-compounding test.

## Ch56–80
Third Field / rival or cross-returner event.

## Ch81–120
Reality city crisis / first overlap.
No need for Rule #4.

This better serves unknown-author retention.

# 12. Prototype Campaign Required Before Full Opening Rewrite

Do NOT write 100 chapters.

Run four isolated proofs:

## P-A｜Breakout Surface
title/synopsis/first screen / Cheng Ye voice.

## P-B｜Second Rule Field
full 15–25 chapter-equivalent arc architecture + first 3–5 prose chapters, then if behavior strong complete the field.

## P-C｜Reality-Only Span
after Field #2, prove 5–10 consecutive chapters are wanted without a new rule.

## P-D｜Rival / Terminal Clue
test whether future-fragment clues and a charismatic rival increase desire rather than add lore.

# 13. Final Head-Tier Claim Standard

Only after P-A through P-D succeed can the project claim:

> candidate is not merely innovative on one axis;
> it has evidence across cold start, field engine, reality engine, protagonist, ensemble and macro spine.

Until then:
> **HEAD-TIER FRAMEWORK, NOT HEAD-TIER BOOK.**
