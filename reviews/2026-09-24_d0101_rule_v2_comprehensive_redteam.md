# D-0101｜Rule-Reality V2 Comprehensive Red Team

# Verdict

> **REVISION REQUIRED, NOT ROOT SCALE FAILURE.**

V2 is materially broader than V1 and now has a plausible head-tier combination.

However:
> several parts are still “architecturally attractive” rather than causally inevitable.

The following blockers must be resolved before claiming comprehensive head-tier readiness.

---

# 1. BLOCKER｜Failed-Future Logic and Return Choice Are Not Yet One Mechanism

Current V2 says:
- fields are failed future branches;
- after clearing, survivor chooses a rule to bring back;
- imported rule makes that branch more compatible with present.

Missing causal answer:
> if the future failed under those rules, why is importing one of them useful to present reality?

Risk:
> “future fragments” becomes decorative mystery pasted onto an existing reward mechanic.

Required unification:
> a Rule Field must be a **failed local future generated by a SMALL SET of candidate public laws whose interaction was survivable for a while but ultimately collapsed under a human/system failure.**

The field therefore acts as:
> **a stress-tested future cell.**

On clear:
- survivors do NOT select an arbitrary reward;
- they salvage ONE foundational law from that failed configuration.

Present reality is unstable and can only anchor one law fragment at that return event.

This makes:
> field story -> law candidates -> human choice -> reality consequence -> terminal branch weight
one causal chain.

---

# 2. BLOCKER｜Permanent Rule Count Still Risks Cognitive Explosion

Even if only 1–3 rules are foregrounded per arc, a 500–700 chapter book could accumulate dozens of permanent public laws.

That threatens:
- reader memory;
- writer consistency;
- scene usability;
- ordinary-life plausibility.

Required:
> **Rule Cadence Budget.**

Recommended ceiling:
- Ch1–100: 3 globally/locally important foreground laws total;
- Ch101–200: +2;
- Ch201–400: +2–3;
- Ch400+: very few genuinely new core laws.

Target:
> ~8–12 book-level CORE returned laws total.

Other returners may have minor/local rules, but:
- they remain geographically/background-bounded;
- they are not reader-memory obligations;
- they cannot repeatedly become surprise solutions.

Old laws should deepen more often than new laws appear.

---

# 3. BLOCKER｜Personal Growth Still Risks Being “Permission Growth”

Access Authority is structurally elegant but can feel administrative:
> enter early / choose entrance / synchronize teammate.

Reader may understand it without feeling a visceral reward.

Required:
> each authority upgrade must immediately create a scene-level advantage AND a harder cost.

Examples of function:
- early entry lets Cheng Ye reach someone before the field fully closes, but costs him preparation time;
- choosing between two openings means abandoning the people in the unchosen field;
- synchronized entry lets him protect a teammate, but binds their exit conditions.

This turns:
> permission -> power/choice.

Also require material state:
- family's store/cashflow;
- real prep assets;
- ability to refuse dangerous paid offers;
to visibly improve.

---

# 4. BLOCKER｜No Magnetic Co-Lead Yet

Family/Fang Cen are good structural anchors.

But compared with:
- 《十日终焉》 ensemble pull;
- 《异兽迷城》 trust relationships;
- strong infinite-flow recurring casts;

the architecture still lacks a character who makes readers say:
> “把这个人和程野放一起，我就想看。”

Required:
> by the second Rule Field, introduce ONE recurring survivor with unusually strong chemistry/conflict.

Do NOT decide romance in architecture.

The co-lead must have:
- unrelated life desire;
- a behavioral style opposite/complementary to Cheng Ye;
- a rule-choice philosophy;
- ability to win a scene without Cheng Ye;
- a reason to sometimes reject his protection logic.

One magnetic person is more valuable than three functional teammates.

---

# 5. BLOCKER｜Family Store Can Become Sitcom Hub

The store is a strong ordinary-life meter.

But if every reality consequence visits the shop:
> it recreates the old Sheqi “small situational drama” failure.

Required:
> use store at sparse high-value intervals.

Functions:
1. baseline normality;
2. visible economic improvement/loss;
3. recurring neighborhood faces;
4. future-overlap horror when the same physical place appears wrong.

Reality arcs must still move across:
- school;
- streets;
- police/rescue;
- transport;
- housing;
- other people's homes;
- public spaces.

The shop is an anchor, not the stage.

---

# 6. BLOCKER｜Terminal Mystery Can Still Become “Interesting Lore”

The repayment ladder is good.

But each answer must change Cheng Ye's available action.

Required coupling:

- “other returners exist”
  -> now he can seek/avoid them.

- “fields map to future real places”
  -> present-day geography becomes preparation data.

- “returned laws weight branches”
  -> rule choice becomes measurable strategic consequence.

- “present is unstable”
  -> rejecting all rules is no longer a viable moral shortcut.

- “归一”
  -> creates a deadline/physical contraction, not merely a theory.

If an answer only upgrades what characters know:
> mystery engine underperforms.

---

# 7. WATCH｜Future-Fragment Premise Could Feel Familiar/Derivative

“failed futures” is not unique by itself.

Uniqueness must come from:
> **the same public law that shaped the failed future is what the survivor may import into current ordinary life.**

Hard boundary:
- Rule Fields are NOT general future-tourism;
- no stock-market lottery;
- no convenient future-tech shopping;
- no arbitrary “see tomorrow”.

Future evidence should primarily reveal:
- law consequences;
- human histories caused by those laws;
- branch incompatibilities.

The core remains:
> rule choice, not time travel.

---

# 8. WATCH｜Dungeon Quality Is Still an Unproven Critical Gap

V2 has a strong Dungeon Quality Engine.

But this is still specification, not evidence.

Before P20:
> write/prototype at least THREE candidate second Rule Fields at architecture level, select one, then write ONE complete field arc and blind-read it.

The direct-comp benchmark is not cleared until:
> a clean reader would want the field even without the return-rule reward.

---

# 9. WATCH｜Black Comedy Can Flatten Horror

Good route:
> aftermath / adaptation / character chemistry.

Bad route:
> every death scene has a punchline.

Hard:
- field danger gets tonal priority;
- reality adaptation earns more comedy freedom;
- humor comes from rational people vs irrational physics.

---

# 10. WATCH｜Endgame Native-Law Creation Is Too Early to Canonize

“write one law born from the living present” is thematically elegant.

But currently:
> it is a desired ENDING FUNCTION, not proven mechanics.

Keep as:
> ENDGAME HYPOTHESIS.

Do not seed a “write law” power system in early/mid book.

Late architecture must earn it through:
- return-choice history;
- rejected branches;
- human relationships;
- understanding present instability.

---

# 11. WATCH｜Iconic Rival Needs More Than Philosophy

The rival's belief:
> survival first, livability second.

Good.

But they need an operational advantage.

Requirement:
> rival chooses harsher rules that repeatedly produce better short-term survival metrics.

This creates real temptation:
- fewer deaths;
- more control;
- worse life.

Cheng Ye cannot win debates by being morally nicer.
He must prove his choices sustain people over longer horizons.

---

# 12. WATCH｜Qidian Paid-Value Still Needs More Frequent State Fruits

Fanqie fit is naturally strong.

For Qidian:
> every ~8–15 chapters should usually leave at least one tangible changed state:
- money;
- asset;
- authority;
- relationship;
- clue answer;
- rule consequence;
- status/access.

This is a rhythm target, not a checklist.

If 30 chapters pass with only atmosphere/mystery:
> paid-value weakens.

---

# Final Red-Team Result

V2 now has:
- strong unique Premium;
- plausible terminal spine;
- broad engine relay;
- reality advantage;
- mystery ladder;
- human home base;
- growth concept.

It is NOT yet “comprehensively head-tier” because:
1. failed-future mechanics must fully merge with rule-return choice;
2. rule-count budget must be explicit;
3. personal authority needs visceral costs/benefits;
4. one magnetic co-lead is missing;
5. second Rule Field quality is unproven.

Fix 1–4 at architecture level.
Test 5 in prose/prototype.

Then:
> **PASS_TO_RULE_FIELD_PROTOTYPE_GATE** is plausible.
