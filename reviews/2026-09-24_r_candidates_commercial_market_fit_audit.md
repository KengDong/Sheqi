# R Candidate Commercial Market Fit Audit

Audit question:
> If the goal is specifically to maximize the probability of a mass commercial hit on Qidian / Fanqie, should the frozen current version proceed unchanged?

Allowed outputs only:
- KEEP ORIGINAL FOR P1
- COMMERCIAL MUTATION REQUIRED
- HOLD / NICHE
- DROP FROM COMMERCIAL TRACK

No Primary selected.

---

# R1｜Future Holdings

## 1. Natural platform
Qidian > Fanqie, but neither is naturally strong in current form.

Reason:
- adult relationship / speculative social mechanism can support paid discussion and character complexity;
- but Fanqie first-screen consumption needs a clearer repeatable external reward;
- Qidian also lacks an obvious conventional paid progression line.

## 2. Natural category
Urban speculative fiction / near-future social SF / relationship drama.

## 3. Existing reader pool
Partial pools:
- urban speculative readers;
- high-concept SF readers;
- relationship/ethics readers.

Missing:
> a large proven male-frequency mother market comparable to cultivation, urban high-power, rule-survival, apocalypse, business-growth.

## 4. Expected core reward at first glance
Current one-line promise gives:
- curiosity about "selling future presence";
- emotional pain of present love colliding with past contracts;
- moral/relationship re-evaluation.

These are strong premium rewards, not yet a mass-market chassis.

## 5. Repeatable reward?
Current frozen engine:
- old dates surface;
- protagonist must negotiate;
- partner / buyer / past poverty recontextualize a future commitment.

This is repeatable drama, but the reward is mostly:
> another harder relationship/ethical collision.

It does not reliably produce:
- power;
- money;
- asset;
- status;
- solved rule-game;
- organization;
- territory;
- accumulating information advantage.

## 6. Visible growth?
Weak.

Relationship history can compound, but the reader cannot easily answer:
> "After 50 chapters, what does Shen Zheng visibly own/control/understand that he did not before?"

## 7. 500k / 1m / 2m viability
- 500k: plausible with strong execution.
- 1m: risky without market/asset/status expansion.
- 2m: weak in frozen form; likely requires new contract types, buyers, institutions, legal systems or market-scale worldbuilding, which changes the concept.

## 8. Big-market innovation or demand education?
Mostly:
> demand education / small-market breakout.

## 9. Risk of writing P1 unchanged
HIGH.

A good Ch1 could prove prose and emotional hook while hiding the more important commercial weakness:
> low compounding reward density.

## 10. Can commercial mutation preserve Premium?
Yes, potentially.

The Premium worth preserving:
> poor young people monetizing future presence; past poverty acquires claims on present success.

But it would need to be attached to a stronger mature chassis:
- business/asset growth;
- organization/status climb;
- survival / rule settlement;
- or another visible compounding axis.

This report does not mutate the concept.

## Verdict
> **COMMERCIAL MUTATION REQUIRED**

# R2｜Second Autobiography

## 1. Natural platform
Neither is natural for mass male-frequency commercial scale.

Qidian could host it as speculative SF / social fiction.
Fanqie could potentially click strongly on the premise.
But long-term market fit is the issue.

## 2. Natural category
High-concept social SF / identity ethics / relationship drama.

## 3. Existing reader pool
Small-to-medium:
- split-self stories;
- speculative identity fiction;
- relationship ethics.

No clear mature mother-market supply line.

## 4. Expected core reward
- two lives in one body;
- "who gets to live today?";
- shared relationships become impossible to divide cleanly.

This is an excellent dramatic question.

It is not yet a repeatable male-commercial fantasy.

## 5. Repeatable reward?
Current repeatable unit:
- same body;
- two autobiographies;
- conflict over friend, partner, work, money, body marks, future time.

This creates strong scenes.

But commercial settlement is usually:
> a negotiated boundary / emotional choice.

That is qualitatively rich and quantitatively hard to compound.

## 6. Visible growth?
Very weak.

There is no natural single-axis accumulation of:
- strength;
- wealth;
- rank;
- resources;
- organization;
- solved-game advantage.

Relationship complexity rises, but that is not equivalent to commercial progression.

## 7. 500k / 1m / 2m viability
- 500k: possible as a strong finite novel.
- 1m: likely repetitive unless social institutions and additional systems expand.
- 2m: poor fit in frozen form.

## 8. Big-market innovation or demand education?
Clearly:
> new-demand education.

## 9. Risk of writing P1 unchanged
VERY HIGH for this commercial objective.

It may win a blind-read first chapter precisely because its premise is emotionally sharp.
That does not prove it has mass long-form commercial compounding.

## 10. Can commercial mutation preserve Premium?
A mutation strong enough to give it a large market would likely alter the actual identity of the book.

If we add:
- combat;
- game rules;
- power system;
- large occupational ladder;
- resource economy;

the "two autobiographies sharing a body" may become a gimmick rather than the book's center.

## Verdict
> **DROP FROM COMMERCIAL TRACK**

Important:
> not a literary quality verdict; retain in concept library if desired.

# R3｜Borrowed Tribulations

## 1. Natural platform
Qidian first, Fanqie second.

## 2. Natural category
Cultivation / xianxia / eastern fantasy.

## 3. Existing reader pool
VERY LARGE.

This is the only current R candidate already born inside one of the strongest proven mother markets.

## 4. Expected core reward
Current frozen concept:
- protagonist carries risks/tribulations transferred from stronger cultivators;
- old risks wake as he cultivates;
- source fingerprints expose the hidden cost behind others' glory.

Readers can immediately expect:
- cultivation;
- survival through tribulation;
- uncovering old power histories;
- moral reversal about who paid for strength.

## 5. Repeatable reward?
Yes, but currently misallocated.

Existing repeatable unit:
> another borrowed risk awakens → identify source → survive / confront relationship.

Commercial risk:
> it becomes case-of-week by source.

The mature chassis wants:
- his own cultivation progress;
- useful gains from surviving / studying borrowed tribulations;
- techniques, body changes, resources, reputation, alliances;
- visible new capability generated by the very risks that hurt him.

Frozen concept evidence already showed:
- situation reservoir strong until ~34;
- then marginal decline;
- new source / old strongman's debt / new bearer begins to repeat.

## 6. Visible growth?
Present but underpowered.

There are hints:
- own cultivation;
- risk-recognition expertise;
- public identity;
- mutual-aid relations.

But they are not yet a single dominant commercial growth ladder.

## 7. 500k / 1m / 2m viability
- 500k: strong.
- 1m: plausible if growth is re-centered on cultivation assets rather than new source cases.
- 2m: current frozen version still risky; would need a clearer compounding cultivation grammar.

## 8. Big-market innovation or demand education?
Best current position:
> **big-market innovation.**

Familiar:
- cultivation / tribulation / realm progression.

Premium:
- powerful people externalized the cost;
- the body of the poor preserves the source history;
- growth wakes old debt.

This is exactly the market pattern seen in successful works:
> mature chassis stays legible; Premium reinterprets its social meaning.

## 9. Risk of writing P1 unchanged
MEDIUM-HIGH.

It was held before because of finite runway.
Commercial recalibration actually re-opens it relative to R1/R2/R7:
> its chassis is much stronger.

But unchanged P1 would still test a concept whose growth ledger is not yet commercially optimized.

## 10. Can commercial mutation preserve Premium?
Yes.

Of all five, this has the cleanest potential for a mutation that does NOT destroy the original Premium.

Necessary direction:
> keep cultivation as main food; make borrowed tribulations generate a visible accumulating personal advantage/cost ladder, instead of treating each source as a new story case.

No mutation is performed here.

## Verdict
> **COMMERCIAL MUTATION REQUIRED**

# R7｜Dead People Still Work

## 1. Natural platform
Qidian slightly > Fanqie.

## 2. Natural category
Near-future workplace SF / AI social fiction / career drama.

## 3. Existing reader pool
Topical but not clearly proven as a giant long-form male-fiction market.

It benefits from:
- AI anxiety;
- workplace competition;
- dead predecessor hook;
- one-line click clarity.

But current market evidence does not show "AI workplace ethics" itself as a repeatably dominant mother market.

## 4. Expected core reward
- compete with a dead predecessor's model;
- finally beat it;
- negotiate what work, legacy and replacement mean.

Near reward is strong:
> "the dead expert admits the living junior's solution is better."

## 5. Repeatable reward?
Current engine:
- living worker vs dead model in projects, promotion, standards, family legacy.

Potential rewards:
- career wins;
- promotions;
- better projects;
- model shutdown / succession choices.

This is more commercial than R2 because a career ladder could be visible.

But the frozen promise centers:
> ethical/occupational shadow of the dead model,
not a broad career ascent fantasy.

## 6. Visible growth?
Medium-low.

Possible:
- role;
- salary;
- influence;
- professional skill.

But not yet structurally guaranteed by the concept.

## 7. 500k / 1m / 2m viability
- 500k: plausible.
- 1m: uncertain.
- 2m: weak unless it becomes a broader career/organization-growth novel, which may subordinate the original death-AI Premium.

## 8. Big-market innovation or demand education?
Mostly:
> topical niche / profession-SF breakout.

It has a familiar "workplace ascent" substrate, but that substrate is not currently the frozen dominant promise.

## 9. Risk of writing P1 unchanged
HIGH.

A first chapter can be excellent because the line is sharp and contemporary.
That does not prove multi-million-character consumption.

## 10. Can commercial mutation preserve Premium?
Possibly, but unlike R3 the mutation pressure is substantial.

The strongest possible mature chassis would be:
- career / organization ascent;
- AI-assisted industrial competition;
- project ownership / company building.

But that may turn "dead people still work" into one issue among many.

## Verdict
> **HOLD / NICHE**

Keep as a potentially sharp, timely finite/medium-form book or specialty lane.
Do not treat it as the current best mass-hit chassis.

# R8｜Missing Day List

## 1. Natural platform
Fanqie > Qidian.

## 2. Natural category
Suspense brain-hole / urban anomaly / rule fiction.

## 3. Existing reader pool
LARGE adjacent pool:
- rule suspense;
- urban anomaly;
- infinite / survival readers;
- identity-mystery readers.

Among current R candidates, this is the closest to a proven Fanqie mass-market chassis.

## 4. Expected core reward
Current frozen promise foregrounds:
- everyone can see you, but nobody can map you back to your old identity;
- systems also fail;
- next day restores;
- partner must choose whether to trust a stranger who is actually you.

This gives:
- a strong first-screen anomaly;
- relationship pain;
- practical identity chaos;
- potential stealth / freedom / exploitation fantasy.

## 5. Repeatable reward?
Current frozen engine is not yet the same as the market-proven rule-survival engine.

It repeatedly produces:
- "what happens to this relationship / job / family when nobody recognizes me?"

But the protagonist already knows how to use missing days to make money.

The missing part is:
> a stronger permanent settlement after each missing day.

Market leaders in rule fiction usually leave behind:
- points;
- information;
- ability;
- item;
- rank;
- permanent relationship/coalition;
- access to a new layer of world truth.

R8 currently resets identity every next day.

That daily reset is elegant dramatically, but commercially dangerous if most external state also resets.

## 6. Visible growth?
Medium-low.

Potential axes:
- money;
- skill using missing days;
- trust protocols;
- network of people who know his pattern;
- information about the rule.

But frozen concept deliberately does not rely on controller/organization/solution mystery.

That preserves intimacy but limits large-scale progression.

## 7. 500k / 1m / 2m viability
- 500k: plausible.
- 1m: possible if permanent state accumulates strongly.
- 2m: current frozen version risks episodic "new social situation during a missing day."

## 8. Big-market innovation or demand education?
Halfway:
> a familiar high-demand rule/anomaly chassis, but the current version uses it mainly for relationship ethics rather than settlement/survival/progression.

This is why it is commercially more promising than R1/R2/R7, but not yet ready unchanged.

## 9. Risk of writing P1 unchanged
MEDIUM-HIGH.

Its first chapter likely tests extremely well because the visual hook is immediate.
But P1 could overreward "great premise + intimate scene" while hiding weak permanent accumulation.

## 10. Can commercial mutation preserve Premium?
Yes.

Premium to preserve:
> a day when identity mapping breaks but physical presence remains;
> love, work and ownership must operate without recognition.

Commercial mutation would need:
> permanent state gains / stakes / usable information / external pressure attached to missing days, without merely adding a controller conspiracy.

No mutation is performed here.

## Verdict
> **COMMERCIAL MUTATION REQUIRED**

# Commercial Gate Conclusion

No:
> KEEP ORIGINAL FOR P1

Why:
> This gate was created specifically because earlier story-engine tests did not test mass-market chassis.

The current best evidence says:
- R3 has the strongest native mass-market chassis;
- R8 has the strongest adjacency to a proven Fanqie chassis;
- R1 has strong Premium but weak chassis;
- R7 has strong topicality but uncertain mass longform;
- R2 is strongest as a finite high-concept identity novel, not as the project's "maximize Qidian/Fanqie hit probability" vehicle.

This is NOT a Primary selection.

The correct next step is:
> launch Commercial-Native Concept Forge and, if editor_in_chief chooses, separately design commercial mutations for R1/R3/R8 so they can compete on equal commercial terms.
