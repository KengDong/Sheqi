# D-0096｜Hit-Scale Amplification Audit
## 《劫痕道途》 / 《把一条副本规则带回现实》 / 《养一条会跟我走的灵脉》

Trigger:
> Author explicitly reports that even the strongest Sheqi openings feel small when casually compared with 《没钱修什么仙？》, and asks whether the books truly have Qidian/Fanqie hit-scale volume rather than merely a good first mini-arc.

This audit treats that criticism as a root-level commercial question.

# 0. First-Principles Correction

The problem is NOT:
> “we need a bigger map / bigger villain / more lore.”

The problem is:
> **the current candidates often operate as single-premium local loops, while top commercial long-form fiction uses the premium as an infrastructure layer that automatically activates multiple independent social systems.**

A story feels large when one ordinary choice can simultaneously move:
- power/cultivation;
- money/resources;
- identity/status;
- relationships;
- organizations;
- rules/institutions;
- mystery/scale;
- next desire.

《没钱修什么仙？》 benchmark:
> cultivation + education + finance + employment + medicine + consumption + family + bureaucracy are one operating system.

Therefore:
> interview / class / job / competition / rent are not side stories. Each is a high-coupling bus through the whole world.

Current Sheqi danger:
> Premium -> one incident -> local people -> settlement.

Required upgrade:
> **Premium -> multiple autonomous systems with conflicting incentives -> permanent state in each system -> those systems later collide without waiting for the protagonist.**

# 1. Current Market Reality

Current strong-platform evidence does NOT support “plain traditional cultivation grammar alone is the highest-probability breakout route for an unknown new book.”

Traditional/eastern-fantasy chassis remains commercially valid.
But current leaders repeatedly pair a familiar chassis with a strong differentiating experience:
- cultivation x school/finance/work;
- folk profession x supernatural growth;
- food/business x period life;
- urban power x accumulable time;
- infinite survival x strong rule/settlement;
- apocalypse x multi-theme worlds/group growth.

Therefore:
> **traditional cultivation is not dead; undifferentiated traditional-cultivation scene grammar is strategically weak for this project's maximum-hit objective.**

# 2. Benchmark Gap｜Why Our Strong Openings Still Feel Smaller Than 《没钱修什么仙？》

## 《没钱》
One ordinary scene often pays multiple channels:
> money + cultivation + rank + relationship + social absurdity + new institution + next choice.

World information is never free-floating.
It enters because the protagonist needs:
- school;
- money;
- medicine;
- work;
- housing;
- competition.

Old systems keep operating after he leaves.

## Current《劫痕》
The Opening Arc is strong, but its current highest-energy chain is still:
> old scar -> hidden route -> bodily cost -> incident/investigation.

It opens:
- waste-end labor;
- medicine;
- examination;
- public tribulation infrastructure;
- bureau work.

This is promising, but these layers are not yet fused into a single economy of cultivation.

## Current《规则》
The Opening Arc strongly opens:
- family;
- debt/collection;
- police;
- platforms;
- schools/hospitals/public behavior.

But several layers appear as social-effect demonstration rather than independent dramatic engines.
The reality side can become huge only if these systems gain:
> money, status, jobs, markets, organizations, rival interests, recurring characters, and permanent adaptation.

## Current《灵脉》
Theoretical reservoir is large (40).
But Opening Arc spends its first commercial budget on:
> shelter + public canal + old property/permit responsibility.

The reader has not yet been paid enough with:
> cultivation power + resource ownership + visible scale + competition/reversal.

Hence “large theoretical runway” does not rescue “weak desired experience.”

# 3. 《劫痕道途》｜Scale Rebuild

## 3.1 What MUST remain

Keep:
- 周野;
- 阮青禾;
- old scar remembers paths/costs;
- healing may erase value;
- scars remain real bodily costs;
- opponent can study/target them;
- costs are not free power;
- current Opening Arc action/relationship quality.

Do NOT turn it into:
> “collect more scars = collect more skills.”

## 3.2 New macro engine

Upgrade the world premise from:
> 周野有特殊劫痕。

to:
> **整个修行文明都在处理“成长代价”，而周野第一次能看见这些代价到底被送去了哪里，并把被别人清掉/外包/丢弃的成本编进自己的修行结构。**

This creates one unified social metabolism:

Cultivator breakthrough
-> tribulation / bodily cost
-> treatment / cleansing
-> infrastructure diversion
-> waste / residue / downstream risk
-> insurance / contracts / public bureaus / sect standards / black market
-> 周野 can read the route.

Premium becomes civilization infrastructure.

# 3.3 Four interlocking surfaces

### Surface A｜Cultivation / Combat
Reader must still eat:
- realms;
- techniques;
- resources;
- combat;
- breakthroughs;
- public status.

Scar value must visibly convert into:
> unique circulation routes / combination / resistance / combat decisions / realm foundation.

Every ~20 chapters, Reader must answer:
> 周野现在具体比20章前强在哪里？

### Surface B｜Medicine / Body
阮青禾 is not romantic garnish.

Her independent career becomes a second engine:
- treatment;
- 净痕;
- rehabilitation;
- medical ethics;
- standardized bodies vs nonstandard bodies;
- whether to remove / keep / reorganize a scar.

She should gain her own:
- qualification;
- reputation;
- institutional conflict;
- professional wins/losses.

Their relationship is compelling because:
> she wants him alive and repairable;
> he increasingly needs some damage to remain part of his Dao.

### Surface C｜Work / Money / Status
周野 needs a legible livelihood.

Possible recurring verbs:
> 接劫务 / 护渡劫 / 验旧路 / 领任务 / 考牌 / 争资源 / 赚功 / 升职级.

Do not hide economic life behind lore.

Reader should see:
- what a job pays;
- what resources cost;
- why a credential matters;
- who can hire him;
- how new status opens new social layers.

### Surface D｜Institution / Industry / History
Build a full economy around growth costs:
- 劫务司;
- 净痕医疗;
- sect breakthrough services;
- old tribulation infrastructure;
- commercial protection;
- liability/compensation;
- residual-material recycling;
- illegal scar grafting / forbidden practices;
- old-city records.

The institution is not a “social-critique essay.”
It creates:
> jobs, money, danger, authority, enemies, clients, resources, and maps.

# 3.4 100-chapter scale ladder

## Ch1–4｜KEEP / polish
Current Opening Arc remains viable.
It proves:
> scar useful + real cost + relationship + old infrastructure + observer status.

## Ch5–15｜From anomaly to livelihood
周野 does not immediately investigate another random old scar.

He enters real劫务 work.
Must gain:
- first recurring income/resource channel;
- first formal tool/credential;
- first public use of scar knowledge;
- first opponent who understands his recovery/body weakness;
- first independent 阮青禾 professional win.

World surfaces:
> labor -> medicine -> bureau -> client/sect -> money.

## Ch16–30｜From body mystery to cultivation path
The first scar must become more than detector.

It is reorganized into:
> the first stable piece of his personal cultivation circulation.

Payoff:
- actual realm/power growth;
- identifiable combat style;
- a choice between “clean standard route” and “scar-integrated route”;
- body cost remains.

## Ch31–50｜From one incident to an industry
Partial answer to 东堤 old case:
> the scar is not a freak accident.

Reveal a concrete upstream/downstream system:
> somebody's “clean” cultivation cost did not disappear; it was routed, stored, sold, buried, or carried elsewhere.

Important:
> do NOT reveal a giant evil conspiracy.
The stronger version is a normal system with many rational participants and bad externalities.

At Ch50 the protagonist should own:
- recognized qualification/status;
- first stable scar combination;
- money/resources;
- continuing relationship contract with 阮青禾;
- one institutional ally;
- one institutional opponent;
- actionable knowledge that opens a larger region.

## Ch51–100｜From city job to regional cultivation economy
Change story mode.

Use:
- sect breakthrough protection;
- public competition / qualification;
- commercial contracts;
- rival nonstandard cultivators;
- black-market cost transfers;
- a major regional tribulation event.

At ~Ch100:
> 周野 is no longer “injured laborer who senses old routes.”
He is becoming a recognized new type of cultivator whose body can organize multiple historical costs into an actual Dao.

# 3.5 200–500+ chapter scale

Escalate:
individual scar
-> scar composition
-> team protection / medicine
-> city tribulation infrastructure
-> sect/industry cost structures
-> regional standards
-> war / mass breakthrough / catastrophe
-> cultivation civilization's method of handling cost.

Endgame-scale question:
> **a civilization can become stronger by making someone else carry the cost; what does a Dao look like when the cultivator refuses to pretend those costs disappeared?**

Commercial requirement:
> this theme must remain embodied in battles, realms, jobs, money, organizations and choices—not speeches.

# 3.6 阮青禾 priority

Author spontaneous attachment is valuable.

Develop her as:
> medical/professional co-engine + emotional anchor + someone with independent ambition.

Do not mechanically write “tsundere”.

What currently works:
- sharp local intention;
- she refuses to let 周野 choose for her;
- medical competence;
- caring expressed through action / irritation / records.

Preserve:
> she can win scenes without protagonist;
> her career sometimes conflicts with keeping his scars;
> she knows his body better than anyone but does not own his decisions.

# 3.7 Kill condition

If by a P7/P20 prototype the book still reads primarily as:
> old problem -> scar senses hidden route -> 周野 bridges it -> scar worsens -> investigate next old account

then scale rebuild has failed.

# 4. 《把一条副本规则带回现实》｜Scale Rebuild

## 4.1 What MUST remain

Keep:
- extremely simple first rule;
- modern familiar surface vocabulary;
- family anchor;
- rules apply to everyone, not only protagonist;
- permanent reality consequences;
- rules are choices with irreversible cost;
- first reality arc.

Do NOT turn it into:
> Rule #1 / Rule #2 / Rule #3 collection.

# 4.2 Two-engine architecture

The novel needs two equally real engines.

### Engine A｜Dungeon Survival
Provides:
- new location/theme;
- immediate life/death pressure;
- teammates/rivals;
- action/strategy;
- new rule candidates;
- clues about the larger mechanism.

### Engine B｜Reality Transformation
Provides:
- family life;
- police/emergency;
- crime;
- work/school;
- business/markets;
- media/platform;
- organizations;
- law/custom;
- permanent relationships and assets.

Rule choice must couple the engines:
> what helps survive / complete a dungeon may be terrible for reality;
> what is safest for society may reduce the protagonist's future options.

# 4.3 Entry mechanism must become simple and inevitable

Current unresolved user question:
> why/how does he enter the next dungeon?

Requirement:
- one sentence understandable;
- predictable enough to create preparation;
- not arbitrary author teleport;
- tied to previous survival/rule choice;
- creates agency where possible.

Preferred structural direction:
> **通关者在返回现实后会得到下一次“规则场开启”的明确倒计时/窗口；随着现实永久规则累积，规则场与现实的边界越来越薄。**

Do NOT front-load cosmic explanation.

Reader only needs:
1. when next entry can happen;
2. whether it is compulsory / avoidable;
3. what can be prepared;
4. what old reality rules matter inside.

The deeper “why” becomes long mystery.

# 4.4 The world cannot revolve only around 程野

Scale requires:
> **other returners exist.**

Do not reveal a global army in Ch5.

Progression:
- first hear evidence of another impossible rule;
- meet another returner;
- discover different survivors choose different rules for different reasons;
- organizations/factions begin competing over which rules should enter reality.

This converts:
> protagonist's strange life

into:
> **humanity is collectively rewriting reality through survival choices.**

# 4.5 Rule cognitive budget

Hard rule:
> each arc foregrounds only 1–3 rules.

Old rules become:
- habits;
- social infrastructure;
- background customs;
- tools used without re-explaining.

Do not force reader to hold 17 legal clauses.

Rule design standard:
> one simple sentence + huge consequence.

Not:
> paragraph-long loophole contracts.

# 4.6 Personal growth / state accumulation

If only society changes, protagonist lacks visible long-form fruit.

Every major arc should leave several of:
- survival competence;
- recurring teammates;
- official / underground status;
- safehouse / protocols / resources;
- information advantage;
- relationship change;
- access to a new rule-space;
- narrow “carrier knowledge/authority” tied to rules already survived.

Avoid adding a separate RPG system.

The protagonist's durable edge should primarily be:
> **he remembers the extreme version of a rule, understands how it behaves under pressure, and makes better irreversible choices than people encountering it for the first time.**

# 4.7 100-chapter scale ladder

## Ch1–4｜KEEP / polish
True-name rule enters reality.
Family/debt/police/social consequence.
Mini-climax closes one threat.

## Ch5–12｜Reality learns
Focus on only a few concrete surfaces driven by protagonist need:
- sister safety;
- police/方岑;
- work/identity;
- preparation for next entry.

Avoid checklist montages of “school / hospital / platform all changed.”

Permanent fruits:
- family protocol;
- official contact;
- first safe preparation space;
- next-entry certainty.

## Ch13–25｜Second dungeon
Must not be another true-name puzzle.

Goals:
- introduce recurring survivor(s);
- prove dungeon itself is exciting enough to carry 10+ chapters;
- old returned rule matters in an unexpected way OR creates a disadvantage;
- protagonist reaches a real choice among candidate return rules.

Mini-climax:
> return with Rule #2, but the true reward is changed team/status/knowledge and a new reality problem.

## Ch26–45｜Reality collision
Rule #1 + Rule #2 create an outcome neither produced alone.
Another returner/another returned rule is discovered.

Now open:
- official response;
- underground use;
- business/crime;
- public adaptation;
but only through concrete protagonist goals.

## Ch46–70｜Third dungeon / competing choice
First time protagonist and another returner disagree over:
> which rule should be allowed into reality.

The choice itself becomes conflict.

## Ch71–100｜City-scale rule crisis
Pay off first large collision.

Partial macro answer:
> these dungeons are not isolated entertainment arenas.
They are changing the baseline rules of reality.

By Ch100 protagonist should own:
- recurring team;
- official/underground social identity;
- multiple permanent real-world relationships;
- usable preparation assets;
- a body of rule knowledge;
- enemies who want different reality outcomes;
- proof that other cities/regions are also changing.

# 4.8 200–500+ chapter scale

Escalation:
family rule survival
-> city adaptation
-> competing returners
-> organizations
-> rule markets / safe zones / cultures
-> regional incompatibility
-> national / global rule ecology
-> reality itself becoming something new.

Potential large-scale conflict:
> groups intentionally seek certain dungeons because they want certain rules returned.

This creates:
- politics without requiring political essay;
- commerce;
- war;
- alliances;
- migration;
- safe/unsafe cities;
- competing visions of daily life.

Endgame macro promise:
> **every dungeon victory permanently changes how everyone lives; eventually the question is no longer “how do I survive the next rule?” but “what kind of reality are the survivors building?”**

# 4.9 Death Note distinction

Author spontaneously recognized intuitive similarity:
> name + person-location consequence.

Preserve simplicity, but differentiate at root:

Death Note-type mechanic:
> exclusive personal tool/weapon.

This book:
> **a chosen rule becomes public physics/social reality and affects everyone.**

Protagonist advantage is:
- origin knowledge;
- choice history;
- preparedness;
not monopoly ownership.

# 4.10 Kill condition

If long-form becomes:
> enter new dungeon -> solve puzzle -> choose new rule -> show several social consequences -> repeat

then CR6's original Reward Sameness / Case Supply warning is CONFIRMED and the book should be stopped or radically restructured.

# 5. 《养一条会跟我走的灵脉》｜Salvage Audit

## 5.1 New author verdict

The author now explicitly says:
> “灵脉我看了很失望，我以为还是很爽的文章，但是真的太差了。”

This overrides prior “AI absolute reader YES” as commercial author evidence.

Current Opening Arc status:
> **COMMERCIAL EXECUTION FAILURE / ROOT CONCEPT NOT YET DEAD.**

The 40-seed reservoir only proves theoretical runway.
It does not prove readers want the current experience.

# 5.2 Root diagnosis

The title/premium promises:
> a major cultivation resource that follows me.

Likely reader appetite:
- ownership;
- cultivation advantage;
- visible resource scale;
- sect envy/conflict;
- impossible movement spectacle;
- “they can seize land but cannot seize my vein” reversal;
- escalating mobile territory.

Current Arc spends too much early budget on:
- shelter;
- old canal;
- public shed;
- vegetable plot;
- property/permit;
- maintenance responsibility.

Those can become good later life layers.
They are badly sequenced as the first major payoff.

The reader is asked to eat vegetables before receiving the main dish.

# 5.3 Salvage requires REWRITE, not polish

Do NOT patch current Ch1–4.

Keep only:
- “灵脉会跟陆照川走”;
- possibly 陆照川's practical survival lens;
- selected human relationships if they still help.

Rebuild Opening Arc from page 1.

# 5.4 New first-arc reward grammar

Within first ~3 chapters, naturally deliver three different fruits:

1. **Impossible ownership reversal**
   - sect / landowner takes the land;
   - the vein leaves with him.

2. **Immediate cultivation/resource fruit**
   - the moving vein lets him actually break a bottleneck / recover a resource / grow something valuable / gain a visible cultivation advantage.
   - Reader must see why a cultivator would desperately want this.

3. **Seizure / lock attempt fails visibly**
   - a stronger actor tries to pin, buy, seal, or claim the vein;
   - the vein's mobility changes the outcome.

First mini-climax:
> not “获得三十日临护”.

It should be closer to:
> **the world has now publicly learned that the valuable resource cannot be owned separately from this person, and he leaves with a first true moving cultivation foothold.**

# 5.5 Re-sequence the “home” theme

Do not remove home/belonging.

Move it AFTER fantasy payment.

Correct order:
> WOW -> POWER/RESOURCE -> CONFLICT/REVERSAL -> PEOPLE CHOOSE TO FOLLOW -> HOME/RESPONSIBILITY.

Current order is too close to:
> WOW -> SHELTER -> CANAL -> PERMIT -> COMMUNITY.

# 5.6 Surface-language reset

First 10 chapters should use low-translation-cost language.

Avoid stacking:
- 渠棚
- 菜畦
- 三岔集
- 分口
- 泄口闸石
- etc.

Principle:
> high-concept novelty sits in the spirit vein; ordinary life objects should usually be ordinary words.

# 5.7 Familiar cultivation chassis must remain visible

Reader must repeatedly get:
- realm progress;
- spiritual resources;
- cultivation-speed advantage;
- market value;
- sect/resource conflict;
- visible power.

The moving-home/community layer is Premium expansion, not a substitute for cultivation payoff.

# 5.8 Scale ladder if salvage succeeds

personal moving vein
-> moving cultivation patch
-> companions follow
-> portable industry/resource
-> route/trade value
-> mobile camp
-> moving sect
-> moving mountain/resource system
-> regional migration/ecology
-> mainland-scale moving vein politics.

This theoretical scale already exists in the 40-seed reservoir.

The failure is:
> the current prose makes the reader wait too long before tasting why that future would be exciting.

# 5.9 Hard salvage gate

Authorize exactly ONE rebuilt Opening Arc experiment.

If author still experiences:
- slow nurturing feel;
- weak爽 point;
- jargon friction;
- “I understand the design but don't want to read”;

then stop this candidate despite reservoir strength.

Do not rescue it a second time by adding more architecture.

# 6. Shared First-Principles Hit Standard Going Forward

For every serious survivor, ask:

## First 20 chapters
Can Reader name:
- 3+ distinct reward types;
- 3+ independent social surfaces;
- 2+ recurring non-protagonists with own goals;
- one visible power/resource/status growth curve;
- one larger world question;
- one changed daily-life state?

## 50 chapters
Have old assets / people / rules collided in ways not possible at Ch10?

## 100 chapters
Has the protagonist entered a larger social/economic/power layer, not merely a larger map?

## 300+ chapters
Can the book change story mode while preserving the original Premium?

## 1–3 million words
Does the Premium become MORE important as scale rises?

If not:
> it is a good concept, not yet a hit-scale engine.
