## External comparison

Current/proven head-tier samples do not require constant multi-POV, but they repeatedly expand the reader's cognitive horizon.

Examples:
- 《没钱修什么仙？》 early chapter functions rapidly move across interview, school, sports, deity-invocation, teacher, work, cultivation, competition, new job, center building, patrol, secrets and martial demonstration.
- 《十日终焉》 early chapters keep adding new professions, truths, social roles, game logic and world interpretation even before the world map becomes broad.
- Current Qidian high-visibility titles such as 《青山》 also show fast early expansion across family, colleagues, supernatural objects, pets, medical visits, revenge, institutional power and交易.

Conclusion:
> **“宽”不是地图多，而是每1—3章让读者重新理解‘这本书还能发生什么’。**

## Dialogue finding

Raw dialogue ratio is NOT the root cause.

C01 rough quoted-text share:
- Ch1 ~35%
- Ch2 ~30%
- Ch3 ~33%
- Ch5 ~34%
- Ch8 ~38%

User-provided 《没钱修什么仙？》 research text:
- early chapters roughly span low-20s to low-40s quoted-text share;
- Ch1 is roughly mid-30s.

Therefore:
> popular fiction can be dialogue-heavy.

The key difference is dialogue function:
- strong sample dialogue often reveals a new absurd rule, hierarchy, relationship or goal;
- C01/C12 dialogue too often explains permissions, process, pricing, risk or case handling.

## Root diagnosis

### C01
The first three chapters expand verbs:
> sell -> buy -> arbitrage/broker.

After that, many chapters expand nouns/rules:
> authorization -> suitability -> dispute -> account status -> disclosure -> result delivery.

This is professionalization, not cognitive expansion.

The protagonist's social identities are also too few:
- son;
- Fang Zhi's friend;
- time-market participant.

There is too little independent:
- old job / ordinary workplace;
- wider peer group;
- neighborhood/living circle;
- competitor cohort;
- non-market aspiration.

So almost every story must come through the same time-market interface.

### C12
Broader than C01, but still heavily filtered through:
> tribulation-safety / exposure / project / clinic / quote.

New locations often do not create a new mental model.
They are variations of the same risk-management lens.

The successful Ch11—15 Life-First correction proves people can widen the book, but the overall story surface still needs another independent social engine beyond the safety industry.

## Scene-description diagnosis

The issue is not “add purple prose.”

Needed:
- concrete spatial anchors;
- object interactions;
- movement through space;
- body state;
- environmental friction;
- other people doing unrelated things;
- visible social texture.

Current generic labels such as “市一院 / 交易所 / 会展” often function as utility locations.
The prose quickly returns to dialogue/UI/process.

This produces screenplay/case-summary feel.

## Method correction

Future reader tests must include:
> **Voluntary Stop Shelf Test**

Reader may stop at any chapter/line.
Once the reader stops:
- exact stop point is primary evidence;
- do not force completion;
- later chapters cannot retroactively improve the continuation score.

## Editorial consequence

1. C01 current Ch4—20 should NOT be treated as forward-authority prose.
2. C01 should be rebuilt from the early arc, not line-polished.
3. C12 should not be discarded, but must undergo a novelty-bandwidth / second-social-engine audit before any Ch21.
4. Both books need:
   - more cognitive expansion;
   - more inhabited scenes;
   - fewer dialogue exchanges whose main job is process explanation.
5. Do NOT interpret this as “randomly add locations/POVs.”
   The target is:
   > new desire / new social layer / new relationship / new world implication / new action verb / new unanswered question.
