## Why it still fails

### 1. Character vectors are subordinate to exposition
Zhou Hang is the clearest example.
He is a paying client, yet behaves like a technical consultant for Cheng Ye.

This is not a local dialogue bug.
It proves:
> the scene has been written from "what information must Reader receive",
not from "what does Zhou Hang want".

### 2. Prose is over-shaped
Many paragraphs end with a neat interpretation or thesis.

Reader can feel:
> setup -> explanation -> conclusion.

Too little:
> mess / hesitation / incomplete social reaction / ordinary motion.

### 3. Cultivation was added as ontology labels
V7 names more cultivation things,
but does not yet make the world feel more inhabited.

### 4. Action is clear but over-instrumented
The body chain is mechanically correct,
but prose often reads like replay analysis.

### 5. Ancient scene is still serving modern plot
Ji Changgeng appears at exactly the right place,
performs exactly the useful branch,
then looks back.

The mechanism demands resonance,
but prose must imply:
> he had an independent crisis before Cheng Ye saw him.
