# REBOOT-V4 Root Cause & Restart Point
## 这次到底要从哪里重开

# 0. Final restart decision

Restart from:
> **MARKET RESEARCH QUESTION / MARKET DESIRE MAP**

Do NOT restart from:
- A02 prose;
- P0 benchmark;
- current 6-candidate re-audit;
- Reservoir;
- Character;
- Horizon.

Reason:
> the candidate-selection bias begins inside the original Market Dossier question itself.

# 1. Earliest corrupted assumption

Original Market Dossier asked:
> “what observable book designs most increase breakout + sustained income?”

Its required fields emphasized:
- first-chapter promise;
- payoff speed;
- repeatable engine;
- relationship engine;
- progression;
- update sustainability;
- newcomer executability.

It did NOT make PRIMARY READER FANTASY / topic demand the central unit of analysis.

Therefore the synthesis naturally converged on:
> low comprehension + fast premise + fixed engine + recurring arena + compounding state + sustainable production.

These are mostly:
> commercial survival architecture.

They are not the same as:
> why readers emotionally/aspirationally want this category.

# 2. Concept Forge amplified the bias

Lane A had 25 required fields.

Only one field was:
> Reader fantasy.

Many fields rewarded:
- recurring engine;
- recurring social arena;
- permanent state;
- no-fresh-gimmick;
- production burden;
- AI fixed-engine support;
- AI state compressibility;
- hallucination blast radius;
- anti-genericity source;
- draft-to-publish gap;
- 1.5M-character capacity.

This makes an implicit objective function:
> stable, trackable, serializable operations.

That systematically favors:
- repair;
- property;
- logistics;
- shop;
- brokerage;
- rescue procedure;
- factory;
- community management.

# 3. Lane B made the same error differently

Lane B selected:
> 《大一实习，你跑去749收容怪物》

as Primary Benchmark.

But it mirrored:
- packaging;
- first-chapter cadence;
- reward timing;
- relationship cadence;
- progression;
- long-run compounding

while intentionally changing:
- setting;
- protagonist role;
- power;
- event chain.

The fatal omission:
> it also changed away the PRIMARY READER FANTASY.

749's appeal is not merely:
> task -> reward -> organization progression.

It includes:
> monsters / danger / special identity / power / elite access / abnormal world.

Mirroring the structural rhythm while replacing that fantasy with:
- infrastructure;
- repair;
- logistics;
- factory;
- shop operations

is not high-fidelity commercial mirroring.

It is:
> **fantasy-stripped structural cloning.**

# 4. Desire Cut failed to catch it

Desire Cut asked 10 questions.

Most were about:
- recurring engine;
- scene variety;
- recurring social arena;
- permanent state;
- no-fresh-gimmick;
- AI execution;
- identity;
- derivative risk;
- long runway.

It explicitly noticed:
> both lanes converged toward profession / operation / asset compounding.

But instead of reopening the root selection objective,
it retained “story-forward controls” and continued.

This was the wrong decision.

# 5. Shelf test was insufficient

Anonymous shelf click answers:
> “would I click this package once?”

They do NOT prove:
> “do I want this fantasy repeatedly for 100 chapters?”

A clever contrast title can win a click while hiding a weak long-term fantasy.

Therefore shelf click cannot validate topic demand by itself.

# 6. Later gates were not wrong; they were answering downstream questions

Reservoir proved:
> can this produce many situations?

Character Collision proved:
> can recurring people have agency?

Minimal Horizon proved:
> can the desire/scale grow coherently?

All useful questions.

But if the primary fantasy is weak:
> they only prove that an unwanted book can be produced coherently for a long time.

Hence:
> all those outputs become archive evidence, not active selection proof.

# 7. What remains valid

Keep as process assets:
- voluntary author/read behavior outranks design;
- benchmark causality / negative control;
- originality boundaries;
- later Reservoir / Character / Horizon methods;
- AI continuity/state tools;
- production-risk diagnostics.

Keep raw factual market evidence where still current.

Invalidate as selection authority:
- old Market Dossier synthesis;
- old Lane A/B candidate pool;
- Desire Cut;
- AI shelf ranking;
- current ACTIVE/RESERVE privileges;
- all downstream candidate passes.

# 8. New root question

New market research must ask:

> **2025–2026 current male web-fiction readers are repeatedly paying attention to WHAT FANTASIES / EMOTIONAL EXPERIENCES, and why?**

Not:
> which structure is easy to serialize?

The unit of analysis becomes:
> READER FANTASY / DESIRE FAMILY.

Examples:
- power growth;
- killing / danger dominance;
- forbidden knowledge / mystery;
- survival;
- wealth / ownership;
- status reversal;
- conquest / leadership;
- exploration;
- collection/evolution;
- romance / attachment;
- sensory comfort / food / home;
- historical agency / rewriting fate.

Profession / system / organization / setting is recorded separately as:
> DELIVERY VEHICLE.

# 9. New research requirement

For each major fantasy family:
1. current strong hits;
2. newcomer / low-author-credit hits;
3. matched ordinary/weaker controls;
4. measurable market evidence;
5. what exact repeated pleasure readers receive;
6. what first 1–3 chapters actually do;
7. what remains pleasurable after novelty fades;
8. breakout differentiator;
9. delivery vehicles that work;
10. delivery vehicles that look clever but weaken desire.

# 10. New human decision point

After Market Desire Map:
> AUTHOR chooses / ranks the mother fantasies worth entering.

AI does NOT rank them.

Only after author chooses:
> direct benchmark deep read -> concept generation.

# 11. New concept order

For each author-selected fantasy:
- 2–4 genuinely different concepts;
- title + one-line;
- primary fantasy;
- protagonist fantasy;
- distinctive premium;
- 3 must-see scenes;
- direct hit analogs;
- obvious risk.

Do NOT yet require:
- 1.5M runway;
- full Reservoir;
- AI state compressibility;
- detailed relationship map;
- long-run architecture.

# 12. New proof order

Market Desire Map
-> AUTHOR selects fantasy families
-> Direct hit + matched-control deep read
-> small concept forge
-> AUTHOR ranks concepts
-> 300–600 char micro-prose
-> AUTHOR stop/continue
-> 800–1500 P0
-> AUTHOR stop/continue
-> only then Reservoir / Character / Horizon
-> P1 / P3 / later.

# 13. One sentence

> **重开点不是“把现有Concept写好”，而是回到市场研究第一问：先搞清楚读者真正在买什么幻想，再让作者亲自决定进入哪个欲望池。**
