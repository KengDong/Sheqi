# Candidate-specific proof burdens

## B1 可缺席帝国
Strength:
> very strong old-state / organizational compounding.

Opening risk:
> management fiction / abstract delegation.

Must prove:
> forced absence hurts a concrete human situation, while delegation produces a visible win and a personal cost.

## B2 劫债杠杆
Strength:
> low case supply; debt timing truly compounds.

Opening risk:
> advance debt -> win -> advance debt again.

Must prove:
> one accelerated gain and one permanent safety/body/relationship cost in the same arc; at least one important problem is solved WITHOUT advancing another debt.

## B3 天道赊境
Strength:
> scalable credit engine and strong old-asset compounding.

Opening risk:
> finance lecture / clever debt arbitrage.

Must prove:
> borrowed realm is immediately legible, visually useful, and settles into a permanent owned fruit.

## B4 限制越多，异能越强
Strength:
> old restrictions remain useful and dangerous deep into runway.

Opening risk:
> restriction collection / author-made combat puzzle.

Must prove:
> one or at most two restrictions are enough for the first arc; enemy/reality exploits an existing restriction before mini-climax.

## B5 岗位就是异能
Strength:
> strongest reward diversity among reserves; low case supply.

Opening risk:
> bureaucracy / meeting / permission explanation.

Must prove:
> job authority is visible in physical action; at least one person accepts/rejects a new job for their own career/life reason.

## B6 幸存者必须给下一轮立法
Strength:
> extreme old-rule compounding.

Opening risk:
> puzzle + meeting + vote grammar.

Must prove:
> first survivor-written law creates an immediate bodily/social consequence and returns again before mini-climax.

## B7 个人十级封顶，组织才能继续升级
Strength:
> strong group/life/asset compounding, low case supply.

Opening risk:
> organization panel / game-management abstraction.

Must prove:
> first group unlock comes from feeding/protecting/retaining real people; at least one low-combat/high-life-value member changes the outcome.

## B8 修旧设备，不卖，拿去收租
Strength:
> very strong recurring old-asset compounding.

Opening risk:
> machine-of-the-week / business tutorial.

Must prove:
> the same first machine and/or renter returns before mini-climax and matters more the second time.
