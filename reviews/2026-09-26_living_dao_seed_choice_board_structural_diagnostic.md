# 1. Root problem

Current options over-answer:
> how the mechanism works.

They under-answer:
> why a reader specifically wants "the Dao seed is a LIVING PERSON."

Existing language:
- live seed grows branches;
- shared Dao;
- possibility tree;
- blind spots;
- dual scoring.

These are system descriptions.

They do not yet fully exploit the strongest title-native contradiction:

> **my cheat is a person I cannot own.**

A living person:
- has independent goals;
- can refuse;
- can lie / hide;
- can make choices that hurt my interests;
- can get injured / disappear / sever cooperation;
- changes for reasons unrelated to me;
- has people he cares about more than me;
- can become stronger in a direction I do not want.

This must become progression logic, not just relationship flavor.

# 2. Why current options feel slow

## Problem A｜payoff is abstract
"grow a branch / co-create a Dao / discover a blind spot" is less immediately graspable than:
- eat drug → body changes;
- cross world → this worthless thing is valuable;
- exam score → teacher / resources change.

Need:
> a first payoff a reader can picture and value without learning terminology.

## Problem B｜world asymmetry is too clean
"modern = many answers / ancient = good conditions" is analytically neat but feels author-engineered.

Need:
> differences that emerge from concrete local life and sometimes reverse direction.

## Problem C｜two protagonists are not yet specific enough
"modern methodical / ancient decisive" is AI-cardboard.

Need:
> each has a concrete local goal that would generate a novel even without the other.

## Problem D｜why these two connect is reverse-engineered
"blank seed + unnamed Dao embryo resonance" is a serviceable explanation, not an irresistible causal truth.

Need:
> connection logic that arises naturally from the core cultivation system or can be safely delayed without harming the early book.

## Problem E｜first 20 chapters are too balanced / planned
Current V1 rhythm:
> modern win → ancient win → conflict → shared pattern.

This is structurally neat but predictable.

Strong early books feel:
> current pressure produces the next scene,
not:
> framework requires each side to take a turn.

## Problem F｜"live person" is still replaceable
If the ancient lead could be replaced by:
- an intelligent inheritance;
- an AI;
- a ghost teacher;
- a living database;

and 70% of the mechanism remains,
then the title promise has failed.

# 3. New highest design law

The living person must be mechanically irreplaceable.

The book should exploit:
> **the protagonist's growth source has agency.**

This means:
- progression depends on real choices the other person makes;
- the other person can refuse to produce the progression you want;
- each side's local life changes the other's available options;
- cooperation must be negotiated, not claimed;
- disagreement changes cultivation, not only dialogue.

# 4. New scene-proof gate

No next option can be offered to AUTHOR unless it includes:

1. one-sentence core rule;
2. why a living human is necessary;
3. why these two are connected OR why the answer can be safely delayed;
4. Ch1 current-life pressure;
5. first concrete payoff by Ch1–3;
6. explicit reason BOTH want a second contact;
7. second interaction must differ from the first;
8. first real conflict caused by incompatible local interests;
9. Ch20 persistent state:
   - ability;
   - relationship;
   - social status;
   - access;
10. Ch100 same core pleasure, larger scale;
11. nearest benchmark functions;
12. explicit anti-copy difference.

# 5. Candidate new center of gravity

Do not treat this as final design.

> **别人绑定的是一条听话、定型的仙路。**
>
> **我绑定的是一个活人——我的仙路会跟着他的人生变化，但他根本不归我管。**

This naturally combines:
- novelty;
- uncertainty;
- dual protagonists;
- relationship conflict;
- progression;
- information gap.

The next round should explore different concrete implementations of THIS fantasy, not different abstract Dao-seed taxonomies.
