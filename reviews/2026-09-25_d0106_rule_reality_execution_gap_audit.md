# D-0106｜Rule-Reality Execution Gap Audit
## V2宏观框架通过之后，正文前还缺哪些真正会导致“大书写不出来”的硬门

# Executive Verdict

D-0102～D-0105 have solved most MACRO architecture problems.

Do NOT add more macro engines.

Remaining risks are mainly:
> **execution repeatability, reader fairness, character magnetism, cognitive load, tonal control, serial-read rhythm, and AI-assisted continuity.**

These are not minor polish issues.
They can make a strong framework read like a designed product rather than a bingeable novel.

# 1. Promise-Latency Gate｜标题答应的“副本”不能藏太久

Current V2:
- Ch1–4 Opening;
- Ch5–8 reality/prep;
- Ch9+ second Field.

Risk:
> unknown reader likes the reality hook but may begin asking “副本呢？”

Rule:
> do not canonize Ch9 as correct.
After Field #2 prose exists, test at least TWO splice timings:
- earlier Field entry;
- current Ch9-ish entry.

Behavior decides.

The title/premise promise must be visibly fulfilled before patience debt becomes noticeable.

# 2. Fair-Play Rule Gate｜副本不能靠作者临时赢

Every major Field must satisfy:

1. lethal rule behavior is observable before the climax;
2. at least one clue supporting the winning interpretation appears before the solution;
3. midpoint reinterpretation reuses known evidence;
4. no last-minute secret clause created only to make Cheng Ye smart;
5. Reader may fail to solve it, but after reveal should think:
   > “原来前面有这个。”
not:
   > “作者现在才说。”

This is essential for puzzle/survival trust.

# 3. Field Diversity Must Be Multi-Axis

The 12 semantic motherboards are NOT enough.

Each Field Card must deliberately differ on at least 3 axes:

- rule family;
- story genre:
  rescue / heist / pursuit / social deduction / evacuation / siege / journey / competition / relationship crisis;
- spatial topology:
  vertical / open city / moving vehicle / single room network / underground / outdoor route;
- cast size:
  intimate / team / crowd;
- tempo:
  countdown / slow dread / chase / escalating waves;
- dominant emotion:
  fear / anger / grief / absurdity / triumph / betrayal / wonder;
- immediate human objective:
  save / reach / hide / escape / deliver / protect / trade / choose.

Hard:
> do not run two Fields with the same experiential grammar merely because the rule nouns differ.

# 4. Competence-Fantasy Gate｜程野必须“爽得看得见”

Access Authority is structurally elegant but can feel abstract.

Within every ~20–40 chapters, require at least one scene where:
> Cheng Ye can now do something he absolutely could not do earlier,
> and another person recognizes the difference.

Possible currencies:
- choose entry;
- preserve one person/fact during overlap;
- read a boundary;
- make a preparation decision others trust;
- refuse a dangerous return rule;
- use old returned rule in a new scale.

Also require material/social consequence:
- money;
- access;
- reputation;
- saved time/resources;
- someone specifically seeks him.

No numbers required.
Visible agency IS required.

# 5. Cost / Failure Spectrum

Do not equate “stakes” with character death.

Fields/reality can impose:
- injury;
- wrong rule exported;
- lost asset;
- broken trust;
- public blame;
- family restriction;
- lost entry opportunity;
- teammate separation;
- remembered incompatible history;
- moral debt.

Hard:
> Cheng Ye cannot repeatedly clear the Field and also choose the best public rule with only cosmetic cost.

Some wins must be:
> survival + bad settlement,
or
> good settlement + personal loss.

# 6. Magnetic Co-Lead Gate

D-0105 Field A currently has a co-lead SEED, not yet a magnetic character.

Before prototype prose, define exactly ONE candidate co-lead with:

- name;
- non-rule life desire;
- behavioral bias;
- annoying/charming trait;
- a competence Cheng Ye lacks;
- different instinct about returned rules;
- one reason they cannot simply become Cheng Ye's follower;
- reality-side reason to recur;
- optional relationship chemistry.

No romance is forced.
No romance is forbidden.

Behavioral PASS:
> clean readers spontaneously mention wanting this person back.

# 7. Reality-Story Variety Gate

The lifecycle:
> Shock -> Exploit -> Adapt -> Institution -> Normalize -> Revalue

is a WORLD model, not a mandatory story sequence.

Reality arcs must rotate actual story containers:
- family conflict;
- crime;
- rescue;
- workplace;
- money/property;
- school/social;
- travel/border;
- neighborhood;
- media/reputation;
- relationship;
- rival returner action.

Hard:
> no “this time another institution adapts” serial grammar.

Each reality span should be pitchable as a human story even if the rule name is removed.

# 8. Question-Debt / Cognitive-Bandwidth Gate

At any one time, foreground at most:
- 1 dominant Field rule family;
- 1–3 Returned Rules;
- 4–6 immediately important named people;
- 1 major terminal-mystery question;
- 1–2 secondary questions.

Before opening TWO new major mysteries:
> pay or materially narrow ONE existing mystery.

New terms must earn their memory cost.

Rule wording:
> ordinary spoken Chinese first; technical taxonomy stays backstage unless characters need it.

# 9. Tone Switching Protocol

Default tonal split:

## Field
- danger is real;
- jokes mostly come from personality under pressure;
- no punchline over fresh death/serious injury.

## Reality
- stronger black comedy allowed;
- humor comes from ordinary people rationally adapting to absurd physics.

Transition:
> after a major horror payoff, give emotional residue before switching to absurd adaptation.

Goal:
> funny world, serious consequences.

# 10. Reader-Language / Naturalness Gate

Before any prototype reaches the user:

Check:
- jargon first use;
- names/aliases anchored;
- place geometry understandable;
- rule wording readable aloud;
- no accidental warehouse-number style continuity errors;
- no semantic half-sentences;
- no “AI explains what scene already showed” sentence;
- no invented-noun pileup;
- no generic thematic closure.

Premium carries novelty.
Surface language carries clarity.

# 11. Serial-Read Rhythm Gate

Architecture span is not enough.

For actual serialization, every 3–8 chapter span should usually contain at least one:
- rule discovery;
- relationship turn;
- material gain/loss;
- danger escalation;
- answer;
- public/social reversal;
- memorable visual.

Every ~8–15 chapter span should leave one visible state change.

Chapter endings may use:
- decision;
- state change;
- reveal;
- arrival;
- consequence.

Do NOT use cliffhanger-only addiction.

# 12. Audience-Contract Alignment Gate

Title / synopsis / first 3 chapters must promise the SAME book.

If title sells:
> “副本规则带回现实”

then early prose must visibly deliver BOTH:
- reality consequence;
- actual Rule Field.

Do not package as pure infinite-flow if half the book is reality transformation.

Do not package as urban anomaly if Fields are a major engine.

# 13. Cohesion Gate｜禁止做成Benchmark缝合怪

Every engine must strengthen the unique center:

> **每一次胜利都会永久改变家。**

If an added feature is interesting but does not strengthen:
- return choice;
- home consequence;
- recurring people;
- failed-future pressure;
then cut it.

We are not assembling:
> 十日 + 诡舍 + 灵境 + 神秘复苏 + 异兽迷城.

We are using them to expose missing FUNCTIONS.

The final book must still have one identity.

# 14. Emotional-Endgame Contract

The terminal line cannot end only in:
> “创造一条新规则。”

The reader's emotional contract should be visible much earlier:

> **程野最初只想让家恢复正常；最后他必须接受“原来的正常回不来了”，并争取一个家人仍愿意共同生活、共同记得、共同选择的现实。**

The endgame solution must therefore pay:
- world rule;
- Cheng Ye's control;
- family relationship;
- recurring team;
- meaning of “home”.

This prevents intellectual ending without emotional settlement.

# 15. AI-Assisted Serialization Robustness

Because this project is heavily AI-assisted and the author is not relying on established professional-author muscle memory, continuity must be designed for maintainability.

Maintain exactly these ledgers once prose starts:

1. CORE RULE LEDGER
   - exact wording;
   - current geographic scope;
   - known loopholes;
   - public adaptations.

2. CHARACTER STATE LEDGER
   - current desire;
   - relationship state;
   - what they know;
   - unresolved debt.

3. FIELD FACT SHEET
   - observable rules;
   - hidden interpretation;
   - evidence planted;
   - casualties/costs;
   - exported rule.

4. MYSTERY DEBT LEDGER
   - open question;
   - evidence;
   - partial answers;
   - next payment window.

5. ASSET / MATERIAL LEDGER
   - money;
   - gear;
   - base;
   - access;
   - public status.

Hard:
> no Writer may silently change an exact rule or relationship state because a scene is easier that way.

# 16. Packaging / Market Refresh Gate

Before P3/final launch package:
- refresh current Qidian/Fanqie adjacent market once;
- test title/synopsis as shelf copy;
- compare promise alignment, not surface similarity;
- then freeze package for prose evaluation.

Do not repeatedly chase weekly trends during manuscript writing.

# 17. Current Priority Implication

D-0105 Card A《封门楼》 remains the first prose prototype PICK.

But the prototype brief must now explicitly test:

1. fair-play;
2. one magnetic co-lead;
3. Cheng Ye competence fantasy;
4. multi-axis experience difference from R1;
5. real cost/failure;
6. one future-fragment clue only;
7. natural language;
8. desire for BOTH:
   - reality aftermath;
   - next Field.

# Final Verdict

The framework is not missing another macro engine.

The missing layer was:
> **execution discipline that prevents a comprehensive architecture from turning into a clever, over-designed, emotionally thin AI novel.**

D-0106 closes that layer.
