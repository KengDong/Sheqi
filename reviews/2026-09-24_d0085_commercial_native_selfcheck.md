## 5. Anti-Cargo Gate

PASS with explicit risks.

Every concept performs the two-way test:
- remove Premium -> chassis remains commercially legible but becomes replaceable;
- remove Mother Market -> Premium loses the mature reward loop that makes it commercially durable.

No concept is intended to survive as pure novelty without its mother market.
