# D-0100｜Unknown-Author Breakout Reassessment

# Trigger

Author observation:
- 《劫痕道途》reads strong but “正规”, like a route a known/established author can patiently execute;
- an unknown author cannot rely on reputation-based reader patience;
- 《把一条副本规则带回现实》 still feels like it needs extra commercial food and one terminal crisis/secret;
- goal remains breakout probability and sustained income, not prestige.

# Market-Calibrated Conclusion

Current evidence supports a distinction between:

## Brand-Subsidized Slow Trust
A known author can sometimes ask readers to wait for depth.

## Self-Funded Trust
An unknown book must manufacture trust through:
- strong shelf signal;
- protagonist identity;
- immediate payoff;
- clear next rung;
- visible future-scale shadow.

Current successful Qidian examples show both:
- deep-system commercial fiction;
- personality/comedy-forward fiction.

Recent new-book tracking also shows high-concept, easily retellable pitches receiving strong early attention.

# Candidate Reassessment

## 《把一条副本规则带回现实》
Unknown-author breakout potential:
> **HIGHER NATURAL FIT**

Reasons:
- title/premise legible;
- modern vocabulary;
- immediate rule consequence;
- naturally dense future questions;
- supports black comedy;
- now has a terminal-spine candidate tied directly to Premium.

New terminal spine:
> rule fields are failed future fragments; importing rules makes those futures approach reality; overlap eventually risks present-world replacement.

This is now:
> secret + crisis + rule-choice consequence.

Status:
> Priority breakout candidate, still HIGH WATCH for formula fatigue.

## 《劫痕道途》
Long-run architecture:
> strong.

Unknown-author front-end:
> insufficiently sharp in current form.

Required reforge:
- stronger shelf promise;
- first-screen “others wash scars / Zhou Ye keeps the cost” contrast;
- earlier Zhou Ye/Ruan Qinghe chemistry;
- more aggressive immediate fruit;
- terminal crisis shadow “万劫回流”.

Status:
> architecture PASS, but BREAKOUT SURFACE NOT YET PASSED.

If D-0100 surface tests remain merely “well-written / steady / professional”:
> HOLD this candidate despite long-run quality.

# Strategic Priority

No formal Primary.

But execution priority for cold-start testing:
1. Rule-Reality first;
2. Scar immediately parallel/after, with a much harder threshold.

Reason:
> the project does not have an existing author brand to subsidize slow trust.

# Do Not Overfit

Do NOT conclude:
> all books must be comedy;
> all titles must be long clickbait titles;
> all openings must explode.

The standard is:
> identity density + payoff speed + future-scale visibility.
