# D-0093B｜Batch A Author First Read

This report records the author's spontaneous reading response after independently reading all seven anonymous Batch A Opening Arcs.

## A｜《无身份资产》｜周启

Author score:
> **80 / 100 versus popular-commercial-opening expectation**

Author reaction:
- story is genuinely gripping;
- immediate curiosity: “这个到底是怎么回事”;
- wants to know why identity/recognition has broken;
- concerned whether there is enough long-form volume after the opening;
- noticed industry-jargon friction: “箱变” is technically correct but reader-unfriendly on first use.

Core author concern:
> strong first mystery and practical-business payoff, but must prove long-run story volume rather than only repeated identity-failure / redundancy-asset rescues.

## B｜《养一条会跟我走的灵脉》｜陆照川

Author score:
> **70 / 100**

Positive:
- first sight of the moving vein is genuinely surprising;
- when Qinghemen comes to inspect, author feels real concern that someone may seize it.

Negative:
- later reading becomes less compelling;
- starts to feel like a slow cultivation / nurturing article;
- opening does not grab strongly enough compared with top commercial fiction;
- prose / naming feels awkward in places.

Specific friction:
- “渠棚”
- “菜畦”
- “三岔集”
- “分口”
- “泄口闸石”
all feel somewhat stiff / literary / engineered rather than grounded.

Character-introduction issue:
> “阿满” appears before the author has a clear sense of who this person is.

Semantic issue:
> 陆照川：“从前不算现在。”
feels incomplete / unclear, possibly missing semantic context.

Author conclusion:
> first-premise surprise works, but the arc gradually loses the author.

## C｜《劫痕道途》｜周野

> **90 / 100**

Strongest positives:
- reads very smoothly, especially compared with B;
- plot has real rises/falls and progression;
- opening mini-climax is highly satisfying;
- 阮青禾 becomes memorable without needing appearance-description;
- author spontaneously likes her and reads her as a potentially popular tsundere / attractive character;
- emotional / relational hook is strong enough that author wants her developed further.

Author concern:

Author conclusion:

## Cross-Batch Author Observation

Author notes that all seven are broadly readable and structurally coherent, but many contain:
> **small local places that feel unnatural / not fully smooth.**

Observed categories:
1. professional shorthand without reader onboarding;
2. invented place/object names that are semantically dense but not grounded;
3. character names appearing before identity anchoring;
4. dialogue that is grammatically complete but semantically one beat short;
5. occasional “writer knows what this line means, reader must infer too much” friction;
6. a general sense of structure being smooth while micro-prose sometimes feels artificial.

This is now a project-level prose-quality issue and should receive a dedicated audit.
