# 《劫痕道途》Scale Red Team

# Verdict

> **REVISION REQUIRED, NOT ROOT SCALE FAILURE.**

The architecture successfully escapes “new scar = new story” dependence.

The strongest proof:
> Ch1–100 can plausibly run on the SAME original scar through changing function:
> clue -> professional tool -> cultivation structure -> public proof -> targeted weakness.

However, three blockers remain before PASS.

# 1. PASS｜No-New-Scar Stress

PASS.

The architecture has enough fuel from:
- profession;
- medicine;
- cultivation;
- money;
- qualification;
- opponents;
- historical infrastructure;
- public competition.

This materially clears the original Case-Supply concern.

# 2. BLOCKER｜Too Much of the Scale Still Arrives Through Profession/Institution

Risk:
> “劫务司职业文变大了” rather than “cultivation novel became large.”

Ch5–100 heavily relies on:
- qualification;
- contracts;
- public service;
- industry;
- regional system.

This is useful, but if dominant:
> Reader may feel Zhou Ye is a highly specialized worker, not a cultivator whose personal destiny is exploding.

Required:
> Ch21–100 must contain at least one major arena where **cultivation itself**, not job performance, is the central desire.

Examples of function:
- Zhou Ye's own breakthrough;
- a public cultivation comparison;
- a resource/realm conflict where his path must win;
- a sect-level cultivation opportunity he personally wants.

The career system should carry cultivation, not replace it.

# 3. BLOCKER｜Personal Life Desire Is Still Thin

Current long-run desire is:
> prove/build a new path.

Good far horizon, weak daily human horizon.

Need one concrete personal life desire that persists across first ~50 chapters:
- afford/choose a treatment;
- stop living as disposable labor;
- move into a better place;
- repay a real obligation;
- gain a qualification that changes family/life.

Do NOT invent family melodrama merely to check a box.

But Reader should see:
> what getting stronger/more paid changes in his actual life.

# 4. BLOCKER｜Ch100 Public Stage Risks Feeling Like Another Assessment

“regional qualification/contract competition” can become:
> exam -> rating -> contract.

Too procedural.

Required:
> the Ch70–100 major arc must contain a **real irreversible cultivation crisis** whose public consequences exceed the certification itself.

The visible ranking/contract can be present.
It cannot be the emotional climax.

# 5. 阮青禾

PASS WITH WATCH.

Architecture gives her:
- independent credential;
- professional conflict;
- right to say “this scar must be treated.”

Required proof in prose:
> one scene/mini-arc where her decision changes another person's outcome while Zhou Ye is not the causal center.

Otherwise she remains a strong accessory.

# 6. Antagonist Adaptation

PASS.

The staged model:
> see -> measure -> exploit recovery -> standard barrier -> public competition -> copy/modify

is structurally strong.

# 7. Mystery

PASS WITH WATCH.

East Dike evolves from:
> personal accident -> old legal method -> regional/civilizational accounting.

This is scalable.

Danger:
> if every answer is delivered by records/reports, story becomes research.

Required:
> each answer must arrive through a decision/action with immediate body/money/relationship consequence.

# 8. Comedy / Personality

Current architecture correctly avoids forcing clown comedy.

Potential:
> dry pain humor + medical banter.

Needs Voice Lab proof.

# 9. Head-Tier Gap

Compared with the integrated OS, remaining missing element is:
> **a stronger non-work personal cultivation hunger.**

Once that is explicit, the architecture can plausibly support:
- Qidian paid long-run;
- Fanqie stronger personality variant.

# Required Revision

1. Add a first-50-ch personal-life ledger.
2. Make Zhou Ye's own cultivation milestone a central arc, not a side fruit.
3. Rebuild Ch70–100 climax around irreversible cultivation crisis, with qualification/contract as secondary payoff.
4. Give Ruan Qinghe one independent causal win.

After these:
> likely PASS.
