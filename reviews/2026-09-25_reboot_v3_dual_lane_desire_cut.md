# REBOOT-V3｜Dual-Lane Desire Cut
## 24 commercial nuclei -> 6 packaging survivors

# 0. Method

No weighted score.

Primary questions:
1. Would an unknown reader understand the recurring pleasure immediately?
2. Is there a strong Ch1 scene implied by the premise?
3. Can the same engine generate different types of scenes?
4. Is there a recurring human/social arena?
5. Do wins leave permanent state?
6. Can the book run without fresh gimmicks?
7. Is the concept plausible for a first serious long-form author using AI heavily?
8. Does it have enough identity to avoid “AI commercial average”?
9. Does it avoid obvious derivative mapping?
10. Is there a plausible 150w-character runway without becoming another book?

Important correction:
> AI-suitability is a constraint, not the winner function.
> We deliberately keep some higher-story-pressure concepts so the funnel does not collapse into six management simulators.

# 1. ADVANCE｜Packaging Gate

## A01｜《怪物死了，地盘还在》
Why survive:
- exceptionally legible “fight -> own consequences/assets” loop;
- action and compounding are naturally tied;
- old locations/tenants/debts can return;
- Ch1 can visibly switch from combat to ownership consequence;
- strong AI state compressibility without becoming pure bookkeeping;
- high distinctiveness inside familiar urban-supernatural demand.

Watch:
> must not become “new monster -> new property” collection.

## A02｜《末日先修水电》
Why survive:
- familiar mother market + very clear premium;
- physical payoff is visible and emotionally meaningful;
- survival, authority, community and infrastructure naturally interlock;
- ordinary chapters exist without new monsters;
- AI can help with situation generation while real-world material resists abstract AI prose.

Watch:
> must not read like a repair manual or slow civic-management diary.

## A04｜《冒险者公会救援队》
Why survive:
- strongest story-forward / danger-forward control in the pool;
- concrete objective naturally creates suspense;
- rescued people become recurring relational assets;
- failure sites create action without requiring formal puzzle design;
- preserves a lane where “story first” can beat lower production tax.

Watch:
> mission-of-week risk and fresh-location invention burden.

## A10｜《没人要的主播，都被我签红了》
Why survive:
- high click legibility and public-feedback reward;
- cast growth naturally becomes the second engine;
- comedy/light tone can come from recurring people rather than one-off jokes;
- strong ordinary-chapter supply;
- AI can support idea variants, but human taste remains decisive in personality.

Watch:
> must not become “new streamer collection cards” or chase temporary internet memes.

## B04｜《夜班公交：跑完一条线，我就多一段路权》
Why survive:
- strongest single visual/hook in Lane B;
- supernatural premium is simple enough to understand immediately;
- fixed lines, regular passengers and route rights create state compounding;
- city map itself becomes a visible long-run asset;
- high story potential without requiring a brand-new world every arc.

Watch:
> must not degrade into “one ghost story per stop.”

## B10｜《灵气复苏，我开二手装备店》
Why survive:
- very low comprehension cost;
- “remaining reliable uses” immediately creates buy/sell/repair/refuse decisions;
- stable shop + recurring teams gives social arena;
- inventory, trust, contracts and equipment history compound cleanly;
- very AI-friendly state and production structure.

Watch:
> generic shop/loot fatigue; must make old equipment and old customers matter.

# 4. Meta Finding

Both Lane A and Lane B converged strongly toward:
> profession / operation / asset compounding.

This is not accidental:
- market dossier rewards fixed engines;
- AI suitability rewards compressible state;
- long-run production rewards recurring social arenas.

But this convergence itself is a new bias.

Therefore later gates must explicitly test:
> **Would I read this as a story even if I did not care about its operating system?**

A04 and B04 are deliberately retained as stronger narrative-pressure controls.
