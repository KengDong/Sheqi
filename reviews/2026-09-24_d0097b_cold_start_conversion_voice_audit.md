# D-0097B｜Unknown-Author Cold-Start Conversion / Character Voice Audit

## Author Trigger

Author observation:
> Sheqi prose is often "正规、完整、中规中矩". Some commercially successful books—sometimes not the deepest or most architecturally ambitious—win readers early by making the protagonist extremely distinctive, the tone funny, the dialogue memorable, and the first few chapters easy to spread/recommend.

Author also scores:
> 顾明川 / 《天道赊境》 = 90

Reason:
> first chapters read well and feel professionally commercial, but still somewhat "中规中矩的大热门" rather than a radically distinctive cold-start voice.

# 1. First-Principles Correction

For an unknown author, there are TWO independent commercial problems:

## Problem A｜Long-Run Retention
> Why should I keep paying/reading at Ch50, Ch100, Ch300?

Solved by:
- world/engine scale;
- compounding;
- visible runway;
- characters/relationships;
- mode changes;
- macro promise.

## Problem B｜Cold-Start Conversion
> Why should I give you Ch2/Ch3 when I do not know your author name?

Solved by:
- immediate voice;
- protagonist personality;
- tonal identity;
- humor / tension / attitude;
- memorable social interaction;
- one-line premise;
- first-chapter payoff.

A book can be excellent at A and fail before readers discover it.

Therefore:
> **Head-Tier Architecture and Cold-Start Conversion are separate gates.**

- ten-wan average subscription;

Its official synopsis sells:
> protagonist's absurd logic / public reputation / comedic worldview.

The commercial lesson is NOT "all books must be comedy."

It is:
> the protagonist has a recognizable behavioral signature before the reader knows the full world.

Synopsis uses several short life-stage statements from protagonist 王平.
Before the reader knows the full system, they already know:

Current official evidence:

## Fanqie mid/high commercial examples
Examples such as:

These are not necessarily the highest literary/architectural samples.
They prove:
> **there is a real market for personality-first cold starts.**

# 3. Why Sheqi Often Feels "正规"

Current Sheqi writers tend to optimize:
- clear causality;
- credible institution;
- no cartoon villain;
- real costs;
- clean mini-climax;
- non-expository worldbuilding.

All are good.

But they produce a side effect:
> protagonists often behave like highly competent, reasonable adults whose personality is subordinate to the plot engine.

Symptoms:
1. dialogue mostly serves decision-making;
2. humor is sparse and situational rather than character-generated;
3. protagonist rarely has a weird preference / petty flaw / private logic;
4. narration has professional neutrality;
5. supporting cast often enters through function;
6. memorable lines come from concept/theme, not personality;
7. the book can sound "good" before it sounds uniquely itself.

This is the **Professional Neutrality Risk**.

# 4. Unknown-Author "Cold-Start Tax"

A famous author may survive:
> 5k–20k words of controlled setup

because existing readership grants patience.

An unknown book receives less patience.

Therefore first 1–3 chapters should pay a Cold-Start Tax:
> **higher-than-normal density of identity, contrast, tonal flavor and immediate reward.**

Not necessarily more action.

Potential currencies:
- protagonist voice;
- humor;
- shamelessness;
- competence porn;
- strange but consistent personal rule;
- emotional sharpness;
- relationship chemistry;
- outrageous but intuitive premise;
- quotable line;
- social reversal;
- visible immediate fruit.

# 5. This Does NOT Mean "Make Everything Funny"

Three viable cold-start surfaces:

## A. Personality-Comedy
Reader continues because:
> "I want to watch this person react to things."

Best for:
- lighter cultivation;
- urban supernatural;
- system / strange rules;
- workplace fantasy.

## B. Personality-Intensity
Reader continues because:
> protagonist has a hard, unusual, dangerous way of acting/thinking.

Can be:
- ruthless;
- hyper-competent;
- stubborn;
- morally strange;
- obsessive.

## C. Relationship-Chemistry
Reader continues because:
> two characters together are immediately fun / tense / emotionally alive.

《劫痕道途》 currently has evidence here through 周野 × 阮青禾.

A book may use one dominant surface, not all three.

# 6. New Cold-Start Gate

By the end of ~3 chapters, a target reader should be able to answer:

1. Describe the protagonist WITHOUT mentioning the power/premise.
2. What would this protagonist do differently from a generic competent MC?
3. Which line/action/interaction is memorable because of personality?
4. What is the book's tonal identity?
5. Is there at least one recurring relationship with chemistry?
6. Does the first payoff arrive before patience runs out?
7. If author name were hidden, would the opening still create a reason to trust the next chapter?

If #1–4 are weak:
> architecture quality does not excuse weak cold-start conversion.

# 7. Candidate Implications

## 《天道赊境》｜顾明川｜Author 90

Why it currently works:
- first sentence is direct;
- premise is instantly legible: borrow a realm now, pay later;
- immediate rescue/combat fruit;
- hidden cost lines create an advantage;
- dialogue with fixed characters is readable;
- cultivation chassis is familiar.

Why it can still feel "正规":
- 顾明川 is currently more "competent protagonist using a clever mechanism" than a person with an unmistakable behavioral signature;
- prose tone is professional but not yet unmistakably this book;
- humor/personality is not a primary retention asset.

Do NOT force him into a clown/comedy lead.

Instead test:
> can 顾明川 gain one strong human signature—e.g. his attitude toward debt, bargains, fairness, embarrassment, risk, or reputation—that repeatedly generates scenes and dialogue?

Preserve 90-point strengths.

## 《劫痕道途》

Already has a stronger character-retention seed:
> 周野 × 阮青禾.

Optimize not by adding jokes everywhere, but by:
- making 周野's own behavioral quirks more recognizable;
- protecting 阮青禾's sharp independent voice;
- allowing conflict/humor to arise from medical/body intimacy and different risk tolerances.

## 《把一条副本规则带回现实》

Current premise cold-start is strong.
Risk:
> 程野 becomes a neutral rule-solver.

Need a distinct survival/social personality that can carry mundane reality scenes even when no rule puzzle is active.

# 8. Benchmark Strategy Correction

D-0097 cannot use only absolute top books as benchmarks.

Add a parallel sample:
> **High-Conversion Personality / Tone Books**

Select 8–12 works across Qidian/Fanqie, including:
- top-tier;
- second-tier but strong conversion;
- strong first-3-chapter reputation;
- humor/personality-forward examples.

Research question:
> what makes readers attach BEFORE long-run architecture is visible?

Focus:
- first 500 / 2k / 5k / 10k words;
- protagonist signature;
- first joke/attitude/reversal;
- dialogue chemistry;
- chapter-title promise;
- immediate fruit;
- prose orality;
- quotability;
- "tell a friend in one sentence" factor.

Do not confuse:
> crude / meme-heavy / noisy
with
> high conversion.

# 9. Final Principle

> **Architecture makes a book worth staying for; personality/tone makes an unknown book earn the chance to be stayed with.**

Sheqi needs both.

The desired result is NOT:
> every protagonist jokes.

It is:
> **no protagonist should feel replaceable by a generic competent male lead.**
