# Genre Desire Failure Postmortem
## 结构学会了，为什么还会选出“末日先修水电”

# 0. Core verdict

The process overlearned:
> HOW a commercial web novel stays structurally alive.

It underlearned:
> WHAT readers actually want to fantasize about for hundreds of chapters.

This is not a small execution bug.
It is a candidate-selection bias.

A02 exposed it clearly:
> apocalypse + plumbing / power maintenance + allocation fairness

has a stable engine,
but the engine itself is not obviously a high-demand primary reader fantasy.

# 1. Category demand is causal, not cosmetic

A “mature chassis” is not merely a wrapper.

Readers do not consume:
> structure in the abstract.

They consume fantasies such as:
- becoming stronger;
- killing / defeating terrifying enemies;
- gaining money / land / status / followers;
- uncovering forbidden secrets;
- surviving impossible danger;
- rewriting fate / history;
- being uniquely competent and publicly revalued;
- collecting / evolving / upgrading;
- intense romance / attachment / jealousy;
- sensory comfort / food / home / nostalgia;
- belonging to a compelling group or world.

A stable procedure does not substitute for these desires.

# 2. Occupation fallacy

The prior process overvalued:
- maintenance;
- property;
- repair;
- brokerage;
- insurance;
- logistics;
- community management;
- shop operations.

Reason:
these are:
- state-compressible;
- easy for AI to track;
- easy to generate recurring scenes;
- naturally compounding.

But:
> production convenience is not reader desire.

A profession can work only when it is a VEHICLE for a strong fantasy.

Examples:
- rickshaw driver works when every day converts into martial growth / survival / national crisis;
- internship works when it becomes monster hunting / special-agency identity / power;
- apocalypse “supplier” works when it means entering doomed worlds to save clients under countdown pressure;
- restaurant works when food itself provides sensory pleasure, social reaction, money, nostalgia and comfort.

The occupation itself is rarely the fantasy.

# 3. Why A02 survived too long

## Shelf gate
AI readers rewarded:
- clear contrast title;
- easy comprehension;
- visible consequence.

But “would click once” was mistaken for:
> “would repeatedly desire the core fantasy.”

## Reservoir
A02 generated many situations.
This proved:
> story supply.

It did NOT prove:
> readers want those situations.

## Character Collision
It proved residents can have independent wants.
It did NOT prove:
> a community infrastructure dispute is a desired entertainment center.

## Horizon
It proved the story can scale from one building to a network.
It did NOT prove:
> readers want that scale.

Therefore every structural gate was answering the wrong question.

# 4. Specific process bias

The candidate forge explicitly rewarded:
- stable engine;
- recurring social arena;
- permanent state;
- AI state compressibility;
- bounded research;
- 1.5M-character capacity.

This systematically selects “operational fiction.”

That is a hidden objective function:
> easiest to manage for AI.

It is NOT the project objective:
> most likely to create mass desire.

# 5. New top-level distinction

Every concept must separate:

## A. Primary Reader Fantasy
What does the reader repeatedly WANT to experience?

Examples:
- power;
- fear + forbidden knowledge;
- revenge;
- wealth/ownership;
- public status reversal;
- conquest;
- exploration;
- romance;
- sensory comfort;
- collection/evolution.

## B. Delivery Vehicle
What job / system / setting delivers it?

Examples:
- 749;
- school;
- restaurant;
- guild;
- shop;
- repair crew;
- family clan.

Hard:
> NEVER confuse vehicle with fantasy.

# 6. New candidate Gate

Before any engine / Reservoir test, a concept must answer:

1. What is the primary reader fantasy?
2. Is that fantasy visibly demanded in current market evidence?
3. What repeated scene gives that fantasy every few chapters?
4. If the job/profession skin is removed, is there still a desirable fantasy?
5. Would a reader daydream about being this protagonist / seeing this situation?
6. Is the fantasy strong enough before “it compounds well” is considered?

If answer is weak:
> do not rescue with production stability.

# 7. AI ranking policy

AI comparative ranking is removed from final selection.

AI may:
- research;
- identify hard failures;
- verify evidence;
- detect repetition;
- check originality;
- summarize differences;
- run logic/continuity QA.

AI may NOT:
- crown final candidate;
- produce final winner ranking for author;
- use synthetic reader preference to overrule author taste.

Author receives:
> a small set of evidence-qualified candidates and personally compares/ranks them.

# 8. Current candidate consequence

A02:
> remove from ACTIVE pending extraordinary market proof.
Current default: DROP / archive as process evidence.

A01/A04/B10:
> no automatic survival.
They must be re-audited on PRIMARY READER FANTASY before any more prose.

B04/A10 and previously cut nuclei:
> may be reconsidered if earlier structural selection bias suppressed stronger direct fantasies.

# 9. One sentence

> **我们之前选的是“最容易稳定生成故事的发动机”，现在必须改成“读者最想反复体验的幻想”，然后才问它能不能写长。**
