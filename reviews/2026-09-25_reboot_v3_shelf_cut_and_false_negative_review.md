# 3. Why We Do NOT Cut to 1–2 Now

Because Shelf tests only:
> acquisition.

It does not test:
- scene supply;
- character magnetism;
- prose voice;
- emotional pressure;
- novelty decay;
- serial production.

Therefore:
> reducing to 1–2 here would confuse “best package” with “best novel.”

That is a false-negative risk.
