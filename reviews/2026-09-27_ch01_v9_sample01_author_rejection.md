# Ch01 V9 Sample01｜Author Rejection

status: HARD AUTHOR FEEDBACK
date: 2026-09-27

Author verdict:
> 不像说人话。

Interpretation:
- dialogue still reads as authored/function-delivery language rather than spontaneous human speech;
- characters still explain context too fully;
- replies are too clean, balanced and purpose-built;
- narrator still over-organizes what people mean.

Hard correction for next sample:
1. dialogue should be shorter but not slogan-like;
2. characters speak from immediate local need, not from what Reader needs explained;
3. leave shared-context information unsaid;
4. no narrator sentence explaining why a line was reasonable / funny / buyer-like;
5. avoid polished punchlines;
6. let pauses, topic shifts and action carry subtext;
7. technique explanation should be embedded in visible action, not in an instructional paragraph.

Do not expand to full chapter until author says the local dialogue reads like people speaking.
