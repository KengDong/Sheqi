# Ch01 V15｜Whole-Chapter QA

## Benchmark application
Applied high-level mechanisms observed across early chapters of 《谁让他修仙的！》:
- ordinary baseline before comic/plot turn;
- humor from event causality, not only quips;
- wrong/sideways practical interpretation can alter action;
- information gaps create delayed payoff;
- once a funny setup exists, later consequence/callback does the work;
- action is described only until the reader understands the decisive rule, then the scene moves.

No source wording, scene sequence, or character configuration is copied.
