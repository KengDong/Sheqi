# Reader Contract

> **规则再怪，她也会想办法把日子继续过下去。**

# Core Personality

Do NOT choose between “高粘度 / 小太阳 / 小坏”.

They are different layers.

## Base Personality
> **小太阳 + 嘴碎 + 有一点小坏 + 护短。**

She is:
- alive;
- fast;
- socially perceptive;
- capable of teasing family;
- willing to bend small rules;
- not afraid to push back on Cheng Ye.

## Post-Disappearance Layer
Cheng Ye disappearing for 18 days creates:
> **新增的关系黏性 / separation aftereffect.**

This is NOT her childhood default personality.

Examples:
- asks when he will be back;
- watches family-group messages;
- notices countdowns / departure prep;
- sleeps lightly when he is away;
- resents being left “for her own good”.

She may deny that she has become clingier.

# Long-Term Function

She is NOT:
> rescue button / protagonist's weakness token.

She can become:
> **ordinary-person adaptation engine.**

Possible strengths:
- internet/social information;
- youth culture around Returned Rules;
- family “night names”;
- discovering loopholes ordinary institutions miss;
- noticing scams / black markets / social behaviors;
- making daily life continue under impossible rules.

She must solve at least one serious reality-side problem without Cheng Ye.

# Flaw

> **她会用活泼、小聪明和嘴硬遮掩真正的害怕，并因此低估危险。**

This lets her make independent mistakes rather than only being protected.

# Sibling Relationship

Target:
> Reader wants to see them go home and interact again.

Allowed:
- grabbing sleeve when genuinely scared;
- stealing food / phone;
- family-only shorthand;
- protecting brother in front of outsiders;
- arguing about Cheng Ye's overprotection.

Avoid:
- infantilization;
- pseudo-romantic possessiveness;
- sexualized sibling coding;
- existing only to be kidnapped/rescued.

# Age
