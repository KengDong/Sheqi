# Core Engine

唐霁 is action-first under immediate human danger.

Her default question is not:
> “规则最终是什么？”

It is:
> “眼前这个人还能不能撑一分钟？”

This naturally collides with Cheng Ye's uncertainty-management style.

# Strength

- emergency medicine / rescue competence;
- quick triage;
- acts without needing complete theory;
- accepts imperfect information;
- does not wait for Cheng Ye's approval.

# Flaw

> **行动过快会留下结构性后果。**

She can save the immediate person and simultaneously:
- destroy a safe node;
- consume scarce access;
- close a future route;
- force later group cost.

This prevents “action hero = always right”.

Required:
