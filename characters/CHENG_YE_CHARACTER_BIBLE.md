# 程野｜CHARACTER BIBLE
status: CURRENT CHARACTER CORE
stable_path: yes
scope: invariant personality / behavior rules
note: mutable plot-state belongs in characters/CHENG_YE_CURRENT_STATE.md

# 1. Identity baseline
Name:
> 程野

Current-life identity:
- Earth-origin soul, crossed many years ago;
- current cultivation society is his lived home, not a foreign place he explains to himself;
- just admitted to cultivation university;
- summer before freshman enrollment;
- ordinary / limited-resource family;
- baseline living and tuition are manageable;
- extra training, recovery, private correction and upward-mobility resources require effort.

Money attitude:
> not poor-and-self-pitying.
> He thinks: 活得下去，卷不起，那就找办法搞资源.

# 2. Core temperament
Keywords:
> 轻松 / 实用 / 脑子快 / 敢试 / 会钻规则但不阴 / 乐观底色 / 有分寸.

He is NOT:
- gloomy;
- cynical;
- swaggering genius;
- shameless clown;
- cold calculation machine;
- deliberately hiding strength for face-slaps;
- permanently sarcastic.

# 3. Repeatable behavior engine
When facing a problem:
1. notices the concrete rule / incentive / constraint;
2. asks what the rule actually allows, not what people usually assume;
3. finds a slightly unconventional but logically valid route;
4. tests it himself;
5. accepts a reasonable small cost;
6. watches the result carefully;
7. updates quickly instead of defending ego.

This pattern should create both:
> humor + agency.

Opening example:
> platform wants extreme data + insurance is active
> -> he chooses to pressure his known weak transition
> -> first source resonance occurs.

The anomaly intersects HIS choice.
It does not randomly save him.

# 4. Humor engine
His humor is not joke-writing.

Main sources:
- literal reading of rules;
- practical self-interest;
- self-deprecating reframing without self-pity;
- treating absurd situations with ordinary logic;
- quick but not cruel replies.

Good function:
> humor reveals how he thinks.

Example pattern:
> someone says "we need to find your problem"
> -> he can answer from the paid-sparring angle.

Do not make every line witty.
One light turn per conversational beat is usually enough.

# 5. Serious-mode switch
When danger becomes real:
- joke frequency drops immediately;
- attention narrows;
- he checks facts;
- he prioritizes what can be acted on;
- he does not become a completely different "cold god" personality.

Desired contrast:
> 平时轻，遇事稳.

# 6. Relationship behavior
With peers:
- naturally competitive without needing hostility;
- can admit the other person is better-trained;
- does not turn every stronger peer into enemy;
- remembers useful/funny interpersonal details.

With teachers / authority:
- understands rules;
- willing to question wording / loopholes;
- not reflexively rebellious;
- if authority is competent, he respects competence.

With source-era actors:
- does not worship historical fame automatically;
- talks to the living person in front of him, not their future encyclopedia entry;
- practical clarity may feel strange to ancient actors;
- trust must be earned reciprocally;
- he can disagree and still preserve relationship.

# 7. Failure behavior
When wrong:
- first reaction is to inspect why;
- can admit a bad read;
- may joke about the miss once;
- does not blame the world to protect ego;
- modifies strategy.

When embarrassed:
> reframes lightly, then moves.

# 8. Success behavior
When he gets a win:
- enjoys it;
- checks whether it is reproducible;
- asks what access/resource it unlocks;
- does not immediately assume he is invincible.

# 9. Knowledge behavior
Earth memories:
- rarely surfaced;
- triggered by strong analogous situations;
- never used for biography dumps.

Current-world institutions:
> he thinks in native shorthand.
> Do not make him explain ordinary society to himself.

Historical knowledge:
- public facts are not treated as perfect truth;
- meeting a person can override archive assumptions;
- he should become increasingly cautious about using future biography as instructions.

# 10. Speech texture
Modern, concise, conversational.

He may:
- answer a question slightly sideways while still answering;
- make practical comparisons;
- use ordinary platform/work language naturally.

He should NOT:
- speak in constant aphorisms;
- stack memes;
- give polished speeches every scene;
- use ancient diction in modern life;
- produce 1-3 word ping-pong merely to look cool.

# 11. Internal narration
Preferred:
> concrete observation -> interpretation -> choice.

Avoid:
- lengthy self-analysis;
- repeated statements that he is clever;
- narrator praising his personality.

Let reader infer him from choices.

# 12. Moral / social floor
Current baseline:
- not cruel for fun;
- does not exploit obvious innocents for small gain;
- can exploit systems / contracts / competitive openings;
- willing to take risk on himself before casually pushing risk onto others;
- not saintly; practical interests matter.

If future plot changes these:
> update explicitly, do not drift silently.

# 13. Long-form personality development
Growth should deepen, not replace, this core.

Early:
> learns to use systems to get access.

Middle:
> learns that relationships and historical interventions create responsibility, not just leverage.

Later:
> same practical mind scales from "how do I afford training" to "how do I alter conditions that make a disaster inevitable."

Continuity:
> small-rule ingenuity should plausibly scale into structural strategy.

# 14. Absolute anti-drift guards
Do NOT write Cheng Ye as:
- permanently mouthy comic relief;
- face-slap addict;
- schemer who sees everyone as assets;
- passive cheat recipient;
- saint who never cares about his own interests;
- edgelord who becomes cold whenever stakes rise;
- idiot so the setting can explain itself;
- genius who instantly understands everything.

# 15. Scene check
Before finishing a Cheng Ye scene ask:
1. What did he notice first?
2. What did he think the rule actually meant?
3. What choice did he personally make?
4. Did humor arise from that thought process?
5. When stakes rose, did his tone appropriately tighten?
6. Did the consequence update his next decision?

If these cannot be answered:
> the scene may contain Cheng Ye's name without actually containing Cheng Ye.
