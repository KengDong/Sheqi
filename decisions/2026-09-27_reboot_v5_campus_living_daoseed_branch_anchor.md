# 3. Cheng / Zhou split inside the comedy

## Cheng Ye
Owns:
- checking official requirements;
- noticing the two-person purchase;
- calculating whether the deal is actually useful;
- refusing supplier extras that violate pre-binding rules;
- refusing private leader kickback and converting it into collective discount if such a beat survives;
- keeping the whole absurd situation technically legitimate.

Do NOT write him as:
- a merchant genius;
- student-union organizer;
- coupon obsessive;
- stand-up comedian.

Core:
> “If the rule allows it and the saving is real, why deliberately pay more?”

## Zhou Hang
Owns:
- noticing larger quantity thresholds;
- technical quality questions;
- whether the sword is actually usable;
- logistics / distribution problem once the order becomes large;
- competence pride that makes him continue after Cheng’s initial small optimization.

Do NOT make him Cheng’s echo.

Core:
> Cheng starts the optimization; Zhou is the person who makes it scale.
